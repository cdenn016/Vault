## Unconditional bound on the excess relative entropy of a Markov filter

A new theorem in the supplement section on approximate meta-agents bounds the stationary excess relative entropy of a meta-agent that filters with a declared Markov model, with no condition on the size of the closure defect, where the existing theorem needed a smallness condition. The proof rests on an exact quadratic identity for the difference of the two predictive covariances and on the contraction of the declared filter in the metric of its own predictive covariance. ## Approximate meta-agent theorem assembled

At the user's request the approximate form of the meta-agent theorem was stated as one theorem in the supplement section on approximate meta-agents, with its proof in the appendix and a check record. The hypotheses on the blanket and on the members' protocol are kept, and the exact abstract physics is replaced by a declared coarse model with a closure defect and an input defect. The blanket and the closure of the coarse internal state remain exact. The path law of the coarse exterior under the collective's own acts, the members' average as a belief about the current exterior, and the law of the joint act each carry an error term in the defects and the members' computation noise, with no smallness condition on the defects. The belief bound is the stationary bound proved earlier today, extended to include the response to the computation noise.

A member-level script built 1050 joint processes with three members and found no inequality violated. An independent proof check reconstructed every step, found no mathematical defect and no counterexample on 250 further instances in ten regimes, and corrected five sentences of the discussion that claimed more than the theorem proves, one sentence of the statement, the wording of the check record and several notation clashes; all were applied. The theorem requires exact blanket conditions with every coordinate of the coarse exterior sensed, a stable exterior and a stable joint process, members that run the consensus filter, and the centralized start with the stationary covariance. Violated blanket conditions, level-0 members, other starts, a singular sensory information matrix and an exterior stabilized only by the collective's acts are outside it and are listed as such in the text.

Nineteen passages in the main text and the supplement that described the approximate form as absent or open were reworded, and the open problems of the section were updated. Both documents were rebuilt, at 215 and 515 pages, with no undefined reference.

## Start and stability restrictions of the approximate meta-agent theorem removed

Files: `manuscripts/Collective_Agents/sections/ext1_apx.tex`, `appext1_apx.tex`, `chkext1_apx.tex`, `13_discussion.tex`, `s01_introduction.tex`, `s10a_meta_agents.tex`, and both rebuilt PDFs.

The approximate meta-agent theorem no longer assumes a stable exterior or the centralized start with the stationary covariance. Stability of the exterior alone was redundant: under the blanket hypothesis the error covariance of the exterior filter from a deterministic start is at most the covariance of the exterior in the joint process, which is bounded because the joint process is stable, and a bounded covariance recursion has a fixed point. A new part (e) of the comparison lemma for the filter equation states the equivalence of a fixed point, bounded iterates and detectability for positive definite noise, and the convergence of the recursion from every positive semidefinite start. The start may now be any law in which the exterior is Gaussian given the initial collective variables with a covariance that does not depend on them, which includes every Gaussian law of the initial state and so the stationary law of the joint process. A new proposition states the same for the stationary filter bound in its general setting: for a detectable fine process and such a start the closed-form bound holds for the upper limit of the excess relative entropy, and at each round an explicit bound in terms of the start holds that is not in closed form.

A member-level script propagated exact second moments on 700 instances, 300 of them with an exterior that is unstable in open loop and stabilized by the members' acts, from four starts each. The excess converged to the stationary value from every start and stayed below the bound in the limit; before the limit it exceeded the closed-form bound on 590 of 600 instances started with a large covariance, which is why the statement concerns the upper limit. An independent proof check reconstructed the arguments, found no mathematical error and no counterexample in its own tests, and corrected three sentences of the discussion that claimed more than the theorem proves, one sentence of a remark, one scope sentence of the proposition, two numbers of the check record and three notation clashes; all were applied, and the round-by-round inequality it pointed out in the proof was promoted to a stated bound.

Still outside the theorem are violated blanket conditions, members that run a level-0 protocol, a singular sensory information matrix, starts in which the exterior is not Gaussian given the initial collective variables, and a closed-form bound that is uniform in the round. Both documents were rebuilt, at 215 and 520 pages, with no undefined reference.
