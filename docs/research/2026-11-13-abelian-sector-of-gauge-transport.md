# The abelian sector of the gauge transport and its relation to three abelian gauge constructions

Date: November 13, 2026.

## Question and answer

The wiki sections added on November 13, 2026 describe the relation between the gauge structure of the multi-agent model and three abelian gauge constructions in the literature as an analogy. The constructions are Polettini's gauge theory of nonequilibrium thermodynamics on a Markov graph, the potential and flux decomposition of Feng and Wang for a Fokker-Planck steady state, and Sakthivadivel's reading of constrained maximum entropy as parallel transport. The question was whether any of them is a special case, a quotient or a restriction of the model's gauge structure as a matter of mathematics.

None of the three is a special case or a restriction of the model. Three exact relations hold, and they are weaker than a reduction. First, every abelian quotient of the model's gauge group factors through the determinant, and the absolute value of the determinant maps the model's transport to a lattice gauge field with Polettini's group and Polettini's transformation laws; this field is what makes the log-determinant term of each Gaussian coupling energy gauge invariant. It carries no rates, no probability over agents and no current, so it is the kinematics of Polettini's theory without its thermodynamics, and it is trivial for pure-gauge transport. Second, Polettini's construction applies as it stands to the attention matrix read as a jump process on agents, when the support of attention is reciprocal; its cycle affinities are $1/\tau$ times cycle sums of the asymmetry of the coupling energy plus cycle sums of $\log(\pi_{ji}/\pi_{ij})$, which vanish for a symmetric prior, and changes of frame of the model leave the chain and its gauge transformations untouched. Third, the constructions of Sakthivadivel and of Feng and Wang live on the sample space of a single agent, which is a fiber of the model's bundle, and a change of frame acts on their structures by a linear diffeomorphism of that sample space together with a constant rescaling of density values, so the only elements of their gauge groups it supplies are the constant ones.

Two derivations were made independently of this one, by a reader working from associated bundles and characters and by a reader working from Polettini's master equation; both reached the statements below, and they supplied the uniqueness in Proposition 1, the role of the row prior in Proposition 4 and the remark on masked attention. The identities were also checked numerically at random instances with three and four agents, where they hold to rounding error; the scripts are scratch files and are not kept in the vault. Numerical agreement is a check of the algebra and is not offered as proof.

## Setting and conventions

Agents sit on the vertices $V$ of a finite graph. Agent $i$ holds a belief $q_i=\mathcal N(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)$ on $\mathbb R^K$ and a frame $U_i\in GL(K)$. A change of frame $g_i\in GL(K)$ acts by pushforward, $q_i\mapsto g_{i\#}q_i=\mathcal N(g_i\mu_i,g_i\Sigma_ig_i^\top)$, and by $U_i\mapsto g_iU_i$. The transport from $j$ to $i$ is $\Omega_{ij}=U_iU_j^{-1}$ in the basic model and $\Omega_{ij}=U_iV_{ij}U_j^{-1}$ in the non-flat variant, with $V_{ij}$ independent of the beliefs and reciprocal, $V_{ji}=V_{ij}^{-1}$, which is assumed throughout. In both cases $\Omega_{ij}\mapsto g_i\Omega_{ij}g_j^{-1}$ and $\Omega_{ji}=\Omega_{ij}^{-1}$. The coupling energy and the attention weights are
$$E_{ij}=D_{KL}\bigl(q_i\Vert\Omega_{ij\#}q_j\bigr),\qquad \beta_{ij}=\frac{\pi_{ij}e^{-E_{ij}/\tau}}{Z_i},\qquad Z_i=\sum_k\pi_{ik}e^{-E_{ik}/\tau},$$
with a row prior $\pi_{ij}\ge0$ and temperature $\tau>0$. These conventions are those of the wiki pages [[GL(K) gauge group]], [[Gauge transformation]], [[Multi-agent variational free energy]] and [[Non-flat connection and the photon analogy]]. Write $\tilde\mu_j=\Omega_{ij}\mu_j$, $\tilde\Sigma_j=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_j\Omega_{ij}^\top$ and $\delta_{ij}=\mu_i-\tilde\mu_j$ for the quantities transported into the frame of $i$.

Polettini's conventions, in the notation of [[polettini-2012-nonequilibrium-thermodynamics-gauge]], are as follows. The rate from state $j$ to state $i$ is $w_{ij}$, the current is $J_{ij}=w_{ij}\rho_j-w_{ji}\rho_i$, and the connection is $A_{ij}=\log(w_{ij}/w_{ji})$. A gauge transformation is $\rho_i\mapsto e^{-\varphi_i}\rho_i$ with $w_{ij}\mapsto w_{ij}e^{\varphi_j}$, under which $A_{ij}\mapsto A_{ij}+\varphi_j-\varphi_i$. Along a path $\eta=(i_0,i_1,\dots,i_n)$ the length is $\Sigma(\eta)=\sum_{\kappa=1}^nA_{i_\kappa i_{\kappa-1}}$, and for a closed path it is the cycle affinity, the logarithm of the Wilson loop.

## The determinant sector

**Proposition 1 (the abelian quotient).** Every homomorphism from $GL(K,\mathbb R)$ to an abelian group factors through the determinant, and the continuous homomorphisms to $(\mathbb R_{>0},\times)$ are $g\mapsto|\det g|^s$ for real $s$. Under a change of frame $g$ the values of a density and its differential entropy transform as
$$(g_\#q)(gx)=|\det g|^{-1}q(x),\qquad h(g_\#q)=h(q)+\log|\det g|.$$
With $\varphi_i=\log|\det g_i|$ and $a_{ij}=-\log|\det\Omega_{ij}|$, the density values at agent $i$ transform by $e^{-\varphi_i}$ and $a_{ij}\mapsto a_{ij}+\varphi_j-\varphi_i$, which are Polettini's laws for $\rho_i$ and $A_{ij}$. For $\Omega_{ij}=U_iU_j^{-1}$ the field $a_{ij}=\log|\det U_j|-\log|\det U_i|$ is exact and every cycle sum vanishes. In the non-flat variant the cycle sum of $a$ around $\eta$ is $-\log|\det\mathrm{Hol}_\eta|$, where $\mathrm{Hol}_\eta=\Omega_{i_ni_{n-1}}\cdots\Omega_{i_1i_0}$.

*Proof.* The commutator subgroup of $GL(K,\mathbb R)$ is $SL(K,\mathbb R)$, so the abelianization is $GL(K,\mathbb R)/SL(K,\mathbb R)\cong\mathbb R^\times$ through the determinant, and the induced homomorphism of $\mathbb R^\times$ is continuous when the original one is, because $t\mapsto\mathrm{diag}(t,1,\dots,1)$ is a continuous section of the determinant; a continuous homomorphism from $\mathbb R^\times$ to $\mathbb R_{>0}$ sends $-1$ to the positive square root of $1$ and is therefore a power of the absolute value. The transformation of density values is the change of variables formula, and the entropy shift follows by taking $-\int\log$ of it. The law for $a_{ij}$ follows from $\det(g_i\Omega_{ij}g_j^{-1})=\det g_i\det\Omega_{ij}/\det g_j$. Exactness in the basic model and the holonomy formula follow because the determinant is multiplicative, and the factors $\det U_i$ cancel around a cycle. $\square$

**Proposition 2 (where the determinant field enters the free energy).** The quantity
$$(Dh)_{ij}=h(q_j)+\log|\det\Omega_{ij}|-h(q_i)=h(\Omega_{ij\#}q_j)-h(q_i)$$
is invariant under changes of frame, and the Gaussian coupling energy is the sum of three separately invariant terms,
$$E_{ij}=(Dh)_{ij}+\tfrac12\bigl[\operatorname{tr}(\tilde\Sigma_j^{-1}\Sigma_i)-K\bigr]+\tfrac12\delta_{ij}^\top\tilde\Sigma_j^{-1}\delta_{ij}.$$

*Proof.* Under a change of frame $h(q_i)$ shifts by $\varphi_i$, $h(q_j)$ by $\varphi_j$ and $\log|\det\Omega_{ij}|$ by $\varphi_i-\varphi_j$, so $(Dh)_{ij}$ is unchanged. For Gaussians $h(q)=\frac12\log\det(2\pi e\Sigma)$, so $(Dh)_{ij}=\frac12\log\det\tilde\Sigma_j-\frac12\log\det\Sigma_i$, which is the log-determinant term of the Gaussian divergence. The trace and the quadratic form are invariant because $\Sigma_i$, $\tilde\Sigma_j$ and $\delta_{ij}$ all transform by $g_i$. $\square$

The entropy of a belief depends on the frame, and the difference of entropies between two agents is completed to an invariant by the determinant field. This is the same mechanism by which Polettini completes the rate of change of the Gibbs entropy to the invariant entropy production, with the difference that here the completed quantity is a term of a divergence and no current multiplies it.

**Proposition 3 (the quotient is not Polettini's theory).** The map from rates on a graph that are positive in both directions on every edge to connections, $w\mapsto\log(w_{ij}/w_{ji})$, is onto, and its fiber over a connection is the set of symmetric parts $\sqrt{w_{ij}w_{ji}}$, one positive number for each edge. The model supplies the connection $a_{ij}$ and none of the following: a symmetric part of the rates, a probability distribution over the set of agents, a master equation, a current. Restriction of the transport to scalar matrices does not supply them either, and a scalar $e^cI$ acts on a belief by dilation of its sample space, which rescales density values by $e^{-Kc}$ as a consequence, where Polettini's group rescales the weight of a fixed state.

*Proof.* Given $A$ antisymmetric and any symmetric positive $s$, the rates $w_{ij}=s_{ij}e^{A_{ij}/2}$ have connection $A$ and symmetric part $s$, which gives surjectivity and the fiber. Each $q_i$ is normalized separately on its own copy of $\mathbb R^K$, so no quantity of the model is a probability mass on $V$ or a flow of mass along an edge. The action of $e^cI$ is $(\mu,\Sigma)\mapsto(e^c\mu,e^{2c}\Sigma)$. $\square$

In the basic model the determinant field is exact, so only the class of detailed balance is reached, and for structure groups inside $SL^\pm(K)$, the orthogonal groups among them, the field vanishes identically.

## The attention chain

**Proposition 4 (Polettini's construction on attention).** Suppose $\pi_{ij}>0$ exactly when $\pi_{ji}>0$ and the support is connected. Read the attention matrix as the rates of a jump process on agents in which a walker at $i$ moves to $j$ at rate $\beta_{ij}$, so that $w_{ji}=\beta_{ij}$ in Polettini's indexing. Then his hypotheses hold, and
$$A_{ij}=\log\frac{\beta_{ji}}{\beta_{ij}}=\log\frac{\pi_{ji}}{\pi_{ij}}+\frac{E_{ij}-E_{ji}}{\tau}+\log\frac{Z_i}{Z_j},\qquad E_{ij}-E_{ji}=T_{ij}+M_{ij}+2(Dh)_{ij},$$
with $T_{ij}=\frac12[\operatorname{tr}(\tilde\Sigma_j^{-1}\Sigma_i)-\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma_i^{-1}\tilde\Sigma_j)]$ and $M_{ij}=\frac12\delta_{ij}^\top(\tilde\Sigma_j^{-1}-\Sigma_i^{-1})\delta_{ij}$. For a closed path $\eta$,
$$\Sigma(\eta)=\sum_{\kappa}\log\frac{\pi_{i_{\kappa-1}i_\kappa}}{\pi_{i_\kappa i_{\kappa-1}}}+\frac1\tau\sum_\kappa(T+M)_{i_\kappa i_{\kappa-1}}+\frac2\tau\log|\det\mathrm{Hol}_\eta|.$$
The chain satisfies detailed balance exactly when every $\Sigma(\eta)$ vanishes. A sufficient condition is a symmetric prior together with $\Sigma_i=\tilde\Sigma_j$ on every edge. Polettini's gauge transformations of this chain rescale the rows of $\beta$, so the normalizers $Z_i$ are pure gauge, and every change of frame of the model leaves $\beta$, and with it the chain and its connection, unchanged.

*Proof.* The first expression for $A_{ij}$ is the definition with $w_{ij}=\beta_{ji}$. Because $\Omega_{ji}=\Omega_{ij}^{-1}$ and the divergence is invariant under a common pushforward, $E_{ji}=D_{KL}(\Omega_{ij\#}q_j\Vert q_i)$, and subtracting the two Gaussian divergences gives $T_{ij}+M_{ij}+\log\det\tilde\Sigma_j-\log\det\Sigma_i$, whose last two terms are $2(Dh)_{ij}$. Around a closed path the terms $\log(Z_i/Z_j)$ and the entropies in $(Dh)$ telescope, and the remaining determinants multiply to $\det\mathrm{Hol}_\eta$. Detailed balance is equivalent to the vanishing of all cycle affinities by Kolmogorov's criterion, as in Polettini's paper. When $\Sigma_i=\tilde\Sigma_j$ both $T_{ij}$ and $M_{ij}$ vanish and $(Dh)_{ij}=0$. Polettini's transformation $w_{ij}\mapsto w_{ij}e^{\varphi_j}$ multiplies all rates out of $j$, which is row $j$ of $\beta$. The energies $E_{ij}$ are invariant under changes of frame, so $\beta$ is. $\square$

The gauge group of this chain is therefore a second copy of one positive number for each agent. As an abstract group it is isomorphic to the determinant quotient of Proposition 1, but changes of frame leave $\beta$ unchanged, so no change of frame induces a nontrivial gauge transformation of the chain. The determinant holonomy of the model appears in the affinity as one term among three.

**Proposition 5 (independence of flatness and detailed balance).** Flatness of the transport and detailed balance of the attention chain do not imply one another.

*Proof.* For flat transport without detailed balance, take $K=1$, three agents with equal means, variances $1$, $2$ and $4$, $\Omega_{ij}=1$ and a uniform prior. Then $M=0$, the determinant term vanishes, $T_{ij}=\sinh(\log\sigma_i^2-\log\sigma_j^2)$, and the affinity of the three-cycle has absolute value $\lvert2\sinh(\log 2)-\sinh(\log 4)\rvert/\tau=0.375/\tau$. For curved transport with detailed balance, take frames $U_i=I$, identical beliefs $\mathcal N(0,I)$ in $K=2$, rotations $V_{ij}$ (so that every $\Omega_{ij}=V_{ij}$ is orthogonal) with nontrivial holonomy around a triangle and a symmetric prior. Then every $E_{ij}=0$, so $\beta_{ij}=\pi_{ij}/Z_i$ and $A_{ij}=\log(Z_i/Z_j)$ is exact. $\square$

**Proposition 6 (leading order).** In the basic model with a symmetric prior, let $\theta$ be the natural parameters of the Gaussian family in a common frame and let $D_1,D_2,D_3$ be the differences of $\theta$ along the three steps of a triangle, each of size $\epsilon$. Then, with $D_\kappa=\theta_{i_\kappa}-\theta_{i_{\kappa-1}}$ along $\eta=(i_0,i_1,i_2,i_0)$, the affinity of the triangle is $\Sigma(\eta)=\psi'''(\theta_{i_0})(D_1,D_2,D_3)/(2\tau)+O(\epsilon^4)$, and reversing the orientation changes its sign. Here $\psi$ is the log-partition function and $\psi'''$ its third derivative, the Amari-Chentsov tensor in natural coordinates.

*Proof.* For an exponential family $D_{KL}(p_\theta\Vert p_{\theta+\Delta})=\psi(\theta+\Delta)-\psi(\theta)-\psi'(\theta)\Delta$ and $D_{KL}(p_{\theta+\Delta}\Vert p_\theta)=\psi(\theta)-\psi(\theta+\Delta)+\psi'(\theta+\Delta)\Delta$. Expanding to third order, the difference of the first and the second is $-\frac16\psi'''(\theta)(\Delta,\Delta,\Delta)+O(\Delta^4)$. Summing over the three steps with $D_1+D_2+D_3=0$ and using $D_1^3+D_2^3+D_3^3=3D_1D_2D_3$ for a symmetric trilinear form gives $-\frac12\psi'''(D_1,D_2,D_3)$ for the cycle sum of the energy asymmetry, and the affinity $\Sigma(\eta)$ is that sum divided by $-\tau$, since $A_{i_\kappa i_{\kappa-1}}$ contains $(E_{i_\kappa i_{\kappa-1}}-E_{i_{\kappa-1}i_\kappa})/\tau$. Moving the base point of $\psi'''$ between vertices changes the result at fourth order. $\square$

The affinity is thus third order in the separation of beliefs and comes from the difference between the divergence and its dual. A symmetric divergence with a symmetric prior gives a chain with detailed balance.

Two limits of this reading should be stated. The model defines the matrix $\beta$ and does not define a walker; reading $\beta$ as rates is a construction added here, exact as mathematics and interpretive as a statement about the model. Under a causal or otherwise ordered attention mask every edge is one-way, Polettini's hypothesis of positive rates in both directions fails, and no affinity is defined.

## The vertical constructions

**Proposition 7 (position of the single-system constructions).** For agent $i$ let $J_i(x)=\frac12(x-\mu_i)^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}(x-\mu_i)$. Sakthivadivel's bundle for the constraint $J_i$ is the trivial bundle with group $\mathbb R_{>0}$ over the sample space $\mathbb R^K$ of agent $i$, his connection is $dJ_i=-d\log q_i$, and $q_i$ is its covariantly constant section. A change of frame $g$ acts on this structure by the linear diffeomorphism $x\mapsto gx$ of the base together with the constant factor $|\det g|^{-1}$ on the fiber. A constant factor is a gauge transformation with $d\varphi=0$, so it leaves the connection unchanged. For Feng and Wang, a diffusion on $\mathbb R^K$ with constant positive definite diffusion matrix $D$ and drift $-(D+Q)\nabla J_i$, with $Q$ constant and antisymmetric, has stationary law $q_i$ and a stationary flux proportional to $q_iQ\nabla J_i$; its connection, $D^{-1}$ applied to minus the drift, is $dJ_i+D^{-1}Q\nabla J_i\cdot dx$, and its curvature vanishes exactly when $Q=0$, in which case the connection is $dJ_i$. The model contains $\Sigma_i$ and neither $D$ nor $Q$.

*Proof.* With $\lambda=1$, $q_i\propto e^{-J_i}$, so $d\log q_i=-dJ_i$ and $(d+dJ_i)q_i=0$. Under $g$ the density becomes $|\det g|^{-1}q_i\circ g^{-1}$, which is the pullback of the section by $g^{-1}$ times a constant. For the diffusion, $\operatorname{tr}(Q\nabla^2J_i)=0$ and $\nabla J_i^\top Q\nabla J_i=0$ by antisymmetry, so the flux $q_iQ\nabla J_i$ is divergence free and $q_i$ is stationary; the form of the connection is that of [[feng-2011-potential-flux-decomposition]]. The one-form $D^{-1}Q\Sigma_i^{-1}(x-\mu_i)\cdot dx$ is closed exactly when $M=D^{-1}Q\Sigma_i^{-1}$ is symmetric, and then antisymmetry of $Q=DM\Sigma_i$ gives $DM\Sigma_i+\Sigma_iMD=0$, which for positive definite $D$ and $\Sigma_i$ forces $M=0$ and hence $Q=0$. For a state-dependent $Q$ the equivalence can fail, and the statement is made for constant coefficients only. That the model has no diffusion on the sample space is a statement about its definition. $\square$

The base of these constructions is a fiber of the model's bundle over the agent graph, so they are vertical where the model's transport is horizontal. The two meet in the decomposition $E_{ij}=\langle\tilde J_j-J_i\rangle_{q_i}+(Dh)_{ij}$ with $\tilde J_j=J_j\circ\Omega_{ij}^{-1}$: the first term is the mean under $q_i$ of the change of constraint that carries $q_i$ to the transported $q_j$, and the second is the covariant entropy difference of Proposition 2.

## What is not established

Whether the model should carry a stochastic process on the set of agents is a modeling question that this note does not answer. Reciprocal support and an ordered mask exclude each other: with reciprocal support the attention chain has the affinities of Proposition 4, and with an ordered mask, which the exact reading of attention as the conditional probability of a source label requires, the jump process still exists but has no affinities. The affinities were computed at fixed beliefs; nothing was derived about them along the belief dynamics or at its fixed points. One of the independent derivations states, for equal reference covariances and attention held fixed, that the receiver-only mean flow is a gradient flow in an agent-weighted metric exactly when the attention chain satisfies detailed balance; that statement was not checked here and is not relied on. A companion statement for unequal covariances is a conjecture of the same derivation.
