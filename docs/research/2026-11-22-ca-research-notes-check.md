# Check of the Collective_Agents research notes and their integration into the manuscripts (2026-11-22)

Scope: the twelve investigation records under `manuscripts/Collective_Agents/docs/research/` (with the raw reports and round-3 synthesis of the meta-agent record and all thirteen check folders), their integration into `manuscripts/Collective_Agents` and `manuscripts/MAgent_Theory_2.0`, and the resulting update of `manuscripts/research_to_do.md`. The check itself edited no manuscript and changed only `research_to_do.md`; the corrections applied afterward at the author's request are listed in their own section below.

Companion files: [appendix A, checker reports](2026-11-22-ca-research-notes-check_checker-reports.md) (per-claim tables and all 118 findings), [appendix B, integration map](2026-11-22-ca-research-notes-check_integration-map.md) (134 manuscript items, the MAgent bearings, the to-do proposal as drafted, and the completeness critic), and `2026-11-22-ca-research-notes-check-artifacts/` (the reviewers' scripts and outputs, and the fresh reruns of the notes' own scripts).

## Verdict

No defect that breaks a core claim was located in any of the twelve records. Of 126 claim rows, 76 were confirmed, 24 confirmed with a correction, 5 found to state a status stronger than their evidence, 2 found defective, and 19 not verified (computed constructions compared with stored outputs only, open claims, and review-log entries that could not be audited). The two defects are a repairable step in `open_prv_minimal.md` C3 and the round-3 synthesis of the meta-agent record, which still marks as proved two statements that round 4 refuted.

The larger problems are of scope and integration. Five records say more in their status line or verdict than their claims establish. Five of the twelve records (seven files with their check folders) were never committed and nothing from them is in the manuscript. The checks also exposed manuscript sentences that are false or stale as they stand, independently of whether any new result is integrated; two of these are contradicted by the manuscript's own formulas or numerical checks.

No change to the mathematics of MAgent_Theory_2.0 is forced. One sentence of its chapter 17 conflicts with its own citation rule, and one note result gives a candidate remark for an open problem of its chapter 18.

Every result here has had one fresh-context reviewer and no adjudication. Skeptic and defender passes were not run, because they are a separately requested step.

## What was executed

All fourteen note-level verification scripts were re-run once, sequentially, on two threads. Each exited with code 0. Twelve reproduce their stored output line for line; `verify_uniform_bound.py` and `verify_prv_minimal.py` differ only in printed residuals at the 1e-10 level or below. The stored Monte Carlo and box-scan scripts of the meta-agent record were not re-run.

Nine fresh-context proof checkers each reconstructed the derivations of the claims marked proved, tested them on small and degenerate instances with their own code and planted-error controls, audited whether each stored script could fail, and compared the notes with the live `.tex`. Seven mappers then verified the checkers' leads against the manuscripts and produced the integration map, and a critic looked for gaps. I verified five of the most consequential findings against the sources myself (the noiseless-record sentence from the manuscript's displayed formula, the level-1 expansion count in `chk:min-disagreement`, the chapter-17 sentence against `AUTHORING.md`, the `(F5)` sentence of `def:agy-nulls`, and the persistent-circulation claim against the note's own C3).

## Major findings in the records

These are the checkers' findings rated major, with corrections from the mappers and the critic where the sources support them. Identifiers refer to appendix A.

`open_prv_minimal.md` C3 (prv_aware_minimal/M1). The asynchronous schedule fails when the last of the disjoint paths is the direct edge, because one step then sends every remaining item to an endpoint and the counting argument no longer applies. The repair is to take a path with an internal vertex, which changes the constant θ − 1 to θ − 2. The asymptotic statements are unaffected.

`open_prv_aware.md` C1(b) and status (M4, S1). The rank per window, with the window cut at the member's next report, is a valid lower bound per message. It is not jointly achievable when evidence about a window's interior arrives after that report: in an exact scalar instance the per-window numbers sum to 3 while the transcript needs 4. The record should be scoped or reopened.

`open_prv_minimal.md` C4 (S2). The "only if" half of the dichotomy uses C3, which for paths of length two holds only without a shared sub-event clock; C4 does not state that restriction.

`open_agy_uniform_bound.md` verdict summary (agy_uniform_bound/M1). The lower bound against all group posteriors of the second class holds only for declared beliefs that are independent across blocks. On the note's own slot model the reviewer built a weakly correlated declared belief that reproduces the limit law to total variation 3e-23, so the first alternative of the open problem is not refuted in general. The factor proved necessary is |A|^(1/8), not |A| − 1. This rests on one computation at the smallest size and is the first item to recheck independently.

`open_agy_uniform_bound-checks/verify_uniform_bound.py` (X1). The script prints the limit values of C3 and C4 and never asserts them, and its two "channels" are two solvers of one transition matrix. The reviewer's `chk2_c3_tree.py` in the artifacts folder computes the matrix-tree factorization with a planted wrong exponent as a control.

`open_agy_meta_agent_exists.md` C8 (meta_agent_rounds12/M1). The consequence "no collective in setting P is a meta-agent under strict typing" uses two assumptions the statement omits (the comparison medium held fixed under the null, and r0 ≥ n − 1) and fails in a computed instance when the baseline is recomputed from the null's precision. C17b of the same record concedes the dependence.

`open_agy_meta_agent_exists.md` R11 (meta_agent_rounds34/F1). Two of the three conditions for reopening the pure-collider pair are met by the adopted rulings, and the record does not state the verdict. If the pair passes, two agents that never interact are a meta-agent by explaining away. The formation mapper found that the standard Collective_Agents cites decides this: MAgent's lemma `inh:no-interaction-null` makes agents that do not interact fail (F5), while `def:agy-nulls` says a null that leaves the model unchanged imposes no requirement.

`open_coh_amplification_noisy.md` C4 (coh_amplification_noisy/F1). C4 to C7 bound the spectral radius of the cycle product of one frozen round. The record's own C3 shows that for noisy codes a cycle product below one does not prevent growth under persistent circulation with retained own terms; the C4 witness itself grows (spectral radius of the sequential operator 1.084).

`open_lrn_noncentral.md` status (record_contrast_lrn/F1). The manuscript's problem asks for the second moment in closed form. A closed form exists only for rank-one codes with an isotropic prior; the rest is an integral representation with a proof that no elementary form exists.

`open_lrn_noncentral.md` and `open_hie_record_contrast.md` evidence (F2). The proofs of both records are in an agent scratch folder outside the vault, and the lrn record states neither the C2 integrand nor the C3 coefficients. Neither can be cited from the manuscript until the derivations are copied in.

## Manuscript text that is wrong or stale today

These need correction whether or not any new result is integrated. The list merges the four area maps and applies the critic's rulings; the full table with quoted text is in appendix B.

The example's own formula `eq:hie-noiseless` gives member off-diagonal entries (ω/n − a_ij)/q, so the precision is diffusive when the collective is closed, δ = 0 and a_ij ≥ ω/n for every pair. The example's unconditional conclusion and its sentence "With outsiders both fail" need the same condition (the joint precision does fail with outsiders; the marginal precision can fail and need not). `rem:col-which-contrast` in `s04_collectives.tex` repeats the claim.

## Bearing on MAgent_Theory_2.0

No statement of MAgent_Theory_2.0 is contradicted by any record, and most leads are interpretive parallels that break on the objects compared (deterministic recognition flows against sampled policies, a dictionary variable per edge against one shared world state). Four points are worth acting on.

`chapters/17_communication_links.tex`, in the paragraph on weakly connected graphs, contains a sentence that names "the companion manuscript on collective agents". `AUTHORING.md` forbids citing another unpublished manuscript by the author, and the proof is given locally, so the sentence can be removed. The earlier premise that MAgent makes no reference to Collective_Agents is therefore inaccurate.

`chapters/18_link_dynamics.tex`, open problem `it2:open-convergence`. On a directed ring with zero loop weight, the Jacobian of the chapter's per-agent mean flow has spectrum {−1} together with −1 + ζ for the roots ζ of the eigenvalues of the Collective_Agents cycle product, and the instability instance in that open problem is `ex:coh-amplification`(i). Through that identity, claim C4 of the amplification note says the law-level flow on a directed ring is stable for every K, which MAgent does not state. This is a candidate remark that MAgent would have to prove locally. It rests on one reviewer's derivation, and the same reviewer found numerically that the statement does not extend to graphs in which one sender is heard by two or more receivers. The persistent-circulation growth of the note does not transfer, because MAgent anchors each agent to a fixed self mean.

The differ clause of `def:agy-nulls` in Collective_Agents says it "is clause (F5)" of the formation standard. The quantifier skeleton matches. The admitted class, the compared object, the constant set (MAgent requires it declared in advance with nonempty interior) and the set of nulls (MAgent requires differing from two deletion nulls and treats the frozen environment as a control) all differ. The wording changes belong in Collective_Agents.

The cross-manuscript reports of 2026-11-21 say the graded meta-agent of `def:agy-meta-agent` has no exhibited instance. That is outdated on the Collective_Agents side (one instance by computation at sampled points, open-subset clause unproved). The MAgent side is unchanged: no construction passes (F4), (F5) and (F6b) together, and the audience is not an MAgent construction. A dated addendum is the right fix; the dated reports were not edited.

## Changes made to `research_to_do.md`

One correction was required. The Cautions paragraph said the world-conditional construction "already separates a blanket produced by a shared driver from one produced by coupling". `def:col-world-conditional` keeps a broadcasting agent's sensing noise as a private input, so a common source outside a candidate collective survives the conditioning; the audience instance of `obs:agy-audience` is a collective at contrast 5.38 produced entirely by such a source, and the manuscript's own `ex:agy-collider` and `ex:agy-masking` show the construction erring in both directions once agents act on the conditioned variable. The paragraph now states what the construction removes, names the member-restricted order parameter as the separating statistic, and notes that `def:agy-meta-agent` reports it and does not require it.

Two citation corrections were made: `sec:fw-reception` does not contain the transport (the translation is in `def:fw-translation`), and `prop:col-markov-vs-correlation` gives the Markov length exactly for the hub family only.

Workstream 1 gained a dated status note and one checklist item on the mode of convergence of the limit theorem, because the plug-in lift under Gaussian code-model error has no second moment for K < r_c + 2 and no first moment for a square code (`thm:lrn-understanding-moment`, already a manuscript theorem). Workstream 2 gained one checklist item on recording whether the conditioned world variable is exogenous to the sites. A final section lists the manuscript backlog in seventeen checklist items with preconditions. No existing checklist item was ticked, because no record discharges one.

Four optional bearings drafted by the mapper were left out as interpretive (a loop observable for workstream 1, a diffusivity pre-test and a domain-length calibration for workstream 2, and a contrast-as-ratio note for workstream 3); their text is in appendix B. The three dated work folders the file cites as sitting on the Desktop are now under `ActInf_1.0\docs`; those paths were left as written.
