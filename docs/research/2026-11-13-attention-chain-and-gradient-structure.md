# Detailed balance of attention and the gradient structure of belief dynamics

Date: November 13, 2026.

## Question and answer

The note [[2026-11-13-abelian-sector-of-gauge-transport]] shows that the attention matrix, read as a jump process on agents, has cycle affinities and generally violates detailed balance. It left unchecked a claim made by one of its independent derivations: that with equal covariances and attention held fixed, the receiver-only mean flow is a gradient flow in an agent-weighted Fisher metric exactly when the attention process satisfies detailed balance. This note settles that claim and what surrounds it.

The claim is true, and it is the standard equivalence between reversibility of a Markov generator and its self-adjointness in the weighted space of its reversing measure. Its scope is narrower than it first appears, in four ways. It concerns the receiver-only truncation; the full natural-gradient flow of the free energy is a gradient flow for every attention matrix and, for equal covariances, does not see the antisymmetric part of attention at all. Under its own hypotheses, with the model's Gibbs attention and a reversible prior, detailed balance holds automatically, so the affinity of the earlier note cannot arise there. When attention responds to beliefs, the receiver-only flow is a game, which is not a weighted potential game in general but has an ordinal potential when the coupling energy is symmetric and the prior is reversible. With unequal covariances held fixed, detailed balance of attention does not give a gradient structure, and it does not in general give stability.

For the program the consequence is about oscillation. A first-order receiver-only flow can spiral toward consensus when attention is irreversible, with no inertia. The linearization of the full flow at any equilibrium has real spectrum, so the full flow cannot spiral there at linear order. An observed spiral therefore does not identify second-order dynamics.

Three derivations were made independently, by the coordinator and by two readers working from dynamics and from variational structure; they agree on every statement below that is marked proved. The formulas and the counterexamples were also checked numerically at random instances and at the stated values, where they hold to rounding error. The scripts are scratch files and are not kept in the vault.

## Setting

Conventions are those of the earlier note and of [[Multi-agent variational free energy]]. Frames are fixed and transport is flat, so beliefs are compared in a common frame with reference means $\xi_i$ and covariances $C_i$, precisions $\Lambda_i=C_i^{-1}$, and $E_{ij}=D_{KL}(q_i\Vert q_j)$. Attention is $\beta_{ij}=\pi_{ij}e^{-E_{ij}/\tau}/Z_i$ and $F_{i,\mathrm{red}}=-\tau\log Z_i$, so that $\nabla_{q_i}F_{i,\mathrm{red}}=\sum_j\beta_{ij}\nabla_{q_i}E_{ij}$ with no term from the derivative of $\beta$. Only the coupling between agents is treated; the terms that tie each belief to its own prior are left out except in the last example. The full flow is the Fisher natural-gradient flow of $\sum_iF_{i,\mathrm{red}}$, in which agent $i$ moves down the gradient of every term that contains $q_i$. The receiver-only flow keeps, for agent $i$, only the gradient of its own $F_{i,\mathrm{red}}$ and drops the terms $\sum_k\beta_{ki}\nabla_{q_i}E_{ki}$ in which $q_i$ appears as a sender. Agent $i$ moves at rate $\kappa_i>0$.

## Frozen attention

**Proposition 1 (the two flows).** For any exponential family with natural parameters $\theta_i$, the receiver-only natural-gradient flow is
$$\dot\theta_i=\kappa_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}(\theta_j-\theta_i).$$
For Gaussians with means and covariances moving at the same rate this is $\dot\Lambda_i=\kappa_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}(\Lambda_j-\Lambda_i)$ together with the same equation for $\Lambda_i\xi_i$. With covariances held fixed, the receiver-only mean flow is $\dot\xi_i=\kappa_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}C_i\Lambda_j(\xi_j-\xi_i)$, which for equal covariances is $\dot\xi_i=\kappa_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}(\xi_j-\xi_i)$. For equal covariances the full mean flow is $\dot\xi_i=\kappa_i\sum_j(\beta_{ij}+\beta_{ji})(\xi_j-\xi_i)$.

*Proof.* With log-partition function $\psi$ and expectation parameters $\eta=\nabla\psi$, $D_{KL}(p_{\theta_i}\Vert p_{\theta_j})=\psi(\theta_j)-\psi(\theta_i)-(\theta_j-\theta_i)\cdot\eta_i$, whose gradient in $\theta_i$ is $-G(\theta_i)(\theta_j-\theta_i)$ with $G=\nabla^2\psi$ the Fisher matrix. Multiplying by $-\kappa_iG(\theta_i)^{-1}$ and summing with weights $\beta_{ij}$ gives the first display. For Gaussians $\theta=(\Lambda\xi,-\Lambda/2)$. With the covariance fixed, $\nabla_{\xi_i}E_{ij}=\Lambda_j(\xi_i-\xi_j)$ and the Fisher metric on the mean is $\Lambda_i$. In the full flow the sender terms add $\sum_k\beta_{ki}\nabla_{\xi_i}E_{ki}=\sum_k\beta_{ki}\Lambda_i(\xi_i-\xi_k)$, which after multiplication by $C_i$ gives the symmetrized weights. $\square$

In the rest of this section attention is a fixed matrix whose support is strongly connected, so that the attention process is irreducible and has a unique stationary distribution with positive entries, $r_{ij}=\kappa_i\beta_{ij}$ are the rates of the attention process, $\mathcal L$ is the matrix with $(\mathcal Lx)_i=\sum_jr_{ij}(x_i-x_j)$, and covariances are equal, so the flow is $\dot\xi=-(\mathcal L\otimes I_K)\xi$. This is the backward equation of the process: $\xi_i(t)$ is the expectation of the initial means under a walker started at $i$.

**Proposition 2 (agent-local gradient structure).** Let the metric be block diagonal with one positive definite $K\times K$ block $M_i$ for each agent. The flow is the gradient flow of some function in this metric exactly when the support is reciprocal, $M_i=m_iB$ for one positive definite $B$ and weights $m_i>0$, and $m_ir_{ij}=m_jr_{ji}$ on every edge. Such weights exist exactly when every cycle affinity of the process vanishes, and then $m_i$ is proportional to the stationary probability $\rho_i$ of the process with rates $r_{ij}$, equivalently to $\nu_i/\kappa_i$ with $\nu$ the stationary distribution of the stochastic matrix $\beta$, and the function is $\Phi=\frac12\sum_{i<j}m_ir_{ij}\lvert\xi_i-\xi_j\rvert_B^2$. The choice $B=C^{-1}$ is the claim under test.

*Proof.* A linear field $-A\xi$ is the gradient field of a function in a constant metric $M$ exactly when $MA$ is symmetric. For $A=\mathcal L\otimes I_K$ and block-diagonal $M$ the off-diagonal blocks give $r_{ij}M_i=r_{ji}M_j$, so along every edge the blocks are proportional and both rates are positive; on a connected support $M_i=m_iB$ and $m_ir_{ij}=m_jr_{ji}$. The diagonal blocks $M_i\sum_jr_{ij}$ are then symmetric. Existence of the weights is Kolmogorov's criterion (Kolmogorov 1936), and self-adjointness of a reversible generator in the space weighted by its reversing measure is standard (Kelly 1979). $\square$

**Proposition 3 (global gradient structure).** The flow is a gradient flow of some function with respect to some constant inner product on the whole configuration space exactly when $\mathcal L$ is diagonalizable with real eigenvalues. This is strictly weaker than detailed balance.

*Proof.* A matrix is the product of a positive definite and a symmetric matrix exactly when it is diagonalizable with real eigenvalues: if $A=PS$ then $P^{-1/2}AP^{1/2}=P^{1/2}SP^{1/2}$ is symmetric, and if $A=VDV^{-1}$ with $D$ real diagonal then $A=(VV^\top)(V^{-\top}DV^{-1})$. This holds for $\mathcal L\otimes I_K$ exactly when it holds for $\mathcal L$. The rates $r=\begin{pmatrix}0&5&1\\1&0&1\\2&1&0\end{pmatrix}$ give the cycle current $J=9/28\ne0$ and the spectrum $\{0,4,7\}$, so the process is irreversible with real simple spectrum. $\square$

**Proposition 4 (detailed balance is automatic in the equal-covariance sector).** If covariances are equal, the prior is reversible, $u_i\pi_{ij}=u_j\pi_{ji}$, and $\beta$ is the model's attention at the current beliefs, then $u_iZ_i\beta_{ij}=u_i\pi_{ij}e^{-E_{ij}/\tau}$ is symmetric, so the attention process satisfies detailed balance at every configuration with stationary weights proportional to $u_iZ_i/\kappa_i$.

*Proof.* For equal covariances $E_{ij}=\frac12\lvert\xi_i-\xi_j\rvert^2_{C^{-1}}$ is symmetric. $\square$

So in the setting of the claim the affinity can come only from an irreversible prior, for example a directed mask, or from an attention matrix frozen at a configuration with unequal covariances or not produced by the model's attention rule; freezing it at another equal-covariance configuration keeps detailed balance by Proposition 4. The third-order affinity of the earlier note requires unequal covariances.

**Proposition 5 (without detailed balance).** Let $\rho$ be the stationary distribution of the process, unique and positive because the process is irreducible, $c_{ij}=\frac12(\rho_ir_{ij}+\rho_jr_{ji})$ and $J_{ij}=\rho_ir_{ij}-\rho_jr_{ji}$. Then
$$\dot\xi_i=\frac1{\rho_i}\sum_jc_{ij}(\xi_j-\xi_i)+\frac1{2\rho_i}\sum_jJ_{ij}\xi_j,$$
where the first term is the gradient flow of $\Phi_\rho=\frac12\sum_{i<j}c_{ij}\lvert\xi_i-\xi_j\rvert^2_{C^{-1}}$ in the metric with blocks $\rho_iC^{-1}$ and the second is skew-adjoint in that metric. The weighted mean $\bar\xi=\sum_i\rho_i\xi_i$ is conserved, and $V=\frac12\sum_i\rho_i\lvert\xi_i-\bar\xi\rvert^2_{C^{-1}}$ obeys
$$\dot V=-\sum_{i<j}c_{ij}\lvert\xi_i-\xi_j\rvert^2_{C^{-1}}=-2\Phi_\rho.$$
The current $J$ vanishes exactly under detailed balance, and it does not enter the dissipation.

*Proof.* Split $\rho_ir_{ij}$ into its symmetric and antisymmetric parts and use $\sum_jJ_{ij}=0$, which is stationarity. Conservation of $\bar\xi$ is $\rho^\top\mathcal L=0$. For the dissipation, $\sum_i\rho_i\langle\xi_i,(\mathcal L\xi)_i\rangle=\frac12\sum_{i,j}\rho_ir_{ij}\lvert\xi_i-\xi_j\rvert^2$ for a stationary $\rho$, by expanding the square and using stationarity on the term $\sum_{i,j}\rho_ir_{ij}\lvert\xi_j\rvert^2$. $\square$

This is the decomposition of a stationary generator into reversible and irreversible parts, the discrete counterpart of the Helmholtz decomposition in [[dacosta-2023-entropy-production-stationary-diffusions]]: the reversible part dissipates and the current rotates.

**Proposition 6 (spirals).** Two agents never spiral. For three agents with nonzero eigenvalues $s_1,s_2$ of the reversible part $\mathcal L_s$, $(\mathcal L_sx)_i=\rho_i^{-1}\sum_jc_{ij}(x_i-x_j)$, with $\rho$ normalized to sum one, and cycle current $J$, the nonzero eigenvalues of $\mathcal L$ are
$$\lambda_\pm=\frac{s_1+s_2}2\pm\sqrt{\frac{(s_1-s_2)^2}4-\frac{J^2}{4\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3}},$$
so the means spiral toward consensus exactly when $\lvert J\rvert>\sqrt{\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3}\lvert s_1-s_2\rvert$. For the uniform cycle with $\beta_{i,i+1}=p$, $\beta_{i,i-1}=1-p$ and $\kappa_i=1$ the eigenvalues are $\frac32\pm i\frac{\sqrt3}2(2p-1)$, a spiral for every $p\ne\frac12$, with cycle affinity $3\log(p/(1-p))$.

*Proof.* For two agents the difference obeys $\dot d=-(r_{12}+r_{21})d$. For three, in an orthonormal basis of the complement of the constants in the space weighted by $\rho$, the reversible part is $\mathrm{diag}(s_1,s_2)$ and the skew part is $\omega$ times the rotation generator, so the eigenvalues are $\frac{s_1+s_2}2\pm\sqrt{(s_1-s_2)^2/4-\omega^2}$; computing the skew part from $J_{12}=J_{23}=J_{31}=J$ gives $\omega^2=J^2/(4\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3)$. For the uniform cycle $\mathcal L=I-pP-(1-p)P^\top$ with $P$ the cyclic shift, whose eigenvalues are the cube roots of unity. $\square$

A bound of Ohga, Ito and Kolchinsky (2023) relates the ratio of imaginary to real part of every eigenvalue of such a generator to its cycle affinities; one of the derivations read it in the primary text and reports that the uniform three-cycle saturates it. The bound was not rederived here.

## Unequal covariances

**Proposition 7.** If means and covariances move at the same rate, Proposition 1 gives a linear flow in natural parameters with generator $\mathcal L\otimes I$, so Propositions 2, 3, 5 and 6 hold with $\xi$ replaced by $\theta$ and with a flat metric in natural coordinates; for an irreducible process the consensus belief is the log-linear pool with weights $\rho$. If covariances are held fixed and unequal, the mean flow has matrix weights $Q_{ij}=r_{ij}C_i\Lambda_j$, and it is a gradient flow in a block-diagonal metric exactly when the attention process satisfies detailed balance with weights $m_i$ and there is one positive definite $R$ with $\Lambda_iR\bar P_i$ symmetric for every $i$, where $\bar P_i=\sum_j\beta_{ij}\Lambda_j$; the blocks are then $M_i=m_i\Lambda_iR\Lambda_i$. The Fisher blocks $m_i\Lambda_i$ occur only when the covariances are proportional along edges.

*Proof.* Symmetry of $MA$ off the diagonal reads $M_iQ_{ij}=Q_{ji}^\top M_j$, that is $r_{ij}C_iM_iC_i=r_{ji}C_jM_jC_j$ after multiplying by $C_i$ on the left and $C_j$ on the right, so $C_iM_iC_i=m_iR$ with detailed balance for $m$. The diagonal block is $M_i\sum_jQ_{ij}=m_i\kappa_i\Lambda_iR\bar P_i$. For $M_i=m_i\Lambda_i$ the off-diagonal condition is $m_ir_{ij}C_i=m_jr_{ji}C_j$. $\square$

The commutation condition holds for two agents, for commuting covariances with $R=I$, and for equal covariances. It fails in general, and then detailed balance gives neither a gradient structure nor stability.

**Counterexample 8.** Take four agents on a path with $\beta_{14}=1$, $\beta_{23}=1$, $\beta_{32}=\beta_{34}=\frac12$, $\beta_{41}=\frac13$, $\beta_{43}=\frac23$, $\kappa_i=1$, which satisfies detailed balance with $m=(1,2,4,3)$, and fixed covariances
$$C_1=\begin{pmatrix}0.81&-1.05\\-1.05&1.77\end{pmatrix},\quad C_2=\begin{pmatrix}0.47&0.33\\0.33&0.57\end{pmatrix},\quad C_3=\begin{pmatrix}0.18&-0.38\\-0.38&7.37\end{pmatrix},\quad C_4=\begin{pmatrix}0.92&2.25\\2.25&6.60\end{pmatrix}.$$
All four are positive definite, and the receiver-only mean flow has an eigenvalue with positive real part, about $0.062$, so disagreement grows. The example is due to one of the independent derivations and was reproduced here; the other derivation found a different unstable example on a path of four agents.

A mean-only receiver update with fixed anisotropic covariances therefore has no convergence guarantee, while the joint flow of Proposition 7 converges for every attention matrix with connected support.

## Attention that responds to beliefs

**Proposition 9 (an ordinal potential).** Let the coupling energy be symmetric, $E_{ij}=E_{ji}$, identically on the configuration space, as for means with equal covariances held fixed (Gaussian divergences are not symmetric once covariances move), and let the prior be reversible, $u_i\pi_{ij}=u_j\pi_{ji}$. Put $\Psi=-\tau\sum_{i<j}u_i\pi_{ij}e^{-E_{ij}/\tau}$. Then $\nabla_{q_i}\Psi=u_iZ_i\nabla_{q_i}F_{i,\mathrm{red}}$. The receiver-only flow is therefore the gradient flow of $\Psi$ in the configuration-dependent metric with blocks $(u_iZ_i/\kappa_i)G_i$, where $G_i$ is the Fisher metric of agent $i$; $\Psi$ decreases along it, it has no periodic orbits, and its linearization at an equilibrium has real spectrum.

*Proof.* Each unordered pair appears once in $\Psi$, and $\nabla_{q_i}$ of its term is $u_i\pi_{ij}e^{-E_{ij}/\tau}\nabla_{q_i}E_{ij}$ by symmetry of $E$ and reversibility of the prior. Summing over $j$ gives $u_iZ_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}\nabla_{q_i}E_{ij}$. At an equilibrium the Jacobian of a gradient field in a metric is the inverse metric times a symmetric matrix. $\square$

With constant weights in place of $u_iZ_i$ no such function exists in general: a weighted potential would need $w_i\partial_j\partial_iF_{i,\mathrm{red}}=w_j\partial_i\partial_jF_{j,\mathrm{red}}$ with constant $w>0$, and for three scalar agents with unit variances, $\tau=1$ and a uniform prior over the other two agents the ratio $\partial_3\partial_1F_{1,\mathrm{red}}/\partial_1\partial_3F_{3,\mathrm{red}}$ changes sign between configurations (checked numerically, by a checker and again here), so the receiver-only flow is not a weighted potential game in the sense of Monderer and Shapley (1996) for three or more agents. At consensus $\beta$ equals the row-normalized prior, and the linearization of the receiver-only flow there is the frozen flow of the prior chain, so gradient structure and spirals at consensus are decided by the prior alone; the asymmetry of the divergence does not enter at linear order.

**Example 10 (a spiral with all affinities zero).** Proposition 9 concerns the coupling alone. With anchoring terms added, $\dot\xi_i=\sum_j\beta_{ij}(\xi)(\xi_j-\xi_i)+\alpha(p_i-\xi_i)$ for three scalar agents with unit variances, a uniform prior over the other two agents ($\pi_{ii}=0$, $\pi_{ij}=\frac12$), $p=(0.13595,2.25173,-1.40583)$, $\alpha=0.5019$ and $\tau=0.14746$, there is an equilibrium near $(-0.279,0.548,-0.649)$ whose Jacobian has eigenvalues $-1.376\pm0.178i$ and $-0.502$. Here the energy and the prior are symmetric, so the attention process satisfies detailed balance at every configuration, and the spiral comes from the response of attention to beliefs. The example was found numerically by one derivation and reproduced here; it is a single instance and no general statement is drawn from it.

## The full flow and what an oscillation shows

**Proposition 11.** The full flow is the gradient flow of $\sum_iF_{i,\mathrm{red}}$ in the metric with blocks $\kappa_i^{-1}G_i$ for every attention prior and every configuration of covariances, so the free energy decreases along it, it has no periodic orbits, and its linearization at an equilibrium has real spectrum. With attention frozen it is likewise the gradient flow of $\sum_{ij}\beta_{ij}E_{ij}$ in the same metric, with the same consequences. For equal covariances and frozen attention its couplings are $\beta_{ij}+\beta_{ji}$, so it does not depend on the antisymmetric part of attention, where the cycle affinity lives.

*Proof.* The first statement is the definition of the flow together with the envelope identity for $F_{i,\mathrm{red}}$; the second is Proposition 1. $\square$

The following reading is interpretive. A decaying spiral in belief trajectories does not identify inertia or second-order dynamics. Within first-order dynamics it can come from a receiver-only update with irreversible attention among three or more agents (Proposition 6), from fixed non-commuting covariances even under detailed balance (Proposition 7 removes the gradient structure; on the path of four agents with unit rate on every directed edge and covariances $\mathrm{diag}(1,16)$, $\begin{pmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{pmatrix}$, $\begin{pmatrix}5&-4\\-4&5\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathrm{diag}(16,1)$ the mean flow has the decaying pair $0.429\pm0.277i$, an example from one derivation reproduced here, while Counterexample 8 shows a real unstable mode), or from the response of attention to beliefs at an anchored equilibrium (Example 10). It cannot come from the linearization of the full continuous-time flow at an equilibrium (Proposition 11), and it cannot occur with two agents in any of the first-order flows with frozen attention. This sharpens the section on what oscillation can and cannot show in [[Hamiltonian belief dynamics]].

One further statement comes from a single derivation and was checked here only through the Routh-Hurwitz condition: for the second-order receiver-only dynamics $\ddot\xi_i+\gamma\dot\xi_i=\kappa_i\sum_j\beta_{ij}(\xi_j-\xi_i)$, a mode with eigenvalue $a+ib$ of $\mathcal L$, $b\ne0$, is unstable exactly when $\gamma^2a<b^2$. When $\mathcal L$ has a non-real eigenvalue, which for three agents happens exactly above the threshold of Proposition 6 and which irreversibility alone does not ensure, a kinetic integrator driven by receiver-only forces is therefore unstable below a damping threshold, which is $\gamma=1/\sqrt2$ for the one-way three-cycle.

## What is not established

The affinity of the earlier note was related to belief dynamics only through the frozen receiver-only flow; no statement was derived about entropy production. Both derivations observe that with added noise of covariance proportional to the inverse metric the resulting diffusion is reversible exactly when the flow is a gradient flow in that metric, which, for an agent-local metric of the form in Proposition 2, would make detailed balance of attention the condition for zero entropy production of the mean process (Proposition 3 shows that non-local metrics allow more); this requires a noise model that the program has not declared, and it is not claimed. Whether the fixed-covariance receiver-only mean flow under attention with detailed balance is always stable for three agents was observed in trials and not proved, and the statement that the receiver-only flow is not a weighted potential game rests on numerical evaluation at sample configurations. No Lyapunov function of free-energy type is known for the receiver-only flow with unequal covariances and responsive attention. Frame dynamics and non-flat transport were not treated. The statement on the bound of Ohga, Ito and Kolchinsky rests on one reader's reading of the primary text.
