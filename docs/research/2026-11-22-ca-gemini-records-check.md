# Check of four new Collective_Agents research records and their bearing on the manuscript (2026-11-22, second pass)

Scope: the four records added under `manuscripts/Collective_Agents/docs/research/` after the first check of this date (`open_col_extent.md`, `open_idn_sharp.md`, `open_lrn_strategic.md`, `open_mis_dynamic_ratio.md`, with their check folders), and the edits made to two earlier records (`open_agy_uniform_bound.md`, `open_hie_record_contrast.md`). The records were written by another model. Each new record was read by one reviewer working from a fresh context, with the record, its proof write-up, its script and the live `.tex` files; no adjudication panel was run. The reviewers' scripts and the reruns of the records' own scripts are in `2026-11-22-ca-gemini-records-check-artifacts/`.

## Verdict

No reviewer located a mathematical defect that breaks a claim. Of the 27 claims, the proofs that were reconstructed hold; the corrections concern wording, hypotheses, evidence lines that cite the wrong computation, and statuses stronger than the evidence. All five scripts of the four records rerun to their stored outputs line for line. Nothing printed in Collective_Agents is shown false. Eleven sentences were understated, misleading or loosely worded and have been corrected in the sources; the larger results are not in the manuscript and are listed in `manuscripts/research_to_do.md` with their preconditions. MAgent_Theory_2.0 is not affected.

Two of the records (`open_lrn_strategic.md`, `open_mis_dynamic_ratio.md`) carry `Status: closed` and `Review: fresh-context` on the strength of a verifier from the same swarm that wrote them. The reviews here support the mathematics of both, and they do not support `closed` for the strategic record, whose results depend on a sender loss and a timing that the printed open problem does not declare.

## Corrections applied to Collective_Agents

Each was verified by hand or by a reviewer's independent computation, and both documents compile after them (229 and 634 pages, no errors, no undefined references).

1. `thm:col-contrast-size` (`04_collectives.tex`) said the constant of the size bound is sharp up to π/√12. It is sharp: an isolated adjacent pair has λ_2 = 2a, Var = 1/4, ς² = a, hence ratio one. The theorem now says so and states the box value for sides of at least three; the proof in `app04_collectives.tex` carries the pair computation and the inequality 1 − cos x < x²/2.
2. The sentence after `cor:col-lstar`, the summary sentence of `sec:col-size`, `open:col-extent` (`s04_collectives.tex`), the results-table row of `01_introduction.tex` and the open-problems sentence of `s01_introduction.tex` follow. The open problem now asks whether collectives of large extent approach one and in which classes of media a smaller constant holds; its baseline and diameter clauses are unchanged.
3. `sec:mis-exact` (`03_misreading.tex`) and `rem:mis-exact-fusion` (`s03_misreading.tex`) called track-to-track fusion the calibrated update without saying that it is the conditional mean given (m_i, y_ij) only when errors are uncorrelated with the state. Under a proper stationary prior it is not (its mean-square error exceeds the exact one by 9.8 percent at the instance of `chk:mis-dynamic`). Both now say so.
4. `chk:idn-bounds` (`chkext8_idn.tex`) and the paragraph after the proof of `prop:idn-white` (`appext8_idn.tex`) called the box D_ii ∈ [Q_ii − v_i, Q_ii] admissible. The box contains the compatible set and is larger: Q − D ⪰ 0 fails at D = diag(Q_ii). The printed numbers are unchanged; the script that produced them was not found, so they are neither confirmed nor refuted.

## Not integrated, and why

The results below are sound on the reviews but are new propositions whose proofs are not in the manuscript. They are entered in `manuscripts/research_to_do.md`.

The extent record supplies the equality cases of the size bound, the sharp constant π/√12 for nearest-neighbor media on Z, exact baselines for nearest-neighbor Z^D and for the five rows of `tab:col-baselines`, and a diameter bound with a ln n factor. The statements about large regions (spindles approaching one, horns realizing ln n) are about cut-free regions and not about collectives, which is the question H4 asks.

The identification record supplies a qualification of `thm:idn-path`(c) that the manuscript should carry once the proof is in the appendix: for k ≥ 2 the separation of interaction from a regular low-rank driver is not uniform, the broadcaster being a relative-entropy limit of interaction-free models. Its finite-sample part yields one usable test (the replicate F test under a white null) and no computable test for the composite null.

The strategic record needs the author's declaration of the sender's loss and of the timing before any of it can be stated; under the commitment reading C1, C2 and the identification statement of C6 are ready.

The dynamic-ratio record supplies the closed-form scalar ratios and the K = 3 inversion. The rows "Scalar world", "Invertible noiseless code" and "Aligned world" of `tab:mis-scope` are static results; the invertible-noiseless row fails dynamically, so the table should say "static" on those rows when the result is entered.

## What was not verified

The moment bound of the collective witness in the extent record; the line-by-line asymptotics of C4 in the dynamic-ratio record; the remainder in C6(4) of the strategic record; the printed numbers of `chk:idn-bounds`; and the two edited earlier records.
