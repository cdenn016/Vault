## Preserved scope

The example assumes common precision, product recognition, context-constant individual shape allocations, a global moment-dependent channel architecture, exact Gibbs rows, fresh extensive responses, and a fixed membership threshold. Its admissible initial class is open only within that architecture. The additive quotients exist initially; the state rule selects when they are used. Common precision does not first appear at finite time from unequal initial precisions. The construction does not establish a uniquely derived threshold, spontaneous creation of operational redundancy, source-routing modularity, unrestricted child probes or Bayesian interventions, a local communication architecture, or emergent spacetime.
