# Physics-lane synthesis: Chris Fields literature

Read on 2026-11-01. All 34 selected physics papers have new source notes and page-specific reading records in `physics-reading.json`. These are targeted full-text readings, not independent proof adjudications or reproductions. Publication years and local preprint versions are distinguished in each note.

The strongest connection to the live research is the operational structure of communication: an observer needs a carrier-identification procedure, an interpretation of the received code, a record mechanism, and a protocol for comparing outcomes. The early identification papers show why agreement in reported values should not be silently promoted to identification of a unique underlying source. This is especially useful beside the live MAgent prediction chapter, which explicitly supplies normalized preparation, encoder, evaluator, and transcript laws. The black-box arguments concern unrestricted physical identification; they do not invalidate exact conditional recovery within a declared statistical family.

The 2019–2024 quantum-reference-frame papers develop a more detailed interface description. Their reference/pointer split separates stable identification criteria from changing measured values. The 2024 thermodynamic paper explicitly separates energy needed to operate an agent from statistical variational free energy. This is useful for physical implementation questions, but does not convert dimensionless KL or VFE directly into joules. Atomicity and composite control in that paper are relative to the chosen QRF representation.

The TQFT/QECC pair is the most constructive communication-to-geometry comparison. Part I supplies encoding, decoding, and specified error operators, then argues for a practical dependence between useful LOCC protocols and error-corrected channels. Its universal language should not be turned into an unrestricted equivalence theorem. Part II adds spin-network, simplicial, and TQFT structures to obtain geometric interpretations. The action and vertex amplitudes remain supplied. Similarly, Addazi et al. explicitly introduces ambient Lorentzian spacetime before specializing its gauge argument to gravity. These papers do not close the live project's separate obligations of physical signature, soldering, dynamics, and endogenous geometry.

The ER=EPR paper gives an operational indistinguishability argument for finite systems with a holographic screen in the monogamous-entanglement limit. It identifies endpoint topology relative to agents' spatial frames, rather than producing a gravitational metric or Einstein equation. The generic-quantum FEP paper already assumes quantum formalism and asymptotically perfect learning in its limiting argument. Neither can be cited as showing that a classical normalized ELBO derives quantum physics.

Two early reconstruction/no-go claims have specific limitations visible in the read text. In `fields-2016-decompositional-equivalence-a-fundamental-symmetry-underlying-quantum-theory`, PDF pages 19–20, normalization of scalar coefficients does not make Equation (3)'s weighted projector sum unitary; the Born-rule section itself calls the rule pragmatic rather than a theorem. In `fields-2014-consistent-quantum-mechanics-admits-no-mereotopology`, PDF page 9, local-factor projectors commute even on entangled states, so the displayed commutation condition does not establish separability. These source notes preserve the proposals and state the limitations; their universal conclusions should not enter wiki synthesis as established results.

The empirical paper `fields-2024-search-for-entanglement-between-spatially-separated-living-systems-experiment-design-results-and-les` reports no demonstrated entanglement. The dark-energy paper provides a resolution-dependent encoding-cost estimate without a cosmological stress tensor or dynamics. These are not empirical support for the physical interpretation of the project's agent geometry.
