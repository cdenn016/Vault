# Active-inference, memory, and biological organization: Chris Fields literature

The active lane contains 27 selected papers. Twenty-six new source notes were written on 2026-11-01; the existing [[friston-2024-federated-inference]] note was preserved. `active-reading.json` records the stored version and targeted PDF passages used for each item. These readings support ingestion and a scoped comparison with the live research. They are not independent proof adjudications, numerical reproductions, or validation of biological proposals.

The closest connection to the current cue-result and interacting-agent work is the distinction between a carrier, a receiver's available distinctions, retained computational structure, and a particular recorded outcome. [[fields-2020-how-do-living-systems-create-meaning]] treats meaning through reference frames, attention, memory, and possible action. [[fields-2022-neurons-as-hierarchies-of-quantum-reference-frames]] explicitly distinguishes an executable reference frame from a sequence of classical records. [[fields-2025-how-do-inner-screens-enable-imaginative-experience-applying-the-free-energy-principle-directly-to-th]] develops internal interfaces through which components make information available to one another. Together these papers motivate specifying how a receiver uses a cue without assuming that it reconstructs all distinctions present in the sender. Their quantum and phenomenological interpretations remain additional commitments beyond a classical normalized record kernel.

[[dobson-2025-constructing-condensed-memories-in-functorial-time]] provides a related constructive account of episodic memory. It treats recall and planning as operations performed in the present, constrained by observed and constructed events, and offers empirical hypotheses about shared resources. The categorical construction has not been independently checked in this ingest. [[tolchinsky-2025-temporal-depth-in-a-coherent-self-and-in-depersonalization-theoretical-model]] concerns remembered and anticipated horizons and the coherence of a self-model; its clinical and computational validation is deferred. Neither paper derives a Fisher-information clock or the scale-dependent slowdown discussed in the live PIFB2 manuscript. Event ordering, planning depth, elapsed physical time, and information-geometric update length should retain separate meanings.

The control papers distinguish which computations a system can perform from which computations it deploys under resource constraints. [[fields-2023-control-flow-i-active-inference]] develops classical attractor, QRF, and TQFT descriptions with explicit separability and boundary assumptions. [[fields-2023-control-flow-ii-tensor-networks]] represents transition sequences using tensors, then makes stronger equivalence and identity-cycle claims on PDF pages 8–10. The representational construction is useful; those stronger claims should remain attributed rather than become an unrestricted theorem in wiki synthesis. [[fields-2022-information-flow-in-context-dependent-hierarchical-bayesian-inference]] separately treats compatibility among measurement contexts. Ordinary dependence on input or attention is not enough to establish intrinsic contextuality, and a diagram of local models does not by itself establish a global normalized joint distribution.

The architecture discussion continues through [[kuchling-2022-metacognition-as-a-consequence-of-competing-evolutionary-time-scales]] and [[fields-2024-meta-learning-goes-hand-in-hand-with-metacognition]]. Their concrete contribution is a higher-level regulator with local memory, feedback, and a distinct timescale. They do not eliminate the need to specify how that regulator is trained. [[fields-2020-scale-free-architectures-support-representational-diversity]] adds the useful warning that categories of experienced thought do not reveal implementation architecture. [[hartl-2026-remapping-and-navigation-of-an-embedding-space-via-error-minimization-a-fundamental-organizational-p]] compares representational changes with navigation through a problem space, while explicitly retaining differences between artificial models and biological organization.

Several papers develop formation mechanisms or hypotheses rather than merely naming higher levels. [[fields-2019-somatic-multicellularity-as-a-satisficing-solution-to-the-prediction-error-minimization-problem]] supplies a toy model in which somatic descendants protect reproductive cells, with explicit costs and exposure assumptions. [[fields-2023-regulative-development-as-a-model-for-origin-of-life-and-artificial-life-studies]] extends the interpretation to environment-assisted organization. [[fields-2024-the-free-energy-principle-induces-compartmentalization]] uses boundary permutation symmetry breaking and differentiated reference frames, while [[fields-2022-the-free-energy-principle-induces-neuromorphic-development]] adds morphological freedom and local resource limits. These are relevant predecessors for endogenous hierarchy. They do not supply the particular normalized parent law, retained contextual section, or complete operational inheritance required by the live research merely by invoking the FEP.

[[friston-2023-a-variational-synthesis-of-evolutionary-and-developmental-dynamics]] is more specifically probabilistic: it relates phenotype paths and slower evolutionary parameters under a nonequilibrium steady-state setting, particular partitions, timescale assumptions, and a genotype–phenotype likelihood construction. Its scope should be preserved when used beside [[fields-2020-does-evolution-have-a-target-morphology]] and [[fields-2020-integrating-evolutionary-and-developmental-thinking-into-a-scale-free-biology]], which propose broader evolutionary-developmental perspectives. A possible large-scale attractor is a biological hypothesis, not an established exact renormalization law.

Biological organization also complicates the choice of agent. [[fields-2018-are-planaria-individuals-what-regenerative-biology-is-telling-us-about-the-nature-of-multicellularit]] distinguishes a coherent body from the persistent, heterogeneous reproductive lineages maintaining it and explicitly leaves their coordination mechanism open. [[fields-2018-multiscale-memory-and-bioelectric-error-correction-in-the-cytoplasm-cytoskeleton-membrane-system]] emphasizes non-genomic retained organization and proposes a role for bioelectric error correction. [[fields-2020-morphological-coordination-a-common-ancestral-function-unifying-neural-and-non-neural-signaling]] reviews communication mechanisms across neural and non-neural tissues. [[fields-2022-competency-in-navigating-arbitrary-spaces-as-an-invariant-for-analyzing-cognition-in-diverse-embodim]] offers operational tasks for comparing such systems. These works suggest useful biological questions without fixing the mathematical boundary or individuality criterion of an abstract agent.

Two wider claims need explicit attribution. [[fields-2021-metabolic-limits-on-classical-information-processing-by-biological-cells]] infers a need for bulk quantum processing from an energetic account of maintaining fine-grained classical states. That conclusion depends on the repeated-decoherence and erasure accounting and is not an observation of cellular entanglement. [[fields-2025-life-its-origin-and-its-distribution-a-perspective-from-the-conway-kochen-theorem-and-the-free-energ]] combines an interpretation of Conway–Kochen with the FEP to argue for ubiquitous agency. The biological and philosophical conclusion is not the content of the quantum theorem alone.

For collective communication, [[fields-2024-snakes-and-ladders-in-the-evolution-of-language]] emphasizes the difference between an innovation and its uptake into a durable group practice. The associated [[friston-2024-federated-inference]] construction should be read in its particular generative setting. The existing source note remains immutable, but wiki synthesis should correct any implication that arbitrary peer-KL dynamics are exactly the ELBO of a fixed normalized joint. That equivalence requires an explicit model and derivation.

Priority reading for the current program is the meaning paper, the two control-flow papers, the contextual information-flow paper, the inner-screen architecture, the constructive-memory paper, and the three formation papers on somatic protection, compartmentalization, and neuromorphic development. The other papers supply biological examples, evolutionary perspectives, and cautions about interpreting cognitive terminology.
