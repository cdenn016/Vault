At the author's request, Section 5.2 gains Remark 9, "Noisy-query routing" (`rem:noisy_query`, `eq:noisy_query_routing`).
- **Construction.** The projected query mean is treated as a noisy measurement, with noise covariance $V_{Q,i}$, of a draw from the key law.
- **Exact posterior.** Under that model, $P(J=j\mid a_i)\propto\pi_{ij}\mathcal N(a_i;b_j,V_{Q,i}+V_{K,j})$. The likelihood is the expected likelihood kernel of Jebara, Kondor, and Howard (2004, Secs. 2.1 and 3.1).
- **Relation to Gibbs routing.** Gibbs routing normalizes $\pi_{ij}\exp\mathbb E_{q_{Q,i}}[\log p_{K,j}]$, whereas this rule normalizes $\pi_{ij}\mathbb E_{q_{Q,i}}[p_{K,j}]$. The two coincide in the point-query limit.
- **Shared key covariance.** Under shared key covariance the rule is Proposition 5 at unit temperature with $V_*$ replaced by $V_{Q,i}+V_*$.
- **Worked example.** The remark gives a one-dimensional example in which the Gibbs weight stays at 0.622 for every query variance, while the noisy-query weight falls to 0.562 and 0.511 at variances 1 and 10. These values were recomputed before insertion.
- **Invariances and temperature.** The rule shares the invariances of Theorem 2. A temperature enters through the binary record of Theorem 8 after a row shift.
- **Scope.** The remark states that the rule is a different operator and that the evaluated model does not implement it.

Two supporting changes accompany the remark:
- Remark 6 gained the label `rem:gaussian_mixture_attention`.
- Because theorem-like environments share one counter, the link-algebra proposition is now Proposition 10 and the flat-identification remark is Remark 11; all pointers are `\ref`-based.
