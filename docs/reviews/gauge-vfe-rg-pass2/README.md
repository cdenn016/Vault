# Status of the specialist review records

These files are preserved as dated, pre-integration audit evidence. They are
not a statement of the manuscript's final architecture.

In particular, recommendations in `05-effective-support-holonomy.md`,
`07-variational-coarse-semantics.md`, and `10-concision-rigor-flow.md` to use
two independent principal bundles were superseded by the 2026-08-01
common-principal-bundle correction. The final ambient theory uses one
principal right (G\)-bundle (P\to\mathcal C\), two associated belief/model
law bundles, possibly inequivalent representations, separately chosen local
principal frames and connections, and separately declared cross-bundle
morphisms. A product principal bundle appears only as an optional extension
for genuinely independent gauge symmetries or principal-bundle topology.

The integrated disposition is recorded in
`../2026-07-29-gauge-vfe-rg-ultradeep-peer-review-pass2.md`; current
mathematical claims must be checked against the manuscript source rather than
against an isolated specialist memo.
