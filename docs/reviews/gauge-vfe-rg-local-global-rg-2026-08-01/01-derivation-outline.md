# Derivation outline

## Part I: local and collective variational free energy

1. Declare a finite but arbitrary agent hypergraph and attach to each factor a normalized interaction-record kernel. Build one normalized joint law without using a recognition distribution as generative input.
2. Condition on one regular interaction record and derive the exact global identity \(\mathcal F(Q)=-\log p(o)+\mathrm{KL}(Q\Vert\Pi_o)\).
3. Disintegrate on an arbitrary block \(B\). Derive its conditional partition function, conditional posterior, and local multi-agent VFE. Prove that a unilateral replacement of the recognition conditional changes the global VFE by the outside average of the local VFE change.
4. Separate this coordinate-potential statement from additive bookkeeping. Derive the total-correlation decomposition and explain why overlapping local objectives cannot sum to the global objective.
5. Add a categorical source label to each receiving agent. Derive the exact posterior attention, the recognition-attention free-energy sector, and its replicator/natural-gradient flow.
6. Prove that continuous local natural-gradient evolution is simultaneously a descent of the collective VFE. State the safe sequential/damped discrete alternative.
7. Compile any standard-Borel observation kernel into an environment-node/message protocol and prove the converse. State exactly when the resulting model is operationally agent-only and why observation records remain epistemically necessary.

## Part II: exact coarse-graining and renormalization

1. Push the normalized joint, posterior, recognition law, and reference measure through one recognition-independent Markov coarse channel. Derive the effective action and the exact conditional-KL loss of VFE resolution.
2. Prove associativity under compatible channels. Close the effective theory under arbitrary induced hyperedges by anchored Mobius inversion, or by retaining the full effective potential when it is extended valued.
3. Construct posterior bridge kernels between each fine agent and each meta-agent. Prove their Bayes-adjoint relation and give the corresponding conditional-expectation operators.
4. Choose compatible rooted forests in the belief and model sectors. Dress all incidence legs into block-root frames, retain non-tree holonomies, and define separate cross-scale operators for the two associated representations. Use \(P^*AP\) for energy restriction and distinguish message compression.
5. Push the joint recipient/source event law to obtain exact meta-agent attention. Derive the coarse source-label mixture, interaction kernels, and the KL split between attention and conditional message laws. Retain parallel transported legs or a Hom operator rather than averaging back into \(G\).
6. Push complete path laws. Derive the memory kernel and the necessary-and-sufficient lumpability condition for a state-Markov truncation.
7. With compatible blocking and rescaling, define the measure-valued RG semigroup. On the dominated tier derive the exact effective-action map, continuous functional beta equation, full-hyperedge component beta functions, and attention beta function.
8. Characterize all fixed points by the invariant-measure equation and local scaling directions by the full spectrum of the reference-dependent action derivative. Exhibit the terminal and identity fixed theories, strictly stable/Gaussian constant-likelihood pairs, fixed-(b) semistable extensions, and the qualified flat-holonomy, uniform-attention, and boundary-attention invariant sectors.

## Audit gates

The construction is complete only if every normalization, reference-measure change, gauge transformation, and conditional law is typed; the local objectives are not falsely summed; source attention is not recognition-dependent in the generative law; arbitrary pairwise or Markov closure is not claimed; the figures render without collisions; the manuscript compiles; and the mathematical claim ledger closes with derivational evidence and adversarial review.
