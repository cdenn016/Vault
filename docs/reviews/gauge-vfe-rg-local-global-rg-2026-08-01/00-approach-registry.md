# Approach registry: local/global ELBO and exact agent-network RG

Date: 2026-08-01

This registry records the genuinely different construction families explored before manuscript synthesis. The inherited foundation is restricted to the principal bundle \(P\to\mathcal C\), the structure group \(G\), and the two associated statistical bundles of belief and model laws. Every probabilistic, variational, interaction, attention, and renormalization statement is treated as a candidate until proved from additional stated hypotheses.

| Family | Mathematical mechanism | Concrete result retained | Failure exposed by cross-audit | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional/disintegration | A fixed normalized joint law, regular conditional probabilities, and KL chain rules | Exact global VFE; exact conditional agent/block VFE; unilateral local changes equal global changes; continuous block natural-gradient descent | Conditional local VFEs neither add to the global VFE nor share its evidence; incident factors overlap | Retain as the local/global compatibility theorem; separate coordinate objectives from additive accounting |
| Interaction-record likelihood | Each interaction is a normalized observation kernel attached to a factor node | Observations are interaction records in one fixed joint; cycles are harmless after normalization | The binary likelihood \(e^{-E}\) requires \(E\geq0\); a generic log likelihood can be negative | Use normalized kernels as the general construction; retain binary handshakes only as a bounded-likelihood example |
| Wilsonian law pushforward | Parameter-independent Markov kernels, pushed references, and conditional expectation | Exact effective law/action; exact ELBO information-loss identity; associative blocking | Pairwise potentials and memoryless dynamics are not closed; an old reference may become singular after blocking | Close the theory in the full hypergraph and path-space sectors; push the reference at every step |
| Gauge path groupoid | Rooted forests, incidence-leg transports, holonomy, and separate belief/model representations | Gauge-covariant agent-to-meta maps; retained loop holonomy; typed belief/model and cross-channel operators | Arbitrary tree choices fail strict nested composition on a nonflat loop; averaging group elements can leave \(G\) | Require a nested rooted-forest filtration for strict flow; otherwise use a holonomy-coherent pseudofunctor; retain parallel legs or Hom operators |
| Operator/Galerkin | Reconstruction \(P\), metric adjoint \(P^*\), and compressed messages | Cross-scale fine/meta operators and exact energy restriction \(P^*AP\) | \(CP=I\) does not make \(CAP\) positive or an energy operator | Reserve \(CAP\) for typed message compression; use \(P^*AP\), Schur complements, or conditional expectation for energies/laws |
| Categorical source labels | A generative source label with prior \(\pi\) and interaction likelihood | Exact attention posterior, recognition-attention KL sector, and an exact coarse edge-event law | \(\sum\beta E+\tau\mathrm{KL}(\beta\Vert\pi)\) is \(\tau\) times a standard ELBO sector | Use \(\mathrm{KL}+\tau^{-1}\sum\beta E\) in the global VFE; state the optimization-equivalent rescaling separately |
| Measure-valued RG | Compatible coarse channels plus explicit rescaling identifications | Exact semigroup/cocycle, measure fixed points, functional beta, and linearized eigenoperators | An action beta is ill typed without a pushed dominating reference and absolute continuity | Make the measure flow primary; introduce an action/coupling beta only on the explicitly dominated tier |
| Agent-only kernel compilation | Randomization of standard-Borel observation kernels into environment-node state and a message policy | Kernel-level equivalence between observations and agent-environment messages | Exogenous noise is not automatically an autonomous agent, and the realized message remains a conditioning variable | Establish operational agent-only closure under an explicit node definition; leave physical agency as an additional criterion |

The surviving construction is therefore not a pairwise, memoryless truncation. It is the normalized gauge-covariant hypergraph/path-space theory containing all induced factors, source-label sectors, bridge kernels, and memory kernels. Pairwise and Markovian models appear as invariant subfamilies only when the corresponding factorization and lumpability conditions are proved.
