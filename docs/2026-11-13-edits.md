## Four follow-ups to the Bayesian mechanics ingest

The user asked for four follow-ups: a comparison of the collective-agents manuscript with three of the new papers, a derivation settling whether the abelian gauge constructions of the literature are special cases of the model's gauge structure, a revision of the Definition on `Bayesian mechanics`, and a recheck of the corrections that had been applied without a second reading.

The manuscript comparison was made by three readers, one for each paper, who read the definitions and theorems of the manuscript that each paper touches. None of the three papers was cited, and none changes a theorem, a hypothesis or a novelty statement of the manuscript. Heins (2026) is a simulated counterpart of the overvaluation case of H2, by analogy only: its static pooling rule is the union-model rule of the pooling proposition, its implemented filter multiplies complete posteriors, and its group-size trend is for patch posteriors, is not monotone and is attributed by its author to several causes, so it is not evidence that double counting grows with group size. Sakthivadivel (2026) defines on continuous path space the quantity that the manuscript calls the window leak, proves a separation criterion that for linear drifts is the continuous-time form of the collider condition, and gives no coefficient-level bound of the kind the manuscript proves. Virgo, Rosas and Biehl (2022) argue for one sensor-motor loop, without a theorem, the distinction that the manuscript proves in linear-Gaussian form with a bound and a repair. Two sentences on Heins were added to the nested-blanket paragraph of `sections/12_related.tex`, sentences on the other two to its paragraph on path blankets, three paragraphs to `sections/s12_related.tex`, and three entries to `references.bib`. An independent reader on a different model then compared the new sentences with the papers and with the manuscript's own results and returned twelve findings; ten were applied, among them a misdescription of the fish model, a bound attributed to the wrong theorem and the initial-state qualification of the commentary. Two findings concern sentences of the author's that the additions may bear on, and they were left for the author: the statement that the construction of Palacios et al. is the one construction of a collective's coarse sensory and active states that was found, and the statement that no precedent was found for a predictive law of a meta-agent's coarse exterior. The readers also noted that Proposition 10.5 and Corollary 17.3 of Sakthivadivel (2026) give sufficient conditions for stationary blankets in nonequilibrium diffusions and may bear on the item on exact stationary blankets in the manuscript's list of precedents still to be checked, and that two sentences of `sections/02_framework.tex` say that agents' blankets hold by construction without saying that the path blanket is meant. Neither was changed.

The derivation is in `docs/research/2026-11-13-abelian-sector-of-gauge-transport.md`. Its answer is that none of the three constructions is a special case or a restriction of the model, and that three exact relations hold. Every abelian quotient of the gauge group factors through the determinant, and the absolute value of the determinant maps the transport to a positive-scalar field with Polettini's group and transformation laws, which is what makes the log-determinant term of each Gaussian coupling energy frame invariant; the field carries no rates and no current and is exact for pure-gauge transport. Polettini's construction applies as it stands to the attention matrix read as a jump process when attention has reciprocal support, with explicit cycle affinities; flatness of the transport and detailed balance of that process are independent, and the affinity is third order in the separation of beliefs. The constructions of Sakthivadivel and of Feng and Wang live on the sample space of one agent. The statements were derived three times independently, by the coordinator and by two readers working from different starting points, and the written note was then checked line by line by two proof checkers on a different model. They confirmed the propositions, found one wrong sentence (the gauge group of the attention chain is, as an abstract group, the determinant quotient; what is true is that changes of frame act on the chain trivially) and one gap (the statement on Feng and Wang holds for constant coefficients and was restated so), and asked for eleven smaller repairs; all were applied. The corrected note was not read again by a checker. The editorial callouts on `Bayesian mechanics`, `Parallel transport`, `Holonomy`, `Gauge transformation` and `Lattice gauge theory`, which had called the relation an analogy, now state what is exact and point to the note.

The Definition on `Bayesian mechanics` no longer says that systems are treated as literally doing inference. It says that the dynamics are read as inference, and it states that the reading is exact only for conditional modes under an invertible synchronization map and restrictions on solenoidal coupling, and that the lineage's latest statement calls it an as-if description.

The recheck covered the seventeen corrections that the first checks had classed as errors of mathematics or fact, ten in source notes and seven in wiki sections. Two readers reread the corrected passages, the first against the typeset pages and the second against the notes. All seventeen corrections were right. In eight cases a neighboring sentence had been left inconsistent with the correction, and the readers found fourteen further sentences in the new sections and the rewritten pages that hardened what a note says; twenty-two repairs were applied, in five source notes written the same day and in seven wiki pages.
