## 1. Contradictions

### C8. Collective active inference summarizes the collective-sensing result more broadly than Bayesian mechanics and the note's results paragraphs (low; uncertain)

- **The wiki summaries.**
  - `wiki/concepts/Collective active inference.md:121`: "In simulation the posterior recovers gradient direction and becomes overconfident as group size grows."
  - `wiki/concepts/Bayesian mechanics.md:104`: "the posterior mean recovers gradient direction, less well at school level for the largest schools; the patch posteriors grow overconfident with group size, and the pooled posterior stays overconfident even when each agent is assigned to a unique window."
  - `wiki/concepts/Federated inference.md:240` also confines the rise with group size to the patch-level ratio.
- **The note.** `sources/papers/heins-2026-collective-sensing-bayesian-inference.md` supports Bayesian mechanics in its summary and results:
  - `:26`: "the patch posteriors become overconfident over a range of group sizes and the pooled posterior is overconfident at the one size at which it is scored".
  - `:38`: the school cosine "is 0.97 at $N = 64$ and 0.54 at $N = 256$".
  
  The note's relevance paragraph is broader. At `:47` it says that pooling "yields a collective posterior that is overconfident, increasingly so with group size". Collective active inference follows that reading.
- **Fix.**
  - Reword Collective active inference `:121` to match Bayesian mechanics `:104`. Patch posteriors become overconfident as group size grows. The pooled posterior is overconfident where it is scored. Direction recovery at school level is weaker for the largest schools.
  - The note is immutable. If the wiki keeps the `:47` reading, attribute it to the note's relevance section.
- **Uncertain.** I did not read the paper, so I cannot tell whether it shows the pooled posterior's overconfidence growing with group size.
