## Corrections and status boundaries

The construction distinguishes flat endpoint identifications from generally nonflat communication. The inverse completion used for triangles is explicit and remains separate from the main manuscript's directed causal-walk result. Low-rank exponential/affine equivalence has a real-logarithm domain; a complement residual requires both selective communication and a declared local preservation rule. The general implemented triangle spectrum depends on the product R G, including its noncommuting order. Its spectral diagnostic can miss unipotent structure. A constant generator is nonflat when its exponential differs from the identity; it is not thereby maximally curved.

Gaussian moment propagation includes shared-source cross-head and residual terms, counts observation noise once, and distinguishes exact kernel moments from local-diagonal projection and floors. The receiver chapter separates the valid ambient geometric opinion pool from likelihood accumulation and from an exact categorical cut mixture. It retains the coefficient derivative in the outer covariance derivative, states fixed-covariance contraction in the appropriate norm, and distinguishes a clipped diagonal optimum from the unconstrained full-family result. The optional channel/closed-form receiver already exists in current code. The theoretical reference-template and structured-posterior extensions are separated from the implemented local template bank, dropout and covariance passthrough.

The frame chapter declares the family whose Fisher metric is used, retains its stabilizer degeneracy, and specifies the completion or quotient needed for inversion. Principal-log and exponential-differential domains remain explicit. Current Frechet-log conversion is separated from theoretical Bernoulli/diagonalization alternatives. Batch statistics count all input occurrences and normalize by scored-token count. The active launcher uses AdamW; geometric frame policies are selectable alternatives. A negative finite covector-displacement pairing establishes a first-order condition, not nonlinear loss decrease.

The larger GL(K) construction already contains an exact predictive-kernel covariance result. Its separate record-likelihood construction supports a categorical posterior at positive temperature, while marginal-mixture and whole-population ELBO claims have additional assumptions. The correlated extension uses expected residual costs and cross-token covariance; it is not automatically the same Gaussian-KL routing score. The companion retains those distinctions and does not certify the whole composed network as one common population ELBO.
