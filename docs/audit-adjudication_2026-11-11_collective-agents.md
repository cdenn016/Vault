# Adjudication of the major findings of the 2026-11-09 audit of "Communication, Coherence and Collective Agents" (2026-11-11)

The audit of November 9, 2026 (`docs/audit-results_2026-11-09_collective-agents.md`) raised 51 major findings and no critical one. Remediation passes changed the manuscript for all of them, and no pass ruled on whether a finding was right. This report is that ruling, requested by the author on November 11, 2026. Each finding was given to one skeptic and one defender, both read-only and both run on the model complementary to the one that wrote the remediation. Each read the finding's entry in the audit report and the current section files, and answered two questions: whether the finding was right at the severity claimed, and whether the current text resolves it without a new error. The text of the audited revision was not available, so validity was judged from the evidence quoted in each entry and from the current text.

The column Ruling combines the two verdicts by a fixed rule and adds no judgment of the coordinator. A finding is upheld when both sides call it valid, upheld in part when both call it valid or partly valid, not upheld when both call it invalid, and split when one calls it invalid and the other does not. The second half of the ruling is resolved when both sides find the current text resolved, residual work reported when either side finds it partly resolved or not resolved, and error in the repair reported when either side finds that the repair introduced an error. A ruling of residual work or of an error in the repair is a report by one or both agents and has not been verified by a further pass; the numerical checks were not rerun.
