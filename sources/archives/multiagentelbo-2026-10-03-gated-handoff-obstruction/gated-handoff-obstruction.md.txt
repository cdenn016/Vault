# Limits of the gated formation extensions

October 3, 2026. Round 2 of the bounded degenerate-valley investigation records two failures of candidate extensions, together with an exact normalized configuration-law construction. Neither the slow-record gate nor the shared-amplitude GL network supplies formation from every fixed initial density on horizons H_T with T H_T tending to zero. This is a result about these specified models and horizons. It neither closes O4 nor excludes hierarchy formation in other models or on longer time scales.

## Why the previous handoff choices fail

In the previous model write s=|u|^2 and r=|v|^2. Its zero-temperature natural flow preserves s-r. For a fixed initial density supported a positive distance from the boundary with s-r at least delta>0, the stopped gap estimate in the previous record gives opposite-branch probability O(T H_T) on every horizon with T H_T tending to zero. Thus the opposite branch cannot supply a positive limiting weight of actual counterfactual handoffs for every initial density in this regime.

At the proved observation time h_T=log^2(1/T), counting coherence in either orientation instead has probability tending to one from every fixed initial density. Its complement therefore has vanishing probability. These particular late handoff choices fail to supply R14's positive-weight actual nonformation histories. This argument concerns those choices, not every possible stopping-time handoff or all longer protocols.

## A slow record can stay inactive

Let u,v,w be in R^D and y be scalar, and put s=|u|^2, r=|v|^2, W=|w|^2. On the smooth compact ball s+r+y^2+W<=1, take fixed epsilon in (0,1], kappa,lambda>0 and a smooth gate g that is positive near zero and identically zero for r>=r_*, where 0<r_*<1/4. Introduce

\[
 E=\tfrac12sr+\tfrac\kappa2[y-g(r)]^2+\tfrac\lambda2 y^2W.
 \tag{1}
\]

An exact Gaussian-recognition realization uses unit circle pairs for every coordinate of u,v,w, a circle of radius epsilon^{-1/2} for y, and auxiliary means u tensor v, sqrt(kappa)[y-g(r)] and sqrt(lambda)yw. With identity recognition covariance and standard-normal posterior,

\[
 D_{\rm KL}(Q_z\Vert\Pi)=\frac{3D+\epsilon^{-1}}2+E(z).
 \tag{2}
\]

The circle coordinates make the family injective and regular on the ball. Use its actual pullback Fisher metric G and the G-normal reflected generator T Delta_G minus grad_G E acting on test functions. Its Gibbs reference density includes sqrt(det G); it is not obtained by adding coordinate-isotropic noise to the natural ODE.

On the open region r>r_*, g and its derivatives vanish. The metric separates into the previous u,v block and

\[
 G_{yw}=\begin{pmatrix}
 \epsilon^{-1}+\kappa+\lambda W&\lambda y w^T\\
 \lambda y w&(1+\lambda y^2)I
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{3}
\]

Solving for the natural gradient gives

\[
 \dot u=-\frac{ru}{1+s+r},\qquad
 \dot v=-\frac{sv}{1+s+r},
\]
\[
 \dot y=-\frac{y(\kappa+\lambda W+\kappa\lambda y^2)}{d},
 \qquad \dot w=-\frac{\epsilon^{-1}\lambda y^2}{d}w,
\]
\[
 d=\epsilon^{-1}+\kappa+\lambda W
       +(\epsilon^{-1}+\kappa)\lambda y^2.
 \tag{4}
\]

In particular r-s is constant and each squared radius decreases along the ODE. Choose a fixed smooth initial density supported strictly inside the ball with r-s>=2r_*, |w|>=c>0, and |y|<=eta. Such a region is nonempty for small fixed c and eta. Let gamma=kappa/max d, with the maximum over the compact domain. Since d>=epsilon^{-1},

\[
 |y_t|\le |y_0|e^{-\gamma t},\qquad
 \int_0^\infty y_t^2dt\le\frac{\eta^2}{2\gamma},\qquad
 |w_t|\ge c\exp\left(-\frac{\lambda\eta^2}{2\gamma}\right).
 \tag{5}
\]

This also yields a small-noise obstruction for the actual diffusion; the deterministic statement alone would not suffice on a growing horizon. Stop before the gap r-s falls to r_*, before the total squared radius consumes its fixed boundary margin, or before |w| reaches

\[
 c_* =\frac c2\exp\left(-\frac{\lambda\eta^2}{2\gamma}\right).
 \tag{6}
\]

On the stopped compact region all coordinate Ito corrections are bounded by T times a fixed constant. Each relevant martingale has expected squared supremum O(T H_T). The gap has zero deterministic drift, and the total squared radius has nonpositive deterministic drift. Ito's formula for y^2 and (4) imply, uniformly over stopped times up to H_T,

\[
 \int_0^{t\wedge\tau} y_q^2dq
       \le\frac{\eta^2}{2\gamma}+o_P(1).
 \tag{7}
\]

Here the error is bounded by a constant times T H_T plus the supremum of the y^2 martingale. Before |w| reaches c_*, the derivatives of log W are bounded. Its natural drift is at least -2 lambda y^2, so

\[
 \log W_{t\wedge\tau}
 \ge\log c^2-\frac{\lambda\eta^2}{\gamma}-o_P(1).
 \tag{8}
\]

The logarithm of the stopping threshold is the right-hand constant minus log 4. Equations (7)–(8), the fixed gap margin and the fixed boundary margin therefore prevent every exit with probability tending to one when T H_T tends to zero. Consequently

\[
 P_\mu\left(\inf_{t\le H_T}|w_t|\ge c_*\right)\longrightarrow1.
 \tag{9}
\]

The intended v-controlled gate never activates with high probability for this initial density. Relabeling the shrinking u block as first-stage coherence does not make w cohere. Thus this extension fails the universal-initial-density two-level requirement on the stated horizons. The stationary comparison must be recomputed for the enlarged energy and metric; the earlier stationary surplus is not inherited. No conclusion follows for horizons of order 1/T or longer, other gates or native partition branching.

## A normalized GL configuration law

Here the variables are configurations, rather than means of the preceding recognition family. Fix a connected network with N>=512, K>=2, matter x=(x_i) in R^{NK}, a shared amplitude a in R^{NK}, and independent oriented links Theta_e in a compact chart inside GL(K,R). Reverse copies are inverses. Declare positive receiver forms H_e and genuine comparisons

\[
 r_e=x_j-\Theta_e x_i,\qquad
 Q_\Theta(x)=\sum_{e:i\to j}r_e^T H_e r_e=x^TA_\Theta x,
 \qquad cI\le A_\Theta\le CI.
 \tag{10}
\]

The last condition is an introduced local uniform coercivity assumption. It is attainable on a compact neighborhood of a network with no nonzero parallel section. For example, choose a connected graph with two independent cycles, identity tree links, one fundamental holonomy A with A-I invertible, and a second noncommuting holonomy B. At K=2, A=diag(2,3) and B=[[1,1],[0,1]] suffice. At larger K extend A by diagonal entries 2 and B by identity entries. Zero residuals force all tree coordinates to coincide; the A cycle then forces them to vanish. The quadratic form is positive definite, and continuity gives (10) on a sufficiently small compact chart. The two small matrices check only this algebraic construction; the network size is arbitrary and fixed. No thermodynamic bound is inferred.

Take a proper fixed configuration prior with smooth positive density p_0 on a compact domain, chosen constant on the local neighborhood used below. For each edge declare an independent binary record with

\[
 P(Y_e=1\mid a,x,\Theta)
  =\tfrac12\exp\left[-\frac{|a|^2r_e^TH_er_e}{2T}\right],
 \qquad P(Y_e=0\mid a,x,\Theta)=1-P(Y_e=1\mid a,x,\Theta).
 \tag{11}
\]

This is a normalized joint law. The graph, prior, forms H_e and energy coefficients are fixed; the physical joint has the displayed thermal dependence, as in the c13 binary-record construction. Observing all records equal to one gives, with m the number of records,

\[
 E=\tfrac12|a|^2 Q_\Theta(x),\qquad
 Z_T=\int p_0 e^{-E/T},\qquad
 \Pi_T=Z_T^{-1}p_0e^{-E/T},
\]
\[
 F_T(q)=T D_{\rm KL}(q\Vert p_0)+\mathbb E_q E+Tm\log2
       =T D_{\rm KL}(q\Vert\Pi_T)-T\log P(Y=\mathbf1).
 \tag{12}
\]

The records observe sampled configurations directly. They do not justify deleting the covariance terms produced by averaging a different likelihood over fixed nondegenerate Gaussian latents. This is also not an equivalence to the implemented Gaussian evaluator. Equal amplitude and matter dimensions and the shared scalar amplitude are introduced modeling choices, not consequences of SPEC.

Use the block mobility and divergence-form generator

\[
 M=\operatorname{diag}(I_a,A_\Theta^{-1},M_\Theta),\qquad
 L_Tf=\frac{T}{p_0e^{-E/T}}
        \nabla\cdot(p_0e^{-E/T}M\nabla f).
 \tag{13}
\]

Here M_Theta is the inverse of a declared smooth GL metric, for example the left-invariant metric with squared norm tr((Theta^{-1}deltaTheta)^T(Theta^{-1}deltaTheta)), expressed in the link chart. Links enter the interaction and evolve by preconditioned descent; no raw Euclidean Lie-algebra update is used. The coordinate drift is -M grad E + T(div M + M grad log p_0). The reference p_0 is specified explicitly rather than confused with Riemannian volume.

Assume an admitted reflecting diffusion on a smooth compact domain lying within the link chart and containing the local neighborhood below. Smooth ellipticity supplies the usual process setting already cited in the preceding construction; the estimates stop strictly before its boundary. Where p_0 is constant, the a and x thermal drifts vanish because A_Theta^{-1} depends only on Theta. The interior zero-temperature flow is

\[
 \dot a=-Q_\Theta(x)a,\qquad
 \dot x=-|a|^2x,\qquad
 \dot\Theta=-\tfrac12|a|^2 M_\Theta\nabla_\Theta Q_\Theta(x).
 \tag{14}
\]

Thus it is possible for the amplitude to decay before matter has relaxed, despite genuine interacting and dynamic noncommuting links.

## A fixed initial density that does not form

Choose rho>0 and a fixed smooth density with 2rho<=|x_0|<=3rho, s_0=|a_0|^2<=eta, and links in an inner compact chart. Choose the outer domain and constant-prior neighborhood to contain the following local tube. Stop when |x-x_0| reaches rho, the link displacement reaches a fixed positive chart margin, or s reaches a fixed cap larger than eta. These conditions can be placed strictly before the outer boundary. Before this stopping time tau, |x| lies between rho and 4rho and Q>=q_0=c rho^2.

The amplitude equation is exact in this neighborhood:

\[
 ds=-2Q_\Theta(x)sdt+2Tn dt+dN_s,\qquad
 d\langle N_s\rangle=8Tsdt,\qquad n=NK.
 \tag{15}
\]

Writing I_t for the integral of s up to t wedge tau gives

\[
 2q_0 I_t\le\eta+2TnH_T+\sup_{q\le H_T}|N_s(q\wedge\tau)|,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E I_{H_T}\le\frac{\eta+2TnH_T}{2q_0},
\]
\[
 \mathbb E\sup_{q\le H_T}|N_s(q\wedge\tau)|^2
 \le32T\mathbb E I_{H_T}.
 \tag{16}
\]

The matter noise has covariance 2T A_Theta^{-1}; its expected squared supremum is at most 8n T H_T/c. Link noise has the same O(T H_T) bound with a fixed chart-dependent constant. Equations (14) and the bounded link thermal correction give

\[
 \sup|x_{t\wedge\tau}-x_0|\le4\rho I_{H_T}+\sup|N_x|,
\]
\[
 \sup|\Theta_{t\wedge\tau}-\Theta_0|
 \le C_\Theta I_{H_T}+C_\Theta T H_T+\sup|N_\Theta|,
 \qquad
 \sup s_{t\wedge\tau}\le\eta+2TnH_T+\sup|N_s|.
 \tag{17}
\]

First choose eta sufficiently small that the deterministic bounds consume less than half each fixed tube margin. Then T H_T tending to zero makes every error in (16)–(17) tend to zero in probability. At a first exit these same inequalities contradict the exit condition with probability tending to one. This closes the stopped bootstrap and proves

\[
 P_\mu\left(\inf_{t\le H_T} Q_{\Theta_t}(x_t)\ge c\rho^2\right)
       \longrightarrow1.
 \tag{18}
\]

Any declared matter-coherence event requiring Q_Theta(x) to approach zero therefore fails from this fixed admissible density on these horizons. Calling vanishing amplitude matter coherence would change the event. The construction cannot satisfy the every-fixed-density clause in the current hierarchy problem in this regime.

## The amplitude floor and the remaining obligation

For comparison, restricting |a|>=a_min>0 removes the vanishing-amplitude branch but changes the model. Under (10), E>=a_min^2 c|x|^2/2. If the prior is bounded above on its compact support and bounded below on a uniform matter neighborhood of x=0 over a positive-volume amplitude/link set, direct Gaussian integral comparison gives E_{Pi_T}|x|^2<=C T. Hence Pi_T(|x|>r_T)<=C T/r_T^2 tends to zero whenever r_T^2/T tends to infinity. Terminal stationary coherence then tends to one, leaving no fixed positive terminal probability surplus for that event. This one-time estimate does not establish a full-window comparison, an entrance rate or a recurrence time. Those protocols require their own estimates.

The round closes these two proposed extensions at the specified scope. It does not close formation generally: neither proof excludes horizons of order 1/T or longer, other normalized link interactions, intrinsic partition branching or other handoff definitions. An admissible continuation must address actual nonformation handoffs from each fixed initial density and recompute the same full-protocol stationary comparison. Practical predictive accuracy and complexity in O1, and the partition/feedback requirements in O4, remain open. No additional simulation is authorized or implied by this record.
