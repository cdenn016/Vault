## prop:glink-law-level-energy (g11 lines 85–163)

**Verdict: VALID_WITH_MINOR_ISSUES** (one citation whose hypothesis is not recorded for the Gaussian fiber; the claim itself is proved by a direct expansion).

Reconstruction. (i) prop:geo-moment-pushforward (g02 435–452) applies since $e^{\mathring\omega}\in\GL^+(K)$ ($\det e^{\mathring\omega}=e^{\Tr\mathring\omega}>0$) and $\rho_b=\mathrm{id}$: $\exp(\mathring\omega)_\#q_j=\mathcal N(e^{\mathring\omega}\mu_j,e^{\mathring\omega}\Sigma_je^{\mathring\omega^\top})$. In the standard Gaussian relative entropy, $\Sigma'^{-1}=e^{-\mathring\omega^\top}\Sigma_j^{-1}e^{-\mathring\omega}$, so $\Tr(\Sigma'^{-1}\Sigma_i)=\Tr(\Sigma_j^{-1}e^{-\mathring\omega}\Sigma_ie^{-\mathring\omega^\top})$, $(\mu_i-e^{\mathring\omega}\mu_j)^\top\Sigma'^{-1}(\mu_i-e^{\mathring\omega}\mu_j)=(e^{-\mathring\omega}\mu_i-\mu_j)^\top\Sigma_j^{-1}(e^{-\mathring\omega}\mu_i-\mu_j)$, and $\tfrac12\log\det\Sigma'=\tfrac12\log\det\Sigma_j+\Tr\mathring\omega$ (Jacobi). This is eq:glink-law-level-energy. Frame independence: local laws are $(U_i)_\#q_i$ and $(U_ie^{\mathring\omega}U_j^{-1})_\#(U_j)_\#q_j=(U_i)_\#\exp(\mathring\omega)_\#q_j$; a common bijection preserves relative entropy. Real-analyticity: polynomial in the entries of the entire function $e^{-\mathring\omega}$ plus a linear term. (iii) First two cases by substitution: with $\Sigma_i=e^{\mathring\omega}\Sigma_je^{\mathring\omega^\top}$ the trace term is $K/2$, the log-determinant term is $-\Tr\mathring\omega$, and the mean term is $\tfrac12(\mu_i-e^{\mathring\omega}\mu_j)^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}(\mu_i-e^{\mathring\omega}\mu_j)$, the twisted pair energy eq:cg-twisted-pair (g08 43) at the means with $W_e=\Sigma_i^{-1}$; at $\mathring\omega=0$, $\Sigma_i=\Sigma_j$ it is prop:ig-fixed-covariance-kl. The same form follows from eq:ig-moment-metric on the velocity $(\mathring\upsilon\mu,\mathring\upsilon\Sigma+\Sigma\mathring\upsilon^\top)$, and it equals eq:link-gaussian-orbit-form (expand $\tfrac12\lVert\mathbf B+\mathbf B^\top\rVert_F^2=\Tr(\mathbf B^\top\mathbf B)+\Tr\mathbf B^2$ with $\mathbf B=\Sigma^{-1/2}\mathring\upsilon\Sigma^{1/2}$).
