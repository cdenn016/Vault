## lem:link-exp-naturality (c08b 99–135)

**Verdict: VALID.**

Reconstruction: γ(t)=φ(exp(tω)) is a smooth one-parameter subgroup of 𝕐 with γ'(0)=dφ ω; differentiating γ(t+t')=γ(t)γ(t') at t'=0 shows γ is the integral curve through e of the left-invariant field with value dφ ω, so γ(t)=exp(t dφ ω) by uniqueness; t=1 gives φ∘exp=exp∘dφ. Conjugation is a smooth homomorphism with differential Ad_g, giving g exp(ω)g⁻¹=exp(Ad_g ω). On GL(𝕍) the left-invariant field with value 𝐇 at I is y↦y𝐇 and d/dt e^{t𝐇}=e^{t𝐇}𝐇, so the matrix exponential is the Lie exponential, giving ρ_x∘exp=exp∘dρ_x. Applying ρ_x to the first identity at tω and differentiating at t=0 gives dρ_x(Ad_g ω)=ρ_x(g)dρ_x(ω)ρ_x(g)⁻¹. The drafted proof is exactly this. Hypotheses complete; status ESTABLISHED with a full proof is R12-compliant because the Hall 2015 locators in the author's record are unchecked (the rule keeps the proof in that case).
