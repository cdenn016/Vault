## Existing theory facts the integration must build on (verify against the files)

- Links: `def:geo-graph-links` (c02): `\Theta^x_e\in G` on oriented edge copies `e:j\to i`,
  `\Theta^x_{\bar e}=(\Theta^x_e)^{-1}`, vertex law `\Theta'=g_i\Theta g_j^{-1}`, path products and holonomy.
  `prop:geo-edge-twist-invariance`: per-edge twist `\mathring\Theta^x_e=U_i^{-1}\Theta^x_eU_j` relative to a
  reference point `\sigma` with `u_i=\sigma\cdot U_i^{-1}`; twists are passive-invariant, a reference change
  conjugates all twists simultaneously.
- `rem:geo-loop-penalties` (c02): unitary loop penalty in `[0,2]`, Haar-normalizable for compact `G`; no norm
  on `gl(n)` is invariant under all of `GL(n)`; an SPD fiber metric `\mathsf G^{\mathrm{fib}}` transforming
  covariantly gives a positive invariant penalty; normalizability for noncompact or general linear `G` is
  NOT asserted there. The new construction supplies normalizability through the generator chart.
- `prop:elbo-random-link-vfe` (c05): links as a latent variable with prior `Z^{-1}e^{-\mathscr S}\nu`,
  recognition `Q(d\Theta,dy)=R(d\Theta)Q_\Theta(dy)`, and
  `\Fenergy=\E_R\Fenergy_{\mathrm{matter}}+\E_R\mathscr S+\KL(R\Vert\nu)+\log Z_{\mathscr S}`. The new chapter
  SPECIALIZES this; it does not re-prove it.
- `prop:gen-exact-normalization` (c04): the joint is a directed latent law composed with conditionally
  independent normalized record kernels, so it is normalized on any cyclic record hypergraph.
  `def:gen-undirected-model` (c04): the normalized undirected alternative with normalizer `Z_X`.
  This is the home of the dichotomy "directed (record-extension) matter induces no link action; jointly
  normalized undirected matter induces `-\log Z_{\mathrm{matter}}`".
- Records: `hyp:local-interaction-kernels` (c05), with binary success records `K_a(y,\{1\})=e^{-E(y)}`;
  the running example's record `a_2` has success probability `\exp(-\frac12|k_2-\rho_b(\Theta^b_{e_2})k_3|^2)`.
  Transported divergences `\KL(q_i\Vert(\Theta_e)_\#q_j)` are law-point energies (c03).
- Two timescales (c08): `def:obs-fast-slow-blocks`, `lem:obs-envelope-slow-flow`,

## Writing rules you must follow (from SPEC.md; read it for the full list)

- Flowing academic paragraphs, American English. No bullet lists in body text. No `\;`, `\,`, `\!`.
  No horizontal rules. Banned words: key insight, crucially, critically, notably, importantly, it's worth
  noting, fundamentally, leverages, underscores. Punctuate displays as sentences.
- Every definition, standing hypothesis, lemma, proposition, theorem, corollary, conjecture, open problem
  and claim-bearing remark is a numbered statement using `\definitionheading{Title}{label}`,
  `\propositionheading{...}{...}`, etc. Hypotheses of a result are stated inside the result. The statement
  runs from its heading to its status tag. Theorem-type statements and claim-bearing remarks carry exactly
  one tag: `\status{ESTABLISHED}` (proved here), `\status{STANDARD}` (known result, cited to a CHECKED
  primary source with a theorem/section/equation locator). Do not use NUMERICAL: numerical checks are for
  our validation only and are not cited in the manuscript.
- Proofs open a new paragraph with `\noindent\emph{Proof.}` and end with `\hfill$\square$`.
- Refer to labeled objects only with `\cref`/`\Cref`; never hard-code numbers.
- No dates, repository paths, code or configuration names, run records, or hashes. Nothing about the
  transformer implementation, its experiments, or its configuration keys.
- No hand-waves ("clearly", "one can show", "in the appropriate limit"): discharge by proof, exact
  condition, or checked citation, or turn the gap into an open problem whose settling condition is
  restricted to a stated class with both branches nontrivial (R16).
- Do not use "declared", "normalized", "actual", "complete", "retained", "exact", "fixed" when the word only
  restates a default (R6).
- Interpretation (photon analogy, Maxwell, fast weights) goes in at most one short unnumbered paragraph that
  restates numbered results with `\cref`; physical time and spacetime are declined claims of the manuscript.
- Citations: natbib `\citep`/`\citet` with keys from the folder's `references.bib`. If a needed key is
  missing, give a COMPLETE BibTeX entry in your notes file, checked against the primary source (title,
  authors, venue, volume, pages, year). Never invent a DOI, page range, or quotation. A STANDARD statement
  needs a checked locator; if you cannot check it, prove the statement and tag it ESTABLISHED.
- Small networks ground and validate; they do not set direction. A finite-size computation is validation of
  an identity, never evidence for a general claim.
