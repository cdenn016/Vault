## Numerical checks and coverage

All four grid reconstructions passed. The maximum independent Euler discrepancy was 2.22045e-16, drift-rate discrepancy 7.10543e-15, probe source-cost discrepancy 1.42109e-14 and final covariance-fiber residual 8.67362e-18. All final hidden principal covariance Cholesky factorizations passed. The largest recorded quadrature error estimate was below 1.25e-11, within the declared 1e-9 runner threshold. Detailed per-case discrepancies are in [independent-checks.json](reverse-budget-validation-evidence/independent-checks.json).

N512 had three proposals and three visible changes on each mesh. N1024 had zero proposals on its fixed seed: its continuous propagation and hypothetical source probes were exercised, but no actual source reset was reached. Neither large-network path exercised an invisible self proposal; the existing tiny checks cover that behavior. Preserve the zero-event result. Choosing a new seed to manufacture event coverage would change this design.

The checker reconstructs sender-inclusive forces, minimum-Q drift correction, shared Brownian increments, transition arithmetic, candidate label selection and scores, assignment history, costs and retained fibers. It does not independently re-integrate source probabilities or reconstruct every intermediate covariance. No global numerical convergence or continuum error certificate follows from these checks. Finite-step conditioning and invisible-proposal mesh dependence retain the limitations in the implementation record.

## Consequence for the next phase

No declared numerical anomaly occurred. The source-dominated totals are observations of the implemented conservative functional, not evidence that the parent is accurate or inaccurate. The exact retained-law question therefore remains the priority. Before a full replicated measurement, distinguish how much of this reverse-weighted upper functional is auxiliary hidden-state comparison cost and what can be bounded in retained conditional-prediction terms. The existing [native-weighted conditional decomposition](conditional-characteristic-error.md) supplies context, but cannot be substituted directly into this different expectation.

If the next objective is to characterize this functional empirically, declare a replicated paired-grid design with more probes before running it; do not call the two-path output a study of its variance or trigger a 32-path run automatically. Further mesh refinement would assess numerical sensitivity, while a useful O1 result still requires an accuracy argument in the stated horizon regimes. The exact-Gaussian analytical investigation has one round remaining; the exhausted O4 investigation is not restarted. The previous projection-parity failure stays unresolved and failed.
