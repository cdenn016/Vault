Each compact path contains every original field except the two full final covariance matrices. The complete original archives remain at the local absolute paths in summary.json; those were the inputs used by the passing checker. The compact files alone cannot rerun the final-covariance checker. No matrix was approximated to make a check pass.

source.zip holds the executed source and pre-run contract. Its recorded base revision precedes the uncommitted runner and contract captured in that archive.
