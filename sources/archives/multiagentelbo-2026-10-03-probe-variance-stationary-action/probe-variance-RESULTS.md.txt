## Exact statistical identity and its empirical use

Let F_i contain the simulation random streams, both grids and both native/message histories, excluding probe times and prefix uniforms. The sampler reserves streams 0 through 3 for simulation and 4 and 5 for probes. Probes do not alter the histories. Under that intended independent-stream model, the two visible-cost probes G_i1,G_i2 are conditionally iid. They include both time sampling and native-prefix sampling, and already subtract the whole-current-assignment self correction. The lift's source cost is exact conditional on its sampled time.

For horizon H, write Y_i=D_i+H(G_i1+G_i2)/2. Conditional independence gives

$$
E[(G_{i1}-G_{i2})^2\mid F_i]=2\operatorname{Var}(G_{i1}\mid F_i),
$$

so v_i=H^2(G_i1-G_i2)^2/4 is conditionally unbiased for Var(Y_i|F_i). For independent path tapes with the same declared law, E[s_Y^2]=Var(Y_i) and E[v_bar]=E[Var(Y_i|F_i)]. Expanding Y-EY as (Y-E[Y|F])+(E[Y|F]-EY), whose cross expectation is zero, proves

$$
E[s_Y^2-\bar v]=\operatorname{Var}(E[Y_i\mid F_i]).
$$

The same proof applies to any paired arm or grid contrast by taking its drift difference and probe-wise source difference first. The empirical subtraction may be negative even though its target is nonnegative. Deterministic pseudorandom seeds implement the sampling model; the analysis does not prove PRNG independence.

## Consequence for the next study

These data do not support treating two-probe noise as the only source of the fine-grid budget variation. More source probes could improve precision of a grid-sensitivity comparison, where the point estimates attribute a larger share to probe noise. They would not remove a candidate-independent hidden-information contribution or turn the large auxiliary means into small retained-path error. The next O1 accuracy obligation remains the true conditional predictive characteristics, rather than another absolute auxiliary-budget measurement.

The present sample cannot choose an optimal probe count, distinguish time noise from prefix noise, or estimate variability across model realizations. It has only two probes per path, sixteen paths per size, and one model per size. No new experiment is launched from this posthoc result. The [grid moment proof](continuous-grid-moments.md) concerns an exact-arithmetic qualitative limit, not a finite-grid bias bound or a float64 confidence certificate. O1 and O4 remain open.
