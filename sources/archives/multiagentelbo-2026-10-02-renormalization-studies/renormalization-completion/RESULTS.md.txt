The exact augmented path divergence is the expectation of the accumulated equal-noise Gaussian row KL. Projection gives a signed reference-density correction under the actual path law. Independently, the sum of conditional projected-row KL is the actual parent path divergence plus a nonnegative information gap that is independent of the candidate parent. Thus paired score differences estimate actual path-KL differences at one fixed output and discretization. Neither estimator computes the absolute retained-path KL; sample means and empirical standard errors are not certified upper confidence bounds.
