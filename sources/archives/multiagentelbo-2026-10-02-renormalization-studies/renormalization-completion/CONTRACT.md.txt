## Declared finite-step law

Use the normalized Gaussian-record/source-label model of the coupled-learning contract. Initial X is the product Gaussian recognition law at the retained time-5 learn-both state; J is independently distributed by that state's optimal recognition rows. These numerical activation inputs are copied under evidence/activation. The fixed prior means and precisions are reconstructed with their original seed 20261002. The four continuation arms begin from this same complete activation. Subsequent parameter schedules come from the implemented law-level VFE learner, with source/link rates (0.2,0.05), (0,0.05), (0.2,0), or (0,0); belief rate remains one. This is deterministic learning of recognition and generative coordinates, not learning driven by realized particle histories.

At each inference interval h, refresh all source labels under one global Bernoulli clock per trajectory with probability 1-exp(-h). On refresh, receiver labels are conditionally independent with probabilities beta^P_ij(X)=softmax_j(log pi_ij-D_ij(X)/tau). Otherwise retain the previous labels. Next update X by the explicit Gaussian kernel with mean X-h M grad U_J(X) and covariance 2h M. U_J is the proper on-site Gaussian prior energy plus the selected comparison-record energies divided by tau. M is block diagonal with the time-5 recognition covariances, held across the experiment and carried under frame changes. Every sender term remains in grad U_J.

This is a normalized discrete splitting model. It is not an assertion of exact continuous switching-diffusion simulation or Gibbs invariance at nonzero h. The source temperature is tau=0.5; the diffusion temperature in this chosen kernel is one. The structural-parameter learning clock and the path clock share the declared numerical interval, with any smaller accepted optimizer substeps completed before the next interval. No stationary reset is made.

## Reference and error functionals

The Gaussian reference has the same initial X law and the same augmented source kernel, but its X drift uses fixed recognition weights beta^Q_t instead of sampled assignments. Its X marginal is exactly affine Gaussian because that transition is independent of J. The full augmented path KL equals B=sum_t h/4 E_P[(g_J-g_betaQ)^T M(g_J-g_betaQ)], including source-assignment fluctuations. At any declared linear projection R, and any positive Gaussian predictor S_R, the reference identity bounds retained-path KL by B+E_P log(q_R/s_R). The expectation is under the actual switching path law, not under Q.

A second bound sums E_P KL(N(m_t,C_t)||S_t(.|R_0:t)), where m_t=P(X_t-h M g_J), C_t=2h P M P^T. Its gap above true retained-path KL is conditional information discarded by conditioning only on the retained history, independent of the candidate S. Thus differences between these expected scores are exact differences of parent path KL even when its absolute value cannot be evaluated. The derivation and all hypotheses are recorded in trajectory-construction.md. No triangle inequality for KL is used.

## Execution design

The study uses N=512 and 1,024, K=2, eight intervals h=0.125 (horizon one after activation), the sparse cycle-plus-stride-17 support, four matched continuation arms, and 1,024 independent switching trajectories per arm with common random numbers across controls. Block-mean projections use blocks of 16, 32, and 64, with the same projected paths reused at every level. For each level, compare a rank-zero Markov reference, an X2 spectral hidden rank equal to the output dimension, and a matched-rank coordinate control. No parent rank is presumed optimal for the actual switching law. Nested projection matrices must compose to floating-point accuracy; this tests the declared linear channels, not full gauge-retained network identification.

Report both upper-bound functionals as Monte Carlo estimates with empirical standard errors. Such estimates are not certified upper confidence bounds. Paired predictor contrasts use per-trajectory differences and their standard errors. Reuse the complete actual activation and all common noise. Report Gaussian-reference path KL separately as a check of the existing machinery. Record failures rather than retrying to pass. A tiny test packet precedes this study; runtime/memory measurements choose CPU or available GPU without competing with unrelated jobs.

Acceptance for this finite study is correct kernel/force and density identities on tiny systems, positive finite reference and candidate laws, direct-versus-staged projection agreement, the specified control preservation, finite Monte Carlo scores, and complete provenance for the single executed run. Outcomes can favor or disfavor any parent; low error is not a pass condition. No Gaussianity of the switching law, autonomous parent closure, continuum-time convergence, generic thermodynamic theorem, or endogenous recursive formation is asserted.
