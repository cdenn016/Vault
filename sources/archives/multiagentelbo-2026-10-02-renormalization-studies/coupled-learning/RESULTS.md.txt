## Remaining obligation

This model learns pi and Theta jointly with product-Gaussian recognition. It does not yet learn separate model-channel distributions, random structural latents, or the support graph. Exact coordinate optimization of beta supplies no sampled source-jump process. There is consequently no declared stochastic trajectory law to which X2's Gaussian path-KL identity can simply be attached. Extending parent prediction requires an explicit stochastic or controlled history model, actual-law activation, and a suitable reference calculation or estimator for the resulting non-Gaussian histories. Autonomous parent closure, recursive formation, and thermodynamic limits remain open.

The next bounded scientific step is to specify that trajectory semantics and connect the existing predictive-interface machinery to it with an explicit approximation/error account. Further VFE reduction alone would not answer that question. Base transport remains deferred.
