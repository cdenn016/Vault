Declared October 3, 2026 before the large-network comparison. This phase addresses the measured twelve-point source-integration error. It adds an opt-in CPU float64 backend for two-label source rows, leaving existing profiles and the default cubature unchanged. Each selected nonlinear tilt is replaced by a Gaussian with consistently integrated first and second moments; this remains sequential assumed-density filtering, not exact joint posterior filtering.

## Moment construction

For standard normal z and Z=z^T A z+b^T z+c, retain the preceding characteristic function phi. Let S(t)=(I-2itA)^{-1} and h(t)=itS(t)b. Completing the Gaussian square yields E[z exp(itZ)]=phi h and E[zz^T exp(itZ)]=phi(S+hh^T), using ordinary transpose without complex conjugation. Integrating their imaginary parts against 1/sinh(pi t), with baselines zero and I/2, gives unnormalized first moment u and second moment V for label zero. Probability uses baseline 1/2. At the origin the first/second integrands are b/pi and ((c+trace A)I+2A)/pi, by Gaussian second and fourth moments.

The complementary label has mass 1-p, first moment -u and second moment I-V. Its conditional mean is first/mass; conditional covariance is second/mass minus the mean outer product. Thus the two tilts exactly reconstruct the incoming Gaussian moments in exact arithmetic. For a physical coordinate factor L, map the selected mean displacement by L and its covariance factor by L times the Cholesky factor of the selected standard-coordinate covariance.

Weighted Fubini follows from Gaussian polynomial absolute moments. In diagonal Gaussian coordinates, every probability/first/second component has absolute tail beyond cutoff 12 bounded by (2/pi)atanh(exp(-12pi)): use |E[z_i exp(itZ)]|<=E|z_i|<=1 and |E[z_i z_j exp(itZ)]|<=1. This bound is before dividing by a label probability and is componentwise, not a dimension-free matrix bound. The primary inversion citation and logistic derivation are in the preceding [activation contract](activation-source-calibration-CONTRACT.md).

Use SciPy 1.16 quad_vec on [0,12], norm=max, epsabs=epsrel=1e-10, limit=200. A failed integration status, error estimate above 1e-9, non-interior probability or failed conditional Cholesky stops the calculation. No probability clipping, variance floor, rank truncation or retry is introduced. Adaptive errors are estimates rather than rigorous enclosures; small-probability cancellation remains a numerical limitation.

The existing square-root update preserves the Gaussian residual covariance using thin QR. Full block sums remain fixed. Complete stars that contain an observed block use the existing independent-coordinate reduction; full retention uses native rows without quadrature. The integral route is CPU float64 with exactly two source labels. Continuous-budget configuration source_backend=binary_integral reaches the same route in sampled-prefix probes, self-assignment scoring and visible source updates.
