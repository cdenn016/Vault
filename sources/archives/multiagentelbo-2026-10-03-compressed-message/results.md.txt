# Compressed source-adapted message pilot

October 3, 2026. The reference-conditioned compression adapter is implemented, and the declared four-path pilot completed its numerical checks. On these saved histories it uses substantially fewer persistent mean/covariance entries but predicts worse than the existing dense Gaussian. It modestly improves over the reset control. This is an implemented accuracy/storage tradeoff, not practical O1 closure.

## Construction and runtime path

The prior [X2 study](../2026-10-01-predictive-interfaces/RESULTS.md) compiled small filters for a controlled Gaussian law. This phase reuses its reference-conditioned lifting idea in the existing source-adapted switching-history scorer. The basis is the previous half-block coordinate control, not a claimed optimal switching-law interface: each block of sixteen agents supplies the difference of its two eight-agent means in each of two coordinates. Neither future observations nor pilot scores enter basis selection. Live g07's exterior-interface result and c11's regular-filter scope were consulted; their exact controlled-Gaussian optimality statements are not transferred to this predictor.

`gaussian_compression.py` compiles the activation Gaussian conditional given `(P X,W X)`. QR construction of the residual factor avoids subtracting nearly equal full covariances. Between observations the persistent message contains only the mean and covariance of `W X`. At each step `gaussian_joint_scores` lifts that message, applies the existing sequential source cubature and unchanged-assignment convention, propagates with receiver and sender forces, conditions on the actual next block means, and encodes the result again. The discarded conditional law is reset to the activation reference. No native state, source law or structural schedule changes. The existing scorer remains the default when compression is absent.

The [standalone profile](../../../experiments/gaussian_compressed_message.py) passes its block size, shortcut threshold and compiled decoder to that scorer. Native metadata supplies the actual activation, eight-interval coefficient schedule expanded to 64 microsteps, model seed 20261002, K=2, GL comparison maps, temperature 0.5, rate-one global source refresh and dt=1/64. Only saved paths 000 and 001 at each of N512/N1024 are used. Matched dense and reset scores are loaded from the completed source-adapted archive; those arms and native trajectories are not rerun. `run_lab.py` remains on `PROFILE="neighborhood"`.

The [pre-run contract](compressed-message-CONTRACT.md) fixes this design. Execution used C:/anaconda/python.exe, Python 3.12.7, Torch 2.10.0.dev20251210+cu128, CPU float64, one Torch thread and sequential MKL. CUDA was available; no GPU was used. The sole run exited zero in 340.68490930000553 seconds. Compilation took 0.0989284 and 0.6335114 seconds; scoring the two paths took 52.9390821 and 286.3979513 seconds, respectively. These timings have no matched runtime-control interpretation.

These samples and the single model realization per size are **underpowered**. They support no significance, population-scaling or retained-KL conclusion. The comparison is motivated by prior work on these paths and is not independent held-out validation.

## Remaining work

This completes the bounded compression adapter and coordinate-control pilot. It establishes neither a useful small absolute prediction error nor the continuous-time admission or two horizon regimes required by O1. A next comparison should test a basis informed by source prediction against this fixed coordinate control at the same rank, with its basis construction declared before scoring. The prior X2 Gaussian-record spectral optimum cannot be presumed optimal for informative quadratic source rows. Scaling a dense temporary computation to more paths is not yet justified as a route to a practical small parent.

The provisional reverse retained-cost derivation still awaits the missing formal verification workflow. The historical projection-residual failure remains unresolved, O4 remains open, and neither exhausted analytical loop was restarted.
