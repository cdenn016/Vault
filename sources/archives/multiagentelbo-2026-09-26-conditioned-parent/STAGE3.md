## Scope

Take the completed duration-one selected-coherence event on [t_L,t_L+1], at Euclidean resolution T^(1/4), as the past activation event. Its probability tends to one along the joint schedule. Preserve the complete retained distribution conditional on this event. The parent inherits the microscopic rows at activation and subsequently follows its own source-learning equation. The comparison includes every retained coordinate and the complete source-assignment path over a following T^(-1/4) interval.

The static proof must use the actual entropy estimate and event-information identity; total variation alone does not control discarded KL. The dynamical proof must control the inherited proposal kernel under the parent law; one-time Gaussian concentration alone does not identify a path law. Neither calculation conditions on a future event or assumes an independent Gaussian activation state.

This is a parent after late restored equilibrium coherence. It does not prove parent closure for every realized microscopic history, arbitrary rare conditioning, the first contrast in the original faster-learning regime, or a second recursive formation. Those limitations are properties of the theorem's scope, not suppressed errors.
