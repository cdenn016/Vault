## Findings to address before extending the implementation

### P2: the row-only covariance solver silently admits inaccurate stable cases

[locality.py](../../../src/multiagent_elbo/realizations/gaussian/locality.py), lines 157â€“181, solves the Lyapunov equation by inverting an eigenvector matrix and applying exactly three refinements. Positive real drift eigenvalues do not ensure a usable eigenbasis. The function returns without checking its defining residual or positive definiteness.

A tiny admissible directed GL+(2) ring with N=3, radius=1, a=2, kappa=1, and one shear map returned max absolute Lyapunov residual 7.48874109473e-4. A three-dimensional stable Jordan drift returned 12.55% covariance error against an exact finite-series solution; a four-dimensional case returned an indefinite matrix. The [probe and output](evidence/locality-lyapunov-jordan-probe.json) use CPU float64 through the CUDA-capable interpreter, with one thread. Small systems are used here only to ground the numerical solver's contract. They establish no obstruction to large-network formation.

Use a Schur-based Lyapunov solution for the nonsymmetric branch, with a normalized residual and covariance positivity check. This finding does not invalidate the archived X3 random cases, whose recorded maximum residual is 2.66453525910e-15, or the symmetric joint-descent covariance path.

### P3: held-action correspondence needs a temperature-scaling correction

[CONTRACT.md](../../research/2026-09-30-locality-parent-memory/CONTRACT.md), line 11, equates the fixed weighted Gaussian action with the tau to infinity limit of def:obs-attention-label-model. Canonical c06's collective attention contribution is KL(beta||pi) plus the expected energy divided by tau. At fixed energies and reference, beta approaches pi while that interaction vanishes. X3 retains nonzero kappa pi coupling.

Describe the study as a held-prior weighted Gaussian-record reference, or explicitly declare the simultaneous scaling needed to recover its action and kinetics. Equality of the limiting attention weights alone does not establish equality of the collective dynamics. The declared X3 action and its finite measurements remain valid.

### P3: analytic-zero reporting mixes total and per-block units

[RESULTS.md](../../research/2026-09-30-locality-parent-memory/RESULTS.md), line 22, reports 3.4e-13 nats as the largest absolute analytic-zero discrepancy. The N=2,048 identity-complete record stores that scale as path KL per block. Across 128 blocks its total path KL is 4.36557456851e-11, and its maximum total identity residual is 4.61568561149e-11.

The runner applies the 1e-12 zero threshold to the total numerical path KL, classifying this analytically zero control as nonzero. Accordingly, result.json reports an ungated maximum relative error of 1.05729166667, while the prose's 7.0e-6 excludes it. Explicitly identify the analytical control and label total and per-block residuals. This is a reporting/classification correction; a study rerun is unnecessary.

First make the bounded solver and documentation corrections above. Then implement the existing X2 idea with an explicit record objective and a matched information budget. Start with the original full-row-rank block-mean map P as a fixed output objective, a positive-definite actual Gaussian activation covariance, and deterministic observed kernels X_(j+1) = F_j X_j + u_j + eta_j with independent positive-definite process-noise covariances Q_j. In an invertible retained/hidden decomposition, condition hidden activation H_0 on R_0 = P X_0. Stack the fixed future records (P X_1, ..., P X_H) as O_H = C_H H_0 + b_H(R_0) + epsilon_H. The hidden columns of P F_(j-1) ... F_0 give the j-th block of C_H. Derive the full record-noise covariance from shared process-noise propagation. The full-row-rank map and independent positive-definite current noises make this stacked noise covariance positive definite. Do not replace activation with a stationary law, or let the output objective change with the candidate interface.

With Lambda equal to the inverse conditional activation covariance, g07 supplies the generalized eigenproblem and the exact discarded activation-to-future information tail. Add N=2,048 if the measured memory and runtime warrant it. Check the tail independently by Gaussian log determinants and carry both the reference and record metrics under passive frame changes. This is a study, not a large-model unit test.
