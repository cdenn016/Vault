# X2 Gaussian predictive interfaces, October 1, 2026

The first X2 increment is implemented and measured. A reference-whitened spectral interface selects hidden activation modes relevant to a fixed future-record stack. An offline actual-pair projection then compiles a controlled predictor whose deployed hidden filter is smaller than the fine-network filter. Four declared cases at 512 and 1,024 agents passed their applicable numerical gates. This is a finite controlled approximation with a measured path budget, not an autonomous formation law.

## What the interface and predictor mean

Conditional on actual R_0, hidden activation H_0 is Gaussian with covariance Sigma_h. The future stack O=(P X_1,...,P X_4) has conditional form C H_0+b(R_0)+epsilon. Its noise covariance includes all shared propagation terms; independent noise at different record times would be wrong. The live g07 exterior-interface proposition applies with Lambda=Sigma_h^{-1}. It gives the rank-r information tail one half of the sum of log(1+nu_j) over discarded modes. Direct Gaussian conditioning supplies an independent numerical evaluation.

The fixed output stays the same for every rank and control. Coordinate retention keeps the first r coordinates of the declared hidden completion. The block control keeps the first r within-block differences of half-block means. Both are declared coordinate choices. The spectral statistic is optimal for the specified activation-to-future information objective among matched linear ranks. It need not optimize full dynamic path loss.

For each statistic W, Y_t=(R_t,W H_t) has an actual pair conditional. Compiling those Gaussian conditionals gives time-dependent Markov kernels on m+r coordinates, with the actual initial marginal. At deployment the predictor integrates only r hidden coordinates, retaining r means and an r by r covariance. It does not carry fine hidden moments online. Fine moments and dense mode selection are still required offline; this is not an end-to-end network-size-independent computation.

The path budget uses the history statistic consisting of current R and the candidate filter's hidden mean. Its conditional covariance is deterministic for the supplied schedule. Dense actual Gaussian conditioning gives omitted-history information and the expected conditional-row mismatch. Their sum is checked against a separately assembled complete Gaussian path KL. The initial R marginal agrees, so activation mismatch is zero.

## Remaining scope

This increment establishes a usable finite controlled predictor and an executed error decomposition. It does not establish endogenous finite-temperature attention, source/link learning, autonomous own-law parent dynamics, recursive formation, long-horizon control, uniform-in-N forgetting, or the thermodynamic limit. Ranks and time-dependent kernels were chosen offline using the known fine law and a fixed horizon. Endogenous structural histories can invalidate Gaussianity and innovation independence.

The next scientific stage remains a declared coupled collective-VFE model with source labels, recognition family, priors, natural-gradient metrics, and retained control/history state. Alternatively, extending this controlled reference to a stated rank/error family and sequential updates is a separate bounded investigation. Neither result is inferred from tiny systems. Base transport remains gated, and no Lean work was performed.
