## Stable nonnormal Lyapunov solve: demonstrated defect

The implemented contract is to return the stationary covariance for every real stable drift K, meaning Re lambda(K)>0, solving K Sigma + Sigma K^T = 2I. The code checks stability at locality.py:167–169, diagonalizes through an eigenvector inverse at :170–175, and performs three refinements at :179–180. Stability does not imply a usable eigenbasis.

Let N be the 3 by 3 nilpotent shift with ones immediately above the diagonal and K=I-N. Every eigenvalue of K equals 1. Define A(B)=(NB+BN^T)/2. Since N^3=0, A^5=0, and the exact solution is Sigma=sum_{j=0}^4 A^j(I), namely

    [[15/8, 7/8, 1/4],
     [ 7/8, 3/2, 1/2],
     [ 1/4, 1/2,   1]].

Substitution gives K Sigma + Sigma K^T=2I exactly. The investigator's recorded return instead has Sigma_00=3/2 with all other entries as above, giving residual -3/4 in entry (0,0). This is a valid stable input, not an inference from agreement between reviewers.

The defect is also reachable in the declared directed-link realization. Take three agents, radius-one directed ring prior, K=2 fiber coordinates, a=2, kappa=1, identity links except Omega_02=[[1,1/2],[0,1]]. This nonidentity map is exp((1/2)E_12), hence belongs to GL+(2,R). Reordering coordinates by fiber makes the drift block triangular with diagonal blocks A=3I-S, where S is the cyclic shift. Its eigenvalues are 2 and 7/2 plus or minus i sqrt(3)/2, each repeated twice, so it is stable. An independent exact rational solve of its 36 Lyapunov equations gives a symmetric positive-definite covariance; its six leading principal minors are explicitly positive in verifier-lyapunov-exact.json. The investigator's returned covariance has maximum entry error 1.4502375845243387e-4 and equation residual 7.488741094729967e-4. Reusing the same ill-conditioned diagonalization for refinement does not solve this case.

I executed verifier-lyapunov-exact.py with C:/anaconda/python.exe. The script independently constructs the drift and solves in exact rational arithmetic, then checks the recorded numerical return against that solution. Its output is verifier-lyapunov-exact.json. The run exited zero; it did not rerun the large X3 study or allocate a GPU. A solver that does not require a well-conditioned eigenbasis, together with a residual check on the returned covariance, is the bounded remediation. This finding does not invalidate the archived random X3 arm, whose reported maximum residual is 2.6645352591003757e-15. It does invalidate the helper's general stable-drift contract.

## Held action versus the high-temperature canonical model: demonstrated scope error

X3 CONTRACT.md:11 calls the held-assignment action equivalent to tau tending to infinity in def:obs-attention-label-model. The canonical c06_attention.tex:123–124 instead contains

    F_att(beta)=KL(beta||pi)+(1/tau) sum_j beta_j d_j,

where d_j=E_Q D_ij. For fixed finite costs and finite support, its minimizing row is beta_j=pi_j exp(-d_j/tau)/sum_k pi_k exp(-d_k/tau). Thus beta tends to pi, but the minimized interaction is

    -log sum_j pi_j exp(-d_j/tau)
      = E_pi d/tau - Var_pi(d)/(2 tau^2) + O(tau^-3).

It vanishes in the canonical VFE as tau tends to infinity. Holding beta=pi also leaves its interaction at E_pi d/tau. The nonzero X3 quadratic interaction with fixed kappa therefore does not follow from this literal limit. The manuscript at :134–136 explicitly distinguishes the row-rescaled tau F_att, which has the same minimizer, from the functional actually contained in the collective VFE. No corresponding scaling declaration appears in the X3 contract.

The held quadratic action remains a valid separately declared normalized Gaussian reference. The minimal correction is to remove the claimed equivalence or state a new rescaled objective and match its baseline, record strengths, and dynamics. Changing this description does not require rerunning the recorded held-action calculation.

## Proposed next phase: defensible with explicit predictive scope

The nearest bounded addition is to apply the new exterior-informed interface to future observations of a fixed original parent, rather than to claim autonomous closure or repeat X3. Its exact target should be stated as follows. For each fixed finite horizon H, fixed full-row-rank observation map P, deterministic held schedule of affine Gaussian kernels, and positive-definite actual fine activation covariance, choose a rank-at-most-r linear statistic of the hidden activation that minimizes the conditional information discarded about O_H=(P X_1,...,P X_H). Require independent innovations eta_s~N(0,Q_s), with Q_s positive definite and independent of activation. The observation map remains fixed across candidate ranks and controls.

Choose coordinates X_0=A_R R_0+A_H H_0 with R_0=P X_0 and an invertible coordinate completion. The actual Gaussian activation gives H_0|R_0=r_0~N(mu_h(r_0),Sigma_h), with Sigma_h positive definite. Define Phi_{t,s}=F_t...F_{s+1}, Phi_{t,t}=I. Then, conditional on r_0,

    O_H=C_H H_0+b_H(r_0)+epsilon_H,
    (C_H)_t=P Phi_{t,0} A_H,
    (b_H)_t=P Phi_{t,0} A_R r_0+P sum_{s=1}^t Phi_{t,s} u_s,
    Cov(epsilon_t,epsilon_u)
      =sum_{s=1}^{min(t,u)} P Phi_{t,s} Q_s Phi_{u,s}^T P^T.

The off-diagonal noise covariance is required: the observations share propagated innovations. The stacked noise covariance Sigma_epsilon is positive definite. Indeed, for a nonzero stacked coefficient vector, take its last nonzero time component v_t. The independent eta_t contributes positive variance v_t^T P Q_t P^T v_t, since later coefficients vanish and P has full row rank. The joint hidden/observation covariance is therefore positive definite.

The hypotheses of g07_coarse_maps.tex:434–520 now hold with Lambda=Sigma_h^{-1}, h=Lambda mu_h(r_0), C=C_H, Sigma_o=Sigma_epsilon, and o_0=b_H(r_0). The generalized eigenvalues nu_1>=...>=nu_d>=0 of (C_H^T Sigma_epsilon^{-1} C_H,Lambda) give the rank-r optimal statistic and exact discarded predictive information

    L_r=(1/2) sum_{j>r} log(1+nu_j).

Equivalently, L_r is the expected conditional KL between the future-record predictor using all H_0 and the predictor using only its selected rank-r statistic, conditional on R_0=r_0. This is a checked finite-horizon activation-information construction. It does not yet produce a recursively propagated low-dimensional parent or bound its entire path law. Later innovations can enter discarded hidden modes; the activation spectrum alone does not settle that issue. A declared sequential reduced state and actual candidate rows are still required to evaluate the path-law decomposition of c11_network_coarsegraining.tex:1504–1543 into activation mismatch, omitted history, and row prediction risk.

A schedule learned from the endogenous fine state is not covered merely by conditioning on its observed link values: such conditioning can destroy Gaussianity and innovation independence. Keep the first application to deterministic or explicitly exogenous held schedules, and retain coupled link/source learning as its separate obligation. The global contracted-width corollary at c11:320–372 supplies no substitute: generic quadratic Gaussian residuals have unbounded oscillation, and translated Gaussian kernels on an unbounded state space do not supply a strict global Dobrushin coefficient without further restrictions.
