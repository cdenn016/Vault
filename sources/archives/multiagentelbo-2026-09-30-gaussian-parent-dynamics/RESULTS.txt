## Mathematical contract

For the declared finite affine schedule, X_(n+1)=F_n X_n+u_n+eta_n with independent eta_n~N(0,Q_n), SPD Q_n, and a proper joint Gaussian activation law, the retained path is Gaussian. Write F in retained/hidden blocks A,B,C,D and the hidden conditional given the retained history as N(m,P). The predictive retained mean and covariance are A r+B m+u_r and V=B P B^T+Q_rr. The next hidden mean and covariance are C r+D m+u_h and W=D P D^T+Q_hh. Their cross covariance is Z=D P B^T+Q_hr. Conditioning on the next retained value gives hidden mean m_next+Z V^(-1)(r_next-mu_r) and covariance W-Z V^(-1)Z^T. Including Q_hr is necessary for correlated process noise. Initialization applies the same Gaussian conditioning to the supplied activation covariance, preserving its correlations.

For M=L L^T, whitening the held OU equation by L gives symmetric drift S=L^T J L. Its exact kernel has F=L exp(-S dt)L^(-1), u=L S^(-1)(I-exp(-S dt))L^T h, and Q=L S^(-1)(I-exp(-2S dt))L^T. The implementation evaluates the two response factors with expm1. A passive coordinate change T carries mobility as M'=T M T^T along with J'=T^(-T)J T^(-1) and h'=T^(-T)h. Holding identity mobility after a nonorthogonal frame change would select different kinetics.

The one-step comparator uses the actual propagated conditional P(R_(n+1)|R_n), with the same initial retained law. The path entropy chain rule decomposes KL to any Markov product into the history information plus the initial-law mismatch and the expected mismatch of each candidate row to this actual-pair conditional. Hence these rows minimize forward path KL over Markov products. For the deterministic Gaussian schedule, conditional covariances do not depend on observation values. The minimizing path KL is

D_path = (1/2) sum_n [log det V_one-step,n - log det V_history,n].

Gaussian conditional entropy gives this expected identity. It is not a pathwise log-density ratio or a score for arbitrary equilibrium rows. The first term is zero because the available history is just R_0. Individual later terms are nonnegative analytically; numerical values are reported without clamping. The matrix case's first computed term was -8.527e-14, a recorded roundoff residual.

The filter retains the full hidden conditional: 768 hidden real coordinates in the real cases and 1536 in the complex cases, with dense covariances. It is an exact conditional predictor in this model, not a network-size-independent closure.
