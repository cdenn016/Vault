## Declared basis

Keep the retained block mean P and activation Gaussian N(mu,Sigma). Write Sigma=L L^T, choose orthonormal U spanning ker(P L), and put B=L U. The Gaussian used to select the basis is X~N(mu,B B^T), the activation conditional at R=P mu. This is a fixed design reference, not a fit to observed retained values. Use only the first saved structural state, whose source comparison maps and priors define the first scored row. No later schedule entries, paths, assignments, or scores select the basis.

For each binary receiver row let ell_i(X)=log beta_i0(X)-log beta_i1(X) and freeze w_i=beta_i0(mu) beta_i1(mu). The declared sensitivity proxy is G=sum_i w_i E[grad ell_i(X) grad ell_i(X)^T] under that conditional reference. The gradients are linear because the log odds are quadratic. Evaluate their second moments analytically, including covariance contributions that remain when mu=0. Select the leading r eigenvectors V_r of B^T G B and set W=V_r^T U^T L^{-1}. The first basis contains r=64 rows at N512 and r=128 at N1024, exactly matching the half-block control. W is fixed thereafter. Store the chosen W and the full raw eigenvalue spectrum. Eigenvector signs and tied-eigenvalue orientations carry no optimality claim.

This is a frozen-weight gradient-sensitivity heuristic. G is not the expected source Fisher information because beta(X)(1-beta(X)) is replaced by its value at mu. It is not a path-prediction optimum, nor a theorem about switching filters. Live g07, section sec:cg-exterior-interface, motivates whitening and reference-conditioned decoding but proves optimality only for a linear Gaussian exterior record. The present source rows are quadratic. Live c11, def:rg-regular-finite-filter and open:rg-pointwise-parent-dynamics, remains the O1 scope. The decoder and finite scheduled scorer are unchanged from the completed compressed-message pilot.
