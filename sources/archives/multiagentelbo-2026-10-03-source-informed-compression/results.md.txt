# Source-informed compression at matched rank

October 3, 2026. Source-only sensitivity did not consistently improve total prediction loss in this four-path pilot. Relative to half-block compression, mean total loss improved by 1.295775 nats at N512 but worsened by 0.085335 at N1024. Both remained about eight nats worse than dense prediction. The small exploratory sample establishes no population conclusion. It changes only the fixed statistic used by the reference-conditioned Gaussian message and does not close O1 or O4.

## Construction and active execution path

The [pre-run contract](source-informed-CONTRACT.md) fixes a frozen-weight source-sensitivity heuristic. Under the activation Gaussian conditioned at its mean retained value, accumulate the Gaussian second moment of each quadratic source log-odds gradient. Weight each receiver by beta_i0(mu) beta_i1(mu), whiten the hidden coordinates and select the leading directions. The covariance contribution is retained even when gradients at the mean vanish. Only activation data, the retained block map and the first structural state enter the basis. Later observations, assignments, coefficients and scores do not select it.

This proxy freezes the probability weights outside the expectation. It is neither expected Fisher information nor a source-path optimum. The live g07 exterior-interface theorem supplies a linear Gaussian precedent for whitening; its optimality does not apply to these quadratic switching rows. The open c11 regular-filter and two-horizon requirements remain unchanged. No new analytical round or canonical theory edit was performed.

The standalone [profile](../../../experiments/gaussian_source_informed_message.py) calls source_informed_basis with activation moments, block-16 P, rank len(P) and schedule[0], compiles the existing GaussianCompression, and passes it to gaussian_joint_scores. The scorer still decodes, performs the existing ascending-receiver source cubature and unchanged-assignment hold approximation, propagates with sender-inclusive forces, conditions on the observed block means, and encodes. run_lab.py remains on PROFILE="neighborhood"; the dedicated profile is invoked directly.

The data and baselines match the previous [half-block pilot](compressed-message-RESULTS.md): saved paths 000/001 at each of 512/1024 agents, K=2, model seed 20261002, GL comparison maps, tau=0.5, rate-one source refresh, dt=1/64, 64 microsteps, and the saved eight-interval structural schedule. The shortcut threshold remains 1e-12. Actual retained arrays and assignments equal the archived half-block inputs. Dense, reset and half-block controls use their existing scores. No native trajectory, control filter or learning schedule was rerun.

## Interpretation and next obligation

At N512 the mean source component is slightly worse than half-block while configuration improves. At N1024 the mean source component improves while configuration worsens by more. The two larger-network paths disagree in the sign of total improvement, and one has no assignment changes. This is not evidence for a generally superior compressor, nor a negative result about source-informed compression as a class. This particular activation-only source proxy did not produce lower total loss on every declared path or at both size-specific means.

The next candidate comparison should include the future retained-configuration record alongside source labels, with channel weighting derived from the declared transition law and fixed before scoring. It should preserve the same matched-rank controls and evaluate both components. This is a proposed continuation for this predictor, not a theorem or an O1 acceptance change. Replication, practical low-dimensional execution, continuous-filter admission and the two required horizon regimes remain distinct obligations. O4's endogenous second formation is also separate and open.
