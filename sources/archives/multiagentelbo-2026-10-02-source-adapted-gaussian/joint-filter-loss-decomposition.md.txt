For persistent64, the source excess is 34.7679 +/- 2.9201 nats at N=512 and 83.4793 +/- 6.9754 at N=1,024, versus conditional configuration excesses of 0.8240 +/- 0.1837 and 2.3566 +/- 0.2741. ## Scope and exact decomposition

The supplied domain is fixed c_*, N in {512,1024}, K=2, block size 16, 64 steps of length 1/64, the positive supported prior and rate-one global hold/redraw source kernel, and the recorded activation and structural schedule. Candidates are precisely filter16, filter64, reset64 and the static-lift baseline of [joint-filter-CONTRACT.md](joint-filter-CONTRACT.md). The additional choices in this diagnostic are posthoc decomposition and stratification; no scientific parameter changed.

Source continuity is docs/DIRECTION.md, the current STATUS checkpoint, Chapter 11's def:rg-regular-finite-filter and open:rg-pointwise-parent-dynamics (live c11_network_coarsegraining.tex, lines 1288-1346), and the retained-law/covariance distinctions in Research/manuscripts/pifb2/09_section.tex. This comparison concerns a normalized joint predictor, not a Gaussian covariance proxy or an admitted regular filter.

For each candidate A, each allowed next assignment j', each finite retained history h, and each r' in the projected Euclidean configuration space, the joint predictive density is positive. Define

$$
s_A^J(j'|h)=\int s_A(j',r'|h)dr',
\qquad
s_A^{R|J}(r'|j',h)=s_A(j',r'|h)/s_A^J(j'|h).
$$

For a particle candidate, Gaussian normalization makes the first factor exactly sum_l w_l k(j'|x_l,j). For baseline B, it is k(j'|m_0(r),j), where m_0 is the activation conditional mean. Both factors are normalized in their respective next variable. Direct substitution proves the row identity

$$
\log\frac{s_B(j',r'|h)}{s_A(j',r'|h)}
=
\log\frac{s_B^J(j'|h)}{s_A^J(j'|h)}
+
\log\frac{s_B^{R|J}(r'|j',h)}{s_A^{R|J}(r'|j',h)}.
$$

The common initial retained law cancels. The saved log_source is log s_A^J; log_joint minus log_source is log s_A^{R|J}. This subtraction introduces no Gaussian-mixture approximation. Positive contrasts mean A has larger negative log loss. The decomposition is exact for every evaluated row; standard errors do not add.

Each statistic first sums selected rows within a path, then reports its empirical mean and paired standard error sd(values)/sqrt(number of paths), with sample standard deviation (ddof=1). Changed-step and unchanged-step sums retain all 128 paths, including zero sums for empty selected sets. Steps are never treated as independent replicates. Path classes instead condition on zero versus at least one observed assignment change and use their own counts.

These contrasts concern expected tape-conditioned joint-path risk, not absolute retained-path KL or tape-marginalized KL. The source component sums conditional source-row contrasts given retained histories; it is not the KL of the standalone assignment path after marginalizing configurations. One graph/model instance per N and one tape per native path remain the replication limits. Cross-instance and separately resolved tape uncertainty are [underpowered]. The source share is a descriptive ratio of sample means, not a confidence statement about a universal fraction.

## Narrow implication

The dominant observed discrepancy is in the whole-network source integral at observed changes. Improving a source-predictive integral with a checkable actual-law error quantity is the next justified target; the evaluation must distinguish a better integral estimate from a different predictive model. Changing only a Gaussian decoder while keeping its hidden cloud and source predictor fixed leaves the dominant source loss unchanged. Merely increasing cloud size is not established as sufficient.

The configuration deficit remains separate. Persistent64 loses to reset64 there even after source-score removal, including on no-change paths in the N=1,024 sample. Future work should retain the decomposition rather than claim a useful history effect from an improved joint number alone. This diagnostic proves no causal collapse mechanism, dimension-uniform limitation, or absence of useful hidden memory. Finite-particle log-risk control, regular-filter admission, endogenous feedback and intrinsic recursive formation remain unresolved.
