## Target

Item 3 activated the parent of prop:rg-formation-endogenous-cycle(b) after a late, high-probability coherence prefix on slow schedules (epsilon_L <= T^2). This item activates the same parent on the original early contrast of prop:phil-seeded-contrast-formation: T = eps^8, initial law N(m_0, I_12) times uniform assignments, product receiver rows learning at mobility epsilon_L, with the original coupled mobility epsilon_L = eps = T^(1/8). The seeded sub-event carries the probability floor c(m_0). That floor is bounded below but not close to one, so plain event information gives only an order-one bound, as rem:phil-conditioned-hidden-sign shows.

The obligations were three. The first was an event-specific hidden-information bound for the seeded early prefix. The second was parent path coupling on the following window, reusing item 3's stopped coupling. The third was to decide whether both hold at epsilon_L = T^(1/8) or only on slow schedules. A negative result was an admissible outcome.

## Results

**Obligation 1: the bound holds at a deterministic, thermalized activation time.** The seeded prefix factors into a seed (initial configuration in a ball of radius rho_circ about +-x_circ, a matching assignment, and a silent clock on [0,1/4]) and a remainder (a noise tube and no assignment change up to theta). The seed is measurable with respect to the time-zero data and an exogenous clock. Its only effect on the discarded coordinates is the imprint |F_M^T X_0| < rho_circ, because +-x_circ has zero hidden component for both matchings. The held Ornstein-Uhlenbeck flow contracts that imprint by e^(-K_Z theta), with hidden rates at least 4. The remainder fails with probability at most p_theta given the seed, and event information charges it only S_2(p)/(1-p). Convexity handles the union of the four seeds, and the seed probability never enters. The result, prop:phil-early-seeded-parent, bounds the discarded optimizing conditional KL by [eta(theta)+S_2(p_theta)]/(1-p_theta), with eta(theta) = 9 rho_circ^2 e^(-8 theta)/(2T) + O(e^(-16 theta)); the physical evaluator-retaining KL adds at most T. The bound is uniform in m_0.

The source used a Chebyshev tube bound. A new sub-Gaussian tube bound, 24 exp(-eps^(-4)/(36864 theta)), sharpens the rate on the source window [t_eps, t*]: the bound becomes (9/2) rho_circ^2 e^(-8(theta - t_eps)) T + O(T^4), which is O(T), where the Chebyshev tube gives only O(T^(1/2) log^2(1/T)). At eps = 1/100 and theta = t_eps the two tubes give 4.5e-20 and about 0.04. The proposition also bounds the native future marginals under the same fixed prefix, through a likelihood-ratio lemma against the frozen stationary kinetics. That bound holds at fixed m_0 and is not uniform in m_0. It also covers a path-measurable version of the prefix, which removes the clock clause.

rem:phil-conditioned-hidden-sign does not obstruct this, because the low-probability part of the conditioning acts at time zero and is forgotten by the dynamics. The only part correlated with the hidden path at activation is the remainder, which has high conditional probability. The remark's phenomenon returns only when the imprint has not yet been dissipated.

**The activation time matters, and the literal first-contrast clock fails.** The matching lower bound in part (c) of the same proposition shows that the discarded KL diverges when log(1/eps) - theta -> infinity; it is at least (e^(-8) rho_circ^2/7) c_m T^(-1/2)(1+o(1)) at theta = (1/2)log(1/eps)+1. The threshold for deterministic activation is therefore theta = log(1/eps) + O(1) = (1/8)log(1/T) + O(1). Inside that window both bounds are of order one, and neither vanishing nor positivity is proved.

prop:phil-early-first-completion treats activation at the first qualifying completion time t_c of the first-block mark of prop:phil-early-block-null. On the seeded prefix, t_c <= (1/2)log(1/eps)+1, before hidden thermalization, and the discarded KL at t_c is at least 7.2e-8 c_m T^(-5/8)(1+o(1)). The order T^(-5/8) comes from seeds whose first pair forms near (1/4)log(1/eps), while the other matched pair's discarded discrepancy still carries an imprint of order rho_circ eps. The mixture inequality H(q) >= sum_b w_b H(q_b) - H(B) transfers the divergence to the conditional law given the whole contrast event E_T, for each fixed m_0. That transfer carries the weight c(m_0), about 1e-30 at m_0 = x_circ, so it is a limit statement. This is an honest negative: activated at the completion clock, no parent of type (b) has a vanishing conditional error, in either arm and at any mobility in [0,1].

**Obligation 2: path coupling holds without the stationary reference.** Item 3 bounded departures through the stationary held-matching path law and its averaged flux bound (1-mu(M))/mu(M). That route fails here for two quantified reasons, recorded in rem:phil-early-stationary-mismatch. First, early rows lie in [0.4489, 0.5511], so (1-mu(M))/mu(M) >= 0.44. Second, the activation law is nearly singular to the stationary retained Gaussian, with TV at least 1 - 3e-18, because its eight slow retained coordinates are concentrated within rho_circ + delta_eps of a point at distance sqrt3 from the origin.

prop:phil-early-parent-dynamics instead couples the conditioned native retained path and the own-law parent directly. Both start from the same activation sample, share projected Brownian increments, clocks, and proposal draws, and follow identical retained dynamics while held at M. It then bounds both departure hazards pointwise on a retained tube, using the exterior-to-partner cost gap of at least one. The resulting bound is TV <= 24 exp[-1/(76800 T h)] + 4(1+sqrt(8/3)) Lambda_L(theta+h) h e^(-1/T). It is uniform in m_0 and independent of the prefix probability, because the post-activation tube uses noise independent of F_theta. It vanishes for every following window with T h -> 0, for instance h = T^(-1+q). The own-law parent exists and is unique, with rows in [e^(-epsilon_L(theta+u))/2, 1 - e^(-epsilon_L(theta+u))/2].

**Obligation 3: everything holds at epsilon_L = T^(1/8).** All three propositions hold uniformly for epsilon_L in [0,1], which contains the original mobility, the frozen arm, and every slow schedule. The rows enter only through the ratio Lambda_L(t) = 2e^(epsilon_L t) - 1, which multiplies e^(-1/T) in the departure terms, and through the choice likelihood ratio in the future-marginal lemma. The drift, noise, channel, reference hidden kernel, contrast event, and completion time are row-free. Item 3 needed slow schedules because its departure control came from row concentration on the selected matching. Here the seeded cost gap supplies that control. What restricts the early parent is the activation clock, not the mobility.

## Scope

These results concern the seeded sub-event of the original contrast. They do not bound the discarded KL given the whole contrast event E_T at a deterministic activation time, because contrast histories that enter a matching at a random positive time, or start outside the seed balls, are not controlled. They do not compare the learned and frozen first-block laws at epsilon_L = T^(1/8), and they do not compare initial means that differ in their loop coordinate. They also leave the window theta = log(1/eps)+O(1) and following windows of order 1/T or longer undecided. Under SPEC R15 the seeded sub-event is a coherence episode with a restoring clause and a margin over its stationary probability. The parent path over a following window describes persistence of the formed pair, not a second formation. Under R13 nothing here concerns which block the law selects, and under R14 nothing concerns recursion. open:rg-formation-adaptive-family therefore remains open; its statement and settling condition were not changed.

There is one point for the author under R16. The settling condition asks for parents of type (b) "activated from the recognition law of the episode prefix". If the episode prefix is read as ending at the first qualifying completion, prop:phil-early-first-completion shows that the positive branch cannot be met by such parents. The condition may need to name the activation clock, for example a deterministic time after hidden thermalization. This is recorded, not changed.
