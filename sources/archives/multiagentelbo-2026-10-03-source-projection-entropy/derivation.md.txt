## Continuous entropy balance

Put d_t=D(nu_t||q_t), delta=nu(h)-q(h), and ell=grad log(nu/q). The private hidden-noise covariance is
$$
\widetilde Q=Q_z-Q_{zr}Q_r^{-1}Q_{rz}>0.
\tag{1}
$$
The coordinate change W=Z-Q_zr Q_r^{-1}R makes hidden noise independent of observed noise; its affine drift subtracts Q_zr Q_r^{-1}h. Translation by an observed value preserves hidden relative entropy and its scores. It does not discard the shared-noise term.

A direct calculation in the original hidden coordinates gives the same result. For a density p, define the observed-coordinate density vector
$$
H_p=p(h-\bar h_p)-Q_{rz}\nabla p,\qquad \bar h_p=\int hp.
$$
Let dI=dR-bar h_nu dt, with covariance Q_r dt. Between jumps,
$$
d\nu=\mathcal L^*\nu dt+H_\nu^TQ_r^{-1}dI
+\nu(b_j-\nu(b_j))dt,
$$
$$
dq=\mathcal L^*qdt+H_q^TQ_r^{-1}(dI+\delta dt).
\tag{2}
$$
Here the common hidden generator has covariance Q_z. Gaussian affine filtering solves the second density equation. The covariance term in the first is the information in no visible assignment change.

For the entropy functional, the common-generator contribution is
$$
-\tfrac12\int\nu\ell^TQ_z\ell.
$$
The extra q innovation drift and the density Itô correction give
$$
-\int\nu(H_q/q)^TQ_r^{-1}\delta
+\tfrac12\int\nu|H_\nu/\nu-H_q/q|_{Q_r^{-1}}^2.
$$
Use
$$
H_\nu/\nu-H_q/q=-\delta-Q_{rz}\ell,\qquad
\int\nu H_q/q=\delta+Q_{rz}\int\nu\ell.
$$
Expansion cancels the cross term and leaves
$$
-\tfrac12|\delta|_{Q_r^{-1}}^2+
\tfrac12\int\nu\ell^TQ_{zr}Q_r^{-1}Q_{rz}\ell.
$$
Consequently the continuous entropy drift is
$$
-\tfrac12|\delta|_{Q_r^{-1}}^2
-\tfrac12\mathcal I_{\widetilde Q}(\nu||q)
+\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(b_j,\log(\nu/q)),
\qquad
\mathcal I_{\widetilde Q}=\int\nu\ell^T\widetilde Q\ell.
\tag{3}
$$

For a visible mark k, set l_k=nu(b_k), nu_k=nu b_k/l_k, and
$$
\xi_k(z)=\log\frac{q(z)b_k(R,z)}{c_kq_k(z)}.
\tag{4}
$$
The existing source log-ratio identity, now with its visible intensity, gives
$$
l_k[D(\nu_k||q_k)-d]+\psi(l_k,c_k)
=\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(b_k,\log(\nu/q))
+\int\nu b_k\xi_k-l_k+c_k,
$$
where psi(u,v)=u log(u/v)-u+v. Summing only over k different from j cancels the covariance in (3), because the full b row is normalized. The remaining compensator is l_j-c_j. Native jump compensation converts the integral of nu b_k xi_k into the expected sum at actual visible jumps.

Under the stated expectation and entropy-identification assumptions,
$$
\boxed{
D(P_{R,J}^{[0,T]}||S_{R,J}^{[0,T]})
+E d_T+\tfrac12 E\int_0^T\mathcal I_{\widetilde Q}dt
=E d_0+E\mathcal R_T,
}
\tag{5}
$$
where
$$
\mathcal R_T=
\sum_{t\le T:\Delta J_t\ne0}
\log\frac{q_{t-}(Z_t)b_{J_t}(R_t,Z_t)}
{c_{t,J_t}q_{t,J_t}(Z_t)}
+\int_0^T[b_{J_{t-}}(R_t,Z_t)-c_{t,J_{t-}}]dt.
\tag{6}
$$
The right side uses the actual hidden state and candidate densities, not the unknown nu. Exact activation conditioning gives d_0=0. If stopped terminal entropy converges only lower semicontinuously, with integrable residual domination and monotone convergence of the nonnegative costs, Fatou gives the corresponding inequality with left side at most right side. Equality needs the stronger terminal convergence condition.

Dropping terminal entropy and hidden Fisher dissipation gives a conditional upper bound for retained path KL. Replacing each signed residual and survival difference by its positive part gives a looser nonnegative upper budget. Neither a single residual nor a finite-sample average must be nonnegative.

As a correlation check, take zero drifts, Q_r=Q_z=1, Q_zr=rho, and initial hidden laws N(a,s) and N(0,s). Conditional on R, both variances are s+(1-rho^2)t, so their entropy is a^2/[2(s+(1-rho^2)t)]. Its derivative uses the coefficient 1-rho^2 from (1); using Q_z would be incorrect. This small calculation checks the noise convention only.

## Exact finite-grid companion

Condition on the complete independent proposal clock and its event-augmented mesh. This is a declared richer observation experiment: sampled retained configurations, assignments and the full proposal clock are retained. It is not the natural continuous retained filtration.

On each interval the native and candidate hidden laws use the same affine Gaussian transition, with the current sheet. The candidate conditions that Gaussian exactly on the observed endpoint. A common kernel cannot increase relative entropy. Disintegration over that endpoint therefore gives
$$
D(p(R'|H)||s(R'|H))
+E_p D(\nu^-_{\mathrm{next}}||q^-_{\mathrm{next}})
\le D(\nu||q).
\tag{7}
$$
At every proposal, including a self-proposal, use the normalized native mark b and candidate mark c. The existing source identity is
$$
D(l||c)+\sum_k l_k D(\nu_k||q_k)
=D(\nu||q)+\sum_k\int\nu b_k
\log\frac{q b_k}{c_kq_k}.
\tag{8}
$$
For a self-proposal the candidate's actual update is the identity, q_j=q, so its residual is log(b_j/c_j). It cannot be omitted merely because J did not change.

Summing (7)-(8) telescopes the hidden entropies. The common clock law cancels on averaging, yielding, whenever the displayed expectations are finite,
$$
\boxed{
D(P_{\mathrm{sampled}\ R,J,\mathrm{clock}}||
 S_{\mathrm{sampled}\ R,J,\mathrm{clock}})
+E D(\nu_{\mathrm{final}}||q_{\mathrm{final}})
\le E d_0+
E\sum_{\mathrm{all\ proposals}}
\log\frac{q_- b_{J_{\mathrm{new}}}}{c_{J_{\mathrm{new}}}q_+}.
}
\tag{9}
$$
No survival integral is added to (9): the full clock and all categorical proposal outcomes are observed here. The self term in (9) and the survival term in (6) belong to different observation experiments. Forgetting common recorded data is allowed by data processing, but it does not identify the numerical candidate with a continuous native-filtration parent.

For a fixed clock with no proposals, exact Gaussian activation, common affine propagation and exact Gaussian observation conditioning make the discrete retained predictor exact. The right side of (9) is then zero. This statement is conditional on observing the absence of proposals; it must not be transferred to a native history that merely has no visible change.

## Computing a full density ratio from local marginals

At one source receiver, write y=V^Tz for its sufficient local coordinates. The Gaussian regression update preserves the incoming conditional density q(z|y). Only the local Gaussian marginal changes from N(u,LL^T) to N(u+s,L_+L_+^T). Therefore, on the same retained fiber,
$$
\log\frac{q_{\mathrm{before}}(z)}{q_{\mathrm{after}}(z)}
=\tfrac12\left(
|L_+^{-1}(y-u-s)|^2-|L^{-1}(y-u)|^2
\right)
+\log\det L_+-\log\det L.
\tag{10}
$$
Summing (10) over receiver prefixes telescopes the whole Gaussian density ratio. Adding the selected native-minus-candidate mark log probabilities gives (4) or the proposal residual in (9). No full hidden inverse, singular full-space density, or large hidden determinant is required. The fixed independent-coordinate fiber Jacobian cancels from the ratio. This identity concerns the Gaussian regression map; it does not make the tilted native posterior Gaussian.
