For every real symmetric positive-semidefinite covariance Sigma, selected coordinate set I with C=Sigma[I,I]>0, complementary set J, and symmetric tilted covariance T>0, permute the coordinates into the order (I,J) and write

$$
\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}C&B^T\\B&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad
G=BC^{-1},\qquad R=D-BC^{-1}B^T.
$$

Here R is positive semidefinite by completing the square. The source update in [source_gaussian.py:90](../../../src/multiagent_elbo/realizations/gaussian/source_gaussian.py:90) is exactly

$$
\Sigma^+=\Sigma+
\begin{pmatrix}I\\G\end{pmatrix}(T-C)
\begin{pmatrix}I&G^T\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}T&TG^T\\GT&R+GTG^T\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1}
$$

Indeed its three blocks reduce respectively to C+(T-C), B+G(T-C), and D+G(T-C)G^T. Equivalently,

$$
\Sigma^+=
\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\G&I\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\0&R\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}I&G^T\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{2}
$$

These identities require no commutativity. They preserve positive semidefiniteness, including an ambient singular covariance supported on a retained fiber. If P Sigma=0 initially, then P Sigma[:,I] C^(-1)=0 and the exact update also has P Sigma^+=0. Floating-point assembly does not inherit that equality automatically.

Computing the I/I block as T and J/I block as GT avoids cancellation against the old C and B. It does not remove subtraction in R or D+G(T-C)G^T, nor does it enforce the complete covariance-fiber relation structurally. The three block formulas are exact equivalents, not different scientific models.

## Recommendation and minimal regression candidate

Direct block assembly is supported as a local precision improvement. It is not justified as the original failure's repair: the global residual does not improve, the incoming and outgoing captured fiber residuals are already small, and following a highly conditioned global covariance does not itself imply that the next local solve is ill-conditioned. The next forecast also adds positive process noise. No later receiver or forecast was executed here.

A proposed minimal assembly regression uses Sigma=[[1,1/2],[1/2,1]], I={0}, and T=2^(-60). Binary64 evaluation of T-1 rounds to -1, so the combined update produces zero I/I and J/I entries. Equation (1) instead supplies T and T/2 directly. Check those two values and positive definiteness; this regression isolates cancellation without claiming trajectory fidelity. This proposed regression was not executed.

Controlling full covariance positivity and fiber support would require a stronger representation or a demonstrated error bound. For example, with Sigma=UU^T and PU=0, write the selected factor A=U[I,:], and take an orthonormal decomposition with A^T=V_1 R_1. Then the exact residual factor is U V_2, and a tilted factor L_T with T=L_T L_T^T gives the update factor [U V_2, Sigma[:,I] C^(-1)L_T]. This avoids forming the subtractive residual. Carrying such a factor, preferably in explicit hidden coordinates, is a mathematically equivalent candidate; no factor implementation or full-step accuracy claim has been established. The minimal unresolved obligation remains identifying and controlling the actual first damaging numerical operation, rather than treating this local conditioning stop as its cause.
