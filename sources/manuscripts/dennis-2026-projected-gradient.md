# The Projected Gradient Route: Receiver Energies, Entropy, and Stable Updates

## Source and scope

The live source is [projected_gradient.tex](../../manuscripts/Attention/projected_gradient.tex) (12 pages, compiled PDF beside it), a technical note dated October 2026 that works out the theory of `attention_mode="projected_gradient"` in the `vfe3` transformer, the gradient counterpart of [[dennis-2026-projected-predictive]]. It was agent-drafted at the author's request between 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-02, in response to the projected-gradient audit `VFE_3.0/docs/audits/projected-gradient-pr30-32-audit-2026-10-01.md`. Every identity was checked in float64 against automatic differentiation of the literal energies at small dimension ($K\le6$); these checks establish the algebra, not behavior at scale or in training. No training result appears in the note. Its "Implementation map" section still says that the note implements nothing; that sentence predates the implementation, which landed in VFE_3.0 PRs #34 and #38 on 2026-10-02 ([[vfe3-2026-10-02-projected-route-consolidation]]). The local file may change after this dated record.

## Setting

Agents carry $q_i=\mathcal N(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)$ and frames $U_i$, with reference moments $\xi_i=U_i^{-1}\mu_i$, $C_i=U_i^{-1}\Sigma_iU_i^{-\top}$. The transported route ($\Omega_{ij}=U_iU_j^{-1}$ carrying both comparison and content) is declared retired. Head $h$ has comparison maps $S_{Q,h},S_{K,h}\in\mathbb R^{r\times K}$, a sending map $T_h\in\mathbb R^{d\times K}$ and a receiving map $O_h\in\mathbb R^{K\times d}$ ($r=d=K/H$ in the code), and content travels through the matrix link $L_{ij}^{(h)}=U_iO_hT_hU_j^{-1}$. The comparison laws are the exact partial laws $\mathcal N(S_Q\xi_i,S_QC_iS_Q^\top)$ and $\mathcal N(S_K\xi_j,S_KC_jS_K^\top)$, and routing is $\beta_{ij}^h=\mathrm{softmax}_j(\log\pi_{ij}^h-D_{ij}^h/\tau_h)$ with $D$ their KL. A gradient layer snapshots incoming states, routing weights and frames, then moves each receiver against an energy $F_i$ with that snapshot frozen in value (the outer graph stays attached). Fisher steps in local and reference coordinates coincide (Lemma 1), and every energy considered is in one quadratic family
$$F(\xi,C)=\tfrac12\mathrm{Tr}(\Lambda C)+\tfrac12\xi^\top\Lambda\xi-\eta^\top\xi-\tfrac{\kappa}{2}\log\det C,$$
whose minimizer is $(\Lambda^{-1}\eta,\kappa\Lambda^{-1})$ and whose explicit Fisher mean step contracts iff $\varepsilon\lambda_{\max}(C\Lambda)<2$, i.e. $\varepsilon<2/\kappa$ at the covariance optimum. The coefficient $\kappa$ counts how many times the receiver entropy enters.

## Ambient alignment (the PR #30 energy)

The energy implemented by VFE_3.0 PR #30 sums $\alpha\,\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert p)$ and $\sum_{h,j}\lambda_h\beta_{ij}^h\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert\mathcal N(M_h\bar\xi_j,M_h\bar C_jM_h^\top+\nu I_K))$, $M_h=O_hT_h$. The note proves three properties that all follow from lifting a rank-$d$ message to a $K$-dimensional law. The receiving noise $\nu$ is required for finiteness when $d<K$ (otherwise every KL is $+\infty$), not a chosen regularizer. Each head's law has mean zero and variance $\nu$ on the complement of $\mathrm{im}\,O_h$, so every head pins the coordinates it does not write toward zero. Each divergence carries its own copy of the receiver entropy, so $\kappa=c=\alpha+\sum_h\lambda_h$, the stationary covariance is $c\Lambda^{-1}$ (overdispersed when $\Lambda$ is read as a posterior precision), and the Euler step needs $\varepsilon<2/c$. At the shipped block-identity initialization ($C=15I$, $\nu=1$, $H=2$, $K=20$, measured on the kernel) the frozen minimizer keeps about 6.5% of the incoming mean, $C\Lambda\approx16.9I$, the stable range is $\varepsilon<0.12$, and at $\varepsilon=0.9$ the step has eigenvalues near $-14$, reversing and amplifying the outer gradient through each layer. These numbers are scoped to those values.

## Two entropies and cut routing

The note separates the receiver entropy from the routing entropy. A variational free energy contains the receiver entropy exactly once ([[blei-2017-variational-inference]]); ambient alignment counts it $c$ times. The routing entropy $\tau\sum_j\beta_j\log(\beta_j/\pi_j)$ belongs in the receiver gradient only under joint routing (Definition 1), where minimizing over $\beta$ gives weights that also see the content cost and, by the envelope theorem, a gradient carrying the comparison force $\nabla_\xi D=S_Q^\top B^{-1}(a-b)$, $\nabla_CD=\tfrac12S_Q^\top(B^{-1}-A^{-1})S_Q$. Proposition 5 shows that no content-informed functional affine in $\beta$ routes by $D$ alone unless the content term is source-independent. Routing by $D$ alone, as both projected routes do, is instead exact inference in a declared cut model, in which the content likelihood may not update the source label (Plummer 2015; Jacob et al. 2017). Under the cut, holding $\beta$ fixed during the receiver episode is exact, not an approximation, and neither the routing entropy nor the comparison force enters the receiver gradient. Joint routing is a different model to opt into, not a correction.

## Channel likelihood

The proposed energy treats head $h$ as a measurement $P_hx$ of the receiver that the sender's message predicts through a channel with noise $Q_h$: $p(T_h\bar\xi_j\mid x)=\mathcal N(T_h\bar\xi_j;P_hx,T_h\bar C_jT_h^\top+Q_h)$, with cut weights as tempered likelihood powers. It is the quadratic family with $\kappa=\alpha$ (Proposition 6), and its properties (Corollary 7) are the following. At $\alpha=1$ the minimizer is the exact tempered Gaussian posterior (conjugate linear-Gaussian update, [[bishop-2006-pattern-recognition-machine-learning]]). It is finite at $Q_h=0$ when $T_h$ has full row rank, so the noise becomes a declared floor. Directions in $\bigcap_h\ker P_h$ keep their prior conditional law (no complement pinning). The Fisher step is stable for $\varepsilon<2/\alpha$, with $\varepsilon=1$ the Newton step. Pooling costs $d\times d$ rather than $K\times K$, a factor $H^2$ in pooling and $H^3$ in factorization. The tie $P_h=O_h^\top$ keeps parameter names; with orthonormal disjoint heads, source-independent $R_h$ and a vanishing prior, the minimizer is the residual-free predictive head sum $\sum_hO_h\sum_j\beta_{ij}^hT_h\bar\xi_j$ (Proposition 8). Away from that limit the gradient route fuses where the predictive route adds, weights senders by channel precision, and its posterior covariance $\alpha\Lambda^{-1}$ omits the between-source spread of the moment-matched predictive covariance. A channel-alignment variant (KL in the value space) shares the mean but adds one projected entropy per edge and is not preferred.

## Updates

The Fisher flow of the quadratic family has an exact solution in natural parameters, $J(t)=(1-\rho)J(0)+\rho\Lambda/\kappa$, $h(t)=(1-\rho)h(0)+\rho\eta/\kappa$, $\rho=1-e^{-\kappa t}$, which stays SPD and does not increase the energy for every $\rho\in[0,1]$, and reaches the minimizer at $\rho=1$ (Proposition 9). With context frozen the closed form completes the episode in one solve, with outer derivatives $d\xi^\star=\Lambda^{-1}(d\eta-d\Lambda\,\xi^\star)$, $dC^\star=-\kappa\Lambda^{-1}d\Lambda\Lambda^{-1}$ by implicit differentiation, removing the sign-reversing unrolled Euler Jacobian. For the diagonal family the closed form $s^\star=\kappa/\mathrm{diag}\Lambda$ is exact, but partial damping is a declared choice. An implicit mean step is unconditionally stable in both families.

## Profiled state-dependent self-coupling (new section)

With $\alpha$ optimized against $R(\alpha)=b_0\alpha-c_0\log\alpha$, the receiver energy splits as $F=\alpha D(q)+G(q)$, $D=\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert p)$ against the layer prior. Profiling gives $\alpha^\star=c_0/(b_0+D)$ and $E(q)=c_0\log(b_0+D(q))+G(q)+\text{const}$ (Lemma 10). The envelope force $\nabla E=\alpha^\star\nabla D+\nabla G$ is the existing quadratic-family force with $\alpha$ frozen at the current state, with $\kappa=\alpha^\star+\kappa_G$ ($\kappa_G=\sum_h\lambda_h$ ambient, $0$ channel). The Hessian of the self term contains $-(\alpha^\star)^2c_0^{-1}\nabla D\nabla D^\top$, so $E$ need not be convex and the unrolled outer derivative must keep $\alpha^\star$'s dependence on the state. By concavity of $x\mapsto c_0\log(b_0+x)$, the tangent majorizer $M_t=F(\cdot;\alpha_t)+\text{const}$ lies above $E$, so the closed-form minimizer at frozen $\alpha_t$ is a majorize-minimize step (Hunter and Lange 2004) and $E$ does not increase, with equality only at stationary points (Proposition 12).

Damped descent ($\rho<1$) is proved for the full family: with scalar $\kappa$ the frozen energy is $\kappa\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert q^\star)$, and along the natural-parameter segment $\tfrac{d}{d\rho}\mathrm{KL}=-(1-\rho)\delta^\top\nabla^2A\delta\le0$. For the diagonal family the implemented interpolation contracts the mean error coordinatewise and nonuniformly, and the note gives an explicit counterexample: $\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}1&0.99\\0.99&1\end{pmatrix}$, $\eta=0$, $\kappa=1$, $\xi=(5,-5)$, $s=(1/2,2)$, $\rho=1/2$ raises the frozen energy from $1.500$ to $2.503$, while $\rho=1$ lowers it to $1.000$. Damped diagonal steps therefore carry no descent guarantee, for constant $\alpha$ as well. For `state_dependent_per_coord` on the diagonal family with a diagonal prior, $D$ separates over local coordinates, the frozen prior precision becomes $\mathrm{diag}(\alpha_k/p_k)$ and the entropy weight the vector $\kappa_k=\alpha_k+\kappa_G$; the closed form $s_k^\star=\kappa_k/(\Lambda_{\mathrm{loc}})_{kk}$ stays exact and the MM argument holds, but the per-coordinate form is tied to the local basis and is not frame covariant. For the full family, splitting $D$ by the ordered chain rule gives $\Sigma^{\star-1}=\tilde U\mathrm{diag}(d_k/\kappa_k)\tilde U^\top$ from the unit-upper factorization $\Lambda=\tilde U\mathrm{diag}(d)\tilde U^\top$; it was checked at $K=4$ against autodiff (gradients $7\times10^{-16}$ mean, $2\times10^{-15}$ Cholesky) but is not implemented, and the gradient route rejects that combination.

## Relevance to this research

This note supplies the first derivation-backed receiver for the gradient route of [[GL(K) gauge-equivariant attention]] after the transported route was retired, and it explains the audit's instability finding (the $c$-fold entropy and the Euler bound) as properties of a design choice rather than a coding error. The cut-routing argument is the theoretical status of frozen routing on both projected routes, and the channel likelihood connects the gradient route to [[Projected predictive attention]] through an exact limit. The profiled self-coupling section brings the $\alpha^\star=c_0/(b_0+D)$ rule from the multi-agent theory onto the transformer route with a majorize-minimize guarantee.
