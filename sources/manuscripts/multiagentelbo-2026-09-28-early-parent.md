# Conditioned parent on the seeded early first-contrast prefix

This result continues [[multiagentelbo-2026-09-26-conditioned-parent]] in the unchanged scalar shared-link four-cycle at one fixed base point. It moves the activation from the late selected-coherence prefix to the original early contrast of the seeded-contrast proposition: T = eps^8, initial law N(m_0, I_12) times uniform assignments, product receiver rows learning at mobility epsilon_L, with the original coupled mobility epsilon_L = eps = T^(1/8). The following are archived in sources/archives/multiagentelbo-2026-09-28-early-parent/: the manuscript section (manuscripts/MultiAgentELBO_Theory/ca_parent_early.tex, section sec:phil-early-parent), the four derivation records, the six cross-model reports, the validation summary, and the exact and numerical check scripts with their outputs. The repository record is MultiAgentELBO docs/derivations/2026-09-26-selection-to-formation/STAGE4.md (PR #97). The vault manuscript change is Vault PR #41.

The seeded prefix factors into a seed and a remainder. The seed consists of an initial configuration in a ball of radius rho_circ = 1/100 about +-x_circ, a matching assignment, and no clock ring on [0,1/4]; it is measurable at time zero together with an exogenous clock. The remainder consists of a noise tube and no assignment change up to activation. Because +-x_circ has zero hidden component for both matchings, the seed affects the discarded coordinates only through an imprint of norm at most rho_circ. The held Ornstein-Uhlenbeck flow contracts that imprint at hidden rates of at least 4. The remainder fails with probability at most p_theta given the seed, and event information charges it only S_2(p)/(1-p). The seed probability, of order c(m_0), never enters. At a deterministic activation time theta, prop:phil-early-seeded-parent bounds the discarded optimizing conditional KL by [eta(theta)+S_2(p_theta)]/(1-p_theta), with eta(theta) = 9 rho_circ^2 e^(-8 theta)/(2T) + O(e^(-16 theta)); the evaluator-retaining physical loss adds at most T. The bound is uniform in m_0 and vanishes when theta - log(1/eps) -> infinity and eps^4 theta -> 0. On the source window [t_eps, t*] it is (9/2) rho_circ^2 e^(-8(theta - t_eps)) T + O(T^4), which is O(T); this uses a new sub-Gaussian tube bound, 24 exp(-eps^(-4)/(36864 theta)), in place of the Chebyshev bound that gives O(T^(1/2) log^2(1/T)). The same proposition bounds the future marginals under the fixed prefix (at fixed m_0) and a path-measurable version of the prefix. A matching lower bound shows that the discarded KL diverges when log(1/eps) - theta -> infinity. It is of order T^(-1/2) at theta = (1/2)log(1/eps)+1, so the deterministic activation threshold is log(1/eps) + O(1) = (1/8)log(1/T) + O(1). The window theta = log(1/eps) + O(1) is undecided.

prop:phil-early-first-completion is an established negative. At the first qualifying completion time t_c of the first-block mark, which on the seeded prefix satisfies t_c <= (1/2)log(1/eps)+1, the discarded KL is at least 7.2e-8 c_m T^(-5/8)(1+o(1)). The order T^(-5/8) comes from seeds whose first pair forms near (1/4)log(1/eps) while the other matched pair's discarded discrepancy still carries an imprint of order rho_circ eps. The mixture inequality H(q) >= sum_b w_b H(q_b) - H(B) transfers the divergence to the law conditioned on the whole contrast event E_T, for each fixed m_0. The transfer is asymptotic only, because the weight c(m_0) is about 1e-30. Parents of the endogenous-cycle type (b) activated at the completion clock therefore have no vanishing conditional error, in either arm and at any mobility in [0,1]. The derivation record (not the manuscript) also shows that delaying activation to t_c + h works once h - (5/8)log(1/eps) -> infinity.

For the dynamics, prop:phil-early-parent-dynamics couples the own-law parent with the conditioned native retained path directly. Both start from the same activation sample, share projected noise, clock, and proposals, and follow identical retained dynamics while held. Departure hazards are bounded pointwise on a retained tube by the exterior-to-partner cost gap, which is at least one. The result is TV <= 24 exp[-1/(76800 T h)] + 4(1+sqrt(8/3)) (2e^(epsilon_L(theta+h))-1) h e^(-1/T), which vanishes for T h -> 0 and is uniform in m_0. rem:phil-early-stationary-mismatch shows why item 3's stationary comparison is unusable here. Early rows lie in [0.4489, 0.5511], so the averaged flux bound is at least 0.44, and the activation law has TV at least 1 - 3e-18 from the stationary retained Gaussian. All three propositions hold uniformly for epsilon_L in [0,1], including T^(1/8); rows enter only through the ratio 2e^(epsilon_L t) - 1 multiplying e^(-1/T). The binding restriction is the activation clock, not the mobility.
