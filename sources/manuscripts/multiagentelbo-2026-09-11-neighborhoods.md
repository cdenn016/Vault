## Confirmed results and boundaries

The four-domain cover has five pair overlaps, triple overlaps 012 and 123, and no four-way intersection. Occupancy changes active agents across context. Compatible local Gaussian node/pair laws assemble by clique contributions minus separators on the positive tree-Markov class; arbitrary marginal gluing is not asserted. Prior, recognition and reference have separate local inputs.

The nonflat fixture has independently declared belief and model SO(2) connections on a contractible two-dimensional base. Frames represent these fields rather than creating independent connections. Nonzero curvature does not establish nontrivial topology. For fixed-rank surjective $F$ and fine metric $h$, the quotient metric is $g=(Fh^{-1}F^T)^{-1}$, with $R=h^{-1}F^Tg$ and induced connection $(FA-dF)R$. Existence of this induced connection does not make $F$ parallel.

Recognition inference evolves full correlated Gaussian and event laws at a fixed normalized experiment. Retaining the hidden conditional recognition law makes parent evolution agree with fine projection in the fixture; autonomous coarse inference generally differs. The event family has a 39-coordinate realization of its 256-state Fisher flow.

Compression has qualified successes. Two score bins meet fixed-context event-law/rate criteria at about 0.8% of the full conditional floating-coefficient baseline. Four Gaussian modes satisfy the law criterion but fail the rate criterion. Adaptive additional modes with two bins meet the frozen-context criteria and interval/tail bounds. Equal-budget rate ranking selected the same modes and showed no ranking improvement. Small fixed-conditional bin reductions under moving contexts fail accuracy and do not save total stored event coefficients.

Moving/hybrid evolution transports recognition, reconciles support, then relaxes toward the arrival posterior. The full fine Gaussian is retained where identical old parents can continue differently. Zero-clock departure removes conditional KL; birth attaches the declared prior conditional under the vanishing-observation premise. A later normalized latent-curvature sensor learns geometry while keeping every Gaussian component. Prescribed and inferred geometry are distinct experiments. CPU fallback runs made while the GPU was occupied remain CPU evidence.
