## Method and epistemic status

Twenty-seven investigator agents read the manuscript in full, divided by scope. Eight structural editors mapped every section and assigned it a disposition. Fourteen domain experts recomputed the load-bearing results in their blocks, covering gauge theory, differential and information geometry, variational inference, dynamics, statistical physics and RG, proof checking, symbolic numerics, attention, and philosophy of science. Five cross-cutting reviewers covered PIFB2 program alignment, a mechanical label-dependency graph, notation, citations, and prose and style. Recomputations used CPython 3.14 with NumPy, SciPy and SymPy, and each finding names its script and residual. The report's author confirmed four high-impact findings directly against the source.

## Verdict

The recommendation is major revision, mostly structural. Every exact identity at the center of the single-point theory reproduced under recomputation, often to machine precision: the extended evidence identity, the total-correlation chain rule, the local–global potential identity, attention as a latent source label, the common-channel coarse VFE chain rule (residual $8.9\times10^{-16}$), Fisher contraction and score projection, the exact effective action and bounded action calculus, the Hoeffding interaction coordinates (with a sharp $3^n-1$ bound), the kernel–holonomy fixed-sector theorem (40 graphs), and the constants of the finite-depth formation theorem.

The defects fall into three classes. The manuscript is organized around a broader program than the current phase: about 242 of 559 pages serve the single-base-point chain, while about 147 pages are deferred base-transport and spatial-lattice material, about 102 are Gaussian realization, about 21 are off-chain classification, about 20 are provenance and software text, and 25 are notation and ledger apparatus. The structure that defines the current phase is missing from the general theory: there is no typed fast-belief/slow-model decomposition, the level at which the attention energy $\mathrm{KL}(q_i\Vert\Omega_{\#}q_j)$ lives is never constructed, the attention construction changes generative model between Chapters 7 and 13, and participatory feedback in time has no construction. Readability is limited by the SPEC §2.1 one-status-tag-per-paragraph rule (1,374 rendered tags against 489 numbered statements), not by the mathematics.

The four most consequential mathematical defects are these. The 07c hierarchical factorization omits the coarse graph datum from its conditioning sets and is not the disintegration it claims to be (KL $\log 2$ from the true joint in an exact two-state example; found by two investigators and confirmed against the source). The Chapter 13 meta-attention formula holds only for an undeclared single-edge model. The Wilsonian vocabulary of 07b has no instance on the finite networks at $c_*$. The 07j formation theorem is framed as attention- and learning-driven, although its coherence conclusion is linear relaxation of a prepared mean. The review also found status contradictions (the thermodynamic Fisher theorems are ESTABLISHED in one place and OPEN in another; the ledger marks as ESTABLISHED a graph-to-base realization that 07d declines), superseded numerical readings in the 07b NUMERICAL paragraphs, and a provenance package whose source-root path resolves only inside the Research-vault mirror.

## Proposed architecture

The review proposes three documents plus a research log, with every supplement depending on the core and never the reverse. The main manuscript (150–170 pages) carries the single-point theory in three parts: single-point foundations (program and standing conventions, geometry at one base point, probability semantics with a levels-of-description section, the fixed normalized generative law with typed fast and slow variables, one evidence bound and local–collective free energy, one attention chapter at $c_*$ with a transformer correspondence table, fiber information geometry and inference histories, and a new two-timescale chapter), exact coarse-graining at one base point (coarse channels, gauge-retained network state and fixed sectors, exact agent-network coarse-graining with a retained-state table in place of the closure theorem, and the corrected multiscale graph VFE with delayed feedback as an episode-indexed joint), and formation at one base point (the condensed formation witnesses including the September 20 endogenous four-cycle witness, a short interpretation chapter, and related work).

Supplement G (40–50 pages) takes the multivariate-Gaussian realization of Part IV and the Gaussian computations of Chapter 28, deduplicated from about 107 pages. Supplement B (60–80 pages) takes the deferred base-transport and spatial realizations, including the $\mathbb Z^2$ lattice chapters and Appendices B and C. A `docs/` research log takes SPEC §§9–12, the intervention-identifiability block, certificate digests, software-interface text, the dated 07b numerical logs and the orphan grand-canonical note. Before the Gaussian split, five non-Gaussian results filed in Part IV must move into the core: the kernel–holonomy fixed-sector theorem, the rectangular cellular-sheaf closure and cut excess, the frame-sector obstruction, the binary-latent ELBO witness, and the general Fisher-uniqueness citations. The Hermite material moves as one unit, and three Gaussian witnesses stay in the core as statements (the 07j translation-Gaussian formation family, the 05e dot-product reduction, and the declared-root lesson of §28.4).

The review leaves eight questions to the author: how the $\mathbb Z^2$ lattice is typed (agent graph at $c_*$ or discretized base), the framing relative to PIFB2 (the review recommends a bounded program-context section now and a separate program paper later), whether the core may adopt $u^m=u^b$ as a gauge choice, whether the single-point RG is the exact finite-step channel or a projected homogeneous-coupling map, whether coarse Gaussian families admit newly generated PSD self terms, whether the thermodynamic Fisher theorems are ESTABLISHED or OPEN, where the 07j formation chapter belongs, and whether dynamic topology is part of the model.

## New analytical results

Each result below carries the investigator label of its findings file. None has been adjudicated.

### Native growth bound for exact interaction RG (X06)

On the exact Hoeffding interaction tier, the depth-composed derivative along an exact orbit telescopes, because $E_{k+1}\mathsf H_{k+1}=I$, to
$$
D_{n\leftarrow\ell}=\mathsf H_n\overline{U^{\phi}_{n-1}}\cdots\overline{U^{\phi}_\ell}E_\ell .
$$
Every tilted $U^\phi$ is positive and unital, so it does not increase oscillation. With $\lVert E_\ell\rVert\le1$ and $\lVert\mathsf H_n\rVert\le3^{|V_n|}-1$ this gives $\lVert D_{n\leftarrow\ell}\rVert\le3^{|V_n|}-1$ at every depth. Under the chapter's tempered-comparison hypotheses no exact interaction mode can be relevant, and relevance can come only from a declared non-tempered comparison normalization. The sentence naming the interaction assembly as a second source of growth (07b:717–718) is therefore false. Two companion facts close the typed interaction beta on finite networks: for finite alphabets $\dim\mathcal G_\ell=|\mathsf X_\ell|-1$ shrinks under coarse-graining (63 to 7 for six binary agents blocked into three), so the comparison isomorphisms $J_\ell$ do not exist, and vertex-ratio blocking terminates within $\log_b|V_0|$ steps, so asymptotic exponents are never defined. Check: over 4,000 random directions at a nonzero orbit point the worst ratio was 0.457 against the bound 26; at a stationary fixed point the Hoeffding-coordinate spectrum equals the subdominant spectrum of the reversed chain (radius 0.2285). Status: telescoping proof plus numerical instance, not adjudicated.

### Fixed measure pairs are trivial under unique ergodicity (X06)

The rescaling map $\mathcal R_b(\rho_*,m_*)=(\rho_*K_b,m_*K_b)$ pushes both components through one kernel, so a fixed pair consists of two invariant laws of $K_b$. If $K_b$ has a unique invariant probability, which every strictly positive or irreducible finite stochastic matrix has by Perron–Frobenius, then $m_*=M_*\rho_*$ and the fixed action $H_*=-\log(dm_*/d\rho_*)$ is the constant $-\log M_*$. Making the identification kernel randomized with full support, which restores the product-equivalence premise of the Hoeffding coordinates, makes $K_b$ strictly positive and hence trivial at the fixed point. Nontrivial Wilsonian fixed points need a reducible identification kernel or infinite volume. The review recommends presenting, at $c_*$, the exact finite-step effective action, the retained-projection residual and finite-step multipliers, calling the construction exact coarse-graining (a typed cocycle) rather than exact effective RG, and moving the asymptotic apparatus to an infinite-network chapter. Status: two-line argument with a numerical instance (a random positive 8-state kernel with simple eigenvalue 1), not adjudicated.

### Law coherence with nontrivial holonomy (C1, X01)

Three agreement notions must be kept apart: frame agreement ($H_\gamma=I$), mean agreement ($\mu_i=\Theta_e\mu_j$), and full-law coherence (every transported KL is zero). Full-law coherence holds exactly when the root law is stabilized by the network holonomy, the condition 07c already states. In C1's witness, three agents carry $\mathcal N(0,\Sigma)$ with $\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$, links $\Theta_{12}=\Sigma^{1/2}R(0.7)\Sigma^{-1/2}$ and $\Theta_{23}=\Theta_{31}=I$. All three transported KLs are exactly zero, while the loop holonomy is non-orthogonal, lies at Frobenius distance 1.655 from $I$, and has $\dim\ker(H-I)=0$. X01 extended the settled coherence-stabilizer statement to $\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}2&0.7\\0.7&1\end{pmatrix}$ with holonomy $\Sigma^{1/2}R(0.9)\Sigma^{-1/2}$, giving $\lVert H-I\rVert=1.65$ and $\lVert H^\top H-I\rVert=1.96$ with zero transported KLs. The interpretation chapter's frame/mean dichotomy omits this third notion and uses a single-loop quantifier where every closed walk is required. Law coherence up to holonomy stabilizers is the agreement a $K_{ij}\to0$ coalescence mechanism needs. Status: explicit witnesses, not adjudicated.

### Per-edge twist invariants and the active gauge image at one point (X01, E1)

At $c_*$ every agent frame is a point of one torsor. In the manuscript's convention (passive changes $U_i\mapsto a_i^{-1}U_i$ and $\Theta_e\mapsto a_i^{-1}\Theta_ea_j$), the edge twist $H_e=U_i^{-1}\Theta_eU_j$ is invariant under the independent passive vertex group (residual $8.9\times10^{-16}$) and changes only by one simultaneous conjugation under a change of reference point (residual $1.8\times10^{-15}$). Every single edge therefore carries a gauge-invariant conjugacy class, not only every loop: on a path graph, where loop holonomy is vacuous, the edge invariants $(\mathrm{tr},\det)$ were $(1.62,2.33)$ and $(-0.96,0.19)$ against $(2,1)$ for the identity. The manuscript's "flat links" hypothesis $\Theta_e=T_{ij}$ is the identity-link (frame-aligned) specialization and is strictly stronger than trivial holonomy. On a triangle with coboundary links $V_iV_j^{-1}$ and the same beliefs, the summed transported KL is 29.56 against 298.99 for $\Theta=T$, and a two-agent tree with common frames and a rotation link of 1 rad is flat with $\Theta\neq T$.

For the active gauge group, a right-equivariant automorphism satisfies $k_j=T_{ij}^{-1}k_iT_{ij}$ (residual $1.6\times10^{-15}$), so at a design point the active image is a single copy of $G$. For $\mathrm{GL}(2)$ with two agents it has rank 4 inside the 16-dimensional passive product, against dimension 8 for the product of twisted diagonals. The per-agent vertex group of the link network is entirely passive. The choice $u^m_i=u^b_i$ is a gauge choice rather than a hypothesis: no energy, kernel or evaluator reads the relative frame $h_i$, so the relative-frame apparatus and the open $G\times G$ quotient item can be removed. The common-link specialization $\Theta^b_e=\Theta^m_e$ is frame-dependent unless $h_i=e$; its intrinsic form is $\Theta^m_e=h_i^{-1}\Theta^b_eh_j$. X01 names the falsifier: if agents at $c_*$ carried separate fiber copies with no canonical identification, only loops would carry invariants, and dynamical Role-B frames would restore content to $u^b\ne u^m$. Status: group-level algebra rechecked numerically, not adjudicated.

### Orbit Fisher form (X03)

At $c_*$ the attention network compares $q_i$ with a belief moved by an independent link, $\hat\rho(\Omega_{ij})q_j$, not with a base-curve transport. Composing the KL divergence jet with the orbit map gives
$$
\mathcal D\bigl(q,\hat\rho(e^{t\xi})q\bigr)=\frac{t^2}{2}\mathfrak k_q(\xi,\xi)+O(t^3),\qquad \mathfrak k_q(\xi,\eta)=g^F(\zeta_\xi q,\zeta_\eta q),
$$
whose radical is the isotropy algebra $\mathfrak g_q$ when the fiber Fisher metric is positive definite. This is the form in which link-transport, holonomy and plaquette energies expand near the identity at one base point. The manuscript contains it only in the last sentence of a base-direction theorem, and no single-point chapter uses it. Check: Gaussians under the $\mathrm{GL}(K)$ congruence action for $K\in\{2,3,4\}$, 200 draws, normalized $O(t)$ remainder at most 1.24 at $t=10^{-3}$ and $10^{-4}$; the skew isotropy generator $\xi=W\Sigma^{-1}$ gives $\lVert\xi\Sigma+\Sigma\xi^\top\rVert=1.7\times10^{-15}$ at zero mean. X03 also derived the third-order coefficient of the transported-divergence expansion, $\tfrac12g(v,\nabla^*_vv)+\tfrac16T_{\mathcal D}(v,v,v)$ with $\nabla^*$ the dual (for KL, exponential) connection, with SymPy residual 0. Status: corollary of an existing proposition, numerical and symbolic checks, not adjudicated.

### Λ-dependent Galerkin pushforward (X11)

For $\Lambda\succ0$ and injective $S$, put $\Lambda_c=S^\top\Lambda S$ and $P_\Lambda=\Lambda_c^{-1}S^\top\Lambda$. Then $P_\Lambda\Lambda^{-1}P_\Lambda^\top=\Lambda_c^{-1}$, so $P_\Lambda$ pushes $\mathcal N(\Lambda^{-1}h,\Lambda^{-1})$ to $\mathcal N(\Lambda_c^{-1}S^\top h,\Lambda_c^{-1})$, the Galerkin (energy-precomposition) coarse law. On each fixed-precision mean fiber $P_\Lambda$ is one parameter-independent kernel, so Markov monotonicity applies, with explicit Fisher defect $\Lambda-\Lambda S\Lambda_c^{-1}S^\top\Lambda\succeq0$. The maps compose across levels ($3.6\times10^{-16}$), and with an observation-free precision the coarse posterior is an evidence-preserving pushforward (mean and precision differences $3.3\times10^{-16}$ and $7.1\times10^{-15}$). What fails is a single law-independent kernel on the whole precision cone: for $S=(1,1)^\top$ and three diagonal $\Lambda$ no fixed map exists. The manuscript's statement that Galerkin aggregation "is a restriction, not a Markov pushforward" names the wrong obstruction, and two OPEN items narrow to varying-precision families, the singular boundary ray and infinite depth. Status: three-line identity with numerical checks, not adjudicated.

### Gauge absorption of the SPEC rotation witness (X11)

A directed linear-Gaussian law depends on a gain and its structural link only through $T_i=G_i\Theta_{i,\mathrm{pa}(i)}$, which transforms like a link. On a forest, setting $U_r=I$ at roots and $U_i=T_iU_{\mathrm{pa}(i)}$ places the prior precision exactly in the interaction family whenever every $\det T_i>0$, so the SPEC rotation witness for "the interaction family is not a consequence of directed linear-Gaussian models" is pure gauge. X11 states the law-level criterion for one edge: for $J\succ0$ on two agents with $J_{12}$ invertible, there exist $P,Q\in\mathrm{GL}^+(K)$ with $\mathrm{diag}(P,Q)^\top J\mathrm{diag}(P,Q)$ in the interaction family if and only if $(-1)^K\det J_{12}>0$. Necessity follows from determinants and sufficiency from the Schur complement; about 1,500 random sign-admissible precisions for each $K=1,\dots,4$ were all reachable. Chart-independent witnesses are an orientation-reversing gain ($K=1$, $G=-1$) or a v-structure whose co-parent block keeps its sign under every $\mathrm{GL}^+(1)^3$ reframing; the gain $-I$ witness is removable for even $K$. X11 names the falsifier: a typing rule that forbids absorbing gains into frames, for example frames restricted to $\rho_b(G)$ with $T_i\notin\rho_b(G)$, would make the original witness correct as a typed statement. Status: not adjudicated.

### Rate-separated clocks and the correlated slow-manifold shift (X04)

Declaring belief and model rates $w_b:w_m=\varepsilon:1$ is an anisotropic mobility: the natural gradient for $G_\varepsilon=\varepsilon g_B\oplus g_M$ is $\varepsilon\dot b=-g_B^{-1}\partial_bF$, $\dot m=-g_M^{-1}\partial_mF$, and the selected orbit depends on $\varepsilon$. In the example $F(b,m)=\tfrac12(b-m)^2+\tfrac12m^2$ from $(2,1)$ with unit metrics, the value of $b$ at $m=0.5$ is 0.8102, 0.5496, 0.5050 and 0.5015 for $\varepsilon=1,0.1,0.01,0.003$. The unweighted Fisher clock duration tends to $1+\sqrt2\approx2.4142$ (fast layer 1 plus slow manifold $\sqrt2$; 2.4077 at $\varepsilon=0.003$), while the flow-metric clock $G_\varepsilon$ tends to 1 with an $O(\sqrt\varepsilon)$ fast-layer term (1.0097 at $\varepsilon=10^{-4}$), so beliefs stop ticking that clock. In general $|dF/d\tau|\le\lVert\mathrm{grad}^GF\rVert_G$, with equality only when the clock metric is the flow metric. With a correlated metric $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&g\\g&1\end{pmatrix}$, the $\varepsilon\to0$ slow manifold is $\{(G^{-1}dF)_b=0\}$, that is $b=m(1+2g)/(1+g)$, not the conditional VFE minimizer $b=m$; at $g=0.5$, $m=0.5$ the observed value 0.66689 matches the prediction 0.66667 against the argmin 0.5. A rate declaration with a correlated metric must therefore say which slow manifold it means.

The review proposes a two-timescale section with typed fast and slow blocks, the reduced functional $F_{\mathrm{red}}(s)=\min_fF(f,s)$ with its Danskin envelope identity, the rate-separated natural gradient, the reduced-flow semiconjugacy onto $-\mathrm{grad}^{G_M}\bar F$, and a conditional Tikhonov–Fenichel limit stated as OPEN with named hypotheses (uniform $\partial^2_{bb}F\succ0$ along a $C^1$ branch of conditional minimizers and a block-diagonal metric). X04 also found that the manuscript's obstruction to a global unit-speed clock applies only to orthogonal clocks: for $F=xy$ on the open first quadrant a smooth non-orthogonal global unit-speed clock exists, with $|dT(U_F)-1|\le1.9\times10^{-9}$ at 20 random points. Status: explicit examples; the limit theorem is proposed as OPEN; not adjudicated.

### Attention-model mismatch between Chapters 7 and 13, and the reduced attention Hessian (X14)

Chapter 7 (05b §7.5) derives softmax attention from one independent source label per receiver, a product of rows. Chapter 13 (07b §13.7) switches without declaration to a single random receiver-edge mixture with baseline $a_i\pi_{ij}$ and never states its likelihood. The evidence-weighted meta-attention $\alpha'_i\propto a_{i|I}c_iZ_i$ holds only if the record likelihood depends on the receiver exactly as $c_i(o_i,y)e^{-D_{ij}/\tau}H(o,y)$ with $H$ independent of $i$. If unselected receivers keep their own label-free kernels $g_k$, the weight becomes $a_ic_iZ_i/g_i$; with $g=(0.2,1.7)$ the posterior occupancy is $(0.969,0.031)$ against the formula's $(0.785,0.215)$. In the product model the natural collapse is the prior-weighted row, $(0.709,0.291)$ against the evidence-weighted $(0.798,0.202)$, a total-variation difference of 0.089. The receiver occupancy $\alpha$ in the recursive state $\eta=\alpha\beta$ is never derived.

After row optimization the state sector sees $R(\theta)=-\log\sum_j\pi_je^{-d_j(\theta)/\tau}$, with envelope gradient $\sum_j\beta_j\nabla d_j/\tau$ and Hessian $\sum_j\beta_j\mathrm{Hess}(d_j)/\tau-\mathrm{Cov}_\beta(\nabla d)/\tau^2$. X14 showed that this correction is exactly the Schur complement of the joint $(\theta,\beta)$ Hessian over the simplex tangent (residual $2.7\times10^{-15}$); in a random example the frozen-weight minimum eigenvalue $+1.63$ becomes $-3.90$. In the settled normalized witness ($X\sim\mathcal N(0,\tau/\kappa)$, $J$ uniform on $\{-1,1\}$, $O\mid x,j\sim\mathcal N(x-ja,\tau)$), $U''(0)=\kappa+1-a^2/\tau=-2$ at $(\kappa,a,\tau)=(1,2,1)$ although every source energy and the prior are convex. The Theory manuscript lacks this object; it already appears in PIFB2 and in the September 13 attention-admission record. X14 also derived the exact cost of the constant-row restriction, $\sum_i\mathbb E_{Q_Y}\mathrm{KL}(\beta_i^{Q*}\Vert\beta_i^P(Y))$ (1.5661 with residual 0 in a check), and found that the "theory's own regeneration rule" invoked by the 07b NUMERICAL paragraphs is undefined in the manuscript and is not the exact $\eta$ pushforward. Status: brute-force Bayes and symbolic checks, not adjudicated.

### Scope of the finite-depth formation witness (X07)

The manuscript's formation theorem (07j, Chapter 17) is the construction recorded in [[multiagentelbo-2026-09-13-fixed-weakest-energy-adaptive-cyclic-rg]]. X07 reconstructed its proof at detailed-sketch level with every constant checked and found no demonstrated error, but the theorem shows less than its framing. The source record is standardized at the thermal scale $\sqrt{T/J_1}=A\varepsilon^{3+Q_1/2}$ and therefore saturates at the coherence scale: $|\log b_S-\log\pi_S|\le10^{-4}$, the source-dependent force is at most $5.8\times10^{-5}\sqrt T$ at $L=2$, and the coherence conclusion holds for every adapted assignment path, and also with the nonlinearity switched off and the parameter frozen. The calibration record contributes $1/T$ to the model Fisher information, so the learned parameter equals $2-e^{-\varepsilon t}$ up to $\varepsilon^{2Q_1+20}$ ($\varepsilon^{60}$ at $L=2$) whatever the network's law, and it moves only $O(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon))$ during the formation windows. Loop holonomy stays thermally near flat, the links are abelian translations, there is no model-channel record, and beliefs, models and links share unit mobility, so the family has no slow per-agent models. The parent is a partial marginal rather than a coarse agent with its own belief and model fibers.

The doubly exponential regime ($Q_1=20(2^{L-1}-1)$, $T=\varepsilon^{86}$ at $L=2$) comes from a loose early-interface step rather than from the dynamics: the exact Gaussian-core discarded-conditional KL is $0.103(A^2/T)J_{s+1}^4/J_s^3$, against the proof's bound $(A^2/T)J_{s+1}^2/J_s$, and the sharp scaling would permit $3Q_s\ge4Q_{s+1}+20$. The theorem fixes $\varepsilon_L$ before $k_L$ although the entrance conditions need $\varepsilon_L$ to depend on $k_L$, and its continuous-path coupling bound holds under every coupling at resolution $\sqrt T/\varepsilon$. What the family demonstrates is that exact marked parents, actual-law activation and own-law feedback are mutually consistent in one finite family with retained loops and positive-rate jumps. It does not demonstrate attention-driven or learning-driven formation, slow per-agent models, or nonabelian holonomy. The September 20 endogenous four-cycle witness (a proper two-agent episode with probability at least $3e^{-1/4}/512$), the only formation episode without supplied contrasts, is not in the manuscript. X07's next step is to restandardize the source record at the coherence scale, $f(r/(\eta A))$, in the $L=2$ family. Status: not adjudicated.

## Relevance to this research

The review does not change the program's model. It changes how several recorded results should be read. The finite-depth formation results banked on September 12–13 stand as proved constructions, but the September 13 theorem supports mutual consistency of exact parents, activation and feedback rather than attention- or learning-driven formation. The Wilsonian reading of the exact finite theory (relevant modes, interaction beta functions, fixed points) has no nontrivial instance at $c_*$, so the single-point RG is exact finite-step coarse-graining with residuals. At one base point the gauge-invariant link content is the set of edge twists modulo one simultaneous conjugation, the active symmetry is one copy of $G$, and law coherence up to holonomy stabilizers is the agreement notion formation needs. The missing two-timescale structure, the orbit Fisher form, the reduced attention Hessian and a single declared attention model are the concrete additions the review asks of the general theory.
