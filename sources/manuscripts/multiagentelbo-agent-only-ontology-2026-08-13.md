## Agent-only ontological target

At the intended ontological tier, an agent is the paired section

$$
z_i=(q_i,s_i)\in\Gamma(\mathcal E_b\times_{\mathcal C}\mathcal E_m|_{\mathcal C_i}),
$$

where the fibers contain normalized probability laws on declared measurable spaces. Smooth
statistical manifolds, Fisher geometry, exponential families, and multivariate Gaussians are nested
regularity or computational choices, not the ontology itself. "Agent-only" means that agents are
the only state-bearing relata. The base, principal bundle, gauge action, interaction incidence,
normalized record kernels, and variational rule remain relational or nomological scaffolding. A
stronger reconstruction of that scaffolding from agents and their interaction category is open.

Observations are endogenous records generated by normalized interaction kernels, not additional
substances and not automatically autonomous agents. At the finite exact tier, one fixed normalized
joint generates latent variables and interaction records. The full recognition law may retain
correlations that are not determined by the displayed marginal section pairs. A functional on paired
sections is therefore an exact VFE only after a declared lift into a joint recognition family; without
that lift it is an effective section action.

## Timeless inference and apparent history

The contextual base may remain fixed and timeless. Apparent change is a curve in a declared space of
paired sections, ultimately modulo gauge. A VFE objective determines an oriented natural-gradient
orbit only after a configuration metric or mobility is supplied; Fisher arclength then measures
information duration, not physical time. A stationary-action, Hamiltonian, or quantum-phase branch
requires its own measure, kinetic or symplectic structure, and boundary data. These branches are not
interchangeable merely because each is variational.

## Informational physicalization

The rigorous prototype is the connection-relative Fisher pullback

$$
h_s^\omega=(D^\omega s)^*g^F.
$$

It is passive-gauge invariant under the stated equivariance hypotheses, but connection relative and
generally only positive semidefinite, with radical $\ker D^\omega s$. Belief and model channels produce
distinct tensors, and different agents generally produce different tensors. A shared physical
geometry would require a declared channel combination, connection choice, nondegeneracy or quotient
construction, and inter-agent agreement or aggregation theorem. A collective joint-law section is a
promising candidate because it retains dependence discarded by marginal pairs, but that
identification remains a hypothesis.

The broader target is a typed, natural, gauge-invariant, and coarse-graining-compatible family of
physicalization maps from relational section jets modulo gauge to base tensors or other observable
quantities. Scalars and vertical covariant tensors admit composition or pullback constructions.
Connections, causal cones, operators, representation labels, action coefficients, and constants
require different typed constructions. The present results do not derive the topology or dimension
of the base, Lorentzian signature, causality, physical time, field equations, or operational rods and
clocks.

## Units and constants

The working proposal treats a physical dimension as a one-dimensional type line and a unit as an
agent- or community-relative basis choice in that line. This makes unit changes a scale gauge while
leaving the typed quantity and dimensionless predictions invariant. It is a programmatic
interpretation, not a completed derivation of physical dimensions from the agent ontology.

Information measured in bits or nats is dimensionless and log-base dependent. A pullback cannot
manufacture an action dimension. Any information-to-action relation therefore needs a derived or
postulated conversion morphism, for example

$$
S_{\mathrm{phys}}=\kappa_A I_{\mathrm{nat}},
\qquad [\kappa_A]=\text{action/nat}.
$$

Because one bit is `ln 2` nats, identifying one bit with $\hbar$ would set
$\kappa_A=\hbar/\ln 2$. That is a calibration condition, not a derivation of $\hbar$. The current
theory supplies neither a smallest nonzero Bayesian update nor an information-only mechanism that
generates a dimensionful constant. The defensible target is first a gauge-, coordinate-, log-base-,
and unit-invariant dimensionless relation, followed by a theorem or independent consistency
condition that fixes a universal conversion scale.

## Status retained by the ingest

- **Established within the current finite framework:** paired law-valued section kinematics; fixed
  normalized interaction-record joints; exact finite VFE on a declared full recognition law;
  connection-relative positive-semidefinite Fisher pullbacks; and VFE-oriented section histories
  after a metric or mobility is declared.
- **Additional hypotheses:** a smooth regular statistical tier; a lift from displayed sections to a
  full joint law; channel weights or joint statistical geometry; connection and mobility choices;
  and any collective section used for shared geometry.
- **Open:** continuum probability on section space; strict reconstruction of the relational
  scaffolding; intrinsic agency for every environmental node; shared nondegenerate Lorentzian
  geometry, causal structure, and physical time; and the derivation of dimensions, units, or physical
  constants.

## Research consequence

The next buildout should preserve this typing. First construct and verify the finite non-Gaussian
agent/record layer and its joint-law lift. Then define a configuration-space variational dynamics
with its mobility or action data. Only after those layers close should the project test collective
physicalization maps, agreement conditions, and dimensionless emergent relations. Rock, photon,
mass, inertia, and $\hbar$ analogies can guide candidate models, but they are not presently primitive
ontology or established consequences.
