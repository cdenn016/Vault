## What the revision states

A new Section 3 takes the group-valued link $\Theta_{ij}=U_i\exp(X_{ij})U_j^{-1}$, with the generator in reference coordinates, as the primary communication object. The frames carry the passive action, so the generator changes only by the adjoint action under a common reference change; every exponential link is invertible, and Culver's criterion fixes which elements of $\mathrm{GL}^+(K)$ the real chart reaches. Four facts motivate group values: a single head is singular, causal routed products contain no loops through distinct positions while invertible links supply reverse edges, an additive update can reach the singular set in one step of size $\sigma_{\min}$, and for an exact Gaussian pushforward the singular boundary lies at infinite Fisher–Rao distance ($d_{\mathrm{FR}}\ge|\log\lambda_{\min}|/\sqrt2$). For the stored value and output maps the generator $X=OT$ has the closed form $\exp(OT)=I+O\varphi_1(TO)T$, so the link is the flat identification plus a channel term whose first-order part is the projected matrix link $U_iO_hT_hU_j^{-1}$. On the principal-logarithm domain the linear link $I+O_{\mathrm{lin}}T$ and the exponential link are an exact change of coordinates on one group (conversion $O=O_{\mathrm{lin}}\varphi_1(Z)^{-1}$, $Z=\log(I_s+TO_{\mathrm{lin}})$), not a weak-field truncation.

With categorical routing kept outside the exponent, the complement split $\exp(OT)=(I-\Pi)+Oe^{TO}(TO)^{-1}T$ shows that under complement persistence the receiver keeps its own state on the unwritten directions; with tiled heads the complement is empty and the residual $r=1$ has no counterpart in that aggregation. The tiled message resums into the receiving map $\widetilde O=e^{OT}T^{-1}=T^{-1}+O\varphi_1(TO)$, with effective transfer $T\widetilde O=e^{TO}$. Expanding in the generator scale, order zero is a head-routed flat transport through the oblique projectors $\Pi_h=T^{-1}\Delta_hT$, order one is the standard multi-head value path, and order two is cross-head mixing through the head-transfer blocks $T_gO_h$. No finite truncation is group valued. The implementation's three charts are the effective maps $\widetilde O=O$ (affine), $T^{-1}+O$ (first order) and $e^{OT}T^{-1}$ (exponential); they represent the same tiled shared-generator mean maps up to the real-logarithm domain and differ in the meaning of the zero of the stored factor, in the reachable component and in optimization geometry.

A new Section 7 reads the same channels as evidence about the receiver. With channel covariance $R_{hj}=T_h\bar C_jT_h^\top+Q_h^{\mathrm{ch}}$, routing weights as likelihood powers and a Gaussian prior, the receiver energy has the unique minimizer $\xi^\star=\Lambda^{-1}\eta$, $C^\star=\lambda_\alpha\Lambda^{-1}$, which is the Gaussian power posterior; with the tied measurement $\mathsf P_h=\widetilde O_h^\top$ it reduces to the predictive head sum in a stated flat-prior limit, it gives a precision-weighted fusion residual at finite prior precision, and its Fisher flow is solved exactly in natural parameters. The profiled coupling $\lambda_\alpha^\star=c_0/(b_0+D)$ gives a majorize–minimize step. Because the implemented layer prior equals the incoming state at every episode start, the implemented closed-form step is a Kalman update and the profiled coupling stays at $c_0/b_0$.

Section 8 adds inverse-edge triangle holonomy for invertible links (flat fields are recurrent, invertible flat fields are pure gauge, a constant reference link $G\ne I$ is never flat, a second-order BCH formula) and shows that the triangle holonomy of tiled routed links is similar to $\boldsymbol\rho_{ijk}T\widetilde O$, which is $\boldsymbol\rho_{ijk}TO$, $\boldsymbol\rho_{ijk}(I+TO)$ and $\boldsymbol\rho_{ijk}e^{TO}$ in the three charts. It states the latent-link model of [[dennis-2026-dynamic-links]] with the implemented exponential chart as its zero-width static limit, the blindness of continuous class functions to unipotent holonomy for $K\ge2$, and the link field equation; that subsection is theory only. Section 9.3 reports the matched $K=20$ runs of [[vfe3-2026-10-03-link-charts-and-channel-receiver]], and Appendix G records the numerical checks.
