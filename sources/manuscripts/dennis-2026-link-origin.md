# Where the Matrix Links Come From: Group-Valued Links and Their Communicating Parts

## Question

The projected routes communicate through matrix links $L_{ij}^{(h)}=U_iO_hT_hU_j^{-1}$ ([[dennis-2026-projected-predictive]], [[dennis-2026-projected-gradient]]) with rank at most $d<K$, so they are not elements of $\mathrm{GL}(K)$. The note asks whether $L_{ij}$ is the communicating part of a group-valued link $\Theta_{ij}\mapsto g_i\Theta_{ij}g_j^{-1}$ in the sense of Wilson's lattice gauge field ([[wilson-1974-confinement-quarks]]). A literal product $\Theta=L\times N$ is impossible because $LN$ is singular for every $N$, so the relation must be additive, through a block of $\Theta$, or infinitesimal. A second motivation: on causal support every source satisfies $j\le i$, so loops with more than one edge need reversed edges, and these require inverses, which exist for group-valued links and not for singular ones.

## Option A: exponential link

With $\Theta_{ij}=U_i\exp(X_{ij})U_j^{-1}$ the covariance law holds for every algebra element $X_{ij}$, and $\det\exp X=e^{\operatorname{tr}X}$ makes $\Theta_{ij}$ invertible even for singular $X$. For $P$ of size $K\times r$ and $Q$ of size $r\times K$, the lemma $\exp(PQ)=I+P\varphi(QP)Q$ with $\varphi(z)=(e^z-1)/z$ (proof: regroup $(PQ)^n=P(QP)^{n-1}Q$ in the series) gives, for $X=O_hT_h$,
$$\Theta_{ij}=\Omega_{ij}+\widetilde L_{ij}^{(h)},\qquad \widetilde L_{ij}^{(h)}=U_iO_h\varphi(T_hO_h)T_hU_j^{-1}.$$
The non-communicating part is the identity on $\ker T_h$; the image of $\Theta-\Omega$ lies in $\operatorname{im}O_h$; under $X=sO_hT_h$ the matrix link is the first-order term, $\Theta=\Omega+sL+O(s^2)$; and $\det\Theta_{ij}=\det(U_iU_j^{-1})e^{\operatorname{tr}(T_hO_h)}$. Contracted with routing weights, the message is the flat transported message (zeroth order) plus the projected message (first order) plus higher order; the note stresses this is an observation about the form of the message and not a claim that either retired objective is recovered. A routed generator $X_{ij}=OB_{ij}T$ with $B_{ij}=\bigoplus_h\beta_{ij}^{(h)}I_d$ gives $\exp(OB_{ij}T)=I+O\varphi(B_{ij}TO)B_{ij}T$. Loops use reversed edges through inverses; when the generators commute (for example $TO=I$) the loop is $U_iO\exp(B_{ij}+B_{jk}-B_{ik})O^{-1}U_i^{-1}$, and in general it follows the [[Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula]] series, so commutators make it nonabelian.

**Linear and exponential links are two charts of one group** (added the same day). For $TO$ invertible, set $W=\operatorname{im}O$, $W'=\ker T$, $P=O(TO)^{-1}T$ and $\iota(B)=(I-P)+OB(TO)^{-1}T$. Then $\iota$ is an injective homomorphism $\mathrm{GL}(d)\to\mathrm{GL}(K)$ with $\det\iota(B)=\det B$, onto the Levi factor that fixes $W'$ pointwise; $I+OCT=\iota(I+C\,TO)$; $\exp(OT)=\iota(e^{TO})$; and, exactly, if $I_d+TO'$ has no eigenvalue on $(-\infty,0]$ then with $Z=\log(I_d+TO')$ and $O=O'\varphi(Z)^{-1}$ one has $TO=Z$ and $\exp(OT)=I_K+O'T$. So flat transport plus a linear channel term is an exponential link, and "first order" refers only to agreement of the two coordinates near the identity ($X=\widetilde L-\tfrac12\widetilde L^2+\cdots$). In the tiled case the operator $M=O'T$ that the projected predictive route applies is itself the group element, $M=\exp(X)$ with $X=OT$ whenever $M$ has a real logarithm, the identity initialization $M=I$ is the origin $X=0$, and an $r$-edge path with a shared generator is $M^r=e^{rX}$. The charts differ at the singular boundary ($\det$ can pass through zero in the affine chart, never in the logarithmic one), in components (the exponential chart excludes negative determinant and needs paired negative-eigenvalue Jordan blocks, [[culver-1966-existence]]), in optimization geometry (the code's M-step acts on $O,T$, the affine chart), and at order one for finite generators ($e^X=eI$ against $I+X=2I$ at $X=I$). The statement is for an unrouted operator with one generator; the routed head sum and edge-dependent generators are outside it.

## Option B: compression of a primitive link

Take $\Theta_{ij}=U_iG_{ij}U_j^{-1}$ with $G_{ij}\in\mathrm{GL}(K)$ primary and covariant projectors $\Pi_{i,h}=U_iP_hU_i^{-1}$, $P_h=O_h(T_hO_h)^{-1}T_h$ (projection onto $\operatorname{im}O_h$ along $\ker T_h$, assuming $T_hO_h$ invertible). The link splits into four covariant blocks, the communicating one $\Pi_i\Theta\Pi_j$ and the non-communicating remainder $(I-\Pi_i)\Theta(I-\Pi_j)$ with two mixed blocks between. In a basis adapted to $W\oplus W'$, $W=\operatorname{im}O_h$, and with $A$ invertible, $G=n_-\,\mathrm{diag}(A,S)\,n_+$ with unipotent outer factors and Schur complement $S=D-CA^{-1}B$, so $\det G=\det A\det S$. The middle factor lies in the Levi factor $\mathrm{GL}(d)\times\mathrm{GL}(K-d)$ of the parabolic subgroup stabilizing $W$ (references in the note: Humphreys, Linear Algebraic Groups, 1975; Zhang, ed., The Schur Complement and Its Applications, 2005; neither is a vault note). This is the precise multiplicative form of $\Theta=L\times N$: the product holds between the mixing factors and the block-diagonal pair $(A,S)$, not between $L$ and $N$. Assigning $W_i=U_i\operatorname{im}O_h$ to every agent is a section of the associated Grassmannian bundle, a reduction of structure group to the parabolic; the note flags the analogy with Higgs symmetry breaking as formal, with no potential or dynamics proposed. The blocks $B$, $C$, $D$ never enter a message, so they get no gradient from routing or content unless a receiver energy reads them: the `ambient_alignment` energy pins the complement of each output range to zero, while `channel_likelihood` leaves it at the prior, so identifiability of the hidden block is a property of the receiver energy, not of the link. Rotary embedding ([[su2024roformer]]) is the nearest standard precedent, a relative-position orthogonal link acting on scores.

## Option C: the head-sum operator is already group-valued

The edge operator applied at fixed routing, $\mathcal C_{ij}=\sum_h\beta_{ij}^{(h)}L_{ij}^{(h)}=U_iOB_{ij}TU_j^{-1}$ with stacked $K\times K$ matrices $O=[O_1\cdots O_H]$ and $T=[T_1;\dots;T_H]$, transforms as a link and lies in $\mathrm{GL}(K)$ when $O$, $T$ are invertible and every $\beta^{(h)}_{ij}>0$, with $\det\mathcal C_{ij}=\det(U_iU_j^{-1})\det(TO)\prod_h(\beta_{ij}^{(h)})^d$. Invertibility is generic, not structural: it degrades as routing concentrates, and a singular $O$ or $T$, or $K\ne Hd$, removes it. With trivial frames this is the standard multi-head value path $\sum_jW_OB_{ij}W_Vx_j$ ([[vaswani-2017-attention]]). For $TO=I$ the triangle loop is $U_iO(\bigoplus_h\beta_{ij}\beta_{jk}/\beta_{ik}\,I_d)O^{-1}U_i^{-1}$. For softmax routing $\beta_{ij}=e^{s_{ij}}/Z_i$ the log of each factor is $s_{ij}+s_{jk}-s_{ik}-\log Z_j$, and for routing $\beta=\pi e^{-D/\kappa}/Z$ it is
$$-\frac{D_{ij}+D_{jk}-D_{ik}}{\kappa}+\log\frac{\pi_{ij}\pi_{jk}}{\pi_{ik}}-\log Z_j.$$
The Kullback-Leibler divergence has no triangle inequality, and here it compares a query law with a key law under different maps, so the defect has indefinite sign. Reading attention weights as an abelian connection is an interpretation; the proposition proves only the algebra.

## Aggregation, residual and nonlinearity under Option A

The complement split is exact: with $P=O(TO)^{-1}T$ for stacked $O$ ($K\times Hd$) and $T$ ($Hd\times K$), $TO$ invertible, $\exp(OT)=(I-P)+O\,E\,T$ with $E=e^{TO}(TO)^{-1}$. Routing belongs outside the exponent: a product or sum of exponentials over different sources has no source vector to act on and is not normalized by $\sum_j\beta_{ij}=1$, so the generator is taken routing independent and each head draws an independent source label $J^h\sim\beta_{ij}^{(h)}$, giving $\mathbb E[\xi_i']=\mathbb E[(I-P)\xi_{J^0}]+\sum_{h,j}\beta_{ij}^{(h)}OE_{:,h}T_h\xi_j$. The weights depend on the states, $\beta=\beta(\mu,\Sigma)$, but are held fixed while the message is formed, which is the cut model of [[dennis-2026-projected-gradient]]; the formula is the expectation conditional on those states. If only sent content may cross between agents, the complement carrier must be the receiver ($J^0=i$), so
$$\mu_i^+=U_i(I-P)U_i^{-1}\mu_i+\sum_{h,j}\beta_{ij}^{(h)}U_iOE_{:,h}T_hU_j^{-1}\mu_j.$$
The flat term is therefore a residual only on unsent directions. When the heads tile the fiber ($Hd=K$, which the code requires, with $O$, $T$ invertible) $P=I$ and there is no residual at all: Option A reduces to the residual-free projected predictive head sum with receiving maps $O_h'=OE_{:,h}$. Consequently the $r=1$ additive residual of the code has no counterpart in Option A under the code's configuration, and Option A adds no expressive power in the tiled case (the note does not claim the converse, that every current set of receiving maps has this form). A genuine projected residual stream needs $Hd<K$ and describes an architecture where the $K-Hd$ unwritten coordinates are a stream every head can read through $T$.

The Gaussian-fusion argument of the gradient route reaches the same structure: for an isotropic prior of precision $\alpha$ and message evidence of precision $\lambda$ on $W$, $\xi_i^+=(I-P_W)\xi_i+P_W(\alpha\xi_i+\lambda m_i)/(\alpha+\lambda)$, a residual that is full on unobserved directions and has coefficient $\alpha/(\alpha+\lambda)$ on observed ones, with the Option A formula as the limit $\lambda/\alpha\to\infty$. The code's additive residual $\mu_i+m_i$ matches neither derivation; the note attributes its justification to optimization ([[he-2016-resnet]]) rather than inference.

On nonlinearity: for the routing-independent generator $\varphi$ acts on the weight matrix $T_hO_h$, so it is not an activation (the message is nonlinear in the states only through $\beta(\mu,\Sigma)$, like softmax attention). A routed self-link $\exp(OB_{ii}T)$ is a single-source map and, for tiled heads with $TO=I$, equals $\sum_he^{\beta_{ii}^{(h)}(\mu,\Sigma)}O_hT_h$, a block gate in $(1,e)$: it reads the state only through the self-routing weight, acts on whole head blocks, and can amplify by at most $e$ but never suppress or flip sign. A general state-dependent diagonal self-link generator $X(\xi)=\mathrm{diag}(a(\xi))$ gives coordinatewise gates, and since $x\sigma(x)=\exp(\log\sigma(x))x$, SiLU ([[elfwing-2018-silu]]) and GELU ([[hendrycks2016gaussian]]) are exponential self-links with generators $\log\sigma$ and $\log\Phi$. The note says this is a rewriting, not a derivation, because nothing fixes the generator; [[Template routing]] supplies a principle for a normalized gate ([[dennis-2026-template-routing]]).

## Relations and ranking

The options nest: C is B with $G_{ij}=OB_{ij}T$ and head projectors; A with routing in the exponent agrees with C at first order apart from the identity (flat transport); B is the most general frame, and A fixes the hidden block to the identity. By how principled they are (structural group property, every component fixed by the model's fields, standard place in gauge theory, loops defined) the ranking is A, B, C; by closeness to the value path of standard attention it is C, A, B. The note concludes that A is the principled extension and C is the present model written in link form, and then qualifies the first half: with a constant generator, A is the same unrouted operator in logarithmic coordinates, so it adds content only through edge-dependent generators, chart-natural optimization, or the boundary and component structure of the group that the affine chart ignores.

## Numerical checks

Executed in float64 (`checks.py` at $K=6$, $H=3$, $d=2$; `checks_aggregation.py` at $K=7$ with a rank-4 channel), relative errors as in the output files. $\exp(OT)=I+O\varphi(TO)T$: $5.6\times10^{-16}$. $\det\exp(OT)=e^{\operatorname{tr}(TO)}$: $1.5\times10^{-16}$. $\exp(OT)$ fixes $\ker T$: $5.6\times10^{-16}$. $\Theta=\Omega+\widetilde L$: $7.3\times10^{-16}$. First-order defect $(\Theta-\Omega)/s-L$: $2.1\times10^{-2}$ at $s=10^{-2}$ and $2.2\times10^{-3}$ at $s=10^{-3}$ (linear in $s$). Routed $\exp(OBT)=I+O\varphi(BTO)BT$: $9.1\times10^{-15}$. Block factorization $G=n_-(A+S)n_+$: $2.6\times10^{-11}$ (limited by the conditioning of the random block $A$); $\det G=\det A\det S$: $4.3\times10^{-15}$; idempotence of the projector: $1.1\times10^{-14}$. Head-sum loop equals the ratio of weights: $2.7\times10^{-16}$; exponential-link loop $e^{b_{ij}+b_{jk}-b_{ik}}$: $7.4\times10^{-16}$; loop trace invariant under a frame change: error 0. Complement split $\exp(OT)=(I-P)+Oe^{TO}(TO)^{-1}T$: $1.6\times10^{-15}$; $I-P$ fixes $\ker T$ and kills $\operatorname{im}O$: $1.2\times10^{-15}$; exact enumeration over head-label tuples reproduces the closed aggregate: $4.2\times10^{-16}$; tiled $P=I$: $6.1\times10^{-16}$; tiled Option A equals the head sum with maps $OE_{:,h}$: error 0, and $\det E>0$ (the exponential image lies in $\mathrm{GL}^+$); fusion partial residual $\alpha/(\alpha+\lambda)$: $5.6\times10^{-16}$; SiLU equals the exponential of its log-sigmoid generator: error 0. Chart equivalence (`checks_chart.py`, $K=7$, $d=3$; tiled case $K=6$): the exact conversion $\exp(OT)=I+O'T$ with $O=O'\varphi(\log(I+TO'))^{-1}$ holds to $5.2\times10^{-16}$ and $2.5\times10^{-15}$ on the two random trials that met the determinant condition, with $TO=Z$ to $4.4\times10^{-15}$; $\iota$ is a homomorphism to $2.1\times10^{-16}$; $\exp(OT)=\iota(e^{TO})$ to $5.3\times10^{-16}$; $I+OCT=\iota(I+C\,TO)$ to $3.1\times10^{-16}$; $\det\iota(B)=\det B$ to $2.2\times10^{-16}$; tiled $O'T=e^{X}$ with $O'=e^{X}T^{-1}$ to $1.8\times10^{-15}$; at $X=I$ the exponential and affine charts differ by $36\%$. These ground the algebra at one small size; they say nothing about training behavior.

## Measurement in a trained model

Evaluated on the finished run `vfe3_runs/54.73_wikitext-103_K64_depth-scaled` of VFE_3.0 (projected predictive route, $K=64$, $H=2$, four layers, depth-scaled output init, attention residual on, seed 6, 15000 steps, test perplexity 54.73), from `final_model.pt` and twelve operator snapshots; the stacked operator is $M=\sum_hO_hT_h=OT$ in reference coordinates and $I+M$ is the flat-plus-channel link that the residual realizes. One run, one seed; the archived earlier runs cannot be loaded by the current code. The bare operator sits at the singular boundary from early in training: from $M=2^{-3/2}I$ (stable rank 64) at step 0, the stable rank is 5 to 13 by step 1000 and 11 to 17 at the end, $\sigma_{\min}(M)$ is $10^{-5}$ to $4\times10^{-3}$ (global minimum $9.7\times10^{-6}$), 90% of $\|M\|_F^2$ lies in 21 to 30 of 64 directions, the condition number at the end is $10^3$ to $5\times10^4$, the sign of $\det M$ changes 17 times in 40 consecutive snapshot pairs from step 1000 (not interpretable as clean crossings at $\sigma_{\min}\sim10^{-5}$), the final determinant is negative in layers 1 to 3 for every positive routing weight, and 4 to 20 eigenvalues have negative real part (most negative $-0.15$ to $-0.38$ at the end, $-0.57$ in layer 0 at step 1000). The flat-plus-channel link stays inside the group: at every snapshot and layer all eigenvalues of $I+M$ have positive real part (minimum $0.43$ at layer 0, step 1000, and $0.54$ or more from step 1500), so the principal logarithm exists and the exponential chart of the chart proposition applies; $\sigma_{\min}(I+M)\ge0.27$, the final condition number is 4 to 6, and over the per-head weight box $[0,1]^2$ (41 by 41 grid, final weights) $I+OBT$ never has $\det\le0$, with $\sigma_{\min}\ge0.38$ and condition number at most 6.1. Consequences stated in the note: the Option C remark that the head sum is generically in $\mathrm{GL}(K)$ is nominally true and numerically empty here (stacked $\kappa(O)$ 126 to 2265, $\kappa(T)$ 19 to 172); the group element the model applies is $\Omega+L$ of Option A, not the bare $L$; and the trained message operator has a numerical null complement, the learned counterpart of the unwritten subspace $W'$ for $K=Hd$. Not shown: that the residual causes the well-conditioned $I+M$ (no ablation), that $Hd<K$ would train better, the crossing history between snapshots 1000 to 1500 steps apart, and the realized per-edge routing.

## Open

Whether a projected residual stream with $Hd<K$ trains as well as the current additive residual is untrained and unknown. Whether the self-gate $e^{\beta_{ii}}$ or a general state-dependent self-link should replace part of the block MLP is open. The generator is edge independent; an edge-dependent generator is not constructed, and until it is, Option A in the tiled case adds interpretation and loop observables but no expressive power. The identifiability of the hidden block under Option B depends on the chosen receiver energy. The triangle defect is computable from logged routing costs and normalizers in trained models but has not been measured. The chart measurement (sign of $\det M$, smallest singular value, eigenvalue position, per layer and along training) has been made on one run, above. Open: whether the pattern of a bare message operator at the singular boundary and a flat-plus-channel link well inside the group holds across seeds, scales and head counts, whether it persists without the attention residual, and what the realized per-edge routing and finer snapshots show about crossings.

## Relevance

This is the first account in the vault of why the singular matrix links of [[dennis-2026-glk-matrix-links-2026-09-20]] can sit inside a group-valued lattice gauge structure, which is what gives the link field loop observables on causal support ([[Lattice gauge theory]], [[Holonomy]]). It also fixes, for the transformer, what a residual should be in this framework (complement only, absent when heads tile), connects it to the Gaussian-fusion residual of the gradient route ([[Projected gradient attention]], [[Projected predictive attention]]), and gives a gated-exponential rewriting of SiLU and GELU ([[Activation Functions]]).

> [!note] Editorial: The exact identities are low-rank matrix algebra and are checked numerically; the interpretive claims (loops as a cocycle defect of attention logits, parabolic reduction as Higgs-like, the ranking of the three options) are readings of those identities, not results.
