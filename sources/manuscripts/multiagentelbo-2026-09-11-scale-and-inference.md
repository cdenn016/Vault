## Confirmed results and boundaries

Pushing an information projection generally minimizes coarse KL plus a nonnegative conditional-lift penalty. That penalty vanishes on every attainable output exactly when any two channel rows are identical or disjointly supported. Projection, natural-gradient descent and frame compatibility do not automatically commute. Gaussian-star delay and asynchronous convergence retain the actual freshness, damping, noise and scheduling hypotheses.

For Gaussian block scores, degree-$k$ chaos has operator $\sum_i(B_i^T)^{\odot k}$. Compactness needs the stated correlation bounds and self-adjointness is not automatic. A nonlinear iid relative-density basin with fixed first two moments is attracted under two different normalized blocking rules. Keeping the same original sensor gives a common limiting experiment despite different finite-depth observed laws. Their spectral mismatch obstructs stronger differentiable iterate conjugacy. Finite Hermite truncations are not generally nonlinear closed families.

A literal hard-partition hierarchy realizes scalar contrast decay $3^{-n}$; it does not prove full matrix-cone attraction. A zero-field Ising path sector closes exactly with $J_{\rm eff}=\operatorname{atanh}\prod_e\tanh J_e$ and its scalar mass correction, including frustrated cycles. Eliminations outside the classified sector can create higher interactions.

A positive reversible stochastic matrix can have a stochastic square root but no continuous homogeneous Markov embedding because its logarithm has a negative off-diagonal rate. Finite VFE/Fisher contraction does not remove that obstruction. A continuous same-carrier semigroup cannot reach nontrivial deterministic decimation under the declared continuity hypotheses. Gaussian and erasure examples supply separate positive embedding classes.

The Bayesian Gaussian bridge separates model Fisher, posterior Fisher, observation pooling, latent posterior channels and iid averaging. A channel maps the whole posterior family across exposure while retaining the original record, but changes its induced prior at intermediate exposure. Requiring the original prior and evidence with all those posteriors is obstructed by total covariance.

Fixed-evidence nonendpoint selection uses an introduced information-balance utility. The example does not derive a unique utility, a unique intermediate organization or physical scale from the ELBO.
