# Conditioned parent after selected coherence

This result continues [[multiagentelbo-2026-09-26-selected-coherence]] in the unchanged scalar shared-link four-cycle at one fixed base point. The following are archived in sources/archives/multiagentelbo-2026-09-26-conditioned-parent/: its two manuscript proofs (Theory/ca_parent_conditional.tex and Theory/ca_parent_dynamics.tex, Proposition C.18, Remark C.19, and Proposition C.20 of the main manuscript build), the constructive derivation notes, the exact projector check, the same-model and cross-model proof checks, and the Exa request/result/assessment. The repository keeps the asset manifest at docs/derivations/2026-09-26-selection-to-formation/stage3-vault-manifest.json.

The parent is activated from the actual recognition law conditioned on the completed duration-one selected-coherence prefix on [t_L,t_L+1] at Euclidean residual resolution T^(1/4). That law is pushed through the existing fixed channel of the endogenous-cycle proposition, which retains all sixteen assignments, the loop, the common fast normal, and nine continuous coordinates on either matching stratum. No Gaussian reset occurs, and no microscopic row trajectory is imposed. The parent inherits the source rows at activation and then learns from its own law.

The conditional bound comes from computing the relative entropy of (1_E, state) against Bernoulli times the frozen-row optimizer in two orders. If eta bounds the unconditioned KL and the prefix failure probability is at most rho<1/2, the discarded optimizing conditional KL is at most (eta+h_b(rho))/(1-rho). An exact Gaussian evaluator tilt, with normalizer (4+T)/(4+5T), shows that the physical conditional loss with evaluator samples retained adds at most T. For the dynamics, a normalized inherited proposal kernel keeps self-proposals and projects into the destination stratum. Own-law row evolution exists globally and is unique. A stopped coupling against the stationary held-matching reference, whose departure hazard is bounded by stationary flux, gives complete retained configuration/assignment path TV at most 2rho+(h+3)delta+4hd/(1-4d), with d=delta+epsilon_L h<1/4. On the joint schedule with delta,eta<=T, epsilon_L<=T^2, and h=T^(-1/4), the conditional KL is O(T^(1/4)log(1/T)) and the path TV is O(T^(1/4)). These are asymptotic bounds: with delta<=T, rho<1/2 first holds near T=1.6e-8.

The scope is restored equilibrium coherence under SPEC R15. A hidden-sign example (conditioning an exact matching Gaussian on {z_1>0}) has discarded KL exactly log 2 at every temperature, so arbitrary positive-probability conditioning does not suffice. Parent closure at the original first contrast, closure for arbitrary realized histories, an explicit power-law selection theorem, the original faster-learning first-block comparison, loop-mean contrast, and recursive formation all remain open.
