# Communication, Coherence and Collective Agents: the approximate meta-agent results

## Source and scope

The live source is the supplement section "Approximate meta-agents and controlled renormalization" of the manuscript *Communication, Coherence and Collective Agents*: statements in [ext1_apx.tex](../../manuscripts/Collective_Agents/sections/ext1_apx.tex) and proofs in [appext1_apx.tex](../../manuscripts/Collective_Agents/sections/appext1_apx.tex). This note records, at the author's request of November 11, 2026, the theory of three results added on November 10 and 11, 2026: the bound on the excess relative entropy of a Markov filter without a smallness condition, the approximate form of the meta-agent theorem, and the bound on the memory of the average of level-0 members. It records statements, hypotheses and limits only. The numerical checks that accompany these results in the manuscript, and the rest of the manuscript, are not recorded here. The manuscript is a working draft and its statements may change.

## Setting

The exact meta-agent theorem of the manuscript says that a set $C$ of agents of a linear-Gaussian joint process is itself an agent, with coarse internal, sensory, active and external states, when four groups of hypotheses hold: the coarse sensory state is the information-form statistic of the members' sensors and the exterior reads the members only through a coarse act; the coarse exterior $\eta_C$ obeys a closed law given the coarse act, its abstract physics; the members run a consensus filter on information contributions; and the members deliberate under a shared protocol. The approximate results ask what survives when closure of the coarse exterior, or of the coarse internal state, fails by a declared amount.

The common setting is a fine process $x_{t+1}=Ax_t+Bu_t+\mathfrak n_t$ with a retained variable $y_t=Cx_t$ and a declared coarse model $y_{t+1}=\bar Ay_t+\bar Bu_t+\hat w_t$, $\hat w_t\sim\mathcal N(0,\bar Q)$. The closure defect and the input defect of the declared model are $D=CA-\bar AC$ and $E=CB-\bar B$, and $\delta_t=Dx_t+Eu_t$ is the defect along the path. The meta-agent reads a record $s_{t+1}=Hy_t+v_t$ with positive definite noise covariance and runs the stationary [[Kalman filter]] of the declared model, with predictive covariance $P^-_{\mathrm b}$ and belief $\mathcal N(\hat\eta^-_t,P^-_{\mathrm b})$. Its excess relative entropy is the expected relative entropy from the exact predictive law of $y_t$ given the record, $\mathcal N(C\hat x^-_t,P_{\mathrm e})$, to that belief.

## Excess relative entropy without a smallness condition

An earlier bound of the same section compared the two filter equations by scaling and needed a smallness condition that failed on generic instances. The new theorem replaces it. The difference $\mathsf X=P_{\mathrm e}-P^-_{\mathrm b}$ of the exact and the declared predictive covariances obeys an exact quadratic equation, $\mathsf X=\Psi'(\mathsf X-\mathsf XJ_{\mathrm e}\mathsf X)\Psi'^\top+\mathsf N$, where $\Psi'$ is the closed-loop matrix of the declared filter and $\mathsf N$ collects the mismatch of the noise covariances and the part of the defect that the record does not resolve. The declared filter contracts in the metric of its own predictive covariance, with a factor $\theta_{\mathrm b}<1$ that is bounded away from one by the declared noise alone. From these two facts the upper limit of the excess relative entropy is bounded by a covariance part, a function of the eigenvalues of $\mathsf X$ in that metric, plus a mean part, the square of the forgetting time $\tau_{\mathrm b}=(1-\theta_{\mathrm b}^{1/2})^{-1}$ of the declared filter times the energy of the part of the defect that the record resolves. The bound holds for every declared model whose filter is detectable, with no condition on the size of the defect, and it is of second order in the defect and the noise mismatch. A corollary states it in terms of the closure defect $\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{cl}}=I(y_{t+1};x_t\mid y_t)$ for the moment-matched declared drift without input; its constants are far from sharp.

A proposition removes two hypotheses of the theorem in the upper limit. The fine process need only be detectable through the record, not stable, and the start may be any law in which the fine state is Gaussian given the randomization of the policy, with a covariance that does not depend on it. The filter equation then has a unique fixed point to which the covariance converges from every start, so the closed-form bound holds for the upper limit of the excess, and a bound that depends on the start holds at every finite round. The closed form does not bound the excess at each round.

## Approximate meta-agent theorem

The theorem keeps the blanket hypotheses and the consensus protocol exact and replaces the exact abstract physics by a declared model with defects, for a stable linear-Gaussian joint process in which every coordinate of $\eta_C$ is sensed and the exterior starts from a Gaussian law given the initial collective variables. Its conclusions follow the five properties of the exact theorem. The coarse blanket remains exact from every start that satisfies the initial conditional independence, and the coarse internal state remains exactly closed, because both rest on the protocol and not on closure of the exterior. The path law of the coarse exterior under the collective's own acts stays within a stated relative entropy per round of the declared law, asymptotically, when the coarse start is nondegenerate. The belief encoded by the members' average is asymptotically within the bound of the preceding theorem of the exact predictive law, with the members' computation noise included. The law of the joint act is within a stated total-variation distance of a reference group posterior that uses the exact predictive mean and the true drift.

Two conditions that might be expected are absent. The exterior alone need not be stable: under the blanket hypothesis and stability of the joint process, the covariance recursion of the exterior filter is bounded by the covariance of the exterior in the joint process, so it has a fixed point, and an exterior that is unstable in open loop and stabilized by the collective's acts is covered. No smallness condition is placed on the defects. The error term for the path law and the one for the belief are of second order in the defects; the one for the joint act is of first order.

## Memory of the average of level-0 members

Members that fuse messages literally with fixed gains, the level-0 receivers of the manuscript, do not close the coarse internal state: their average obeys an exact recursion with two memory terms. The theorem bounds that memory for scalar members with exact code models. With $W_{CC}$ the matrix of the update of the members' means, the average obeys a closed recursion with coefficient the mean row sum $\bar\lambda$, plus a defect $\delta_t$ that is the inner product of the centered column sums of $W_{CC}$ with the members' deviations from their average. The deviations obey their own recursion, driven by the centered row sums times the error of the average and by the differences between the members' inputs and noise; the innovation of the world, common to the members, drops out. With $\sigma_{\mathrm{row}}$ and $\sigma_{\mathrm{col}}$ the root-mean-square deviations of the row and column sums from $\bar\lambda$ and $\mathcal S_\perp$ the sum of the norms of the powers of $W_{CC}$ restricted to the deviations, the theorem gives the following. The memory kernels of the average on its own past are bounded by $\sigma_{\mathrm{col}}\sigma_{\mathrm{row}}$ times the norm of a power of the restricted update, the discrete-time counterpart of the kernel bound for non-reversible drift in continuous time. The upper limit of the root-mean-square defect is at most $\sigma_{\mathrm{col}}\mathcal S_\perp(\sigma_{\mathrm{row}}\mathsf e_{\mathrm{avg}}+\mathsf v_{\mathrm{het}})$, where $\mathsf e_{\mathrm{avg}}$ and $\mathsf v_{\mathrm{het}}$ are second moments of the process, the error of the average and the heterogeneity of the members' inputs and noise, taken as data. The average stays within that bound divided by $1-\lvert\bar\lambda\rvert$ of the closed coarse recursion fed the realized coarse sensory state. Under a power bound on the restricted update and a gap condition, the update has a real, simple dominant eigenvalue near $\bar\lambda$ whose eigenvectors are nearly uniform, with no reversibility assumed.

The closure defect of the average, the mutual information between its next value and the members' states given its present value and the coarse sensory state, is bounded from both sides. The lower bound depends on the column dispersion and the covariance of the internal message noise and contains no mixing constant; for isotropic internal noise it is $\frac12\ln(1+\sigma_{\mathrm{col}}^2)$. The information-form closure defect of a level-0 average therefore registers the mixing of the members only down to a floor, and no bound on it through the column dispersion and a ratio of timescales that tends to zero with the ratio can hold uniformly over level-0 collectives. The memory of such an average is better stated through the energy of the defect or through kernel weights.

## What is not established

The manuscript states the following limits. The approximate meta-agent theorem assumes exact blanket conditions; a violation leaks at a bounded rate under separate hypotheses, and no combined theorem is given. The closed-form bound on the belief concerns the upper limit and not each round. A singular information matrix of the sensors, and starts in which the exterior is not Gaussian given the initial collective variables, are outside it. For level-0 members there is no counterpart of the belief property, no bound on the Markov error of the average for a non-reversible update, no statement for members with vector-valued states, and no bound that is closed in the parameters of the network; the lower bound on the closure defect is proved for channel noise specific to the receiver. Everything is linear and Gaussian. The open problem of the manuscript on an approximate form of the meta-agent theorem is narrowed and not closed.

## Relevance to this research

These results give the meta-agent of the program an error analysis. The construction of [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]] treats a parent as a normalized summary of its constituents; the theorems here say, in one tractable class, how far the belief of such a summary lies from the exact predictive law when the coarse variables it models are not closed, and which hypotheses carry the conclusion. They connect [[Coarse Graining]] and the projection formalism of [[zwanzig-1961-memory-effects]] and [[mori-1965-transport-collective-motion]] to filtering ([[kalman-1960-approach-linear-filtering]], [[olfati-saber-2005-distributed-kalman-filter]]) and to the closure notions of [[barnett-2023-dynamical-independence-discovering]] and [[rosas-2024-software-natural-world]].
