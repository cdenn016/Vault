## Result and explicit separation assumption

For every prescribed finite depth L>=2, the normalized triangle-chain construction has L actual coalescence episodes with independently dynamic links, all loops retained and complete actual-law parent activation. Source assignments now move: the microscopic process redraws the full assignment at a Poisson proposal rate of one, with self proposals allowed. Parent rates are the exact optimizer-conditioned microscopic rates. They are generally different from a newly generated heat bath on the coarse source marginal. The continuous diffusion and generative-parameter learner still descend the same full normalized restricted VFE under their declared kinetics.

The positive result uses a new explicit accelerated-energy family, Q_L=8, Q_s=2Q_(s+1)+20 and J_s=epsilon^-Q_s, with A^2=epsilon^(3Q_1+20), T=A^2 epsilon^6 and horizon 2/epsilon. Thus the weakest continuous energy grows as epsilon^-8. Continuous mobility and source proposal rate remain one; neither is rescaled per level. The original J_L=1 case is unresolved. The fixed standardized nonlinear coefficient delta=10^-4/n does not vanish.

Supplied contrasts, internal-neighbor source support, a proper correlated prior, a compatible root observation, calibration-assisted learning and a shrinking thermal/preparation regime remain assumptions. The nonempty initial-law neighborhood uses a common strict-margin normal center with small label-dependent smooth transport perturbations, allowing actual label/configuration correlation. Exact laws thereafter need not be Gaussian. At depth two, two triangles and one bridge give 6 -> 2 -> 1 agents and 19 -> 9 -> 8 continuous coordinates, preserving two loops and all 64 source assignments. Here A=epsilon^64 and T=epsilon^134.

## What the new proof supplies

The coupled own-law continuation is established before using mixing. A force bound uniform over arbitrary adapted labels gives actual moment control; source jumps leave the continuous configuration unchanged. Each complete covector includes hidden determinants, prior normalization, categorical priors and calibration. The resulting parameter stays in [1,2.1], has speed O_L(epsilon), and reaches at least 7/4 over the horizon's final ten units.

Actual binary recognition-source mass changes by total variation greater than one half. This strengthens the earlier fixed-assignment theorem's model-posterior-row change. The physical and optimizing first-hidden conditionals each change by KL greater than 1/200, uniformly at the same retained input and label. Physical posterior, restricted-VFE optimizer, actual recognition and model-posterior source rows remain distinct objects.

Jumps can create discarded-conditional KL even while full VFE decreases. The new entropy calculation bounds that contribution by log|J|+2B0 and proves a floor for the joint differential/counting entropy when assignment masses change. It requires no lower bound on actual label masses. These estimates yield the full actual activation entropy budget, discarded-conditional KL O_L(epsilon^8), and complete same-state covector discrepancy O_L(T epsilon^4) after every active entrance interval. Loops are integrated only in an auxiliary bound and remain in each actual parent.

The source-history theorem compares whole histories on one common proposal grid. Exact conditional-expectation composition gives a common averaged assignment row across the fine process and all parents. Uniform fast-sheet contraction, a moving-target entropy estimate and a sequential-kernel argument control the probability of any assignment-path disagreement by O_L(epsilon^7 log(1/epsilon)). ## Evidence, restrictions and the next question

The result rests on written derivations and a separate reconstruction of the frozen target, seven component claims and their joins. The checker shares earlier source continuity and is not described as a blind review. Structural validation checks artifact consistency and bindings only. No trajectory simulation, code implementation, production training, full test suite, GPU run, heavy replay or Lean proof supplies the theorem. Prohibited tamper checks were not run.

Formation windows shrink like log(1/epsilon)/J_s, so most source switching and most model learning occur after formation. The theorem does not show that source learning creates the supplied interaction hierarchy. A growing weakest energy is a substantive restriction, not merely a choice of time units while leaving source kinetics unchanged. Arbitrary mixed-level source choices, arbitrary nonlinear strengths, infinite depth, unique grouping, physical time and resource savings remain outside the result. Base transport is still deferred.

The next narrower mathematical question is whether only the source-relevant fast contrasts and loops can be averaged while the weakest retained normal energy remains one. That requires conditional-history estimates in a moving retained environment. The current global-mixing estimate cannot establish it, but its failure is not a nonexistence proof.
