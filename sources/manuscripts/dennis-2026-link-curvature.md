## Question

The companion note showed that the matrix links of the projected routes are group-valued links in an affine chart and that a constant exponential generator $X$ adds nothing to the current model ([[dennis-2026-link-origin]]). This note asks what an edge-dependent generator can add and in what geometry the link field should be optimized.

## Triangle holonomy, flatness and recurrence

For causal support ($j\le i$) and invertible links $\Theta_{ij}\mapsto g_i\Theta_{ij}g_j^{-1}$, with $\Theta_{ij}=U_iG_{ij}U_j^{-1}$ in reference coordinates, the triangle holonomy for $k<j<i$ is
$$F_{ijk}=\Theta_{ik}^{-1}\Theta_{ij}\Theta_{jk}=U_k\,G_{ik}^{-1}G_{ij}G_{jk}\,U_k^{-1}.$$
It is the holonomy of the loop $k\to j\to i\to k$ based at $k$, whose last step traverses the long edge backward through its inverse. Under a frame change $F_{ijk}\mapsto g_kF_{ijk}g_k^{-1}$, so its conjugacy class (trace, determinant, spectrum) is gauge invariant, and since $F_{ijk}$ is conjugate to $G_{ik}^{-1}G_{ij}G_{jk}$ the frames drop out of the conjugacy class: curvature is a property of the reference-coordinate field $G$, and no choice of frames, positional or not, flattens or creates it. The note presents this as the answer to the referee objection that causal support leaves no invariant loop content (the report is a manuscript-folder file, not a vault note).

Two propositions with short proofs. A field of arbitrary matrices with $\Theta_{ij}\Theta_{jk}=\Theta_{ik}$ for all $k<j<i$ is flat exactly when $\Theta_{ij}=\Theta_{i,i-1}\Theta_{i-1,i-2}\cdots\Theta_{j+1,j}$, that is, when it is recurrent, and any choice of adjacent matrices defines a flat field. If every link is invertible, flat is equivalent to pure gauge, $\Theta_{ij}=g_ig_j^{-1}$ with $g_i=\Theta_{i0}$ up to a common right factor. The equivalence fails for singular links: the shared projector $\Theta_{ij}=P$, $P^2=P$, is flat and cannot be written $g_ig_j^{-1}$ (checked, rank 1). Consequences stated in the note: flat transport of arbitrary frames, including positional frames, is pure gauge; the rotary link $e^{(p_i-p_j)X}$ is absorbed into the frames and is flat; a constant $G$ is flat only if $G=I$; all content of an edge-dependent link beyond a coboundary is curvature. The note adds a remark that the transition products of linear state-space recurrences $h_i=A_ih_{i-1}+\dots$, including input-dependent transitions, have exactly the recurrent form ([[State space models]], [[Recurrent networks]]), so recurrent models are the flat link fields; the remark is explicitly a statement about cocycle structure and not about performance.

> [!note] Editorial: The state-space-model correspondence is stated in the source as a structural observation. The vault has no source note for the two cited models (S4, Mamba), so that part is cited to the manuscript only.

## Holonomy of the candidate generators

For $\Theta_{ij}=U_ie^{X_{ij}}U_j^{-1}$ with $A=X_{ij}$, $B=X_{jk}$, $Z=X_{ik}$, the holonomy is $U_ke^{-Z}e^{A}e^{B}U_k^{-1}$ and, by the [[Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula]] applied twice ([[hall-2015-lie-groups]]),
$$\log\big(e^{-Z}e^{A}e^{B}\big)=(A+B-Z)+\tfrac12\big([A,B]-[Z,A]-[Z,B]\big)+O(3).$$
The first-order term is the discrete exterior derivative of the algebra-valued 1-cochain $X$, zero exactly when $X_{ij}=\phi_i-\phi_j$. The note's table of holonomies (routing matrices $B_{ij}=\bigoplus_h\beta^{(h)}_{ij}I_d$ acting headwise, $TO=I$):

| Link field | Holonomy $F_{ijk}$ | Flat when |
|---|---|---|
| Positional $U_ie^{(p_i-p_j)X}U_j^{-1}$ | $I$ | always |
| Constant $U_ie^{X}U_j^{-1}$ | $U_ke^{X}U_k^{-1}$ | only if $e^X=I$ |
| Routed exponent $X_{ij}=OB_{ij}T$ | $U_kO\,e^{B_{ij}+B_{jk}-B_{ik}}T\,U_k^{-1}$ | $B$ additive |
| Head sum $U_iOB_{ij}TU_j^{-1}$ | $U_kO\,\frac{B_{ij}B_{jk}}{B_{ik}}\,T\,U_k^{-1}$ | $B$ multiplicative |

The constant exponential link is thus the most curved edge-independent choice (the holonomy is the link itself, with first-order part $X$), which is why $L$ carries curvature and is not gauge. The routed exponent has the additive defect of the routing weights as curvature; the head sum has their multiplicative defect, whose logarithm for routing $\beta=\pi e^{-D/\kappa}/Z$ is
$$-\frac{D_{ij}+D_{jk}-D_{ik}}{\kappa}+\log\frac{\pi_{ij}\pi_{jk}}{\pi_{ik}}-\log Z_j,$$
the KL triangle defect of [[dennis-2026-link-origin]], now one row of the table. Proposition (flat normalized routing): for $\beta_{ij}>0$ with rows summing to one, the head-sum link with $TO=I$ is flat on strict triangles exactly when $\beta_{ij}=c_i/c_j$ for $0<j<i$ and $\beta_{i0}=c_i$ (with $c_0=\beta_{00}=1$). This is a separable pattern in which the weight on source $j$ is the reciprocal of $j$'s own first-source weight; uniform causal attention violates the cocycle on five tokens (largest violation $1/6$), and generic content-dependent routing has a nonzero defect. The statement concerns the head-sum link, not attention in general.

## Regression links

An inference-derived edge-dependent link: for a centered joint Gaussian $(\xi_0,\dots,\xi_{N-1})$ in reference coordinates with $\Sigma_{ab}=\mathrm{Cov}(\xi_a,\xi_b)$ and invertible marginals, $W_{ab}=\Sigma_{ab}\Sigma_{bb}^{-1}$ is the best linear predictor of $\xi_a$ from $\xi_b$ and transforms as $W_{ab}\mapsto g_aW_{ab}g_b^{-1}$, so it has the correct link covariance law. Curvature is the partial cross-covariance: with $\Sigma_{ik|j}=\Sigma_{ik}-\Sigma_{ij}\Sigma_{jj}^{-1}\Sigma_{jk}$,
$$W_{ij}W_{jk}=W_{ik}-\Sigma_{ik|j}\Sigma_{kk}^{-1},\qquad F_{ijk}=I-W_{ik}^{-1}\Sigma_{ik|j}\Sigma_{kk}^{-1}$$
(the second for invertible $W_{ik}$). Hence $F_{ijk}=I$ exactly when $\Sigma_{ik|j}=0$, which for a Gaussian is the conditional independence $\xi_i\perp\xi_k\mid\xi_j$ ([[lauritzen-1996-graphical-models]]). For a Gaussian vector Markov chain $\xi_i=A\xi_{i-1}+\varepsilon_i$ with independent noise the regression links are flat and $W_{ij}=A^{\,i-j}$. The converse of the Markov statement is not claimed, since vanishing partial covariance for every single-intermediate triple is weaker than the full Markov property. The reading offered: curvature is the direct, unmediated dependence of token $i$ on token $k$ beyond what passes through $j$, which is what attention exists to provide.

Reduced-rank regression. With canonical correlations $a_1\ge a_2\ge\cdots$ from the SVD $\Sigma_{ii}^{-1/2}\Sigma_{ij}\Sigma_{jj}^{-1/2}=\sum_sa_su_sv_s^\top$, the rank-$d$ minimizer of $\mathbb E\|\xi_i-W\xi_j\|^2_{\Sigma_{ii}^{-1}}$ is $W_d=\Sigma_{ii}^{1/2}\big(\sum_{s\le d}a_su_sv_s^\top\big)\Sigma_{jj}^{-1/2}$ with excess risk $\sum_{s>d}a_s^2$ over the full regression (Eckart-Young in the whitened Frobenius norm; Izenman 1975, Reinsel and Velu 1998, neither a vault note; related: [[Canonical Correlation Analysis]]). A factorization $W_d=OT$ is then the optimal channel of width $d$ with $\operatorname{im}O$ the leading canonical directions. With $K=Hd$ the heads would partition the canonical directions into $H$ blocks; the note states this and the identification of the learned $(O,T)$ with the reduced-rank solution as a hypothesis to test, not a result, since the heads are not trained to this criterion.

Requirement: the cross-covariances vanish in the mean-field family $q=\prod_iq_i$ of the model ([[Mean-Field Approximation]]), where the regression link is zero. A correlated variational family (for example a Gaussian with sparse block precision) or a generative model whose prior couples tokens would supply them, and the learned links $OT$ can be read as an amortized replacement.

## Fisher metric of the link and the singular boundary

A group element acts on a Gaussian by $N(\mu,\Sigma)\mapsto N(g\mu,g\Sigma g^\top)$. For a tangent vector $\delta g=Ag$, with output $(\mu',\Sigma')$ and Cholesky factor $L'$, the pulled-back Fisher-Rao metric is
$$ds^2=\|Z\eta\|^2+2\,\big\|\tfrac12(Z+Z^\top)\big\|_F^2,\qquad Z=L'^{-1}AL',\ \eta=L'^{-1}\mu',$$
which depends on the output belief only and is the metric already derived for the frame tables in [[dennis-2026-phi-natgrad]] ([[Fisher information metric]]). In the exponential chart $g=e^X$, $A=\frac{e^{\operatorname{ad}_X}-1}{\operatorname{ad}_X}\delta X$ ([[hall-2015-lie-groups]]). For the model, the edge metric averages this over source beliefs with the routing weights.

Infinite distance. For $g_t=\mathrm{diag}(e^{-t},1,\dots,1)$ acting on $N(\mu,I)$ ($\sigma_{\min}=e^{-t}$), $\mathrm{KL}(q_t\|q_0)=t+\tfrac12\big(e^{-2t}-1+(1-e^{-t})^2\mu_1^2\big)\sim t$, the path has Fisher length $\sqrt{\mu_1^2+2}\,t$, and the Fisher-Rao distance from $q_0$ to $q_t$ is at least $\sqrt2\,t$ (the univariate metric and monotonicity under marginalization; [[amari-2000-methods-information-geometry]]). The singular boundary of $\mathrm{GL}(K)$ is therefore at infinite Fisher distance from every interior point and the divergence grows linearly in $-\log\sigma_{\min}$, whereas in the affine chart with Euclidean metric the same boundary is at finite distance $\sigma_{\min}$. Corollary: an additive update $M\leftarrow M-\eta\Delta$ can cross $\det=0$ in one step whenever $\eta\|\Delta\|_2\ge\sigma_{\min}(M)$ with $\Delta$ suitably aligned with the smallest singular direction; a multiplicative update $g\leftarrow e^{-\eta A}g$ never does, since $\det(e^{-\eta A}g)=e^{-\eta\operatorname{tr}A}\det g\neq0$; a natural-gradient step needs at least $\sqrt2\,|\log\sigma_{\min}|$ divided by its Fisher length to approach the boundary. The loss does not diverge at the boundary (covariance and noise floors), so the barrier belongs to the geometry used to move and not to the objective. The vfe3 frame tables already use group updates (`m_phi_update_mode` with `pullback_group` and `fisher_group`); the same construction, a multiplicative retraction with a Fisher preconditioner, could be applied to the content maps.

Comparison with the trained run. For the run measured in [[dennis-2026-link-origin]] (`54.73_wikitext-103_K64_depth-scaled`), the content learning rate is $2.5\times10^{-3}$ per element under AdamW and the stacked operator has $\sigma_{\min}$ between $10^{-5}$ and $4\times10^{-3}$, below or comparable to the scale of one step times $\|T\|\sqrt K$, about $3\times10^{-2}$ for $\|T\|\approx1.5$ and $K=64$. The note flags this as an order-of-magnitude comparison and not a measurement of crossings; the affine-chart updates are consistent with the observed repeated sign changes of $\det M$, while group updates would not allow them. It also states that none of this bears on the link that run actually applied: the residual-augmented operator $I+M$ stayed far from the boundary. The barrier matters for designs that apply a bare group element, such as a residual-free route with tiled heads or a group-valued link without a flat term.

## Numerical checks

Executed in float64 at $K=4$ with random invertible matrices (`checks.py`, `checks_output.txt`). Recurrent links satisfy $F_{ijk}=I$ to $1.8\times10^{-15}$; the pure-gauge form $g_ig_j^{-1}$ to $1.1\times10^{-15}$; the shared rank-1 projector satisfies the cocycle exactly (error 0, rank 1). Constant-link holonomy equals $U_ke^XU_k^{-1}$ to $2.0\times10^{-15}$; the positional link has $\max|F-I|=3.3\times10^{-13}$; the routed exponent matches its additive formula to $2.2\times10^{-15}$ and the head sum its multiplicative formula to $2.1\times10^{-15}$. The BCH expansion has first-order error $3.57\times10^{-2}$, $9.11\times10^{-3}$, $2.30\times10^{-3}$ and second-order error $3.62\times10^{-3}$, $4.55\times10^{-4}$, $5.68\times10^{-5}$ at generator scales $0.1$, $0.05$, $0.025$ (scaling as $\varepsilon^2$ and $\varepsilon^3$). The flat routing pattern $c=(1,0.5,0.2,0.05,0.01)$ has positive weights, row sums one and cocycle defect 0; uniform causal attention has defect $1/6$. Regression links of a generic Gaussian: the product identity holds to $1.4\times10^{-16}$ and the holonomy identity to $5.3\times10^{-16}$; a generic joint law has $\max|F-I|=3.37$; an AR(1) chain has $1.2\times10^{-15}$ with $W_{ij}=A^{\,i-j}$ to $1.1\times10^{-16}$; the covariance law under distinct per-token frames holds to $1.3\times10^{-15}$. Reduced-rank regression: the rank-2 predictor has risk $3.1988$ against $3.1019$ for the full regression, the excess $0.096854$ equals the sum of squared discarded singular values $0.096854$, the minimum over 4000 factor perturbations of risk minus risk of $W_d$ is $1.5\times10^{-4}\ge0$, and no random rank-2 matrix does better. Fisher metric: $2\,\mathrm{KL}/(t^2ds^2)=1.0054$, $1.0027$, $1.0013$ at $t=10^{-2},5\times10^{-3},2.5\times10^{-3}$ (converging to one linearly in $t$); the derivative of the exponential map checks to $8.9\times10^{-9}$ by finite differences; along the shrinking path the divergence is $2.133$, $4.499$, $6.809$, $9.112$ at $\sigma_{\min}=10^{-1},\dots,10^{-4}$ against $-\log\sigma_{\min}=2.303$, $4.605$, $6.908$, $9.210$, with increments per decade $2.37$, $2.31$, $2.30$ approaching $\ln10$, and Fisher path lengths $3.855$, $7.710$, $11.566$, $15.421$. These ground the algebra at one small size and say nothing about trained models.

## Open

The note names no test as done. Open items it states or implies: whether the routing weights logged in a trained model have a holonomy closer to the flat pattern $\beta_{ij}=c_i/c_j$ than to uniform attention (the defect is computable from logged routing weights through the log-defect formula and has not been measured); whether the learned $(O,T)$ match the reduced-rank regression directions of a correlated Gaussian law (a hypothesis); a correlated variational family to supply the cross-covariances the regression link needs (zero in the mean-field family); implementing multiplicative or Fisher-preconditioned updates for the content maps; and whether any of these constructions relates to perplexity, which the note says is unknown. Also left open: the converse of the Markov statement, and whether the heads partition the canonical directions.

## Relevance

This turns the loop language of [[Holonomy]] and [[Lattice gauge theory]] into a computable invariant for the causal link field of the transformer (the triangle holonomy, with the KL triangle defect as the head-sum row of a table of candidates), shows that the only content a link has beyond pure gauge is curvature, and ties flat links to recurrent (state-space) transition products. It gives the low-rank channel a second-order-statistics reading through reduced-rank regression ([[Canonical Correlation Analysis]], [[GL(K) gauge-equivariant attention]]), and it adds to the Fisher-geometry thread ([[Natural gradient]], [[Information geometry and natural gradient]]) the statement that the singular boundary is at infinite Fisher distance, with the contrast between additive affine and multiplicative group updates. The program-level directions are listed in [[VFE Transformer Program]].

> [!note] Editorial: The algebraic identities and the infinite-distance statement are exact and checked; the readings (flat links as recurrent models, curvature as direct dependence, heads as canonical-direction blocks, additive updates explaining the sign changes of $\det M$) are interpretations or hypotheses, and none has been tested in training.
