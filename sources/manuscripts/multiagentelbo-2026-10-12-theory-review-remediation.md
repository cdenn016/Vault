## Review verdict

Eleven parallel lenses (variational, gauge, information geometry, attention, coarse-graining, formation, selection and parents, Gaussian supplement, base supplement, framing and citations, implementability against the MultiAgentELBO code) found 6 high, 22 medium, and 39 low findings. No load-bearing theorem was found false. Several hundred identities, witnesses, and constants were recomputed in float64, SymPy, exact rationals, exhaustive enumeration, and small simulations, typically to 1e-13 or better. One sub-item, `thm:rg-memory-inference-descent`(iv), failed under its literal reading for want of the hypothesis that the recognition record conditional equals the record conditional. The high findings were about framing and typing: the program aim of Chapter 1 asserted three theses (participatory theory, formation through coupled inference, parents acting back) that no model family realizes together; the formation theorem's mechanism is the mixture-proportion M-step; prior art was missing for formation (bounded-confidence dynamics, self-attention clustering), attention as a latent label, Markov-blanket coarse-graining (Friston et al. 2021), and exact RG (Kadanoff); and the edge-copy identity under mutual attention was undeclared. Two lenses independently found that the adaptive-tower free energy equals the depth-0 free energy, so parents defined as pushforwards add no inference. Recommendation: major revision for framing, prior art, and the multiscale reading; minor for the mathematics.

## Author conventions decided October 12

Links in the two directions of a mutually attending pair are independent: the two rows read distinct edge copies, each with its own reversal, so $\Theta_{ij}\neq\Theta_{ji}^{-1}$ in general and every mutual pair is a closed walk $i\to j\to i$ with holonomy $\Theta_{ij}\Theta_{ji}$. At the agent level a self edge carries the identity link, so $D_{ii}=0$ follows; coarse loop copies carry internal holonomy. The declared loop family of the generator prior contains every 2-cycle of a mutual pair, once based at each endpoint, so a large loop coefficient pulls the pair toward reciprocal links, the one-reversal-pair convention being its hard-constraint limit. The MultiAgentELBO latent-link code uses the reciprocal convention $\Theta_{ji}=\Theta_{ij}^{-1}$, so its pair bookkeeping implements a different model; VFE 3.0's frame-aligned transports are the trivial-holonomy special case.
