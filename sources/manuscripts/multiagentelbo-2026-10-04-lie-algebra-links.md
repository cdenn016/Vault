## What changed

The theory previously treated the links between agents as held parameters, with an edge twist and a curvature penalty that was a modeling choice. The revision makes the link generators latent fields of the collective evidence bound. Each edge carries a twist $\mathring\Theta^x_e=\exp(\mathring\omega^x_e)$ in the exponential chart, so the link is $\Theta=U_i\exp(\mathring\omega)U_j^{-1}$ with $\mathring\omega_{\bar e}=-\mathring\omega_e$. The generators are invariant under the passive frame action, and a change of reference acts on them by the adjoint representation (`lem:link-exp-naturality`, `prop:link-generator-gauge`). The image of the chart is fixed by Culver's real-logarithm criterion, tagged STANDARD (`prop:link-real-logarithm`, Culver 1966 Theorem 1): a real matrix is an exponential of a real matrix exactly when it is invertible and every Jordan block of each negative eigenvalue occurs an even number of times.

The motivation was structural: the exponential chart is the one that cannot leave $\mathrm{GL}^+(K)$.

Here $K$ is the dimension of the belief sample fiber $\mathbb R^K$, so a link is a $K\times K$ matrix in $\mathrm{GL}^+(K)$ and a generator lies in $\mathfrak{gl}(K,\mathbb R)$.

## Results of the new chapter

The curvature section proves that continuous class functions on $\mathrm{GL}(K)$, $K\ge2$, take at a unipotent holonomy the same value as at the identity (`prop:link-class-function-blindness`), so no invariant loop defect on the general linear group separates unipotent holonomy (`cor:link-no-invariant-defect`). The chapter therefore measures curvature by the loop defect $\mathfrak Q_p=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(G^{-1}(I-\rho(\mathring\Theta_p))^\top G(I-\rho(\mathring\Theta_p))\bigr)$ (`def:link-loop-defect`), which is invariant under reference changes by nonzero multiples of $G$-isometries (`prop:link-loop-defect`).

The generator prior is a Gaussian base law on $\mathfrak g$ with anchor $\mathring\alpha$ and covariance $\mathring\Sigma$, tilted by $e^{-\mathcal S}$ for the loop action $\mathcal S$; for any Lie group with an inner product on $\mathfrak g$ its normalizer obeys $e^{-\mathbb E\mathcal S}\le Z\le1$ (`prop:link-generator-prior`). With latent generators the evidence bound is $F=\mathrm{KL}(R\|\text{prior})+\mathbb E_R(\text{held-link }F)$ (`prop:link-latent-normalization`). The field equation follows from the Fréchet derivative $\mathbf L(A,E)$ of the exponential and the Bonnet and Price identities, and its current has delta-rule form: prediction error times source state (`prop:link-field-equation`, `cor:link-delta-rule`).

Without the generator prior the link problem degenerates (`cor:link-prior-necessary`). The unregularized record energy has a rank-one minimizer $\mathbf M^\star$, outside the chart. Stationary generators exist only at critical points of the exponential map, and an explicit $K=2$ example has one. The chart infimum equals $\mathbf l(\mathbf M^\star)$ exactly when $\mu_j^\top\Sigma_j^{-1}\mu_i\ge0$, which is equivalent to $\operatorname{Tr}\mathbf M^\star\ge0$. Otherwise it lies strictly above $\mathbf l(\mathbf M^\star)$, and for $K=2,3$ it is not attained (`prop:glink-chart-infimum-semisimple` in Supplement G). With the prior the free energy attains its minimum.

Held links are the zero-width limit of the latent model (`thm:link-held-limit`), and conditional statements about held links transfer while link-marginal statements need not (`prop:link-conditional-transfer`). Directed record extension induces no link action. Jointly normalized undirected matter induces $-\log Z$, one half the log-determinant of the connection Laplacian, whose hopping expansion begins with the Wilson triangle term (`prop:link-induced-action`, `prop:glink-hopping`). The natural-gradient recognition flow of the generators has an Euler step that is the Gaussian Bayesian learning rule of Khan and Rue, Eqs. (12) and (37) (`prop:link-recognition-flow`). A history prior over learning episodes adds time-like loop defects with mass $\mathbf m$ (`prop:link-history-prior`). The most probable history obeys a discrete Newton equation of Euclidean sign, with a monotone kink in the one-dimensional double well (`prop:link-map-inertia`). The linearized operator is $\mathbf K=\mathring\Sigma^{-1}+\sum_pc_p\mathbf C^\top\mathbf C+\mathbf m(-\Delta_n)+\text{matter}$, a screened, massive Euclidean Proca structure (`prop:link-linearized-equation`).

The chapter carries 28 ESTABLISHED statements, 1 STANDARD and 1 NOT-CLAIMED. The NOT-CLAIMED item is the scope paragraph. Among other things it declines convergence of the generator recognition flow, global optimality of local minimizers, dynamical frames, a physical-field reading of the linearized operator, and an identification of the model with a fast-weight or delta-rule architecture.

## Corrections to the dynamic-links note

Integrating the theory exposed two errors in `manuscripts/Attention/dynamic_links.tex` ([[dennis-2026-dynamic-links]], dated October 3). Both were corrected on October 4, and the October 3 source note records the uncorrected statements.

The first error concerns invariance under reparameterization. Under $U_k\mapsto U_kh_k^{-1}$, the Frobenius defect satisfies $\Phi(h_kWh_k^{-1})=\Phi(W)$ for all $W$ exactly when $h_k$ is a nonzero multiple of an orthogonal matrix, not exactly when $h_k$ is orthogonal. The proof uses rank-one test matrices: $\|h(uv^\top)h^{-1}\|_F=|hu||h^{-\top}v|$ forces $|hu|\propto|u|$. The scalar factor acts trivially on the holonomy, so the reduction of curvature measurement to $O(K)$ stands.

The second error is in the prior-necessity corollary. The note claimed that without the link prior the free energy has no stationary link in the exponential chart and that its infimum is approached at the singular boundary. In fact stationary generators can exist at critical points of the exponential map, for example $X=-(\log3)I+2\pi J$ with link $\tfrac13I$ at $K=2$. The chart infimum is approached at the rank-one transport $G^\star=\xi'\xi_j^\top S_j^{-1}$ only when $\xi_j^\top C_j^{-1}\xi'\ge0$. Otherwise it lies strictly above it and is not attained for $K=2,3$, while $K\ge4$ is open.

The abstract and the next-steps sentence were updated to match, and the PDF was rebuilt (11 pages, no warnings). A float64 check of both corrections is in `evidence/dynamic_links_revision_checks.py`. It tests invariance under multiples of orthogonal matrices to $2\times10^{-15}$, a non-conformal counterexample, the Sherman–Morrison trace identity, chart approximants of $G^\star$ in the nonnegative case, and the stationary generator, whose gradient is $1.4\times10^{-10}$ by central differences.

## Relevance to this research

The links of both instantiations are now one object. In the transformer, `content_link_chart="exponential"` is the static, shared-generator limit of the latent-link model ([[dennis-2026-dynamic-links]]). In the multi-agent theory the same chart carries per-edge latent generators with a curvature prior. Taking the curvature prior from a loop defect rather than a class function is forced on the non-compact group, because class functions are blind to unipotent holonomy. The prior is necessary for well-posed per-context links. The inference dynamics of the links are first order, and inertia appears only for the most probable history across learning episodes. Nothing here was trained, and dynamic links are not implemented in `vfe3`.
