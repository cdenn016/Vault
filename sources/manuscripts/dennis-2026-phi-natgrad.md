# Frame-chart geometry for the stored frame tables

## Source and scope

The live source is [2026-09-28-phi-natgrad.tex](../../manuscripts/Attention/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad.tex) (compiled PDF beside it), a derivation note dated September 28, 2026 and authored as "VFE 3.0 derivation note". Its design source is `VFE_3.0/docs/derivations/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad-link-spectrum.md`, the record of a three-track workflow (read-only investigator, deriver and adversarial verifier per track). Investigators and derivers ran on Opus 5.5; verifiers ran on Fable 5.1, all three verdicts CONFIRMED_WITH_CORRECTIONS, and the corrections are applied in the tex. Derivers executed float64 CPU checks at block size $d\le6$ (some eigenvalue checks at $d=10$); the tex labels each statement as verified numerically, not independently reproduced, derived here, or cited. No training run was made, and neither optimizer route is shown to train better than AdamW. The local file may change after this dated record.

## Setting

The frame tables `phi_embed` and `pos_phi_free` store Lie-algebra coordinates $\varphi$; the model reads $E_v=\exp\iota(\varphi_v)$, $F_p=\exp\iota(\theta_p)$ and $U_i=E_{v_i}F_{p_i}$. For `block_glk` with $K=Hd$, $\mathfrak g=\bigoplus_h\mathfrak{gl}(d)$ and the active configuration has $K=20$, $H=2$, $d=10$. Three actions organize the symmetry statements: the left gauge action at each occurrence (fixes every reference belief), the common right reference change $U_i\mapsto U_ih$, and the split redundancy $E_v\mapsto E_vh$, $F_p\mapsto h^{-1}F_p$ that leaves every $U_i$ unchanged.

## Route 1: the Frobenius pullback is projected Euclidean descent on $U$

The existing `pullback_group` direction $v=G_0^{-1}\nabla_\varphi\ell$, $G_0=D^\top D$ with $D$ the chart differential, equals $D^+\Gamma$ and moves $U$ by $-\eta P_{\mathcal T}\Gamma$ to first order, with $\Gamma=\nabla_UL$ and $\mathcal T=U\mathfrak g$ (Theorem 3.1; verified to $3.8\times10^{-14}$ over several algebras). The left-trivialized direction solves $\Pi_{\mathfrak g}(U^\top U\xi)=\Pi_{\mathfrak g}(U^\top\Gamma)$, with closed forms for $\mathfrak{gl}$ and block $\mathfrak{gl}$ ($\xi=U^{-1}\Pi_{\mathfrak g}\Gamma$), orthogonal algebras, $\mathfrak{sl}$ and $\mathfrak{sp}$. The ambient gradient is recovered exactly from the stored chart gradient by one Fréchet derivative of the principal logarithm, $c=U^\top L_{\log}(U^\top,m)$, provided the spectrum of $X$ lies in the strip $|\mathrm{Im}\,z|<\pi$ ([[higham-2008-functions-of-matrices]]). The existing series route thus computes a cheap object expensively (about $3.8\times10^7$ versus $3\times10^5$ to $10^6$ flops per row block, arithmetic estimates).

The group retraction $\varphi\leftarrow\iota^{-1}\Pi_{\mathfrak g}\mathrm{Log}(Ue^{-\eta s\xi})$ (`group_log`) stays in $G$ for every subalgebra and differs from the chart step at second order by $\tfrac{\eta^2}{12}[[X,\xi],\xi]$; the chart stager's $10^{-6}$ product-residual certificate is therefore quadratic in the step and caps it, while `group_log` has float64 residual at most $2\times10^{-14}$. The note treats the logarithm branch (re-charting off the strip by at least $2\pi$, existence failure by [[culver-1966-existence]]), a cheap Bendixson strip certificate $\|\mathrm{skew}X\|<\pi-\delta$, a trust bound, and the requirement that rejected rows keep their chart. The pullback step is equivariant only under orthogonal $g$, and by Milnor's characterization no metric on $\mathrm{GL}^+(d)$ is bi-invariant ([[milnor-1976-left-invariant-metrics]]).

## Route 2: the belief-Fisher natural gradient

In the EM reading $\varphi$ remains a slow M-step parameter and only its preconditioner changes. The frame is a parameter of the reference prior $\mathcal N(U^{-1}\mu_v,U^{-1}\Sigma_vU^{-\top})$, and the metric is the Fisher information of that family at layer 0, pulled back from the full Gaussian Fisher-Rao metric ([[skovgaard-1984-riemannian-geometry-normal-model]]) even when the local belief is diagonal. Under $U\mapsto Ue^{tY}$,
$$g_U(Y,Y)=\nu^\top Y^\top C^{-1}Y\nu+\tfrac12\mathrm{tr}\big(C^{-1}(YC+CY^\top)\big)^2,\qquad\mathcal F(Y)=C^{-1}Y(C+\nu\nu^\top)+Y^\top,$$
or in whitened form $\|Z\omega\|^2+2\|\mathrm{sym}Z\|_F^2$ with $Z=L^{-1}YL$, $\omega=L^{-1}\nu$, $LL^\top=C$. The kernel is the stabilizer algebra of the reference Gaussian, of dimension $(d-1)(d-2)/2$ when $\nu\neq0$ (36 of 100 per block at $d=10$), and the generalized spectrum is explicit in $a=\mu^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu$, which is frame independent. The metric is left-gauge invariant, right-equivariant, right-invariant on $G$ for fixed $q$ with identity value $h_q$ (twice Amari's matrix natural gradient for $q=\mathcal N(0,I)$, [[amari-1998-natural-gradient]]), dominates every read-path Fisher by Čencov monotonicity ([[cencov-1982-statistical-decision-rules]]), and evades Milnor's obstruction because it depends on the belief. Under the split redundancy the realized-frame velocity is representative independent (mismatch $6.3\times10^{-15}$ for the Fisher route versus $0.177$ for the pullback and $0.179$ for the Euclidean chart step), so AdamW's trajectory depends on the arbitrary type/position split while the Fisher route's does not. The type-row metric is position independent, so type rows need no pooling; positional rows pool distinct Gaussians and need a dense $d^2\times d^2$ solve.

Because the loss reads $U_i$ outside the reference belief, the gradient has stabilizer components (mean share 0.19 to 0.26 on tiny models), and a completion must be chosen. The covariant family $g_U+\lambda_s\|\mathrm{sym}Z\|^2+\lambda_a\|\mathrm{asym}Z\|^2$ has an explicit spectrum; the default $(\lambda_s,\lambda_a)=(0,2)$ is the single Kronecker product $Y\mapsto C^{-1}Y(2C+\nu\nu^\top)$ with condition number $(2+a)/2$. It raises the near-kernel pair eigenvalues $\approx a/2$ to 2, so it changes the metric on mean-visible directions as well. The default weight is not derived from the belief or shown optimal. Steps are count-normalized ($N_{\mathrm{sc}}/n_v$), the Amari-consistent batch choice, which gives singleton types full-size single-sample steps. The output (cross-entropy) Fisher is derived as the alternative ([[martens-2015-kfac]] for the Kronecker discussion) but not recommended: it needs a sampled-label backward pass, persistent factors, and has no exact Kronecker form. Cost of the Fisher route is estimated at about $3.1\times10^9$ fp64 flops per step with no persistent state (arithmetic, not measured on GPU).

## Track 3 of the design source: the content-link spectrum

The design document's third track is not in the tex. It shows that with $L_{ij}^{(h)}=U_iM_hU_j^{-1}$, $M_h=\hat O_h\hat T_h$, the functions invariant under the left gauge action, the common right reference change ($M_h\mapsto a^{-1}M_ha$) and the per-head value basis are exactly the similarity invariants of the tuple $(M_h)$, read from the $d\times d$ matrix $W_h=\hat T_h\hat O_h$ via $\det(zI_K-M_h)=z^{K-d}\det(zI_d-W_h)$. No norm is similarity invariant, so the logged `relational_content_max_singular_value` is basis dependent; the spectral radius is the infimum of $\sigma_{\max}$ over the value-basis orbit. A dressing statement (with the verifier's correction that the graph must include self-links) says two link fields are gauge equivalent iff the $M_h$ are simultaneously conjugate, so the frames carry no invariant information of their own. The composition defect is $L_{ij}L_{jk}-L_{ik}=U_i(M_h^2-M_h)U_k^{-1}$. Under a causal prior the only closed loops are self-loops, and $\max_i\rho(rI+\sum_h\beta_{ii}^hM_h)$ is exactly the spectral radius of the layer's fixed-routing map. The `holonomy_deviation` and `holonomy_wilson` metrics multiply only the coboundary $U_iU_j^{-1}$ and so measure floating-point error of the stored frame pair, not curvature; relabeling was recommended. Author-only read-only measurements on two K=20 runs at step 15000 (not re-run by the verifier because $d=10$ exceeded the track's size rule) found every head expansive ($\rho(W_h)$ 2.6 to 7.4), far from flat or projector composition, $\sigma_{\max}/\rho$ up to 1.9, and a negative cross-head trace $\mathrm{Tr}(M_0M_1)$ in every layer. A side finding: the moment-matched covariance floor $\epsilon(\mathrm{tr}C_i/K)I$ breaks reference-change invariance at relative order $\epsilon$.

## Implementation and evidence status

Per the design source's amendments, the routes were implemented opt-in on 2026-09-28 as `m_phi_update_mode='fisher_group'` (with the pullback alternatives) and `m_phi_group_retraction='group_log'`, with a frame-condition gate $\max_h\kappa_2(U_h)\le10^3$ on the closed-form pullback (a routing proxy, not derived), rejection of off-strip rows on the Fisher route, and the link-spectrum diagnostic. Not established, per the tex: whether either route trains better than AdamW; the stabilizer leak at production shape; the effect of count normalization and of $\lambda_a$; GPU wall-clock cost; behavior of the pooled positional operator on trained statistics. The first training comparison is in [[2026-09-28-k20-ablation-set]] (Fisher route $+11.1$ PPL behind a matched AdamW run, with three coupled differences).

## Relevance to this research

This is the program's first natural gradient for the frame parameters with an actual statistical-model justification, as opposed to the Killing conditioner or the Frobenius pullback, and it gives the stabilizer-kernel and completion structure that any frame optimizer on a non-compact group must face.
