## Confirmed results and boundaries

Gauge, Fisher and base curvature belong to different tensor domains. Each contribution to VFE requires a declared normalized probability factor; no universal curvature penalty follows from the bare exact ELBO. The constant-weight finite Fisher-action problem has existence on its positive branch catalog and separate small-coupling uniqueness/error bounds. Parameter-dependent field weights require additional equations.

Local analytic/hyperbolic field control retains Sobolev and range assumptions. A radial construction reaches a genuine differentiability boundary while probabilities and Fisher eigenvalues stay positive. This refutes unrestricted global smooth continuation for that class while preserving the conditional local theorem.

The smooth torus construction fixes a conformal class, trivial U(1) bundle, gauge patch, decaying Fourier envelopes and full-support reference. Its normalized geometry/Fisher feedback has a scalar variance equation, finite-coupling existence and contraction range $|\lambda|<8\operatorname{sech}^8(1)$. It is not an arbitrary random-geometry measure.

A later native experiment combines joint matter/flux with a matter-derived smooth conformal metric, bounded normalized interaction and the actual selected Fisher curvature. Observations, VFE, scaling and conditional-refresh hierarchy dynamics use that same joint. Puncture flux is singular, so no curvature-distribution square is introduced; empty flux retains positive probability. Large-coupling root selection has its own branch rule.

Spatial stencils give uniform action error $C_1h+C_2h^2$, controlling solved roots, KL, Fisher and curvature inside the contraction region. The exact finite cluster reference retains connectivity, forgotten mass, sign and defect parity, with potentially exponential cost. A fixed finite homogeneous linear transfer cannot exactly encode the specified closing mass factor; controlled truncation is a separate approximation. No universal efficient simulator or physical spacetime identification is claimed.
