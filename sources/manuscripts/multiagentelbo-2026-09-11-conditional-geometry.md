## Confirmed results and boundaries

The standard-Borel kernel construction supplies jointly measurable posterior versions without a common likelihood density. Null records retain version dependence. Absolute point-record evidence requires a predictive reference; positive-probability event VFE and random-record joint KL are separately defined. Singular posteriors need not be ordinary density reweightings of the latent prior.

Everywhere-equivariant conditionals are constructed for locally compact second-countable Hausdorff groups with the specified measurable covariant experiment. Averaging within the Haar null class avoids an invalid uncountable intersection of conull sets. The countable-diagram reverse theorem additionally requires that invertible composites have invertible factors and that forward kernels commute exactly. These results do not extend automatically to arbitrary measurable groups or categories.

Retained passive frames give a slice on the declared trivialization. Positive receiving covariances and a closed represented group give properness on coefficient spaces; faithfulness alone does not. Strong-Hilbert quotients use closed orbit directions and a strong metric. The Jotz–Neeb closed-range result is prior literature. Properness does not automatically split a weak Fisher metric.

Finite linear projectable configurations form the kernel of fiber-difference constraints with an exact coarse statistical realization under the pushed design measure. Nonlinear constrained sets can have crossings. Observed dynamical descent requires actual semiconjugacy, while critical Fisher-speed zeros or divergences obstruct a regular clock.

The intrinsic finite-dimensional defect classification constrains connection variation through the actual fundamental-action image. Positivity uses contractions of a semidefinite quotient; positive and signed cocycle solutions have different isometric/isotropic parameterizations. Constant-rank smoothness, Lie-algebra lifting and target-connection descent remain extra requirements. Pointwise positivity and cocycle equality can coexist with failed descent.
