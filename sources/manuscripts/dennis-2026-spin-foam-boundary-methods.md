## Setting and boundary recovery

The network remains at $c_*$. Agents are local sections of the belief/model associated bundles, with independent dynamic group links and separately typed matrix communication maps. Ordered loop/cut and joint source-event marks are graph-interface data. All carriers are standard Borel. The reference $\varrho$ is a proper, potentially correlated probability; $\mathsf m_o=L_o\varrho$ has finite positive mass $M_o$, and recognition can be correlated. One common channel acts on reference, evidence, and recognition. Complete free energy is $-\log M_o+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_o\Vert\Pi_o)$ on its finite domain; singular recognition has extended value $+\infty$.

Theorem 2.2 constructs a countable exterior signature and one reference-conditioned, fiber-supported recovery kernel for the declared likelihood catalog. Recovery concerns the projected exterior experiment, rather than inversion of every interior microstate. Proposition 2.3 gives minimality only for exterior-independent lifts retaining the raw marks. Proposition 2.5 separately characterizes exterior-dependent common recovery by likelihood descent. Corollary 2.11 extends recovery to specified integrable likelihood families through $L^1(\varrho)$ separability; it asserts per-record measure recovery, rather than one pointwise density version for uncountably many observations.

Correlated gluing uses conditionals from one joint reference and retains all induced factors. Product marginal messages reproduce every bounded-factor contraction only under conditional independence across the separator (Proposition 2.6). Compatible marginals alone do not determine the original coupling (Remark 2.7). Common-reference diagram reversals require composing full forward maps and additionally require density descent for common evidence recovery (Proposition 2.8). Ordered root-dressing diamonds on connected subtrees of a fixed rooted forest are proved separately (Proposition 2.9), with compatible represented tree isomorphisms at matrix-map endpoints. These raw-mark identities do not construct a full probabilistic factor-through square for a changing boundary interface.

## Gauge and positive-law error contracts

Proposition 3.3 assumes finite complex features, a compact residual group with unitary representations, an intertwining exterior map, and whole-carrier multiplicity projections. Its discarded Hilbert--Schmidt tail is $\sum_\lambda d_\lambda\sum_{a>r_\lambda}\nu_{\lambda,a}$, with full spectral ties retained or separately covariant tie projectors supplied. Proposition 3.4 proves passive GL covariance when positive metrics and coordinate maps transform together. It supplies no invariant positive norm for the full standard GL action, noncompact Haar probability, or universal GL harmonic compression. Proper noncompact link measures remain inputs, and raw joint gauge/source marks remain explicit.

Theorem 3.5 bridges features to laws using a common statistic selected before recognition and slice evaluation, a positive normalized likelihood kernel at both exact/projected inputs, and an envelope on the full normalized log density. An envelope $\varepsilon_o$ bounds the fine/coarse forward posterior KLs and discarded reference-conditional KL by $\varepsilon_o^2/2$, and bounds $|\log\widehat M_o-\log M_o|$ by $\varepsilon_o$. A spectral tail alone does not establish that envelope. Exact recovery still requires likelihood descent.

For a support-preserving multiplier $\widehat{\mathsf m}=e^{-\mathfrak r}\mathsf m$, Theorem 4.1 assumes $a\le\mathfrak r\le b$ on exact evidence support. With $\omega=b-a$, it proves
$$
-b\le\log(\widehat M/M)\le-a,\qquad
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\Vert\widehat P),D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\widehat P\Vert P)\le\min\{\omega,\omega^2/8\}.
$$
For $Q\ll P$ with finite divergence, the complete free-energy difference is $\mathbb E_Q[\mathfrak r]$; the normalized recognition-posterior divergence difference also includes $\log(\widehat M/M)$. Constant residuals can change evidence and complete free energy without changing the posterior. Record-family admission retains the record normalizer (Proposition 4.3). The tower accumulates interval endpoints and widths (Theorem 4.4). The approximate-route bound $\min\{\Omega_1+\Omega_2,(\Omega_1+\Omega_2)^2/8\}$ requires identical exact common-target channels (Corollary 4.5); exact-route mismatch needs a separate estimate.

## Predictive information and activation

Activation uses the actual microscopic law conditional only on an already completed prefix when that prefix belongs to the experiment; it does not condition on subsequent survival. Every hidden exterior coordinate or feedback control needed for prediction remains in the admitted state. The physical or optimizing experiment must be specified. Sample indices are distinct from an intrinsic information clock.

The exact finite-alphabet predictive state is measure-valued and need not have useful finite dimension (Proposition 5.2). The general standard-Borel finite-horizon theorem requires joint history/next-record dominance relative to the suffix row, finite conditional information, normalized measurable competitor kernels with support dominance, and finite source-context row risk (Theorem 5.4). Mixture conditioning alone does not imply that joint dominance. Under those admissions,
$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mathbb P_H\Vert\widetilde{\mathbb P}_H)
=\sum_{n=0}^{H-1}I(D_{n+1};O_n\mid C_n)
+\sum_{n=0}^{H-1}\mathbb E_{\mathrm{source}}D_{\mathrm{KL}}(r_n(\cdot\mid C_n)\Vert\widetilde r_n(\cdot\mid C_n)).
$$
Positive row tilts with square-integrable residual widths bound the second sum by $\sum_n\mathbb E_{\mathrm{source}}\Delta_n(C_n)^2/8$ (Corollary 5.5). The finite-alphabet specialization retains its extended-infinite support-failure branch (Theorem 5.6).

The finite-alphabet forgetting theorem assumes transition coefficient $\alpha_N$, positive bounded emission ratios $a_N,b_N$, and $\varkappa_N=(b_N/a_N)\alpha_N<1$. Its omitted-history bound is $(H-k-1)_+c_N\varkappa_N^{2k}$, with $c_N=(b_N-a_N)^2/a_N$ (Theorem 5.8); Corollary 5.9 directly admits conditional-filter contraction. Uniformity requires common family bounds. The reveal-bit example grounds why unconditional hidden mixing does not determine predictive memory (Proposition 5.10), without drawing conclusions about large-network formation. These path budgets do not establish an autonomous parent under its own learning law.

The auxiliary finite regulator identity (Proposition 6.2) requires bounded real features, a $C^1$ symmetric regulator, positive definite tilted covariance, and local invertibility of the source-to-mean map. Passive feature changes are regulator-independent. It gives $\partial_\upsilon\Gamma_\upsilon=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}[(\partial_\upsilon R_\upsilon)(\nabla_\phi^2\Gamma_\upsilon+R_\upsilon)^{-1}]$. The intermediate regulated mass need not equal $M_o$ and equals it at zero regulator. This is a finite local operator calculation, without a coarse-graining semigroup, truncation closure, or identification of regulator with agent count, depth, or physical scale.

## Four open scientific applications

Chapter 7 retains four `INCONCLUSIVE` entries. Open Problem 7.1 (`open:sb-usable-interface`) requires a finite regular non-Abelian interface with nonconstant exterior prediction, lower cost than the uncompressed boundary, controlled raw marks, and a full normalized-likelihood envelope on its declared record class, or a class-matched lower bound. A countable Borel encoding does not settle usable compression.

Open Problem 7.2 (`open:sb-interface-diagram`) requires complete probabilistic factor-through maps and commuting squares for nontrivial contractions changing boundary coordinates. Root-dressing identities supply only the ordered-mark part.

Open Problem 7.3 (`open:sb-activation-budget`) requires conditional memory and row estimates for a specified evolving non-Abelian link/attention family on its actual completed-prefix formation/handoff horizon, retaining needed feedback controls, or a matching impossibility result within the prescribed memory class.

Open Problem 7.4 (`open:sb-uniform-budget`) requires bounded costs, interface width, accumulated likelihood offsets/oscillations, and predictive constants as finite size and nontrivial depth grow, or matching lower bounds. Results admit arbitrary finite $N$, including $N\ge512$, without proving these bounds. No useful low dimension, autonomous own-law parent, recursive formation, thermodynamic limit, quantum amplitude, base transport, spacetime, or physical time is established.
