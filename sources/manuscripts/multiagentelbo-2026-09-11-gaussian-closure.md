## Confirmed results and boundaries

Gaussian marginalization is a Schur complement with linear term and scalar normalizer. Hard energy restriction is different. A marked residual $\|Au-Bv\|^2$ has a single positive metric/invertible transport representation exactly when endpoint dimensions agree, both maps are injective, and their images coincide. Otherwise a positive residual remains. An invertible cross block does not establish the desired presentation.

For independently arbitrary SPD matrix weights of block dimension at least two on a flat graph, universal positive matrix-Laplacian Kron closure holds exactly when every hidden connected component meets at most two retained terminals, counting ground. Scalar weights have broader closure. The independent-family theorem forces a common congruence-diagonal cone under its positive-anchor pair-admissibility condition; smaller subsets still require scalar-update closure.

A nonflat degree-two hidden sector with no hidden quadratic anchor closes through ordered series composition. For residuals $x-At$ and $t-Bz$, the new transport is $AB$ and weight $(W^{-1}+AV^{-1}A^T)^{-1}$. Linear sources, determinant and volume factors remain. Parallel paths and holonomy loops are allowed; arbitrary branching does not inherit this result.

A proper Gaussian three-star has a rational dual witness excluding every positive one-node/two-node energy presentation on the retained typed nodes, even after changing GL transports or anchors. Its full quadratic marginal still exists. New latent variables or mixing retained nodes changes the requested carrier.

Finite semialgebraic descriptions over exact algebraic inputs admit a terminating quantifier-elimination classification of pair presentations and finite shared diagrams, with good/failure loci, witnesses and measurable selectors. This is a proof of a reduction, not a reported general QE execution or efficiency claim. Smooth/equivariant selection and arbitrary program-described families have explicit obstructions.

PSD laws normalize on a declared energy quotient. With representative metric $H$, covariance satisfies $QCQ=Q$, $CQC=C$, $HCQ=QCH$. Coarse maps must descend through kernels and use the actual target representative projection. Classification extends to changing support, but smooth coefficients do not imply DQM: $Q(t)=\operatorname{diag}(1,t^2)$ has TV distance one from its rank-changing endpoint. Passive transformations carry $H$ as well as the energy.
