# Propriétés des matrices "bien localisées" près de leur diagonale et quelques applications

> [!info] Citation
> Jaffard, S. (1990). *Propriétés des matrices "bien localisées" près de leur diagonale et quelques applications*. Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré C, Analyse non linéaire, 7(5), 461-476. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0294-1449(16)30287-6)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper studies two classes of infinite matrices whose entries decay exponentially or polynomially away from the diagonal, and shows that invertibility on $\ell^2$ implies the inverse has the same type of decay.

## Problem & setting

Infinite matrices with exponential or polynomial off-diagonal decay, treated through symbolic-calculus-type properties.

## Key results

Per the abstract, inversion preserves the decay class and is a local operation: a change in some entries is not felt far from the changed region. Two applications to orthonormal wavelet bases are given. The manuscript's record adds that a review (zbMATH) was read and states the hypothesis on the index space and the inverse-closedness result.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites it for inverse-closedness of exponentially localized matrices, which gives decay of the precision (inverse covariance) at some smaller rate, and states that its own Combes-Thomas bound makes the rate explicit. It also appears in the related-work comparison on precisions. The paper itself was not read in full. See [[Gaussian Belief Propagation]], [[Markov Blanket]] and [[Spectral graph theory]].
