# A Statistical Model of the Gross Analysis of Transaction Flows

> [!info] Citation
> Savage, I. R., Deutsch, K. W. (1960). *A Statistical Model of the Gross Analysis of Transaction Flows*. Econometrica, 28(3), 551-572. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/1910131)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper proposes a null model for matrices of transaction flows among actors, in which the baseline expectation assumes complete indifference among actors and departures from it are the objects of interest.

## Problem & setting

The method applies to any quantifiable transactions among three to more than one hundred actors. Import-export data between countries, arranged like a contingency table with a zero diagonal and money amounts as entries, serve as the worked example, with the North Atlantic area in 1928 as the illustration.

## Method

A matrix of expected baseline flows is built from the assumption of indifference, and for each ordered pair of actors the difference between the observed and baseline transaction is measured. This removes gross size effects.

## Key results

According to the abstract, the departures from the baseline support tentative inferences about preferences among pairs of larger groups, about clustering or integration among actors, and about change over time when several matrices are available. The manuscript's reading record states that the end page, 572, comes from secondary citations and is unverified, with zbMATH later giving 551-572.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript treats the transaction-flow index and modularity as measures of contrast in message rates, and cites this paper, with the communication tests of Deutsch, as the closest precedent for contrast against a declared null. It compares transaction flows with a null built from marginal totals. The work was known from its abstract and secondary descriptions.
