## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites equation 10.32.9, the integral representation of the modified Bessel function of the second kind $K_\nu(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{-x\cosh s}\cosh(\nu s)\,ds$ for $x>0$, in the appendix on abstract collectives. Its record also lists 10.40.2 with 10.17.1, the large-argument expansion and its coefficients, as read. The citation is for these standard special-function formulas and supports computations on correlation functions and decay, see [[Critical Phenomena]] and [[Coarse Graining]].
