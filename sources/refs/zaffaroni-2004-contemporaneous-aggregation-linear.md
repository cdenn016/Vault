## TL;DR

A 2004 Journal of Econometrics paper on contemporaneous aggregation of linear dynamic models in large economies, as its title and venue establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper, in its discussion of environments and aggregate memory, only for what a later review says of it: that its general aggregation scheme for random-coefficient autoregressions includes the case of a common component, or common innovations. The manuscript states that it did not open the paper, and any use of this entry should say so. See [[Coarse Graining]] and [[Renormalization group flow]].
