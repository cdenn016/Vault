## TL;DR

A 1977 Biophysical Journal article on the physics of chemoreception. The manuscript's record says only its PubMed abstract was read, and that the equation cited from it was not confirmed in the paper.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper in two roles. The first is the scaling of the precision with which a cell can sense a concentration, which the abstract states as a least fractional error falling with the inverse square root of the product of integration time, concentration, cell radius and diffusion constant. The second is as the attributed origin of the constant for a sphere that counts molecules in its volume without hindering diffusion. That constant is taken from a later paper by Mora and Wingreen, which attributes it to Berg and Purcell; the equation itself was not seen in this paper, and a second attempt to open the full text on November 10, 2026 also failed. The paper is context for the environment and sensing section, tied to [[Rational inattention]], [[Communication-constrained inference]], [[Channel Capacity]] and [[Precision weighting]].
