# The Martin boundary for random walk

> [!info] Citation
> Ney, P., Spitzer, F. (1966). *The Martin boundary for random walk*. Transactions of the American Mathematical Society, 121(1), 116-132. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1966-0195151-8)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper gives a concrete realization of the Martin boundary for random walks on the integer lattice, through the asymptotic behavior of the relativized Green function.

## Problem & setting

The introduction (the text served as the abstract) says the boundary agrees with the one that Doob and Hunt constructed for arbitrary transient Markov chains with discrete time and countable state space, and obtained by completing the state space under a metric based on the relativized Green function. The new content is a geometric interpretation of the boundary and of convergence to it. The one-dimensional case was done by Doob, Snell and Williamson, and the main extra difficulty in dimension $d>1$ is a renewal theorem for the Green function.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the pointwise decay rate of the Green function of finite-range walks with drift, which it uses to show that an exponential rate is exact with the Ornstein-Zernike power-law prefactor. The manuscript's reading record states that the primary text was not read because the publisher refused automated access; the cited theorem was read only in a restatement by another paper, where it gives decay like $|y|^{-(d-1)/2}\exp(-c_y\cdot y)$ with a direction-dependent vector $c_y$. The manuscript flags it as a result to be read before citing with a theorem number. It is part of the vault's treatment of correlation length, see [[Critical Phenomena]] and [[Graph Laplacian]].
