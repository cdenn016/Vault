## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for the slaving principle as a named method. In the manuscript's account, synergetics has order parameters that the parts produce and that in turn enslave them, a circularity that a later author carried to closed equations for opinion shares. The manuscript places this beside a hierarchy-of-committees renormalization of majority opinion and says it knows those two works only secondarily.
