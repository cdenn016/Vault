# Deep temporal models and active inference

> [!info] Citation
> Friston, K. J., Rosch, R., Parr, T., Price, C., Bowman, H. (2017). *Deep temporal models and active inference*. Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews 77, 388-402. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2017.04.009)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/friston-2017-deep-temporal-models.pdf), 16 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A review that builds active inference on deep temporal generative models, in which a state at one hierarchical level entails a sequence of transitions in the level below, and simulates behavior and electrophysiology in reading.

## Method

The abstract describes hierarchical generative models of state transitions whose inversion is sequential inference, so evidence accumulates over nested time scales and supports inferences about narratives. The paper uses Bayesian belief updating and neuronal process theories to simulate epistemic foraging in reading.

## Key results

The simulations are reported to reproduce perisaccadic delay-period activity and local field potentials seen empirically. Using the deep structure, the authors simulate responses to local violations such as font type and global violations such as semantic ones, reproducing mismatch negativity and P300 responses respectively.
