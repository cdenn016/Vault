# Multilayer Networks

> [!info] Citation
> Kivelä, M., Arenas, A., Barthelemy, M., Gleeson, J. P., Moreno, Y., Porter, M. A. (2014). *Multilayer Networks*. Journal of Complex Networks, 2(3), 203-271. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/comnet/cnu016)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/kivela-2014-multilayer-networks.pdf), 59 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A review of multilayer networks that gives a general framework and a dictionary relating terms such as multiplex networks, interdependent networks and networks of networks, and surveys diagnostics, models and dynamical processes on such networks.

## Method

The article covers the history of the topic, a unifying framework and terminology, existing data sets, generalizations of single-layer network diagnostics, and work on community structure, connected components, tensor decompositions and dynamical processes, and ends with an outlook.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper for terminology only, and its record marks the use as bibliographic. In the section on abstract collectives it says that a population in which layers share their nodes and differ in their edges is a multiplex network in this paper's terminology. In the minimal-model section it uses the term to say the earlier model had one modality and so no multiplex structure.
