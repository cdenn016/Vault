## TL;DR

A 1990 article in Communications in Mathematical Physics by Aizenman and Wehr on the effects of quenched randomness on first-order phase transitions.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this article together with the authors' 1989 letter for the statement that quenched randomness suppresses continuous symmetry breaking up to four dimensions and leaves the two-dimensional random-field Ising model with a unique Gibbs state. The content here is taken from the manuscript's citing sentence, not from the article, which was not read. It supports the disorder discussion in the coherence analysis.
