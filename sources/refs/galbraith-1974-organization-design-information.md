# Organization Design: An Information Processing View

> [!info] Citation
> Galbraith, J. R. (1974). *Organization Design: An Information Processing View*. Interfaces, 4(3), 28-36. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/inte.4.3.28)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper explains why task uncertainty is related to organizational form, using Herbert Simon's theory of cognitive limits as the guide. It traces the consequences of those limits to several organization design strategies.

## Problem & setting

The abstract states that the framework integrates organizational interventions, such as information systems and group problem solving, that had been treated separately before.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper in its survey of economics, which treats the firm as an information processor, for the view of organization design as information processing.
