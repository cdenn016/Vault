# Frontière de Furstenberg, propriétés de contraction et théorèmes de convergence

> [!info] Citation
> Guivarc'h, Y., Raugi, A. (1985). *Frontière de Furstenberg, propriétés de contraction et théorèmes de convergence*. Zeitschrift für Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie und Verwandte Gebiete, 69(2), 187-242. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02450281)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/guivarc-h-1985-frontiere-furstenberg-proprietes.pdf), 56 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A paper in French on the asymptotic behavior of products $X_n = Y_1 \cdots Y_n$ of independent, identically distributed random elements of a connected semisimple Lie group.

## Problem & setting

The introduction takes a probability measure on a connected semisimple Lie group and studies the asymptotics of the product of independent variables with that law. It lists earlier work, by Furstenberg and Kesten among others, that assumed the law to be absolutely continuous with respect to Haar measure, spread out, or built from positive matrices. The authors say their study needs neither a spreading nor a positivity hypothesis and refines those earlier results, and they illustrate the setting with the group of real matrices of determinant one.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper, together with a later work on Lyapunov exponents of products of random matrices, for the statement that the top two Lyapunov exponents of products of independent hop maps differ when the generated semigroup is strongly irreducible and contracting. It states these results only at the level of their records and restatements. The manuscript's reading record lists the Crossref record and a secondary statement, so the theorems themselves were not checked against the paper's text, and this note does not state them. The use is in the manuscript's scale analysis of how shared content changes along a chain of hops; see [[Bundle scale space]], [[Renormalization group flow]] and [[Coarse Graining]].
