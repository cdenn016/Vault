## TL;DR

A monograph in Springer's Grundlehren series on Markov diffusion operators, as the title and series establish. The manuscript did not read it directly.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript uses one theorem of the book, the logarithmic Sobolev inequality and the exponential decay of relative entropy along the semigroup, for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup whose Mehler kernel is Gaussian. The reading record states that the primary text was not read and that the theorem was read in the restatement of Ledoux's work on Stein's method, Section 2.3, so the manuscript flags it as a result known through a restatement. It is used for the decay of relative entropy in the coherence analysis of Gaussian collectives, which relates to [[Mutual information]], [[Canonical Correlation Analysis]] and [[Nonequilibrium steady state]].
