## TL;DR

A 68-page chapter in the Séminaire de Probabilités XXXIII volume (Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1709) on the dynamics of stochastic approximation algorithms, as the title and records establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the chapter for the ordinary-differential-equation method of stochastic approximation, which passes from a stochastic recursion to a deterministic flow as the step size goes to zero; the manuscript does not carry that step out for its formation analysis. In the minimal-model section it also names a limit-set theorem of this kind as the condition behind condensation with random limits, and states that it cites the theorem without checking it against the source. The reading record says the chapter is known only through its bibliographic records.
