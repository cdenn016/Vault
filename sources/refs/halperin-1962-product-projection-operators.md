## TL;DR

A 1962 note on products of projection operators, cited in the manuscript for Halperin's theorem on cyclic products of projections.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper for the statement that cyclic products of orthogonal projections converge, in any Hilbert space, to the projection onto the intersection of the subspaces. The manuscript's reading record says the theorem was read in a later restatement (equation 1.2 of a work on convergence rates) and that the entry was verified against zbMATH Open; the primary text was not read, and the paper has no DOI. The manuscript uses the theorem for relays of fixed codes in a common metric, which it describes as alternating projections, and lists the paper among primary texts known only from records or secondary accounts.
