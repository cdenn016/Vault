## TL;DR

A 1960 article in the Czechoslovak Mathematical Journal, written in Russian, on a convergence principle in Hilbert space, as the title and the records (Crossref, zbMATH Zbl 0090.08902, Czech Digital Mathematics Library) establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper, with a 1965 paper on convergence, for the statement that in finite dimension products of projections in any order converge when each subspace recurs infinitely often. It adds that there is no uniform rate when the gaps between recurrences grow. The citation concerns iterated projections onto subspaces, as in repeated pairwise agreement among agents. The manuscript's reading record shows that only the record was seen. See [[Fixed-point iteration]] and [[Fixed Points]].
