A 2008 book in the Springer series Texts in Applied Mathematics (volume 53), on averaging and homogenization in multiscale problems, as its title and series establish.

The manuscript cites the book among works on the rigorous elimination of fast variables in the limit of infinite timescale separation, and says it knows these works from records and abstracts.
