# A Brief History of Stigmergy

> [!info] Citation

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

A historical account of stigmergy, a class of mechanisms mediating interactions among animals, in the context of social insects, with a discussion of two kinds: quantitative and qualitative stigmergy.

## Problem & setting

The abstract states that Grassé introduced stigmergy in 1959 to explain why individuals in an insect society act as if alone while their collective activity appears coordinated.

## Method

The paper is a historical review followed by a discussion of the general properties of the two stigmergic mechanisms. The evidence here gives no further detail.
