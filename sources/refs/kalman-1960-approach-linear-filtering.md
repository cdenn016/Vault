# A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems

> [!info] Citation
> Kalman, R. E. (1960). *A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems*. Journal of Basic Engineering, 82(1), 35-45. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper re-examines linear filtering and prediction using the Bode-Shannon representation of random processes and the state-transition method for dynamic systems.

## Problem & setting

Classical filtering and prediction for stationary and nonstationary statistics, with growing-memory and infinite-memory filters.

## Key results

The abstract lists three results: the formulation applies without change to stationary and nonstationary statistics; a nonlinear difference (or differential) equation for the covariance of the optimal estimation error yields the filter coefficients; and the filtering problem is dual to the noise-free regulator problem.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites it for the Kalman filter as the reference for exactness of belief propagation on trees (equality with the filter run on the observations that could have reached a node), for the Kalman gain as the Gaussian case of reception as prior completion, and for the optimal write weight of a decaying record being the leak of the optimal predictor.
