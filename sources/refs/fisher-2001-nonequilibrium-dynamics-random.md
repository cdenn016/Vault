# Nonequilibrium dynamics of random field Ising spin chains: Exact results via real space renormalization group

> [!info] Citation
> Fisher, D. S., Le Doussal, P., Monthus, C. (2001). *Nonequilibrium dynamics of random field Ising spin chains: Exact results via real space renormalization group*. Physical Review E 64(6), 066107. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.64.066107)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/fisher-2001-nonequilibrium-dynamics-random.pdf), 55 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

The paper studies the nonequilibrium dynamics of classical random Ising chains with an asymptotically exact real-space renormalization group, and computes many universal properties of domain-wall coarsening.

## Problem & setting

The introduction frames the work around domain-wall dynamics in random systems, where slow glassy dynamics prevents experiments from reaching equilibrium. The main case is the random-field Ising chain with and without a uniform field, in the regime where field and temperature are much smaller than the exchange coupling, so that the Imry-Ma length is large. Ising spin glass chains in an applied field are obtained from it by a gauge transformation.

## Key results

The abstract reports that the two types of domain walls diffuse in opposite random potentials and annihilate on meeting, and that wall configurations converge to system-specific positions independent of initial conditions, so the long-time behavior is pseudodeterministic. It reports energy, magnetization and domain-size distributions, exact scaling forms of two-point and two-time correlations, persistence properties, and a nonequilibrium fluctuation-dissipation ratio showing an aging regime unlike mean-field theory.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper in its coherence discussion, in a conjecture that domains and switches in a chain of agents agree in number up to one, as being in line with the real-space renormalization of the random-field Ising chain.
