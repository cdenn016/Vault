# TTB or not TTB, that is the question: a review and analysis of the empirical literature on travel time (and money) budgets

## TL;DR

A review and analysis of the empirical literature on travel time and money budgets, in Transportation Research Part A.

## Key results

As the manuscript reports it, the review covers more than two dozen studies and concludes that travel time is not constant except perhaps in aggregate. The manuscript's record says the abstract was read; no full text was used.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the review as the qualification to Marchetti's claim of a fixed daily travel budget of about one hour: the budget holds only in aggregate. The manuscript uses the budget as a bound on the diameter of the sets reachable in one round of communication. The connection is to [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]] and [[Collective active inference]]. See also [[marchetti-1994-anthropological-invariants-travel]].
