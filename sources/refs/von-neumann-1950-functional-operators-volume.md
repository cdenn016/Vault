## TL;DR

A volume of the Annals of Mathematics Studies series, number 22, on the geometry of orthogonal spaces, as its title and series establish. The available abstract is a generic series description.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this book, with Halperin, for the statement that alternating projections converge to the projection onto the intersection, which it applies to noiseless relays of fixed codes in a common metric. It states that the book is known only from records, and the alternating-projection theorem was not read. The conventional year 1950 was checked against zbMATH, while the digital edition is dated 1951. See [[Fixed-point iteration]] and [[Coarse Graining]].
