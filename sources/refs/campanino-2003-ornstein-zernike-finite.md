# Ornstein-Zernike theory for finite range Ising models above $T_c$

> [!info] Citation
> Campanino, M., Ioffe, D., Velenik, Y. (2003). *Ornstein-Zernike theory for finite range Ising models above $T_c$*. Probability Theory and Related Fields, 125(3), 305-349. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-002-0229-z)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/campanino-2003-ornstein-zernike-finite.pdf), 45 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A rigorous derivation of the Ornstein-Zernike asymptotic formula for the decay of the two-point function of finite-range Ising-type models on $\mathbb Z^d$ throughout the high-temperature region.

## Problem & setting

The classical Ornstein-Zernike formula gives the asymptotics of pair correlations away from the critical point. The introduction notes that the original argument assumes a renewal structure for correlations without justification, and that earlier rigorous justifications covered perturbative high-temperature or low-density regimes.

## Method

According to the abstract, the proof relies on prior knowledge of strict exponential decay of the two-point function, together with the sharp characterization of the phase transition due to Aizenman, Barsky and Fernandez. The table of contents lists a renormalization section, a reduction to Ruelle's setting, a Perron-Frobenius theorem for countable alphabets and a local limit theorem.

## Key results

The abstract states that the formula holds for every inverse temperature $\beta<\beta_c$, and that as a byproduct the inverse correlation length is an analytic and strictly convex function of direction.

## Relevance to this research
