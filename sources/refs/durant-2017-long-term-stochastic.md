# Long-Term, Stochastic Editing of Regenerative Anatomy via Targeting Endogenous Bioelectric Gradients

> [!info] Citation
> Durant, F., Morokuma, J., Fields, C., Williams, K., Adams, D. S., Levin, M. (2017). *Long-Term, Stochastic Editing of Regenerative Anatomy via Targeting Endogenous Bioelectric Gradients*. Biophysical Journal 112(10), 2231-2243. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2017.04.011)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

An experimental study of planarian regeneration reporting that a brief perturbation of endogenous bioelectric networks permanently rewrites the regenerated anterior-posterior pattern. The altered body plan is stored in animals that look normal.

## Problem & setting

The abstract concerns amputated planarian trunk fragments and the question of where the target body plan is stored. It reports that the stored information is not visible in histology, polarity-gene expression or neoblast distribution.

## Method

The abstract describes a temporary modulation of regenerative bioelectric dynamics in amputated fragments, followed by repeated amputation of the morphologically normal regenerates in water to probe the stored pattern.

## Key results

According to the abstract, the treatment yields a constant ratio of two-headed to normal regenerates, and this ratio recurs on later cuts, which the authors call a cryptic phenotype. The state is carried by global patterns of cellular resting potential, is multistable, and reverts to wild type after reversal of the bioelectric state. The manuscript's record notes that only the bibliographic data were checked and the paper itself was not read.
