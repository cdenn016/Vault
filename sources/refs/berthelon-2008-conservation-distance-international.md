## TL;DR

An article in the Journal of International Economics on the conservation of distance in international trade, as the title and venue establish. The manuscript's record says the abstract was read; its text is not in the packet.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the article as empirical evidence that the effect of distance on trade has not weakened as communication improved: the distance elasticity rose over time, mostly within industries, according to the manuscript's use. It appears in the section on abstract collectives, as support for distance-dependent coupling that persists across scales, and is cited alongside a meta-analysis of distance effects and a model of firm sizes.
