## TL;DR

A 1966 article in The Annals of Mathematical Statistics on limit theorems for indecomposable multidimensional Galton-Watson processes.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript names the Kesten-Stigum condition after this paper. In the scale section it describes reconstruction on a $b$-ary tree as holding when $b$ times the squared second eigenvalue of the channel exceeds one, tight for the binary symmetric channel and not for some other channels. The related-work section calls the condition sufficient in general and not always sharp. The manuscript cites the paper for the name of the condition only, from its bibliographic record. See [[Data processing inequality]], [[Channel Capacity]] and [[Belief Propagation]].
