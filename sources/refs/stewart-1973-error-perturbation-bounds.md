# Error and Perturbation Bounds for Subspaces Associated with Certain Eigenvalue Problems

> [!info] Citation
> Stewart, G. W. (1973). *Error and Perturbation Bounds for Subspaces Associated with Certain Eigenvalue Problems*. SIAM Review, 15(4), 727-764. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/1015095)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper describes a technique for error bounds on characteristic subspaces in the eigenvalue problem, the generalized eigenvalue problem and the singular value decomposition, based on an iterative process for generating the subspaces.

## Method

According to the abstract, the bounds come from an iteration that generates the subspaces, and one or more steps of that iteration yield perturbation estimates whose own error can be bounded. The same method gives perturbation bounds for isolated eigenvalues and information about clusters of eigenvalues.
