# Estimating the Lieb-Robinson Velocity for Classical Anharmonic Lattice Systems

> [!info] Citation
> Raz, H., Sims, R. (2009). *Estimating the Lieb-Robinson Velocity for Classical Anharmonic Lattice Systems*. Journal of Statistical Physics, 137(1), 79-108. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-009-9839-5), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.0025)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/raz-2009-estimating-lieb-robinson.pdf), 25 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

The paper proves locality estimates of Lieb-Robinson type for classical systems of oscillators on a lattice, covering the harmonic case and several anharmonic perturbations with long-range interactions. The first-pages text carries the arXiv v1 title, "Lieb-Robinson bounds for classical anharmonic lattice systems".

## Problem & setting

In non-relativistic models a local observable generically spreads over all sites at once, so locality has to be stated as a quasi-locality estimate. The authors recall that Lieb and Robinson obtained such an estimate for quantum spin systems, with a propagation velocity set by the interaction strength, and that an analogous estimate for classical oscillator systems was first given in 1978 by Marchioro and coauthors. The present paper returns to the classical setting, motivated by an earlier Lieb-Robinson result for quantum anharmonic lattices.

## Method

Each site of a finite subset of $\mathbb{Z}^\nu$ carries an oscillator with coordinate and momentum, the Hamiltonian generates a flow on phase space, and observables are complex-valued functions of phase space. The anharmonic estimates are stated for the special class of Weyl functions.

## Key results

The bounds are proved for the harmonic system and for a variety of anharmonic perturbations with long-range interactions. For the anharmonic case the bounds do not depend on the volume or on the initial condition.
