# Hierarchies and the Organization of Knowledge in Production

> [!info] Citation
> Garicano, L. (2000). *Hierarchies and the Organization of Knowledge in Production*. Journal of Political Economy, 108(5), 874-904. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/317671)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper studies how communication allows specialized acquisition of knowledge and argues that a knowledge-based hierarchy is a natural organizational form when matching problems to those who can solve them is costly.

## Problem & setting

In the model described by the abstract, production workers learn the most common or easiest problems, while specialized problem solvers handle the exceptional or harder ones. The abstract states that the model is consistent with stylized facts of organization theory and is used to analyze the effect of changes in production and information technology.
