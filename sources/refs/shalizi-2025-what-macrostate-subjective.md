# What Is a Macrostate? Subjective Observations and Objective Dynamics

> [!info] Citation
> Shalizi, C. R., Moore, C. (2025). *What Is a Macrostate? Subjective Observations and Objective Dynamics*. Foundations of Physics, 55(1), 2. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10701-024-00814-1), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0303625)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/shalizi-2025-what-macrostate-subjective.pdf), 15 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

The paper argues that thermodynamic macrostates are both subjective and objective: they form the unique maximal partition of phase space that is consistent with the chosen observations and obeys a Markov process, and they coincide with the causal states of computational mechanics. The local file is the arXiv version (v1 dated 2003, revised through 2024).

## Problem & setting

The introduction describes a long tension between epistemic readings of entropy and macroscopic variables, as reflections of ignorance, and physical readings, as quantities with their own dynamics and consequences. The authors ask whether macrostates are objective consequences of dynamics or subjective reflections of what an observer can see.

## Method

Starting from a set of observables, the partition of phase space is refined until the resulting states predict their own future, that is, until they are Markovian. The authors review computational mechanics, an information-theoretic method for finding optimal causal models of stochastic processes, to identify these states with causal states. The contents list also covers levels of description, emergence, hydrodynamics and coarse-graining in cellular automata.

## Key results

Macrostates obtained this way are, by the authors' claim, provably optimal statistical predictors of the future values of the observables. Proofs were not read for this note.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper in its related-work discussion of closure, as the statement that causal states, which are maximally predictive and minimal, are macrostates. It uses the paper to position its own closure results, noting that the cited framework has no environment, blanket or bound for approximate closure.
