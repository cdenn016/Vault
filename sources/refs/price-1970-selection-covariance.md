# Selection and Covariance

> [!info] Citation
> Price, G. R. (1970). *Selection and Covariance*. Nature, 227(5257), 520--521. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/227520a0)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A two-page note in Nature expressing the change in a gene frequency as a covariance between offspring number and gene frequency, plus a transmission term.

## Method

According to the manuscript's reading record, which was made from a scan of both pages, the first equation gives the change of frequency $Q$ as $\mathrm{Cov}(z,q)/\bar z$, where $z$ is the number of successful gametes of a parent and $q$ its gene frequency. The derivation leads to a fourth equation with an added term, the offspring-weighted mean of the change from parent to offspring divided by $N\bar z$.

## Key results

The record states that the added term vanishes, except for sampling effects, when meiosis and fertilization are random with respect to the gene. The paper therefore contains both the covariance (selection) term and the transmission term.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the note for the Price decomposition applied to collectives, noting that Price's fourth equation includes the transmission term, which the manuscript's own statement keeps or drops explicitly. The citation supports the manuscript's treatment of selection among collectives and ties to [[Replicator dynamics]], [[Cultural group selection]] and [[Evolutionary game theory and cooperation]].
