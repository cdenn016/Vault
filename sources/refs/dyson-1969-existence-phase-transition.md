## TL;DR

A 1969 paper in Communications in Mathematical Physics on a phase transition in a one-dimensional Ising ferromagnet. The manuscript knows its hierarchical model through a later account.

## Relevance to this research

The collective-agents manuscript cites Dyson's hierarchical model for the fact that a block recursion for a massive Gaussian hierarchical model is exact. It uses this to say that its nested medium of agents renormalizes exactly. According to the reading record, the hierarchical model and its exact Gaussian recursion were read in the account of Jona-Lasinio (2001), Section III, and not in Dyson's paper, so the citation rests on that secondary treatment.
