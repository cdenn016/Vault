# Parcels and particles: Markov blankets in the brain

> [!info] Citation
> Friston, K. J., Fagerholm, E. D., Zarghami, T. S., Parr, T., Hipólito, I., Magrou, L., Razi, A. (2021). *Parcels and particles: Markov blankets in the brain*. Network Neuroscience 5(1), 211-251. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00175)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/friston-2021-parcels-particles-markov.pdf), 57 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A technical note that uses Markov blankets and the renormalization group to argue that much of the phenomenology of network neuroscience is an emergent property of a particular partition of neuronal states over progressively coarser scales.

## Problem & setting

The introduction contrasts two approaches to functional integration in the brain: mechanistic models of directed effective connectivity, and phenomenological descriptions of undirected functional connectivity, intrinsic networks and critical dynamics. The paper treats effective connectivity in a way meant to speak to the emergence of intrinsic networks and critical dynamics.

## Method

Per the abstract, the treatment rests on Markov blankets in the self-organization of systems far from equilibrium and applies the apparatus of the renormalization group to partitions of neuronal states at increasing scales. The extract covers the abstract and the start of the introduction only.

## Key results

The abstract claims a link between dynamics on directed graphs and the phenomenology of intrinsic brain networks. The extract does not reach the quantitative results.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper for constructions that group variables and retain slow modes, and states that in the parts it read they leave open how errors accumulate at finite timescale separation. It also quotes the scale-wise growth of time constants and spatial supports from a targeted extraction of the full text, and lists the retention threshold and treatment of noise correlations between particles as open points.
