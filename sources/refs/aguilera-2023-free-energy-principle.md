# From the free energy principle to a confederation of Bayesian mechanics: Reply to comments on "How particular is the physics of the free energy principle?"

> [!info] Citation
> Aguilera, M., Millidge, B., Tschantz, A., Buckley, C. L. (2023). *From the free energy principle to a confederation of Bayesian mechanics: Reply to comments on "How particular is the physics of the free energy principle?"*. Physics of Life Reviews, 44, 270-275. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2023.01.018)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/aguilera-2023-free-energy-principle.pdf), 12 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

This is the authors' reply to commentaries on their earlier technical critique of one theory of Bayesian mechanics within the free energy principle. It defends the use of concrete models to test the mathematical steps of the theory and sketches a more plural view of the field.

## Problem & setting

The first pages describe the earlier article as a technical deconstruction of the approximate Bayesian inference lemma, together with a modelling exercise in a simple, analytically solvable dissipative nonequilibrium system. They state the two claims at issue: that a statistical boundary, described as a Markov blanket, separates internal from external states, and that the dynamics of the most likely internal states can then be read as following a gradient of a variational free energy while predicting external states.

## Method

The reply responds to the commentaries, argues for modelling exercises as tests of the consistency of abstract derivations, and notes the shift toward formulations on densities of paths. I read only the opening pages of the extract, not the later sections.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this reply together with a companion workshop paper for the exchange over whether Markov blankets certified in the free-energy literature hold in nonequilibrium models, and says it knows that exchange through secondary accounts. I checked the framing against the first pages only. It bears on the vault's treatment of communicating active-inference agents because blanket conditions for collective boundaries rest on the same steady-state assumptions.
