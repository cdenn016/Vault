## TL;DR

A 1990 paper in Physica D on the symbol grounding problem, a problem of how symbols acquire meaning.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper, together with work on joint attention, as a conceptual antecedent for its result that a receiver can learn a sender's code from the co-occurrence of messages with its own observations of the world, which the manuscript calls grounding meaning in shared attention. The manuscript's reading record says only that the abstract was read, and the abstract is not part of the packet, so this note states nothing about the paper's argument. The connection is to the manuscript's account of communication as action and of interpretation levels; see [[Active inference communication and collective boundaries]], [[Generative model]] and [[Theory of Mind]].
