## Reviewed result and scope

Section III, equations (3.4)–(3.7), describes Hermitian congruence and the SL(2,C) double cover of the proper orthochronous Lorentz group; Section V develops polar decomposition. The sign-of-trace assertion near equation (3.5) requires the causal domain. It is not valid as a blanket claim for spacelike Hermitian matrices, and the refactor uses the positive-definite case instead.
