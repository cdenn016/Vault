# The Effect of Aggregation on Prediction in the Autoregressive Model

> [!info] Citation
> Amemiya, T., Wu, R. Y. (1972). *The Effect of Aggregation on Prediction in the Autoregressive Model*. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 67(339), 628-632. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1972.10481264)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper treats temporal aggregation of an autoregressive process of order $p$ and the prediction of the aggregate variable.

## Key results

The abstract states that the non-overlapping moving sum of a $p$th-order autoregression follows a $p$th-order autoregression with at most a $p$th-order moving average of an independent sequence, whatever the summation length. From this aggregate model the authors derive the optimal aggregate predictor and report that it performs well against the optimal disaggregate predictor, by theoretical and numerical analysis.
