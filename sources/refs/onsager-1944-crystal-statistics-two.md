# Crystal Statistics. I. A Two-Dimensional Model with an Order-Disorder Transition

> [!info] Citation
> Onsager, L. (1944). *Crystal Statistics. I. A Two-Dimensional Model with an Order-Disorder Transition*. Physical Review, 65(3-4), 117-149. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.65.117)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper computes the zero-field partition function of the two-dimensional Ising model exactly, and finds an order-disorder transition at a temperature fixed by a condition on the two coupling strengths.

## Method

According to the abstract, the problem for a long strip of width $n$ joined around a cylinder is reduced to an eigenvalue problem solved by direct-product decomposition, and an integral replaces a sum when $n=\infty$. Different couplings along the two lattice directions do not complicate the problem. The Kramers-Wannier dual transformation appears as an automorphism of a quaternion algebra natural to the problem.

## Key results

The abstract gives the transition condition $\sinh(2J/kT_c)\sinh(2J'/kT_c)=1$, a continuous energy, and a specific heat diverging like $-\log|T-T_c|$. For finite-width strips the specific-heat maximum grows linearly in $\log n$. The paper also computes the free energy of a boundary between regions of opposite order.
