No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A three-page 1938 paper on the large-sample distribution of the likelihood ratio for composite hypotheses. The manuscript's record states it is cited for the asymptotic chi-squared law of that statistic in a regular model.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the test of collective agency: the hypothesis sets a block of the conditional cross-covariance to zero, a smooth constraint in a regular model, so the likelihood-ratio statistic converges in law to a chi-squared law with degrees of freedom equal to the product of the two block dimensions. The paper was not read; it is cited from the bibliographic record. See [[Mutual information]], [[MDL and BIC model selection]] and [[Singular statistical models]].
