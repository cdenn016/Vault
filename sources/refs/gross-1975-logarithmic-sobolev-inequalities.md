## TL;DR

A 1975 paper in the American Journal of Mathematics that the manuscript cites for the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper as the standard attribution for the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality, which it uses to express the decay of relative entropy along the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup, and then applies the inequality in the form of a textbook statement by other authors. In the manuscript's reading record the primary text was not read; only the first page (Crossref) and a zbMATH entry were consulted, so the note carries no account of the paper's argument. The use is in the argument that canonical correlations are below one; see [[Canonical Correlation Analysis]] and [[Entropy]].
