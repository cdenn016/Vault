# La relation entre la distance spatiale et la distance lexicale

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A 1971 article in Revue de Linguistique Romane whose title concerns the relation between spatial distance and lexical distance.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the article for the finding, reported through a later review by Nerbonne, that aggregate linguistic variation grows sublinearly with geographic distance. The manuscript's reading record states that the article itself was not opened and that it is known only through the reference list of that review. The record places the first page at 335 and the next article at 358, leaves the last page unverified, and attributes authorship on the strength of a search excerpt of a later paper. The relation to the vault is through dialect spread as a case of belief transmission over space; see [[Opinion dynamics]], [[Conformist transmission]] and [[Cultural evolution and social learning]].
