# Random Walk in a Random Environment and First-Passage Percolation on Trees

> [!info] Citation
> Lyons, R., Pemantle, R. (1992). *Random Walk in a Random Environment and First-Passage Percolation on Trees*. The Annals of Probability, 20(1), 125-136. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176989920), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0404045)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/lyons-1992-random-walk-random.pdf), 11 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper shows that, for certain random environments on an arbitrary infinite locally finite tree, transience or recurrence of a random walk is decided by the branching number of the tree. It also shows that the branching number sets the rate of first-passage percolation on trees.

## Problem & setting

A random walk in a random environment (RWRE) on a tree has random transition probabilities. The authors assume all but finitely many of the ratios $A_\sigma$, each a transition probability from a vertex's parent to the vertex divided by the transition probability from the parent to the grandparent, are identically distributed. By a zero-one law the walk is almost surely transient or almost surely recurrent, and the paper determines the phase-transition boundary.

## Key results

The main theorem generalizes and unifies earlier separate work of the two authors. The paper also reports that the transition point for edge-reinforced random walk is determined by the branching number, and that the branching number determines the rate of first-passage percolation, also called the first-birth problem. The techniques are quasi-Bernoulli percolation and large deviation results.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript uses this paper as a restatement of the 1990 criterion of Lyons for the biased walk on a tree: transient or recurrent according to whether the bias is below or above the branching number. It is cited for the proof that a Gaussian broadcasting condition on trees is decided by the branching number. The reading record says the arXiv rendering was read for that restatement and the definition of the branching number.
