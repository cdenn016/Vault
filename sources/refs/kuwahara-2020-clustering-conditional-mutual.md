# Clustering of Conditional Mutual Information for Quantum Gibbs States above a Threshold Temperature

> [!info] Citation
> Kuwahara, T., Kato, K., Brandão, F. G. S. L. (2020). *Clustering of Conditional Mutual Information for Quantum Gibbs States above a Threshold Temperature*. Physical Review Letters, 124(22), 220601. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.220601), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09425)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/kuwahara-2020-clustering-conditional-mutual.pdf), 24 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper proves that quantum Gibbs states of spin systems above a threshold temperature are approximate quantum Markov networks, with conditional mutual information decaying exponentially with distance for short-range interactions and as a power law for long-range ones.

## Key results

The abstract lists consequences: efficiency of quantum Gibbs sampling, a strong version of the area law, quasilocality of effective Hamiltonians on subsystems, a clustering theorem for mutual information, and a polynomial-time algorithm for classical Gibbs state simulation.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the result in related work, saying the decay length of conditional mutual information is finite for Gibbs states above a threshold temperature and that it imports this length into blanket theory as the Markov length.
