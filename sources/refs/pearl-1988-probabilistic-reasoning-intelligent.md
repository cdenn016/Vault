## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for three points: a variable that reads both sides of a boundary in one round is a common child of interior and exterior, so conditioning on it couples them; the exactness of belief propagation on trees, cited for attribution; and d-separation in the time-unrolled network. The cavity construction is related to channel filters in decentralized fusion. See [[Belief Propagation]], [[Markov Blanket]] and [[Decentralized Bayesian inference]].
