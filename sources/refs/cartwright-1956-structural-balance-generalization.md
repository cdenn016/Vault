## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper for the two-camp partition of a balanced signed graph, which it uses as the simplest example of an inconsistency cut. In the coherence section, balance of signed graphs (with gains of plus or minus one) is the special case of a more general balance test on gain graphs, and the partition into two camps is the cut that separates inconsistent edges. The connection is to [[Opinion dynamics]], [[Sociophysics]] and [[Graph synchronization and connection Laplacians]].
