## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for Kolmogorov's criterion for reversibility of a Markov chain, in an appendix on agency. It uses the easy direction: a reversible chain gives each closed path the same probability in both directions, so the manuscript's pairwise loop quantities through the null policies vanish.
