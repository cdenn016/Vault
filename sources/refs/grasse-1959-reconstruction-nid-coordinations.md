## TL;DR

A 1959 article in Insectes Sociaux on nest reconstruction and coordination among individuals in two termite taxa, whose title announces a theory of stigmergy as an interpretation of the behavior of building termites. The manuscript's reading record gives it as the origin of the term stigmergy.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the idea of an agent writing into a record that others read later, as termites build on earlier work, and for the attribution of the word stigmergy. It treats such records as one mode of a transfer kernel that also covers direct and performative channels. Only the bibliographic record was checked.
