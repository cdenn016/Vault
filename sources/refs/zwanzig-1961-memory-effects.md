# Memory Effects in Irreversible Thermodynamics

## Citation and archived version

Robert Zwanzig (1961). *Memory Effects in Irreversible Thermodynamics*. Physical Review 124(4), 983–992. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.124.983). Publisher PDF, 10 pages. [Local primary text](../pdfs/zwanzig-1961-memory-effects.pdf). SHA-256: `cceeecb6ad7aea1d3086ee83a3915204afe91a9be37fcea51974d15a7827edbc`.

## Reviewed result and scope

The paper derives a non-Markov kinetic equation after projecting microscopic dynamics to macroscopic variables. Its memory terms are time convolutions, and an instantaneous Onsager description is a short-memory approximation. The treatment specifies initially constrained equilibrium preparation and excludes time-dependent external forcing. These hypotheses are part of the source, not general facts about every reduced agent process.
