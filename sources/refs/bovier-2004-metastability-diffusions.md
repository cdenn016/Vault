# Metastability in Reversible Diffusion Processes I: Sharp Asymptotics for Capacities and Exit Times

## Citation and archived version

Journal of the European Mathematical Society 6(4), 399–424. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/14). Official EMS journal PDF, 26 pages. [Local primary text](../pdfs/bovier-2004-metastability-diffusions.pdf). SHA-256: `2d015e365407e98cef1bb819f6018536baaa0c32f917b154483cd751de84f9f3`.

## Reviewed result and scope

The paper uses potential theory to obtain sharp small-noise capacity and exit-time asymptotics for reversible diffusions with a smooth landscape and controlled wells and saddles. Applying its conclusions to a new process requires matching the diffusion, landscape, and nondegeneracy assumptions. A long lifetime is distinct from a slow deterministic local relaxation rate.
