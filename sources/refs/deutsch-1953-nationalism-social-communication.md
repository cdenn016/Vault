# Nationalism and Social Communication: An Inquiry into the Foundations of Nationality

> [!info] Citation
> Deutsch, K. W. (1953). *Nationalism and Social Communication: An Inquiry into the Foundations of Nationality*. Technology Press of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and John Wiley, Cambridge, MA and New York. Second edition, MIT Press, 1966.

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A book on the foundations of nationality, whose title ties nationalism to social communication.

## Scope

The evidence available here shows only the title, the publisher record and a chapter-level reading record. The manuscript's reading record is not consistent about how much of the book was read: one entry says the book was not read and another says chapter 4 of the 1966 second edition was read in OCR. No chapter content is described here.

## Relevance to this research

The Collective Agents manuscript cites the book in three places: for the thesis that a people is defined by the complementarity of its members' communication, as a precedent for communication-based tests of collective membership, and, for part of a scale result, as a formal counterpart of the thesis that nations are defined by the reach of social communication, which the manuscript labels an interpretation. It connects to [[Active inference communication and collective boundaries]], [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]] and [[Community detection and modularity]].
