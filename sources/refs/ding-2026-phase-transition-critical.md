# A phase transition and critical phenomenon for the two-dimensional random field Ising model

> [!info] Citation
> Ding, J., Huang, F., Xia, A. (2026). *A phase transition and critical phenomenon for the two-dimensional random field Ising model*. Journal of the European Mathematical Society (volume and pages not yet assigned in the Crossref record). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1793); preprint arXiv:2310.12141

[Local PDF](../pdfs/ding-2026-phase-transition-critical.pdf), 65 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A mathematical study of the two-dimensional random field Ising model in a box of side $N$ with Gaussian field of variance $\epsilon^2$, giving a phase transition in the boundary influence at the critical temperature and the behavior of the correlation length below it.

## Problem & setting

The boundary influence is the difference between spin averages at the center of the box under plus and minus boundary conditions. The paper studies how disorder changes its decay.

## Method

The extract read covers the abstract and the definition of the model and Gibbs measure at temperature $T$. The proof method is not described there.

## Key results

At $T = T_c$, for $\epsilon \ll N^{-7/8}$ the boundary influence decays as $N^{-1/8}$, and for $\epsilon \gg N^{-7/8}$ it has an extra factor $e^{-\Theta(\epsilon^{8/7}N)}$. As $\epsilon \downarrow 0$ the correlation length goes from $\Theta(\epsilon^{-8/7})$ at $T_c$ to $e^{\Theta(\epsilon^{-4/3})}$ for $T < T_c$.
