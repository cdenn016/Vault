# Spreading of Sets in Product Spaces and Hypercontraction of the Markov Operator

> [!info] Citation
> Ahlswede, R., Gács, P. (1976). *Spreading of Sets in Product Spaces and Hypercontraction of the Markov Operator*. The Annals of Probability, 4(6), 925-939. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176995937)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper studies, for i.i.d. pairs of finite-range random variables with positive joint distribution, how large a set $\mathscr{B}$ must be when the conditional probability of landing in it, given that the first coordinate lies in $\mathscr{A}$, exceeds a constant. It shows that if the probability of $\mathscr{B}$ tends to zero, that of $\mathscr{A}$ tends to zero faster.

## Problem & setting

The abstract poses an information-theoretic question about subsets of product spaces for pairs $(X,Y)$ with $X=(X_1,\dots,X_n)$ and $Y=(Y_1,\dots,Y_n)$. It states that generalizations and some exact exponent estimates are given and that the method connects to hypercontraction.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper as the origin of strong data-processing inequalities, the family of results that governs how much information survives a noisy channel. It uses a contraction coefficient it says is known in substance from later work, and a tree-broadcast condition it proves itself. The note rests on the abstract and bibliographic record, so it does not describe the paper's theorems beyond that.
