# Critical Exponents for Long-Range Interactions

> [!info] Citation
> Fisher, M. E., Ma, S., Nickel, B. G. (1972). *Critical Exponents for Long-Range Interactions*. Physical Review Letters 29(14), 917-920. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.29.917)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A renormalization-group derivation of critical exponents for $d$-dimensional systems with an $n$-component order parameter and attractive interactions decaying as $r^{-(d+\sigma)}$.

## Method

By the abstract, the exponents are obtained as power series in $\epsilon = 2\sigma - d$ (for fixed $\sigma \neq 2$) or in $\Delta\sigma = \sigma - d/2$ (for fixed $d$), and separately to order $1/n$ for all $d$ and $\sigma \neq 2$.

## Key results

According to the abstract, for $\epsilon < 0$ the exponents take fixed classical values. At $\epsilon = \Delta\sigma = 0$ fractional powers of a logarithm of the reduced temperature appear, and for $\sigma > 2$ the exponents take their short-range values.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the letter for attribution of the long-range Gaussian fixed point: the exponent of the leak under long-range couplings is the thermal eigenvalue $\sigma$ of that fixed point. Its proof does not use the letter. The reading record says only the record and abstract were read; the values $\nu = 1/\sigma$, $\eta = 2 - \sigma$ and thermal eigenvalue $\sigma$ for $\sigma < d/2$ were taken from a later paper that attributes them to this letter.
