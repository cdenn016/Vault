## TL;DR

A 1965 paper in Acta Scientiarum Mathematicarum (Szeged) on convergence of random products of contractions in Hilbert space, as its title states.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper, with an earlier one by Prager and Halperin-type results, for the statement that in finite dimension products of projections in any order converge when each subspace recurs infinitely often, with no uniform rate when the gaps between recurrences grow. The reading record says only secondary sources were read, so the claim is the manuscript's attribution and I did not verify it. It supports the scale analysis of repeated projections. See [[Fixed-point iteration]], [[Fixed Points]] and [[Coarse Graining]].
