## TL;DR

A 1914 paper in the Amsterdam Academy Proceedings whose title concerns accidental density deviations and opalescence at the critical point of a single substance.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper as the source of the Ornstein-Zernike form of correlation decay: on $\mathbb Z^D$ the covariance decays at least at the Ornstein-Zernike rate, with prefactor $r^{-(D-1)/2}e^{-m(\mathbf u)r}$ in the full form, and along an axis the rate $m$ satisfies $\cosh m=1+\varepsilon/(2a)$, giving a correlation length of about $\sqrt{a/\varepsilon}$ hops for a small leak.
