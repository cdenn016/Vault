## TL;DR

A 1989 paper on biased graphs, treating bias, balance and gains, as its title and venue establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the balance test of gain graphs on a fundamental system of cycles, which with gains of plus or minus one reduces to the balance of signed graphs. It uses this to describe frustration as a cycle condition, related to the product of signs around a plaquette and to agreement as a global section of a discourse sheaf. The manuscript read the paper only through later restatements and the record.
