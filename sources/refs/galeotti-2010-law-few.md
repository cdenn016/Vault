# The Law of the Few

> [!info] Citation
> Galeotti, A., Goyal, S. (2010). *The Law of the Few*. American Economic Review, 100(4), 1468-1492. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.100.4.1468)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper starts from the empirical finding that most individuals get most of their information from a small group of influencers who differ little in observable traits, and builds a model in which that pattern arises in equilibrium.

## Problem & setting

Players in the model acquire information personally and form connections to access the information of others. The abstract states that every robust equilibrium of the model exhibits the law of the few.
