# Long range estimates for Markov chains

> [!info] Citation
> Varopoulos, N. Th. (1985). *Long range estimates for Markov chains*. Bulletin des Sciences Mathématiques, Deuxième Série, 109, 225-252.

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A 1985 paper on long-range estimates for Markov chains. The manuscript's record, taken from a zbMATH review, describes a bound on the transition probability of a reversible chain between two states, of the form $C n^{3/4}\exp(-d(i,j)^2/(Cn))$ in the distance $d(i,j)$ and the number of steps $n$.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this estimate, with Carne's proof by a Chebyshev-polynomial argument, for the diffusive spread of influence when weights are dominated by a reversible chain. It states that it knew the estimate only from a review, did not read the paper, and proves the form it uses in full. See [[Bundle scale space]], [[Graph Laplacian]] and [[Spectral graph theory]].
