## TL;DR

A book first published in 1962 by Clarendon Press, Oxford; the manuscript's record is a library catalog entry and the text itself was not read.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for performative acts, utterances that change what the world and the other agents' actions lead to, such as a promise or a transfer. It contrasts them with the fixed, honest content of the earlier model, which had no effect on the world. This connects the communication model to action on the environment rather than reporting only, and to [[Active inference communication and collective boundaries]], [[Collective active inference]] and [[Niche Construction]].
