# Random-Field Instability of the Ordered State of Continuous Symmetry

> [!info] Citation
> Imry, Y., Ma, S. (1975). *Random-Field Instability of the Ordered State of Continuous Symmetry*. Physical Review Letters, 35(21), 1399-1401. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.35.1399)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The letter considers phase transitions where the field conjugate to the order parameter is static and random, and shows that for a continuous symmetry the ordered state is unstable to an arbitrarily weak random field below four dimensions.

## Problem & setting

Systems with a quenched random field coupled to an order parameter that has continuous symmetry.

## Key results

The abstract states that the ordered state is unstable against an arbitrarily weak random field in fewer than four dimensions, and that the borderline dimensionality above which mean-field results hold is six. The argument is a domain argument, as the manuscript describes it.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites it as the source of the Imry-Ma scaling, which it says its one-dimensional domain law for conventions on rings confirms in one dimension; higher-dimensional forms are left as conjectures. It also lists it among the random-field results collected for comparison with disorder in coherence and synchronization.
