## TL;DR

A 1966 article in Regelungstechnik whose title concerns constructing linear state-variable models from input/output functions.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites it for minimal-realization theory: the least dimension of a linear memory that reproduces a given input-output memory equals the rank of an observability or block Hankel matrix. It also credits the origin of minimal realization to this work when comparing with memory-kernel and aggregation results. The content is attributed from the title and the manuscript's use; the paper was not read. See [[State space models]], [[Kalman filter]] and [[Coarse Graining]].
