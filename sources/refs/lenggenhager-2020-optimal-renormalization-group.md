# Optimal Renormalization Group Transformation from Information Theory

> [!info] Citation
> Lenggenhager, P. M., Gökmen, D. E., Ringel, Z., Huber, S. D., Koch-Janusz, M. (2020). *Optimal Renormalization Group Transformation from Information Theory*. Physical Review X, 10(1), 011037. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.10.011037)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/lenggenhager-2020-optimal-renormalization-group.pdf), 27 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages and abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper studies the information-theoretic properties of real-space coarse-graining. It proves that a perfect coarse-graining that maximizes real-space mutual information does not increase the range of interactions in the renormalized Hamiltonian, and for disordered systems suppresses correlations generated in the renormalized disorder distribution.

## Problem & setting

A real-space renormalization group algorithm had been introduced that finds the relevant low-energy degrees of freedom by maximizing the real-space mutual information (RSMI) between the coarse-grained variables and the environment. The authors ask what information-theoretic properties such coarse-grainings have, for translationally invariant and disordered systems.

## Key results

The two statements above are proved. The authors also verify empirically, on arbitrary coarse-grainings of the clean and random Ising chain, that the measures of complexity decay as the information retained by the coarse-graining grows. A final part treats constraints on the number and type of coarse-grained degrees of freedom.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript names this paper as a precedent for information-theoretic renormalization, to be checked before a sensing result is called new, and as giving conditional results not yet extended to controlled adaptive processes.
