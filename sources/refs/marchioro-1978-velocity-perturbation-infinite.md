## TL;DR

A 1978 article in the Journal of Statistical Physics on the velocity of a perturbation in infinite lattice systems.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the article, with Lieb and Robinson and later work, as a classical-lattice counterpart of Lieb-Robinson bounds on how fast a local perturbation spreads, in connection with its own discrete-time bounds on the spread of influence in message passing. The manuscript lists it among primary texts known only from records, abstracts or secondary accounts, to be read before results are cited from it. Related pages are [[Belief dynamics]] and [[Coarse Graining]]. A later study of the same question for classical lattices is [[metivier-2014-spreading-perturbations-long]].
