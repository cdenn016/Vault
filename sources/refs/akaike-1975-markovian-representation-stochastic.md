# Markovian Representation of Stochastic Processes by Canonical Variables

> [!info] Citation
> Akaike, H. (1975). *Markovian Representation of Stochastic Processes by Canonical Variables*. SIAM Journal on Control, 13(1), 162-173. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0313010)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

The paper analyzes the information interface between the future and the past of a discrete-time stochastic process by canonical correlation analysis, and obtains two extreme Markovian representations whose states are the canonical variables of the past projected on the future and of the future projected on the past.

## Key results

According to the abstract, the result clarifies the probabilistic structure of the Faurre realization algorithm, and an extension gives the Ho-Kalman algorithm a stochastic interpretation.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for attribution of the canonical-variable construction of Markovian representations, in its treatment of predictive information and of what an agent's sensing of a time series must retain.
