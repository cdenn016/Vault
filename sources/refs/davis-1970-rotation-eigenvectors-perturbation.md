# The Rotation of Eigenvectors by a Perturbation. III

> [!info] Citation
> Davis, C., Kahan, W. M. (1970). *The Rotation of Eigenvectors by a Perturbation. III*. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis, 7(1), 1-46. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0707001)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A study of how far the invariant subspaces of a Hermitian operator can move under a small perturbation, characterized through angles between subspaces and bounded using an eigenvalue gap.

## Problem & setting

Two related questions are treated: how much invariant subspaces change under a perturbation, and how far the span of approximate eigenvectors of a real symmetric matrix can be from the true eigenspace, given an estimate of the gap separating the eigenvalue cluster from the rest.

## Method

The difference between two subspaces is described by the angles through which one must be rotated to reach the other. The abstract says these angles unify geometric, operator-theoretic and error-analytic questions.

## Key results

Sharp bounds on trigonometric functions of the angles are obtained from the gap and from a bound on either the perturbation or a computable residual. An example is included.
