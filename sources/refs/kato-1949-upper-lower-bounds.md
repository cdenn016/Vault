## TL;DR

A 1949 article by Tosio Kato in the Journal of the Physical Society of Japan on upper and lower bounds of eigenvalues.

## Relevance to this research

The collective-agents manuscript cites this paper for the attribution of the Kato-Temple inequality, together with Temple (1928). The manuscript proves that inequality in full in an appendix on collectives, so the citation records priority and does not supply a result used from the paper. The reference was known only from its bibliographic record. The inequality bounds eigenvalues of the population Laplacian, which connects to [[Graph Laplacian]] and [[Spectral graph theory]] in the vault's treatment of collectives as connected populations.
