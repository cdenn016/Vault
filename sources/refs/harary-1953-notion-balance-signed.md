## TL;DR

A 1953 paper in the Michigan Mathematical Journal on the notion of balance of a signed graph, as its title and venue establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the paper for balance of signed graphs, which it presents as the case of gain graphs with gains $\pm 1$, and for the statement that frustration of a cycle is the same condition as balance on cycles. The manuscript's reading record lists only the Crossref record, so the paper's text was not read and this note makes no claim about its results. The link is to the manuscript's coherence analysis, where agreement is a global section and balance is a cycle condition; see [[Cellular sheaves and sheaf Laplacians]], [[Graph synchronization and connection Laplacians]] and [[Spectral graph theory]].
