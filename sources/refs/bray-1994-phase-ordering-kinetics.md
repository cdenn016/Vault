# Theory of phase-ordering kinetics

> [!info] Citation
> Bray, A. J. (1994). *Theory of phase-ordering kinetics*. Advances in Physics, 43(3), 357-459. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00018739400101505); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9501089)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/bray-1994-phase-ordering-kinetics.pdf), 85 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A review of phase-ordering dynamics, the growth of order by domain coarsening after a quench into a broken-symmetry phase, centered on the scaling regime at long times and on topological defects.

## Problem & setting

The abstract poses how to determine the growth laws for the characteristic length scales and what can be said about the associated scaling functions. It gives particular attention to vector and tensor order parameters, for example in nematic liquid crystals, and uses topological defects (domain walls, vortices, strings, monopoles) as a unifying framework. The contents page lists the scaling hypothesis, domain walls, and the nonconserved Allen-Cahn and conserved-field cases among the early sections.
