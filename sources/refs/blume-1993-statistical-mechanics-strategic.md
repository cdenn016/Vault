# The Statistical Mechanics of Strategic Interaction

> [!info] Citation
> Blume, L. E. (1993). *The Statistical Mechanics of Strategic Interaction*. Games and Economic Behavior, 5(3), 387-424. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/game.1993.1023)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A study of strategic interaction among players on a lattice, each interacting directly with finitely many neighbors, under stochastic strategy revision processes.

## Problem & setting

According to the abstract, any two players interact indirectly through a finite chain of direct interactions. The revision processes considered include myopic best response and stochastic choice.

## Key results

The abstract states that stationary distributions are partially characterized, and that the asymptotic behavior of stochastic choice for near-best-response rules is related to equilibrium selection in symmetric $2\times2$ and $n\times n$ coordination games.

## Relevance to this research

The collective-agents manuscript attributes to this paper the Gibbs form of the stationary law of asynchronous logit revision in potential games, which it uses in its treatment of agency and coherence. The reading record says the paper was known from the record and from secondary accounts of the Gibbs-law statement, so the attribution rests on those accounts and not on a reading of the article. The link to the vault is the use of Gibbs laws as the stationary behavior of interacting decision makers, see [[Evolutionary game theory and cooperation]], [[Bounded Rationality]] and [[Multi-agent variational free energy]].
