## Reviewed result and scope

Sections 1–3 develop supervised structure learning through candidate augmentations of discrete generative models and model selection with specified priors. Expected-free-energy model priors and supplied candidate state, path, and factor additions are explicit ingredients. The source does not establish that ordinary VFE alone chooses a unique ontology or hierarchy without those ingredients.

## Relevance to this research

Addresses the historical precedent for model structure learning while retaining its input assumptions. PIFB2 uses it to distinguish learning within a model family from deriving that family. See [[Generative model]] and [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]]. The refactor context is [[PIFB2 refactor foundations September 2026]].
