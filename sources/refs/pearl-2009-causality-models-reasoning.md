## TL;DR

A monograph, in its second edition of 2009, presenting the probabilistic, manipulative, counterfactual and structural approaches to causation as a mathematical theory with tools relating causal connections to statistical associations, according to the publisher's description.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for the distinction between intervention and observation: performative acts coordinate by changing what other agents' actions lead to, informative messages by changing what the others believe, and treating one as the other confuses intervention with observation. The manuscript's reading record shows only a Crossref record was consulted.
