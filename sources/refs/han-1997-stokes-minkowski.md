## Reviewed result and scope

Sections II–IV relate classical polarization coherency matrices and Stokes parameters to rotations, boosts, and the Minkowski quadratic form. Complex two-component classical data can therefore support this representation without being quantum amplitudes. The representation is standard Lorentz-group algebra; the source does not derive physical spacetime from inference.
