## TL;DR

A 1989 note in Statistics & Probability Letters giving a proof of the Markov chain tree theorem, as its title states.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the statement of the Markov chain tree theorem: the stationary weight of a state is a sum over spanning trees directed toward it of products of transition probabilities. The proof of a tree bound on stationary probabilities of agent profiles uses that statement. The reading record says the paper itself was not read. See [[Collective active inference]] and [[Nonequilibrium steady state]].
