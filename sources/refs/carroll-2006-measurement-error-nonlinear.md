## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book for regression dilution, the attenuation of a regression coefficient when the regressor is observed with error, and for its repair by regression calibration. The citation appears in the framework section where the manuscript treats a receiver's reading of a sender's noisy report as a regression on an error-prone quantity, which ties it to [[Communication-constrained inference]] and [[Active inference communication and collective boundaries]].
