## Reviewed result and scope

Section 8, equation (17), and Appendix A.1 formulate parameter learning through posterior factorization and accumulated sufficient statistics in discrete-state active inference. Hidden-state expected counts require the stated variational factorization. They should not be conflated with the exact Dirichlet update for directly observed categories.

## Relevance to this research

Shows that fast state inference and parameter learning can already be described within probabilistic updating. It does not prove the spontaneous appearance of a smooth slow-model subbundle. See [[Generative model]], [[Variational free energy]], and [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]]. The refactor context is [[PIFB2 refactor foundations September 2026]].
