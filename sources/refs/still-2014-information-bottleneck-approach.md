# Information Bottleneck Approach to Predictive Inference

> [!info] Citation
> Still, S. (2014). *Information Bottleneck Approach to Predictive Inference*. Entropy, 16(2), 968-989. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/e16020968)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

A synthesis of work on automated predictive model building that is motivated by rate-distortion theory and the information bottleneck method. It reads predictive inference as a strategy for efficient communication and discusses a link to thermodynamic efficiency.

## Problem & setting

The abstract frames predictive model making as a communication problem and states the aim of presenting the information-theoretic approach as one overarching framework for predictive inference.

## Method

The paper applies the information bottleneck method, which descends from rate-distortion theory, to predictive inference. The evidence available here does not describe the formal details.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper as a treatment of predictive inference by the bottleneck method, next to the Gaussian bottleneck and the past-future bottleneck, in its related-work discussion of compressed representations. The manuscript knew the paper only from its abstract and bibliographic record. The connection to the vault is the view of inference as constrained communication; see [[Information bottleneck]] and [[Communication-constrained inference]].
