# Gravity with Gravitas: A Solution to the Border Puzzle

> [!info] Citation
> Anderson, J. E., van Wincoop, E. (2003). *Gravity with Gravitas: A Solution to the Border Puzzle*. American Economic Review, 93(1), 170-192. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/000282803321455214)

No local copy: no open-access file passed validation on November 10, 2026. Reading basis: abstract.

## TL;DR

The paper argues that commonly estimated gravity equations for trade flows lack a theoretical foundation, develops a method that estimates a theoretical gravity equation and computes the comparative statics of trade frictions, and applies it to the McCallum border puzzle.

## Key results

The abstract reports that national borders reduce trade between industrialized countries by 20 to 50 percent, a moderate effect.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the estimate that borders reduce trade between industrialized countries by 20 to 50 percent, as evidence that boundaries between collectives carry a measurable cost to interaction. The same passage cites McCallum and Engel as known through secondary reports. It enters the discussion of nations and borders as abstract collectives.
