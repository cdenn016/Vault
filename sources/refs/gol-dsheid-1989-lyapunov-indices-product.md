## TL;DR

A survey-length article on Lyapunov indices of products of random matrices. The record gives only a table of contents, which lists the multiplicative ergodic theorem, quasi-projective transformations, a contraction property of a semigroup, a fundamental theorem on Lyapunov indices, and an example for a difference matrix Schrödinger equation.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper, with a work of Guivarc'h and Raugi, for the statement that the top Lyapunov exponent is strictly larger than the second for invertible maps whose generated semigroup is strongly irreducible and contracting. It states that these results are cited at the level of their records and restatements, so the paper was known only from its record. The use is in the discussion of scale and correlation lengths; see [[Coarse Graining]] and [[Renormalization group flow]].
