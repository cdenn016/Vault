# Linear lattice gauge theory

> [!info] Citation
> Christof Wetterich (2014). *Linear lattice gauge theory*. Nuclear Physics B 884, 44–65. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2014.04.002). [Primary text, version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/1307.0722v2).

## Setting and contribution

The link variables are unrestricted real or complex matrices rather than group elements. Section II specifies their endpoint transformation law under compact site gauge groups, together with a link potential and plaquette interactions. Singular matrices are admitted. The paper investigates the relationship to conventional lattice gauge theory within its stated parameter regimes.

## Relevance to this research

This is a precedent for treating rank-deficient communication operators as matrix link fields. It does not establish a GL(K)-invariant attention action or transfer its continuum conclusions to the agent model. The manuscript-specific construction and its limits are recorded in [[dennis-2026-glk-matrix-links-2026-09-20]] and synthesized in [[Lattice gauge theory]], [[GL(K) gauge-equivariant attention]], and [[Gauge equivariance and geometric deep learning]].
