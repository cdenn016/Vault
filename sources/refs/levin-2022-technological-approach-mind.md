# Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere: An Experimentally-Grounded Framework for Understanding Diverse Bodies and Minds

> [!info] Citation
> Levin, M. (2022). *Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere: An Experimentally-Grounded Framework for Understanding Diverse Bodies and Minds*. Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience, 16, 768201. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/levin-2022-technological-approach-mind.pdf), 80 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages and manuscript reading record.

## TL;DR

A Perspective introducing TAME, the Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere, a framework for understanding and manipulating cognition in unconventional substrates, treating agency as a continuous rather than binary property.

## Problem & setting

Synthetic biology and bioengineering allow embodied cognitive systems built from evolved and designed parts. The author argues this unsettles familiar notions in the philosophy of mind and calls for ways to compare diverse intelligences whose composition and origin differ from natural model species.

## Method

The framework is conceptual and draws on experimental work in regeneration and development. It treats morphogenesis as basal cognition, holds that problem-solving in anatomical, physiological, transcriptional and behavioral spaces shares a deep symmetry, and names developmental bioelectricity, via ion channels and gap junctions, as a medium by which active subunits are joined into larger agents.

## Key results

This is a perspective article, so the contribution is a framework and a set of hypotheses about how cognitive capacities can scale in evolution, not a theorem or a single measured effect.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper, with a related paper by Fields and others, as an account of cellular collectives that act in morphospace and other problem spaces and treat a collective as an agent with its own external states. The manuscript's point is that such accounts do not give conditions under which the collective's states have the properties of an agent.
