## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites the book, together with Benaim (1999), only to name the ordinary-differential-equation method of stochastic approximation. The name is attached to the passage from a stochastic recursion to a deterministic flow as the step size goes to zero, which the manuscript leaves as an open problem in its formation section. No theorem of the book was read or used. The record confirms the bibliographic details against Crossref and that the first edition is the one cited.
