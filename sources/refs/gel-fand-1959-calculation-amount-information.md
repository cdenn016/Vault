## TL;DR

A translated article, from a Russian original of 1957, on the amount of information that one random function contains about another, as its title and venue establish.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript attributes to this paper a formula for an information rate between jointly stationary Gaussian processes, expressed through the average over frequencies of the logarithm of one minus the squared coherence. According to the reading record the paper itself was not read: the attribution comes through a secondary source, and only the zbMATH record was checked.
