# Correlation length of the two-dimensional random field Ising model via greedy lattice animal

> [!info] Citation
> Ding, J., Wirth, M. (2023). *Correlation length of the two-dimensional random field Ising model via greedy lattice animal*. Duke Mathematical Journal, 172(9), 1781-1811. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2022-0077); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.08768)

[Local PDF](../pdfs/ding-2023-correlation-length-two.pdf), 51 pages; retrieved November 10, 2026. Reading basis: local PDF, first pages.

## TL;DR

A mathematical result on the correlation length of the two-dimensional random field Ising model with i.i.d. Gaussian fields of variance $\varepsilon^2$.

## Problem & setting

The correlation length is defined as the critical box size at which the influences of the random field and of the boundary condition on the spin magnetization are comparable. The introduction defines the model with plus or minus boundary conditions and a Gibbs measure at inverse temperature $\beta$, and measures boundary influence by half the expected difference of the central spin under the two conditions.

## Method

The title names greedy lattice animals as the technique. The extract read does not go further into the method.

## Key results

As $\varepsilon \to 0$ the correlation length scales as $e^{\Theta(\varepsilon^{-4/3})}$ at zero temperature, and the upper bound holds at all positive temperatures.

## Relevance to this research

The Collective Agents manuscript uses this result in its coherence section, identifying the domain size of consistent conventions on a two-dimensional population with the correlation length, so that the log of the domain size scales as the coupling-to-noise ratio to the power $4/3$.
