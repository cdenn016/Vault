## TL;DR

A 1978 article in Mathematical Programming on approximations for maximizing submodular set functions, first of a pair as the title indicates.

## Relevance to this research

The manuscript cites this paper for the classical guarantee on greedy maximization of monotone submodular functions, in the form of a bound of the type $(1-1/\kappa)^\kappa\le e^{-1}$ on the shortfall, used in its section on misreading and attention. It uses the guarantee to say that literal attention is given a guarantee that the true value does not support. The manuscript's record states that only the bibliographic record was read, so the content of the cited bound is the manuscript's attribution and not something checked here. The context is selective attention over messages, see [[Attention Mechanism]] and [[Bounded Rationality]].
