## Verified content

Title and bibliographic metadata only: the paper proposes common fate, similarity, and other indices of the degree to which an aggregate of persons is a social entity. Terms attributed to it in secondary sources (for example "entitativity" and a "proximity" index) were not verified and are not asserted here.

## Relevance to this research

On the reading suggested by the title alone, group-ness is graded and measured by indices rather than a yes-or-no event, which fits the view that a collective has no single moment of formation. "Common fate," members moving together, would be the operational counterpart of the slow shared coordinates a parent keeps in [[multiagentelbo-2026-09-28-early-parent]]. Nothing in the verified material addresses what is discarded or on what timescale. The mapping is interpretive and unverified from text. See [[Soft formation and the activation clock]].
