## Contribution and scope

This proposed reconstruction starts with time symmetry, decompositional equivalence, small observers, reliable classical memories, and shared observables. A minimal observer is a functional architecture that recognizes and reports definite outcomes. The paper argues that finite observational resources constrain replication and compatibility, then states a Born-rule result as equality between outcome probability and the fraction of environmental configurations assigned to that outcome. Its proof assumes representative sorting and unbiased sampling, and the following section motivates the usual Hilbert-space formalism as suitable for these requirements. The construction is therefore a proposed physical interpretation with nontrivial sampling and observer assumptions. A frequency-counting identity alone does not derive the squared-amplitude law or uniquely select quantum theory from all alternatives.
