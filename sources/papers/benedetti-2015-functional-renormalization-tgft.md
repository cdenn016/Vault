# Functional Renormalisation Group Approach for Tensorial Group Field Theory: a Rank-3 Model

> [!info] Citation
> Benedetti, Dario; Ben Geloun, Joseph; Oriti, Daniele (2015). *Functional Renormalisation Group Approach for Tensorial Group Field Theory: a Rank-3 Model*. [Retained arXiv 1411.3180v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.3180v2). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP03(2015)084).

## Source result and scope

The paper develops the Wetterich functional-RG formalism and studies a real rank-three U(1)^3 tensorial model with a linear kinetic term and a quartic truncation. Its compact group radius leaves an additional scale, giving a nonautonomous projected flow.
