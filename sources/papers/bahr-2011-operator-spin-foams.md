# Operator Spin Foams: holonomy formulation and coarse graining

> [!info] Citation
> Bahr, Benjamin (2011). *Operator Spin Foams: holonomy formulation and coarse graining*. [Retained arXiv 1112.3567v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.3567v1). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/360/1/012042).

## Source result and scope

The operator-spin-foam holonomy representation uses ordered link products to compare refined and coarse complexes. The paper explicitly conditions its positive-measure interpretation on positive weights and allows enlarged nonlocal interaction families.
