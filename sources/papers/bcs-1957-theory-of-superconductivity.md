# Theory of Superconductivity

> [!info] Citation
> John Bardeen, Leon N. Cooper, and John R. Schrieffer (1957). "Theory of Superconductivity."
> *Physical Review* 108(5):1175–1204. doi:
> [10.1103/PhysRev.108.1175](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.108.1175). (Pair binding itself:
> L. N. Cooper, "Bound Electron Pairs in a Degenerate Fermi Gas," *Phys. Rev.* 104:1189, 1956.)

## TL;DR

The microscopic theory of superconductivity: a weak phonon-mediated attraction makes the Fermi
sea unstable to the formation of bound electron pairs (Cooper pairs), whose condensate is
described by a coherent variational ground state. The paired state opens a gap, and the
effective low-energy theory is written in terms of the pair field alone — conventional pairing
is spin-singlet ($S = 0$; the two-electron spin space $2 \otimes 2 = 1 \oplus 3$ decomposes
into singlet and triplet sectors, and triplet pairing is the unconventional case).
