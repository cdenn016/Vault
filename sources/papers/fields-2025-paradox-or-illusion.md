# Paradox or illusion?

> [!info] Citation
> Fields, C. (2025) Paradox or illusion? Physics of Life Reviews 53: 310-311 (doi: 10.1016/j.plrev.2025.04.009).

[Local full text](../pdfs/fields-2025-paradox-or-illusion.pdf). [Primary source](https://chrisfieldsresearch.com/battaglia-comment.pdf).

## Reading basis

Author manuscript of the 2025 commentary. Read PDF pages 1–2 (commentary body). This is a targeted ingestion reading, not a full proof audit.

## Contribution and scope

Fields challenges the premise that introspection and perception provide independent kinds of evidence about consciousness. He describes both through a hierarchy of interacting active-inference systems: an organism, a neural subsystem, and its components have different accessible environments and different outputs. On this account, noticing a thought and noticing a spoken sentence are different ways of encountering the output of related inferential processes. The proposed difference between evidence about self and other concerns the source of input and its interpretation, rather than a separate epistemic mechanism. The argument is conceptual and relies on an active-inference interpretation of the hierarchy; it contains no experiment distinguishing the proposed explanation from alternatives.

## Relevance to this research

For [[Consciousness and Active Inference]], the paper cautions against granting an agent privileged access to its own implementation merely because its records refer to itself. For [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]], it reinforces the need to specify receiver-accessible observations separately at each level. It does not prove that subjective reports, component states, and whole-agent states share one posterior or one variational objective.
