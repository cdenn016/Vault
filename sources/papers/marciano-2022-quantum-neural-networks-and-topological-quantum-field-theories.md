## Contribution and scope

The paper develops topological quantum neural networks whose states are supported on spin networks and whose processing is represented by TQFT amplitudes. It distinguishes topological parameters of graphs and complexes from metric parameters associated with representation labels and coherent-state weights. Classical deep networks are proposed as a semiclassical limit of this larger construction. The later sections explicitly label the explanation of generalization as a working hypothesis: learning selects relevant geometries or topological structures in transition amplitudes, with metric fitting supplying another layer of information. The conclusion lists empirical validation and suitable complexity measures as future work. Consequently, the paper supplies a theoretical architecture and dictionary, not benchmark evidence that topology explains ordinary deep-network generalization or a ready implementation with established computational advantage.
