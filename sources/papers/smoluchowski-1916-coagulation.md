# Drei Vorträge über Diffusion, Brownsche Molekularbewegung und Koagulation

> [!info] Citation
> M. von Smoluchowski (1916). "Drei Vorträge über Diffusion, Brownsche Molekularbewegung und
> Koagulation von Kolloidteilchen." *Physikalische Zeitschrift* **17**, 557–585.

## TL;DR

Introduces the coagulation equation: cluster growth modeled as a cascade of **binary mergers**,
with the size distribution evolving under a kernel giving the merge rate of a size-$i$ and a
size-$j$ cluster. The founding mathematical model of aggregation kinetics, and the origin of the
gelation question (whether a giant cluster forms in finite time).

## What it establishes

Aggregation as pairwise-merger kinetics. The equation's structure — growth through repeated
small mergers rather than wholesale assembly — is the physics prototype of staged hierarchy
formation.

## Relevance to this research

The kinetic baseline for meta-agent formation dynamics: if agent hierarchies grow the way
colloids coagulate, formation proceeds by pairs, and the laboratory's partition posterior can be
asked whether free energy actually favors such staged paths over direct ones
([[Staged hierarchy formation and RG composability]],
[[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]]).
