## Contribution and scope

Fields models the information used to identify a system as a finite set of stable reference outcomes, distinct from measurements of its changing pointer state. Alternating attention to these components is represented as entanglement swapping between reference and pointer sectors and their environments. Under the stipulated interaction probability and repeated measurement schedule, the probability of continuing to probe the same pure pointer carrier decays as a power of the noninteraction probability. This recovers an exponential-like decay form familiar from decoherence models while making the system boundary observer-relative. The construction is an alternative representation of the bookkeeping involved in repeated identification and observation; it is not a new measured decoherence law or a proof that all physical decoherence mechanisms reduce uniquely to this description.
