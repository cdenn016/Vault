## Contribution and scope

The authors extend their TQNN program toward a correspondence between computation and scattering. They use Reshetikhin–Turaev and Turaev–Viro constructions to represent universal quantum computation through topological transition amplitudes, then propose a mapping from quantum execution traces to amplituhedra. Theorem 1 defines the relevant sense in which a TQNN supplies processes for universal computation. Theorem 2 makes a stronger uniqueness claim for the associated amplituhedron and scattering process, under assumptions excluding gravity and anomalies and treating the Standard Model as complete. The conclusion qualifies uniqueness by state purity, isolation, and coarse-graining. Universal computational capability alone does not establish a unique geometric representation, and the theorem's uniqueness step is not independently verified here. The correspondence should remain a preprint construction with its additional representational assumptions visible.

## Relevance to this research

This is an advanced comparison for [[Emergent spacetime and holography]] and physical realizations of communication. It may help identify what a map from an operational process to geometric amplitudes would need to preserve. It does not derive the project's classical Fisher tensor, a GL(K) connection, or a normalized ELBO from scattering. Its topology, quantum amplitudes, and specific TQFT data are additional structures relative to the live agent model.
