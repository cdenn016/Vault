# Eigenforms, interfaces and holographic encoding: Toward an evolutionary account of objects and spacetime

> [!info] Citation
> Fields, C., Hoffman, D. D., Prakash, C. and Prentner, R. (2017) Eigenforms, interfaces and holographic encoding: Toward an evolutionary account of objects and spacetime. Constructivist Foundations 12: 265-291 (includes commentaries by E. Dietrich, U. Kordeš, L. H. Kauffman, K. Werner and A. Marcianò plus author response).

Local PDF: [PDF](../pdfs/fields-2017-eigenforms-interfaces-and-holographic-encoding-toward-an-evolutionary-account-of-objects-and-spaceti.pdf)

## Summary

The authors compare eigenforms, conscious-agent networks, Markov blankets, and quantum-information descriptions of observer-environment interaction. An eigenform is a stable percept under a corresponding action loop; the comparison locates this stability at an interface through which observations and actions pass. A theorist may describe the coupled system, while neither participant has direct access to the other's interior. The paper connects this restriction with black-box identification limits and interprets interface contents as information about fitness consequences rather than a transparent inventory of external objects.

Its later proposal is that experienced spacetime provides redundancy for encoding these consequences. Binary repetition codes and Hamming graphs illustrate how additional independent coordinates can support correction. The move from this coding illustration to three-dimensional experienced space is explicitly suggested as a hypothesis. The conclusion says sufficiently precise, discriminating predictions remain to be extracted. The generalized interface use of holography should also be distinguished from a derived gravitational area law.

## Relevance to this research

For [[Participatory realism (it from bit)]], this gives a source for treating apparent objects as stable interaction patterns and asking which retained interface makes them distinguishable. It motivates declared communication tasks and error models. It does not establish that Fisher geometry is spacetime, that three repetition bits determine physical dimensionality, or that coupled belief dynamics select new agents.
