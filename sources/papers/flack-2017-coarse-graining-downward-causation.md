## TL;DR (abstract level)

Downward causation, the idea that higher levels of organization causally influence lower ones,
is made operational by asking how adaptive systems identify regularities in evolutionary or
learning time and use them to guide behaviour. In many adaptive systems components collectively
compute their macroscopic worlds through coarse-graining. Apparent (weak) and effective (strong)
downward causation arise when components tune behaviour in response to estimates of collectively
computed macroscopic properties. Examples come from biological and social systems and from deep
neural networks (Section 5).
