# Implementation of classical communication in a quantum world

> [!info] Citation
> Fields, C. (2012) Implementation of classical communication in a quantum world. Information 3: 809-831 (doi: 10.3390/info3040809).

[Author-hosted or author-linked full text](../pdfs/fields-2012-implementation-of-classical-communication-in-a-quantum-world.pdf). [Primary source](https://chrisfieldsresearch.com/class-comm-information-pub-2012.pdf).

## Reading basis

Published article, Information 3 (2012), 809–831, published December 13, 2012. Read PDF pages 1 and 18–20, setting, Sections 5.2–5.3, and Section 6 conclusions. PDF page numbers include cover material.

## Contribution and scope

Fields examines the physical interpretation needed for an observation to become a communicable classical record. The setting is finite observers using POVMs within minimal quantum mechanics and decompositional equivalence. Memory retrieval is treated as communication because a physical memory must itself be measured and interpreted as a record. The paper argues that observers need shared measurement conventions to identify reports and their referents, and that decoherence alone does not supply those conventions. It further identifies classical information transfer with entanglement and interprets public classicality as observer-relative semantics. That latter identification is stronger than the usual statement that measurement can generate entanglement: classical correlations can also occur in separable mixed states. The paper therefore should not be used as an unrestricted equivalence theorem between classical communication and entanglement.

## Relevance to this research

This is an early, direct source for physics as communication and for the role of persistent records in [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]]. The distinction between a record carrier, its interpreted content, and a common measurement convention is useful. The project's normalized transcript kernels already make interpretation conditional on a supplied protocol. They neither require the paper's universal entanglement identification nor establish a quantum mechanism for the project's classical message passing and Fisher dynamics.
