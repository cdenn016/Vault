# On background-independent renormalization of spin foam models

> [!info] Citation
> Bahr, Benjamin (2014). *On background-independent renormalization of spin foam models*. [Retained arXiv 1407.7746v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.7746v2).

## Source result and scope

Bahr formulates renormalization as cylindrical consistency of measures on a partially ordered family of complexes. The compact configuration-space extension argument is distinct from the approximate restricted-complex, observable, and coupling calculations.
