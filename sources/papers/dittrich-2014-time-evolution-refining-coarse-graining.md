# Time evolution as refining, coarse graining and entangling

> [!info] Citation

## Source result and scope

The paper relates refinement, coarse-graining, and entangling operators to evolution, distinguishing kinematical and dynamically informed embeddings. Their identification with equivalent physical states has a gauge-time scope; ordinary proper-time evolution must be separated from pure refinement.
