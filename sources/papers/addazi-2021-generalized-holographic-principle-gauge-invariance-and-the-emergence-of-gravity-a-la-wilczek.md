## Contribution and scope

The authors begin with a finite quantum bipartition maintained in a separable state and formulate a generalized holographic restriction on accessible information. At fixed interaction Hamiltonian, changes in unobserved internal descriptions are argued to be gauge redundancies. Theorem 2 expresses this as invariance of the bulk dynamics under local basis descriptions. The paper then introduces geometric assumptions and discusses familiar gauge formulations of gravity, including MacDowell–Mansouri and Wilczek constructions involving SO(3,2) and SO(4,1). The conclusion explicitly conditions the Poincaré interpretation on realization in an ambient Lorentzian spacetime. Thus the gravity discussion contains supplied group, geometry, and action structure. The generalized information-access argument alone does not select spacetime dimension, Lorentz signature, or a unique gravitational theory; possible links to holographic dualities and renormalization are left for further work.

## Relevance to this research

This is a direct comparison for [[Gauge transformation]] and [[Emergent spacetime and holography]]. It makes a communication-based motivation for gauge redundancy explicit. The project's independent GL(K) interactions and connection-relative Fisher tensor still require their own transformation laws. An invariance of unobservable descriptions is not yet a dynamical gauge field, and the paper's Lorentzian specialization cannot close the project's separate problem of constructing physical spacetime from statistical data.
