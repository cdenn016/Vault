## Contribution and scope

Fields connects the symbol-grounding problem to identifying the physical degrees of freedom responsible for quantum observations. Under minimal quantum theory and decompositional equivalence, recorded outcomes do not uniquely identify the implemented POVM or its Hilbert-space domain. The paper invokes Moore's theorem to argue that a finite record leaves an equivalence class of possible physical realizations. A symbol tied to such records therefore has an underdetermined physical referent if grounding must proceed without any prior semantic commitments. The text explicitly distinguishes this strong requirement from practical identification: observers can distinguish systems and coordinate useful reference while accepting contextual assumptions. Its impossibility conclusion targets unique, assumption-free grounding over an unrestricted realization class, not all learning of meaningful representations.
