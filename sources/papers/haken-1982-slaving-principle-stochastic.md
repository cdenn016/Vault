## TL;DR (abstract level)

Multidimensional nonlinear noisy maps whose variables split into order parameters $u$ (growing
or undamped in the linearization) and slaved variables $s$ (decaying). The slaved variables are
expressed explicitly as functions of the order parameters using the fully nonlinear equations.
Taking the limit of vanishing time step with a Wiener process and Itô calculus gives the
corresponding formulas for stochastic differential equations, including multiplicative noise, so
a high-dimensional problem reduces to a much lower-dimensional Itô SDE.
