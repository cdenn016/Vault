## Contribution and scope

The paper formulates ER=EPR as operational indistinguishability in a finite, two-agent LOCC arrangement. Alice, Bob, and their environment are assumed mutually separable, while a holographic screen supplies an ancillary boundary-qubit description. Theorem 1 argues that, as the accessible channel approaches pure pairwise monogamous entanglement, measurements cannot establish a nonzero number of intervening environmental degrees of freedom. Corollary 2 interprets the endpoint pair as a topological identification of locations measured in independently selected local spatial reference frames. The authors also discuss nontraversability through the need for a classical channel to compare preparations and measurements. The result is explicitly operational and independent of an embedding geometry. It supplies no gravitational metric, Einstein equation, or identification of a laboratory wormhole geometry.

## Relevance to this research

This is a direct comparison source for [[Emergent spacetime and holography]] and [[Quantum reference frames]]. It proposes how indistinguishability of communication implementations can underwrite a topological description. The project's Fisher pullback and directed GL(K) links remain different mathematical objects. A useful comparison must preserve the paper's finite-screen and monogamy hypotheses, rather than treating ER=EPR as an unrestricted equality between entanglement and spacetime or as a derivation of the project's contextual base.
