## TL;DR (abstract level)

For linear systems in which some variables evolve much faster than the rest, a systematic
perturbation theory in the time-scale ratio yields a reduced dynamics in the slow variables
alone, valid after an initial transient period. The procedure extends ordinary adiabatic
elimination and gives corrections to it. The long-time asymptotic expressions for the slow
correlation functions do not extrapolate back to the equal-time correlations as $t\to0$; the
paper explains this "initial slip" and calculates its magnitude.
