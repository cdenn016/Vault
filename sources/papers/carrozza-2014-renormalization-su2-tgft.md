# Renormalization of an SU(2) Tensorial Group Field Theory in Three Dimensions

> [!info] Citation
> Carrozza, Sylvain; Oriti, Daniele; Rivasseau, Vincent (2014). *Renormalization of an SU(2) Tensorial Group Field Theory in Three Dimensions*. [Retained arXiv 1303.6772v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6772v3). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-014-1928-x).

## Source result and scope

For a specified rank-three SU(2) tensorial model with Boulatov-type gauge constraint and Laplacian covariance, the paper proves perturbative renormalizability of interactions through degree six. Power counting uses stranded faces and incidence information.
