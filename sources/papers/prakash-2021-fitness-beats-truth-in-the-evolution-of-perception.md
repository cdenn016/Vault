# Fitness beats truth in the evolution of perception

> [!info] Citation
> Prakash, C., Stephens, K. D., Hoffman, D. D., Singh, M. and Fields, C. (2021) Fitness beats truth in the evolution of perception. Acta Biotheoretica 69: 319-341 (doi: 10.1007/s10441-020-09400-0).

[Local full text](../pdfs/prakash-2021-fitness-beats-truth-in-the-evolution-of-perception.pdf). [Primary source](https://chrisfieldsresearch.com/FitnessBeatsTruth_ActaB_submitted_2019.pdf).

## Reading basis

Author manuscript of the 2021 article; 50 PDF pages. Read PDF pages 23 and 28–32 (strategy definitions, Theorem 4, and discussion). This is a targeted ingestion reading, not a full proof audit.

## Contribution and scope

Prakash and colleagues compare two perceptual decision strategies within evolutionary games. Both receive sensory information, but the Truth strategy acts using a maximum-a-posteriori world-state estimate, whereas Fitness-only ranks available percepts by expected fitness. Expected-fitness maximization weakly outperforms acting on the fitness of a point estimate in the stipulated decision problem. The paper then states a generic strict-dominance probability bound and relates the payoff comparisons to evolutionary replacement under several population assumptions. The scope depends on how Truth, sensory maps, fitness functions, priors, and competition are defined. The broad philosophical conclusion that accurate perception is disfavored should not be detached from those definitions; ordinary Bayesian decision theory can itself maximize posterior expected utility.

## Relevance to this research

This informs [[Collective active inference]] by distinguishing learning a probability law from choosing an action under a utility function. It does not show that accurate probability estimates are useless, or that fitting an evidence-based model conflicts with adaptive action. For the project, likelihood calibration, retained information, and action objectives remain separate quantities. The generic probability bound has not been independently proof-checked in this ingest.
