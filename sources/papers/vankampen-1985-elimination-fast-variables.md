## TL;DR (abstract level)

Nonlinear ordinary differential equations with a small (or large) parameter evolve on two time
scales. A general scheme extracts equations for the slow evolution, including higher-order
corrections, and unifies the many methods in the literature. The scheme sorts problems into three
categories determined by the structure of the equations. Linear equations such as the
Fokker–Planck equation are treated as well, with special attention to the definition of the
required projection operator.
