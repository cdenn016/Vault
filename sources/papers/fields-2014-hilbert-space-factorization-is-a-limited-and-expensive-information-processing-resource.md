## Contribution and scope

Fields treats uncertainty about the provenance of an observation as uncertainty over possible factorizations of the surrounding world. He introduces a factorization entropy to distinguish this uncertainty from uncertainty about the state of a supplied system. Building and monitoring physical reference frames is then interpreted as reducing the range of compatible factorizations at an energetic and memory cost. Persistent memory distinguishes previous from current records and thereby supplies an observer-relative ordering. The conclusion emphasizes that observers can at best maintain a useful equivalence class of factorizations. The cost estimates are schematic and depend on the chosen monitoring representation. The paper does not specify a universal prior over all factorizations or establish that abstract tensor-factor decompositions themselves dissipate energy independently of a physical implementation.

## Relevance to this research

This source connects [[Quantum reference frames]] to the cost of maintaining the identities and conventions behind communication. It is useful for distinguishing a mathematical factorization from the physical work required to stabilize a laboratory reference. For [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]], that distinction applies to dictionaries, records, and persistent interfaces. Its observer-relative memory ordering is also different from a Lorentzian time coordinate or the project's Fisher information clock, so no equivalence of clocks should be inferred.
