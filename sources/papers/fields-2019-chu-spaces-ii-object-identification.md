## Contribution and scope

Part II applies Chu spaces and Channel Theory to object identification and the representation of part-whole relations. Finite object files encode features, locations, and velocities; sequences are classified by a relation interpreted as consistency with object permanence. Cone-cocone diagrams organize upward abstraction and downward constraints, making token and type roles relative to a level of description. The object-permanence relation is informed by empirical perception research rather than deduced from category theory. The authors discuss simplicial representations and mereotopology, while acknowledging open questions about learned whole-object representations, persistence, and attentional control. The construction describes semantic organization and does not alone supply a learning dynamics.

## Relevance to this research

This is closely relevant to [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]] and [[Quantum reference frames]] because it treats identities and part-whole structure as inferred classifications. It provides mathematical language for consistent translations among representations. A categorical colimit is not automatically a normalized statistical parent law, and inferred visual geometry is not automatically physical spacetime. Those identifications require additional models rather than a change of terminology.
