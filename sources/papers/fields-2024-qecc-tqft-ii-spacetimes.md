## Contribution and scope

Part II develops the proposed relation between communication, quantum error correction, and effective spacetime. Spin-network boundary states are associated with dual simplicial geometries, and a TQFT evolution supplies cobordisms between them. The construction makes its additional geometric choices explicit: a bulk action and vertex amplitudes select the theory, while representation labels and intertwiners carry the spin-network data. A three-dimensional SU(2) BF example gives a flatness constraint and a gauge constraint; later sections discuss Chern–Simons, extended gravitational constructions, and higher-dimensional possibilities. The conclusion advances the hypothesis that spatial separation provides the redundancy needed for communication. The concrete examples employ already specified TQFT and geometric machinery, so the paper does not derive arbitrary spacetime dynamics from an unstructured communication channel.

## Relevance to this research

For [[Emergent spacetime and holography]], this is the detailed construction accompanying the authors' communication-first program. Its codes and spin-network geometry offer a comparison to retained interfaces and coarse-graining. The project's statistical Fisher tensor is not a TQFT amplitude or a QECC. Connecting them would require a map of states, observables, dynamics, and recovery conditions; the existence of gauge terminology on both sides does not establish such a map.
