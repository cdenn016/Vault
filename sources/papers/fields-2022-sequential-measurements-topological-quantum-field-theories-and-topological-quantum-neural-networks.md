## Contribution and scope

The paper represents finite quantum reference frames as cone–cocone diagrams of Barwise–Seligman classifiers and seeks a functorial description of sequences of measurements. Its construction embeds the associated finite Hilbert spaces in a larger space and associates the prescribed diagram morphisms with cobordisms; Theorem 2 states the required commuting correspondence. Sequential preparations and observations are then represented by TQFT evolution. Later developments connect this framework to spin networks, topological quantum neural networks, index-theoretic data, and possible implementations. The categorical construction supplies a proposed representation of finite measurement processes. It does not by itself demonstrate that an arbitrary experimental sequence uniquely determines a physical field theory, its action, or its embedding spacetime. The broad language about measurable systems inducing TQFTs should be read with the specified categories and embeddings attached.

## Relevance to this research

This is the technical precursor to the two communication/QECC papers.
