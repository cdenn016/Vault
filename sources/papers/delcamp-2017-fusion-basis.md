# Fusion basis for lattice gauge theory and loop quantum gravity

> [!info] Citation
> Delcamp, Clement; Dittrich, Bianca; Riello, Aldo (2017). *Fusion basis for lattice gauge theory and loop quantum gravity*. [Retained arXiv 1607.08881v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.08881v2). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP02(2017)061).

## Source result and scope

The finite-group fusion basis organizes magnetic and electric excitations through representations of the Drinfeld double and a hierarchy of ribbon observables. Coarse tracing occurs in an adapted basis rather than by deleting original spin-network edges.
