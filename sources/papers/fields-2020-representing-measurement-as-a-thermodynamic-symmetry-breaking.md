## Contribution and scope

This account includes the operations needed to identify an apparatus within the physical process of measurement. Stable reference outcomes distinguish the system being tracked; variable pointer outcomes characterize its changing state. The authors use classifier diagrams and groupoid transitions to represent simultaneous compatible measurements and changes of observational context. They also separate information treated as meaningful by the observer from environmental exchanges used to obtain free energy and dissipate heat. Maintaining reference criteria and records imposes resource demands that conventional pointer-only descriptions can omit. The authors interpret the resulting asymmetries as inducing decoherence, disturbance, and contextuality. These claims are developed within finite, separable quantum-system assumptions; the paper does not provide measured energy budgets or a general dynamical derivation of a classical observer from arbitrary matter.

## Relevance to this research

For [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]], the valuable distinction is between the record payload, the criteria identifying its source, and the resources needed to maintain both. Persistent transcript and identification data can be part of a retained interface even when current beliefs agree. The groupoid language offers a comparison to directed dictionaries, but the paper's context switches and the project's gauge changes have different definitions. Their equality would require explicit state and operator maps.
