## Contribution and scope

This paper proposes a model-theoretic interpretation of environmental superselection. Fields first questions whether einselection identifies an observer-independent system without an already selected system/environment boundary. The constructive alternative treats POVMs as interpretation maps from a global quantum state to observed informational states. A sequence of those states can be represented as a stochastic finite-state machine, while the underlying physical evolution remains unitary. Observable systems are then described as virtual machines relative to an observer's measurement specification. The conclusion explicitly calls this a bootstrap: it assumes observers deploy POVMs producing classical information and explains the resulting records within that framework. It therefore does not derive an observer or its semantics without prior structure, nor negate the usefulness of ordinary decoherence calculations on supplied subsystems.
