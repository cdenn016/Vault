# From the discrete to the continuous - towards a cylindrically consistent dynamics

> [!info] Citation
> Dittrich, Bianca (2012). *From the discrete to the continuous - towards a cylindrically consistent dynamics*. [Retained arXiv 1205.6127v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6127v1). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/14/12/123004).

## Source result and scope

Dittrich develops cylindrically consistent dynamics using richer boundaries and composing injections. Its examples distinguish classical configuration embeddings from wave-function embeddings and show that unsuitable boundary choices can impose unwanted fluctuation constraints.
