## Verified content

The blurb describes metastability as a sequence of visits of the path to metastable sets, analyzed through precise estimates of hitting probabilities and hitting times obtained from the potential theory of reversible Markov processes. The table of contents includes Chapter 7, "Potential Theory"; Chapter 8, "Key Definitions and Basic Properties"; Chapter 9, "Basic Techniques"; Chapter 11, "Diffusion Processes with Gradient Drift"; and Chapter 13, "The Curie–Weiss Model".
