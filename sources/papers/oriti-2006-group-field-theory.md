# The group field theory approach to quantum gravity

> [!info] Citation
> Oriti, Daniele (2006). *The group field theory approach to quantum gravity*. [Retained arXiv gr-qc/0607032v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0607032v3).

## Source result and scope

GFT uses fields with several group arguments, a kinetic kernel, and prescribed argument contractions. Stranded Feynman graphs generate labeled complexes and their amplitudes. This is an amplitude/combinatorial expansion, not a generic attention-graph probability distribution.
