## Contribution and scope

This early paper treats a system/apparatus/environment decomposition as information physically represented by an observer. It argues that a functioning apparatus must include internal decohering degrees of freedom and identifiable pointer and ready states. The observer's own readiness must contain the information needed to identify the apparatus, interpret its pointer, and relate repeated observations. Section 4 describes the decomposition as a data structure in which some roles are fixed and others are inferred from the task. The conclusion claims that consistent apparatus descriptions remove the traditional measurement problem. That conclusion is a foundational interpretation resting on its functional requirements for apparatus and on internal decoherence; it is not an independently established solution of all quantum measurement problems. The architectural requirements are more directly reusable than that universal claim.

## Relevance to this research

This source connects [[Participatory realism (it from bit)]] to physically maintained descriptions and records. For [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]], it motivates separating the underlying carrier from the represented system and the operational function assigned to it. A retained parent must preserve that function through its admitted kernels and dynamics, not merely receive a new name. The paper does not supply such a statistical inheritance proof or show how new agent boundaries are selected by interaction.
