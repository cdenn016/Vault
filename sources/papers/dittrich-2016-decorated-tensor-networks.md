# Decorated tensor network renormalization for lattice gauge theories and spin foam models

> [!info] Citation
> Dittrich, Bianca; Mizera, Sebastian; Steinhaus, Sebastian (2016). *Decorated tensor network renormalization for lattice gauge theories and spin foam models*. [Retained arXiv 1409.2407v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.2407v2). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/18/5/053009).

## Source result and scope

Decorated tensors keep explicit gauge data while additional indices encode correlations. Decoration-conditioned SVD can preserve the labels needed to interpret coarse tensors. Demonstrations use finite Abelian gauge groups.
