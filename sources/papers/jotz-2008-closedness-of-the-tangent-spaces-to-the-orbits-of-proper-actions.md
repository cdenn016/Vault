## Core claim, method and scope

Proves closed range for infinitesimal orbit maps of proper Banach-Lie actions and obtains a manifold quotient for free proper Hilbert actions. This is prior authority for the quotient construction. The additional strong-metric consequences and weak-Fisher counterexample belong to the project derivations.
