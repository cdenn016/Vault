## Core claim, method and scope

Studies information contraction with average input-cost constraints through nonlinear Dobrushin curves. A linear contraction coefficient can be one even when information decays. Used to distinguish contraction mechanisms and avoid importing finite-alphabet exponential rates into other channels.
