## Growing loop and central scale law

Any block-root change conjugates the loop matrix and preserves its determinant, including when the change depends on the whole configuration. Compact GL sets have bounded log determinants, so their probabilities go to zero. This is an arbitrary-size property of this supplied hierarchy and topology. The edge changes with depth: the result does not contradict a product topology retaining only fixed-original-edge queries, nor does it refute interacting priors, different identifications or formation.

Its exact normalized kernels compose for dyadic scale ratios.
