## Existing factorization and implemented field

The manuscript already provides the first/second exponential-moment formulas in `prop:glink-sample-record`, `eq:glink-expected-record`, `prop:glink-expected-loop` and `eq:glink-expected-defect`, using the Kronecker convention in `sec:glink-setting`. For a product Gaussian law over primitive links and episodes, each original quadratic matter record needs the signed mean transport and its full second Kronecker moment. Ordered loop means and second moments factor as ordered products only when the walk contains distinct primitive reversal-pair IDs. Both orientations reuse the same primitive law and nodes; reverse moments are evaluated from `exp(-omega)`, rather than obtained by inverting the forward mean. A repeated primitive within a loop is rejected, while separate loops may share primitives because expectation of their sum is linear. Part (iv) of the manuscript already identifies the repeated-reversal-pair failure. These are recovered source formulas, not new theory or a factorization of a correlated native posterior.
