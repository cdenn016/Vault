## Native-law boundary and continuation

The adapter's law is explicitly fitted product recognition with fresh records. For the normalized finite history, native causal filtering uses the backward-normalized root and transitions. The R1 one-episode objective needs the correction E_R log(dP_one_episode/dr_n), where r_n is the filtered predictive prior; even episode zero needs the backward boundary factor. Reusing a previous initializer while retaining the original one-episode prior does not carry the native history law.

The contract also distinguishes deterministic recognition descent from native switching-diffusion trajectories, forward predictive/path error from variational reverse KL, and finite empirical record mixtures from continuous generator paths. These are scoped implementation obligations and explicit derivations, not a newly certified complete parent theorem.
