## Conditional construction and native-law bound

One shared component supplies every occurrence. For source pattern $a$, the difference-record coefficient map $D_a$ adds repeated fine-node contributions before forming the joint Gaussian mean and covariance. The exact conditional original-record marginal depends on this incident catalog, not on omitted rooted paths or parent geometry. Its native mixture uses the pushforward of the unchanged full-prefix generator posterior; it does not assume independent link marginals or certify an empirical approximation to that posterior.

Projecting the full joint comparison law to the catalog and standardized parent matter contracts its KL. The existing generated-prefix average/high-probability original-record bound therefore carries to this restricted handoff. Per component it needs $O(r d_{out}K^2)$ future generator values, local source/baseline/noise tags and component identities. The exact source-pattern sum remains exponential in $r$. Query projections preserve the joint law when extracted from the same component realization; independent catalog draws cannot reconstruct that union law. Shared sender matter, hard source zeros and inverse reversal are retained.

This catalog gives an exact record marginal, rather than an exact decoder conditional on raw parents. Omitted tree factors change that regression and the next normalized channel.
