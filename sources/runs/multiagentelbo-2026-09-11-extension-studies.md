## Confirmed results and boundaries

The E1 fundamental SU(2) and SO(3)/adjoint pilots had unresolved contrasts at their initial precision. Later fixed confirmation uses 1,048,576 tuples per family. Its reported 99% simultaneous intervals are $[-0.0056015412009067572,-0.004651854067574044]$ and $[-0.0025107815282887666,-0.0020386620138504838]$. Both fall below the prespecified $-0.001$ threshold and meet the width criterion under the declared ideal independent-Haar and binary64 contract. E1b is a newly defined adjoint matter/prior family, not a quotient-equivalence result for fundamental E1a matter.

E2a constructs normalized Gaussian laws and exact Schur reductions with means, covariance, sources, scalar offsets, evidence and mixed belief/model coupling retained. E2b keeps two or four graph-conditioned Gaussian components as the exact parent. Coordinate-moment discrepancy from a matched Gaussian is not a KL estimate. The later 48-view approximation study has 18 passes and 30 unresolved views at 0.01 nats: 16 passes are identical-component controls, only two are nonzero approximation passes. The first arithmetic-admission failure remains a failure.

E3 keeps contextual connection geometry, rank loss and cross-map compatibility separate. Its half-turn control has full-loop transport $-I_2$ with a vanishing cross-map defect at the stated floating tolerance. E5 corrects an unmatched-distance comparison by matching graph distance on open chains and rings; its 84 cases and 42 paired comparisons concern that corrected observable.

E4a supplies the driven reference. Dropping preparation/memory terms in E4b fails TV tolerance 0.001 on 50 of 180 positive-time endpoints; 130 satisfy it. Of 60 preparation/projection cases, 20 are exact for all times, 22 have a sampled counterexample, and 18 remain unresolved for a continuous-time guarantee. This does not establish projected path-law accuracy. Unresolved outcomes remain unresolved.
