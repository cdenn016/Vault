## Findings

### L13-1: The thesis that PIFB2 realizes Wheeler's participatory it-from-bit has no refuting observation, yet it is asserted in realization voice, including in Part I
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:81 (introduction, "supplies the formal objects those programs lack"), :1677 (`sec:cross_scale_shadows`), :1727 (`sec:ouroboros`), :1830 (`sec:time_info`), :1872 and :1892 (`sec:pullback`), :2091 (`sec:lahav_convergence`), :2158 (global conclusion); against fence F5 at :114 and the admission at :2109.
**Severity:** critical
**Evidence:** Goal 1 of the brief makes the Wheeler realization the paper's thesis. The paper's own fence F5 (:114) says the participatory-physics reading "has no identified falsifying observation and may be unfalsifiable as currently stated", and :2109 concedes that "any observed physics can be reinterpreted as reflecting cognitive constraints". Despite that register, the thesis is stated as achieved in five places: "this bidirectional coupling realizes Wheeler's participatory loop" (:1677, a Part I section at Level 2), "This realizes the formal feedback structure of Wheeler's self-excited circuit" (:1727, Part I), "this granular bit reading is what realizes Wheeler's 'it from bit'" (:1830), "that proposal is given a precise fixed-gauge realization as the ordinary pullback" (:1872), and "gives 'it from bit' a precise sense" (:1892). None of these sites carries the F5 pointer, which appears only at :102, :1800, :1806, and :1812. The project's rigorous companion says the opposite about the same loop. `Theory/c14_interpretation.tex` (`sec:phil-participatory`) states that block coordinate ascent between parent and child "resembles Wheeler's participatory proposal without being derived from it or evidence for it", and that "None of these separates the program from a hierarchical model with learned priors; formation could."

A Duhem-Quine test gives the same verdict. If a tower run shows no hierarchy, the prospective protocol (:1746) refutes the tower "for the tested configuration", and nothing in the Wheeler reading is given up. If a run does show a hierarchy, a standard hierarchical Bayesian model with learned priors predicts the same result. The Wheeler claim therefore forbids no outcome that the non-participatory reading permits, so by Popper's criterion it has no empirical content, and a passed test has zero Mayo severity against the rival "ordinary hierarchical inference". The closing sentence (:2158) extends the overclaim: it says C1-C6 "convert the speculative readings of Part II from standing interpretations into a sequence of decidable mathematical problems". Solving C3 or C4 would decide mathematical questions. It would not decide the reading at :2109, which the paper itself calls possibly unfalsifiable.
**Fix:** Replace "realizes" at :1677, :1727, :1830, :1872, :1892, :2091 with "has the structure of" or "models one aspect of", and attach "(fence F5)" at each site. Rewrite :2158 as "convert the mathematical supports of Part II into decidable problems; the physical and participatory readings remain interpretive (fence F5)". Then either adopt the Theory's single-point falsifier for the participatory reading (complementarity note 1) or say in the abstract that the Wheeler connection is an interpretive framing, not a result.
**Falsifies:** An observable, stated in the manuscript, whose outcome the participatory reading forbids while a hierarchical variational model with learned priors and no participatory interpretation permits it.
**Status:** S (structural analogy). The prose inflates it to a realization, which a physics reader will take as P or D.

### L13-2: The Wheeler correspondence conflicts with Wheeler's own text on bits, the continuum, laws, and self-excitation
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:75, :79, :88 (O3), :1727-1729, :1830, :1872.
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** Wheeler 1990 (text checked in the scanned chapter; reprint pagination from p. 311) defines it from bit as every it, "even the spacetime continuum itself", deriving its existence "from the apparatus-elicited answers to yes or no questions, binary choices, bits". He lists "Third no: No continuum. No continuum in mathematics and therefore no continuum in physics" and "Second no: No laws", and concludes "continuum-based physics, no; information-based physics, yes." PIFB2 departs from each point.

(a) The "bit" at :1830 is a continuous KL increment of a Gaussian belief update, s^2/(2 ln 2), not a registered yes-no outcome, and the manuscript concedes at :124 that there is "no Born rule, and no measurement postulate". Calling this quantity Wheeler's bit overloads an established term (Lipton and Steinhardt 2018, failure mode iv). (b) The pullback at :1872 induces a metric on a pre-given smooth manifold C (O3, :88), so the continuum is an input, whereas Wheeler lists the continuum among the things to be derived. (c) A fixed variational functional with fixed postulates P1-P10 is a law "from everlasting to everlasting", which Wheeler's second no rejects. (d) :1727 claims the apex closure realizes the "self-excited circuit", but :1729 shows, via the Łojasiewicz inequality, that the autonomous flow converges to stationary points and that "only observation injection supplies persistent exogenous drive". A loop that needs exogenous drive to keep participating is the opposite of self-excited. Item 8 of the C1 review (2026-09-23) already noted that static descent cannot support sustained participation. What is new here is that this contradicts the specific Wheeler construct PIFB2 claims to realize.
**Fix:** After :81, add a short paragraph stating which of Wheeler's commitments the framework keeps (observer-relativity, participatory feedback between levels) and which it drops (discrete yes-no registration, no continuum, no laws, self-excitation). Rename the :1830 quantity "information gain in bits" and drop the Wheeler attribution there. Change :1727 to "closes a feedback loop that, unlike Wheeler's self-excited circuit, requires exogenous observation to avoid relaxation".
**Falsifies:** A construction inside PIFB2 in which C, or its metric and dimension, is produced from discrete registered outcomes rather than presupposed, or in which the apex loop sustains non-stationary dynamics without exogenous input.
**Status:** S. The prose presents a selective analogy as a realization.

### L13-3: The novelty claims about Wheeler's program and "law without law" ignore the informational-reconstruction literature
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:81 ("Where Wheeler's 'it from bit' has remained a slogan, Kant's transcendental aesthetic a doctrine without mathematics ..."), :1767 ("Wheeler's 'law without law' strand would acquire a candidate concrete form").
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** Searches run: "Müller Law without law observer states algorithmic information theory" and "Höhn Toolbox reconstructing quantum theory rules on information acquisition", plus a grep of PIFB2's citations for Hardy, Chiribella, Masanes, Müller, Höhn, Zeilinger, and Brukner. Only Brukner2018, on Wigner's friend, is cited. The canonical counterparts are as follows.

- Höhn, "Toolbox for reconstructing quantum theory from rules on information acquisition", Quantum 1, 38 (2017). An observer interrogates a system "with binary questions" and qubit quantum theory is reconstructed; this is the most literal formalization of Wheeler's yes-no bits.
- Müller, "Law without law: from observer states to physics via algorithmic information theory", Quantum 4, 301 (2020). It takes Wheeler's phrase as its title and derives, from observer states, the appearance of an external world with simple probabilistic laws.
- Hardy (2001, quant-ph/0101012); Chiribella, D'Ariano and Perinotti, Phys. Rev. A 84, 012311 (2011); Masanes and Müller, New J. Phys. 13, 063001 (2011). These are informational derivations of quantum theory.

The vault already holds notes on Hardy 2001 and Chiribella 2016, so the gap is in the manuscript, not in the project. On the Kant half, Reichenbach's 1920 *Relativitätstheorie und Erkenntnis apriori* and Cassirer's 1921 relativity essay (bundled in the cited Cassirer1923 volume) are neo-Kantian treatments that engage the mathematics directly. By Worrall's (2014) use-novelty standard, the reconstruction programs derive Hilbert-space structure from informational postulates, which realizes "it from bit" in the quantum domain Wheeler meant. PIFB2 at present contains no Hilbert space (:124). A physics referee in quantum foundations will read :81 as unaware of this literature.
**Fix:** Delete "has remained a slogan" and "a doctrine without mathematics". Add one Related Work paragraph citing Höhn 2017, Müller 2020, Hardy 2001, Chiribella et al. 2011, and Masanes and Müller 2011, stating that they formalize Wheeler's program in the quantum domain while PIFB2 proposes a classical, multi-agent, information-geometric construction. At :1767, cite Müller 2020 as the existing concrete formalization of "law without law".
**Falsifies:** Evidence that none of the cited works formalizes any part of Wheeler's it-from-bit or law-without-law program (for example, that Höhn's binary-question rules are not informational in Wheeler's sense).
**Status:** The novelty claim is false as stated. The construction's own content is D/S.

### L13-4: O1-O2 exclude nothing, and O4 plus the "cognitive gauge structure" thesis carry no physics content; they belong in a philosophy venue
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:83-92 (O1-O4, "an electron is a scale-0 agent"), :2101 (panprotopsychism), :2109 (gauge structure of physics as a property of human cognitive architecture), register entries P6 (:1821, "|D_obs| = 4 for human agents") and P10 (:1825).
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** The paper applies Kretschmann's objection to its own shareability criterion (:2113, `rem:kretschmann`: any theory can be rewritten in covariant form, so covariance alone has no empirical content) but not to O1-O2, where it applies with equal force. O1 says every system "carries gauge-frame structure together with participation in variational dynamics on a statistical manifold"; O2 adds a principal bundle and transport. Every system satisfies both trivially: take the trivial bundle, phi = 0, Omega_ij = I, a Gaussian N(x, s^2 I) around the state, and a residual functional. `L13_o1_vacuity.py` shows that a rotational, non-gradient flow (|A - A^T| = 2.0) is the exact minimizer of S[x] = sum |x_{t+1} - x_t - h f(x_t)|^2 / 2: S = 1.3e-31 at the solution, max |dS| = 8.0e-17 by central differences, and S is about 0.04 on perturbed paths. So O1-O2 exclude no system. "An electron is a scale-0 agent" (:92) adds no consequence for any electron observable: the scale-0 construction is deferred (:86, C5 at :2142), and :2028 concedes that no Standard Model factor is derived.

For the :2109 thesis, a physics journal would need either a derivation of a known gauge group or its representation content from stated constraints on cognitive architecture, or an observable difference between agents with different architectures. The paper supplies neither. :2121 calls the counterfactual architecture "a thought experiment, not a prediction, since we have no empirical access", and :2109 itself concedes possible unfalsifiability. P6 hardcodes the observed 1+3 dimension count for "human agents" as a postulate, so the one number that could have tested the thesis is an input. O4 and :2101 place the position in the panpsychism taxonomy (Chalmers 2016, Strawson 2006), and that placement has no mathematical role anywhere in Parts I or II: deleting it changes no equation, proposition, or prediction.
**Fix:** For a physics venue, reduce O1-O4, :2086-2106, and :2107-2132 to one "Interpretive stance" paragraph of about half a page, stating the cognitive-first reading, F5, and the Kretschmann-type vacuity of O1-O2. Move the panprotopsychism, consciousness, and philosophy-of-science material to a companion paper for a philosophy venue (Synthese, BJPS, or Studies in History and Philosophy of Science; Neuroscience of Consciousness or Journal of Consciousness Studies for the consciousness section).
**Falsifies:** A system that cannot be given a trivial bundle and a residual-functional variational principle, or a PIFB2 equation whose content depends on O1, O2, or O4.
**Status:** O1-O4 are metaphysical postulates with no P/D/E status; the :2109 thesis is C and self-declared possibly unfalsifiable. The sentence at :2109 is candid and does not inflate; the inflation comes from making this material the organizing ontology of a physics paper.

### L13-5: The principal falsifier (the gauge-curvature test) leaves a Duhem-Quine escape and has low severity against a curvature-irrelevant null
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1780, :1782, :1792, :1794 (`sec:gauge_curvature_conjecture`); :2138 (C1 calls it "the framework's principal experimental anchor").
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** The conditional reads "*if* the Regime II edge-relaxed cocycle ... is realized for natural language, *then* linguistic evolution should drive" W_ijk toward K_q (:1780). No operational criterion for the antecedent "is realized for natural language" is given, so a non-positive partial correlation can be absorbed by denying the antecedent (Duhem; Quine 1951). The test at :1794 predicts a positive partial correlation between plaquette-averaged Wilson curvature and cross-entropy per token, "controlling for model size and training-token count". It does not fix the unit of analysis (models, checkpoints, corpora, or plaquettes), the sample, the estimator of the connection, or a tolerance.

Severity (Mayo 1996) is low. The connection delta_ij is learned by the same model that is trained to lower cross-entropy. Any account in which unregularized connection parameters are noisy early in training and settle later predicts the same sign across checkpoints, with no curvature-minimization principle at all. The measuring instrument is constituted by the fitted model; this is the precise theory-ladenness charge (Hanson 1958 gives the general thesis). A pass therefore licenses little unless it is compared with a null arm, for example a randomized or frozen-connection model, or a sweep over a curvature penalty.

Two further problems. :1792 extends the conjecture to "shared social norms, standardized scientific terminology ... cortical wiring, and market institutions" and says generalization "is settled by the same instrument in each domain", but the instrument needs a corpus cross-entropy, which markets and cortical wiring do not have. And :1780 concedes that for causal (autoregressive) attention the holonomy is "an index-space invariant" with no dynamical content, which excludes the dominant model class.
**Fix:** State the antecedent operationally (for example, "a Regime II model whose held-out cross-entropy is within a stated margin of the Regime I model on the same corpus"). Fix the unit of analysis, and add a baseline arm with the connection frozen at a random or flat value. Restrict the test to encoder models. Delete the "settled by the same instrument in each domain" clause, or name each domain's cross-entropy analog.
**Falsifies:** A protocol text elsewhere in PIFB2 that fixes the antecedent, the unit of analysis, and a null arm; or a demonstration that no plausible curvature-irrelevant training dynamics produces a positive partial correlation.
**Status:** C (a conjecture, conditional on the unbuilt Regime II). "Pre-registered" and "principal experimental anchor" inflate it to a severe test.

### L13-6: The ontological status of the base manifold C, the "it" of it-from-bit, is characterized in four incompatible ways, and the Kantian terminology contradicts itself
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:77, :79, :88 (O3), :146, :1800, :2125, :2129; :1800 against :2121.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The manuscript describes C in four ways: (a) "a common noumenal substrate" (:77; also :2095, and :2125's "the noumenal manifold C"); (b) "a non-cognitive index floor ... that blocks an infinite regress" (:79); (c) "an abstract framework of relations derived from agentic structure" (:146); (d) "a domain of inquiry, not a thing" (:77, :146). Then :1800 says "information geometry is the structure that constitutes the noumenal realm, with no further individuals beneath it", while :2129 says C "carries only the smooth index structure" and that "no agent-independent geometry exists in the formalism". The Fisher geometry lives on the fibers, not on C, so :1800 and :2129 cannot both describe the noumenal realm. (b) and (c) also contradict each other. C is a presupposed input to Definition `def:base_manifold`, not something derived from agents, so (c) is false as a statement about the formalism.

The Kantian positioning has a second inconsistency. :1800 "explicitly disavow[s] Kant's stronger constitutive-a-priori commitment", while :2121 adopts "a constitutive constraint on shareable description in roughly Friedman's relativized-a-priori sense". Reichenbach (1920) split the Kantian a priori into "apodictically valid, true for all time" and "constituting the concept of object", and kept only the second. Friedman's *Dynamics of Reason* (2001) revives exactly that constitutive, revisable a priori. What the paper actually disavows is the apodictic a priori, not the constitutive one.

On structural realism, "no further individuals beneath it" is the eliminative ontic thesis (SEP: "there are no individuals (but there is relational structure)") and fits Cassirer's invariants. It does not fit Worrall's epistemic structural realism, which suspends "judgement as to the nature" of the relata (SEP). Listing Worrall as a precursor is acceptable, but the positioning should say that it takes the ontic side, not the epistemic one.
**Fix:** Choose one status for C and use it throughout; the formalism supports "a presupposed smooth index manifold carrying no metric, called noumenal by analogy only". At :1800, replace "constitutes the noumenal realm" with "information geometry on the fibers is the structure available to agents". Replace "constitutive-a-priori commitment" with "apodictic (fixed, universal) a priori, while keeping a revisable constitutive role in the sense of Reichenbach 1920 and Friedman 2001".
**Falsifies:** A construction in PIFB2 in which C is produced from agent data (which would make (c) true and (b) false), or a reading of Friedman 2001 in which the relativized a priori is not constitutive.
**Status:** Metaphysical postulate (declared so at :1800). The defect is incoherence, and it changes what a reader can take the pullback to be a metric on.

### L13-7: "No external time is presupposed" is circular: the clock is defined along a flow parameterized by an external t, with same-time global coupling
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1830 ("No external physical time is presupposed; time emerges from the information-processing dynamics themselves"), :1835, :1842-1847 (`eq:clock_dissipation`), P5 at :1820.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The clock identity (d tau/dt)^2 = eta (-dF/dt) is written in an external flow parameter t, and the trajectory whose arc length defines tau is generated by x' = -eta G^{-1} grad F in that same t. Arc length is reparameterization-invariant, but the ordering of states comes from the shared t. So does, in the multi-agent case, which state of agent j is coupled to which state of agent i. PIFB2's inter-agent coupling is same-time (the distinction between lagged and same-time coupling is registered (ledger)), and P5 postulates "a shared update parameter tau" outright. A global simultaneity slice is exactly the external time the sentence says is not presupposed. "Different agents accumulate different arc lengths and so experience different time flows" (:1835) relabels each agent's progress along a common ordering; it does not show an ordering emerging. The distinction from Page-Wootters and thermal time claimed at :1835 is fair, but those programs derive an ordering from correlations or states, and that step is what is missing here.
**Fix:** Replace the first sentence of :1830 with "The dynamics are ordered by an update parameter t shared by all agents; under P1 each agent's clock is the Fisher-Rao arc length along its own trajectory, so clock rates are agent-relative while the ordering is presupposed."
**Falsifies:** A construction in PIFB2 in which the ordering of updates, or the coupling between agents' states, is derived from belief data without a shared parameter.
**Status:** P1 is a postulate; the "emergence" claim is S and circular as worded.

### L13-8: "Settles signature availability" advertises a result whose conclusion is its input
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:63 (abstract), :1975 (`prop:4d_signature`), :2140 (C3), :2156 (global conclusion); postulate P4 (:1819).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** P4 postulates "one temporal direction of negative trace-square and three spatial directions of positive trace-square", and the proposition then delivers signature (-,+,+,+). `L13_signature_input.py` computes the trace-form Gram matrix and a generic full-rank pullback for commuting disjoint-support sets of p skew and q traceless diagonal generators. For (p,q) = (1,3), (2,2), (3,1), (1,1), (0,4), (2,5) the signature is exactly (p,q) in every case, with commutator residual 0.0. The script also shows that the trace form on gl(K,R) has signature (K(K-1)/2 negative, K(K+1)/2 positive) for K = 2, 3, 4, 6; for gl(2,R) that is already (1,3). An indefinite restriction is therefore available in every gl(K,R) with K >= 2, and the 1+3 count is chosen, not found. The mathematics is correct, and the text at :1896 and :1996 candidly says the signature "is a postulate, not a derivation". The inflation lies in promoting a zero-severity existence fact to the abstract and the conclusion as something "settled". The honest content is that the commuting constraint forces K_q >= 6. The result agrees with the registered statement on gl(K,R) invariant forms (ledger); I did not re-derive that statement.
**Fix:** In the abstract and at :2156: "exhibits an all-real frame-twist pullback of signature (-,+,+,+) for K_q >= 6; the signature is fixed by the choice of one skew and three diagonal generators (P4), so availability is immediate and selection (C4) is the open problem."
**Falsifies:** A commuting generator set of the P4 type for which the pullback signature differs from the chosen (p,q).
**Status:** P (correct, under P4). The verb "settles" inflates its significance.

### L13-9: The Level 1/2/3 taxonomy mixes proof status with subject matter, and Level-3 content appears in Level-1 or Level-2 voice
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:98, :100, :1812, :2156; leaks at :1677, :1727, :1775, :1788, :1800, :1806.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Level 1 is "implemented and empirically probed" (:98), but its content is the attention recovery, an analytic theorem (P) that no experiment probes; the sweep probes that the architecture trains, not the limit. Level 2 is the "mathematical implementation" of dynamics whose behavior is untested (:100), but :1812 redefines Level 2 as "theorems and constructions ... whose proofs do not depend on any physical reading". As a result, the P-grade no-go theorem, the RG covariance theorem, and the signature propositions sit at Level 2, while an equally proved limit sits at Level 1. The levels track subject matter (transformer, tower, physics), not epistemic grade. That contradicts :2156 ("What separates the two parts is epistemic grade rather than subject matter"), because by :1812's own statement Part II contains Level-2 theorems.

Sentences that assert Level-3 content in the declarative voice of a Part I (Level 1 or 2) section:
- :1677 and :1727: the Wheeler realization (L13-1).
- :1775: "Each token functions as an autonomous agent", and PriorBank entries "are the cross-scale shadows of the corpus-level meta-agent's belief field", although no corpus-level meta-agent exists in the transformer.
- :1788: "In natural language, gauge curvature manifests as semantic ambiguity" and "Garden path sentences represent local curvature spikes", although curvature vanishes identically in Regime I (:1780).
- :1800: the noumenal positioning, placed in the Part I Discussion.
- :1806: "a toy-model demonstration that spacetime-like geometry might emerge", although the pullback there is Riemannian and anything spacetime-like needs P4 or P5.

Two support claims in the same sections have zero severity. "The recovery of attention on a language-modeling task supports an interpretation in which language is at root a multi-agent coordination problem" (:1775): the recovery is analytic and independent of any task. "Consistent with a gauge-theoretic reading of attention" (:1806): no matched baseline exists, and :126 concedes the trend "cannot be attributed to capacity rather than to the accompanying change in optimization schedule". Any width sweep of any architecture would have passed both.
**Fix:** Redefine the levels by grade: "Level 1: proved (P) or measured (E); Level 2: constructed or implemented, behavior untested (D, C); Level 3: physical or phenomenal interpretation (S)". Place the no-go theorem and the signature propositions in Level 1. Delete the "epistemic grade rather than subject matter" sentence or make it true, and apply the table below to the leaks.
**Falsifies:** A reading of :98-:102 under which a single level assignment determines a claim's P/D/S/E/C status.
**Status:** Taxonomy defect; the leaked sentences are S or C stated as fact.

### L13-10: Three philosophy-of-science citations do not support their use
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:2127 (Laudan and Leplin), :2125 (Laudan1984), :79 and :1800 (Ladyman-Ross PPC).
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** (a) :2127 says the free-energy tie-breakers "resolve rather than represent underdetermination, consistent with Laudan and Leplin's argument ... and supporting constructive empiricism and scientific pluralism over convergent realism". Laudan and Leplin (J. Phil. 88, 449-472, 1991) argue that empirically equivalent theories "may admit of differing degrees of evidential support". That is an evidential tie-breaker, and it undercuts the empirical-equivalence argument usually offered for antirealism. Consensus and simplicity, used as tie-breakers, are pragmatic virtues that van Fraassen (1980) holds do not warrant belief. The cited work therefore does not support the "over convergent realism" conclusion; at best the inference is neutral. (b) :2125 cites Laudan 1984 (*Science and Values*) for "Laudan's problem-solving account of scientific rationality". That account is Laudan 1977, *Progress and Its Problems*; *Science and Values* presents the reticulated model. (c) :79 and :1800 say the Primacy of Physics Constraint "defines Ladyman-Ross OSR". In *Every Thing Must Go* (2007, ch. 1) the PPC is a methodological principle of their naturalized metaphysics, alongside the Principle of Naturalistic Closure, while OSR is the metaphysical thesis. Reversing the PPC departs from their naturalism, not from OSR as such; French's OSR does not rest on the PPC. I checked (c) against the standard formulation in the book, not a page scan.
**Fix:** (a) "which break ties pragmatically; whether such criteria are evidential (Laudan and Leplin 1991) or pragmatic (van Fraassen 1980) is left open"; (b) cite Laudan 1977; (c) "reverses the Primacy of Physics Constraint of Ladyman and Ross's naturalized metaphysics".
**Falsifies:** A passage in Laudan and Leplin 1991 endorsing constructive empiricism over realism, or a passage in *Science and Values* presenting the problem-solving model as its account.
**Status:** Citation-support defects; the surrounding claims are S.

## Physics-audience and standalone notes

A physics referee (Foundations of Physics, J. Math. Phys., J. Stat. Phys., or Phys. Rev. E for Part I) will stumble on six things.

(1) A large share of the introduction, and all of :2086-2132, is philosophy of mind and philosophy of science.
