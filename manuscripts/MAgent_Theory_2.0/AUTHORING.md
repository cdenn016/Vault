# MAgent Theory 2.0 authoring conventions

The main entry point is `MAgent_Theory_2.0.tex`. The separate proof supplement is `MAgent_Theory_2.0_supplement.tex`. The explanatory companion is `MAgent_Theory_2.0_companion.tex`, with chapters and editable vector figures under `companion/`. All three compile within this directory and use its local style and bibliography. The main manuscript must be readable without the supplement. The companion is self-contained and explains the constructions through physical pictures, worked examples, and short derivations.

The subject is a gauge-covariant theory of agents that hold beliefs and generative models, carry general probability laws and internal records, interact through separate belief and model channels, and may form inherited recursive organization. Exact constructions, identities, and counterexamples take priority over approximation estimates. Do not add a renormalization or error-bound program by default. Elementary variational inequalities remain permissible when they are part of an exact identity or optimization result.

## Writing and structure

Write concise, connected American English prose. Define every space and map before using it. Give the hypotheses inside each result and provide human-readable proofs. Important constructions and their decisive proofs belong in the main text or its appendices. Secondary algebra and supporting proofs belong in the separate supplement. Avoid duplicated proofs, repeated disclaimers, proof outlines presented as proofs, status badges, and end-of-chapter summaries that merely repeat the chapter.

Use semantic numbered headings supplied by `preamble.tex`, and refer to labels through `\cref` or `\Cref`. Punctuate display equations as parts of sentences. Do not use `\;`, `\,`, or `\!`, bullet lists in mathematical prose, decorative horizontal rules, or formulaic promotional language. Tables may collect notation. Statements should distinguish definitions, constitutive choices, proved consequences under stated assumptions, and open problems.

No document may cite or link another unpublished manuscript by the author. All required definitions, constructions, and proofs must appear locally. Cite only external literature. When a result is an instance of named classical mathematics (lumpability and Markov functions, sufficiency and Blackwell comparison, Cencov monotonicity, Shahshahani and replicator Fisher dynamics, the mechanical connection and O'Neill quotient metrics, symmetric criticality and fixed-point subspaces, asymptotic phase, centroid clustering), say so and cite the primary source; state what is new relative to it. Do not include repository paths, implementation modules, workflow history, review records, or tool names in the scientific text. Build instructions and source-work records remain outside that text.

## Notation

Notation uses a base `\mathcal C`, a principal bundle `P`, state and model spaces `\mathsf K,\mathsf M`, joint agent law `\zeta_i(dk,dm)=q_i(dk\mid m)s_i(dm)`, and marginals `q_i,s_i`. The product restriction is `\zeta_i=q_i\otimes s_i`. Use sector superscripts `q,s` for links, generators, reference laws, frames, and interface maps. Use `\beta` and `\gamma` for their respective source rows. A record readout `\rho_i:\mathsf S_i\to\mathsf O_i` is distinct from its random value and from the kernel that produces it. The complete configuration is `x\in\mathsf S`; reserve sample coordinates `k,m` for represented possibilities.

One structure group `G` acts on both sectors through two representations, `\varrho_q` on the belief space `V_q` (dimension `K`) and `\varrho_s` on the model space `V_s` (dimension `\tilde K`). There is one principal bundle `P` and two associated content bundles `\mathcal E_q,\mathcal E_s`; the statistical bundle carries laws over their fiber product with the diagonal action. A gauge transformation of an agent is one element `g_i\in G` acting on both sectors; the product group `G_q\times G_s` is not used. Bundle morphisms `\Phi,\tilde\Phi` of the two content bundles are induced by one principal bundle map through intertwining maps. Group-valued links are `\theta_{ij},\tilde\theta_{ij}\in G` with generators `\omega_{ij},\tilde\omega_{ij}\in\mathfrak g`, acting as `\Theta^q_{ij}=\varrho_q(\theta_{ij})` and `\Theta^s_{ij}=\varrho_s(\tilde\theta_{ij})`. The general theory keeps both link fields; the main focus is the tied case `\theta_{ij}=\tilde\theta_{ij}`, which results must state as a hypothesis. The sector mismatch `\Xi_{ij}=\tilde\theta_{ij}\theta_{ij}^{-1}` is covariant under conjugation at the receiver.

Default interaction graphs are directed. Opposite attending edges need not be inverses. Geometric reversal of a particular transport is its inverse. A self channel is constitutive data: identity by default, nonidentity where a construction declares it (as in the Wishart and open-law constructions). Primitive self-comparisons and inherited coarse loop information must be distinguished. Finite-dimensional matrices and finite alphabets are examples, not restrictions of the measure-theoretic definitions. A smooth statistical family is an additional hypothesis wherever derivatives are taken.

## General laws and Gaussian simplifications

The theory is stated for general probability laws on standard Borel spaces, and its definitions, objectives, and constructions must not depend on Gaussian structure. Gaussian laws, linear-Gaussian records and codes, and linearizations at a tangent point are computational simplifications: they make examples explicit, give closed forms, and keep numerical checks cheap. A result proved only in such a family states the family in its hypotheses, says which conclusions are expected to hold for general laws and which are features of the family, and poses the general-law version as an open problem when it is not proved. Features of the Gaussian family include posterior covariances that do not depend on the data, mean-field means that equal exact posterior means, closed-form Schur complements and log-normalizers, finite-dimensional sufficient statistics, and the transitive action of affine groups on Gaussian laws; none of them may be presented as a property of the theory. Where a general-law statement is available, such as a chain-rule, data-processing, or disintegration identity, state it first and give the Gaussian case as its instance. The same rule applies to the working documents in this directory.

## Beliefs, generative models, and the three objectives

An agent holds beliefs and generative models. A model presentation `m` indexes a generative model: the evaluator `G_i(m,dk)` is its prior over represented contents and a record kernel `\ell_i(do\mid k,m)` is its likelihood. The reference `r_i` is the agent's prior over presentations. The recognition law `\zeta_i`, with marginals `q_i` (belief, perception) and `s_i` (belief over generative models), approximates the posterior of that model. A model law alone predicts nothing; prediction requires the evaluator and record kernel. Agents of one kind (a species) share a generative model up to frame: equal presentation spaces with references and evaluators related by a frame map. Agreement of model laws transfers to agreement of predictions only with matched evaluators, preparation, and information.

Three objectives are studied side by side and must never be conflated.

1. The global picture: one normalized joint over the contents and records of all agents, with product recognition. Each agent's local free energy is the global free energy as a function of its own block, so local updates are coordinate descent of one functional. Peers enter only through expectations of their contents, linearly in their laws.
2. The per-agent picture: each agent has its own normalized generative model in which its own evaluator and the channelled laws of its peers are mixture sources for its contents (and its own reference and peers' channelled model laws are mixture sources for its presentations), with latent source labels and record likelihoods. Peers' laws are data for the agent, not variables it controls. The resulting dynamics is non-reciprocal and generally has no global Lyapunov functional.
3. The reciprocal law potential `def:dyn-potential` (the cooperative consensus potential). Its Fisher gradient flow moves each source to reduce the costs of the receivers that attend to it. It is not the free energy of any fixed normalized model in which `q_i,s_i` are recognition laws; the chapter on dynamics proves this obstruction. Chapters 6 to 12 study this flow. Do not call it inference without qualification, and do not transfer its conclusions to the other two pictures without proof.

Records enter both free-energy pictures as likelihood terms and persist in the objective. In the reciprocal potential they act only as declared assimilation events; state that composition explicitly where it is used. Relating the three pictures (consistency of per-agent models with a common joint, the linear-Gaussian families on which their stationary points may coincide, and whether a shared species model with matched evaluators yields a common joint) is an open program, stated as such.

## Formation standard

`inh:recursive-formation` is the formation standard. A construction is a recursive formation only if it satisfies every clause: a declared admitted class open in a declared topology (or a declared invariant family); state-determined handoffs selecting nontrivial blocks with a persistence criterion; inherited laws, evaluators, responses, and dynamics with the probe class fixed before the retention; compression (each handoff retention is non-injective on the post-handoff invariant set and some admitted child-level probe distinguishes a fiber); interaction necessity (the membership history differs from that of the null flow, with peer costs removed and everything else fixed, on a nonempty open subset of the admitted class); and a second stage that depends on the first (it does not form from every counterfactual handoff in the inherited class). Amplification relative to the null flow and the strong form of interaction necessity are higher grades. A construction that meets the inheritance and selection clauses but fails compression or interaction necessity is called a two-stage selection with exact inheritance, not formation. Report the null-flow control for every selection result.

Inherited parent dynamics and boundary responses are the main formation obligations. Static marginalization, posterior approximation, and dynamical inheritance are different claims. Do not claim endogenous formation, recurrent formation, derived base geometry, or emergent timescale separation from a definition or a supplied example.

## Regularity and gauge

The Fisher flow is proved on finite positive tables. Any use on densities, circles, or general standard Borel spaces requires a declared chart (for example bounded log-density charts in a Banach space), stated Lipschitz hypotheses on the first variation, well-posedness, and uniqueness of every coarse flow whose autonomy is claimed. State these hypotheses in the result.

Per-agent frame covariance of the constitutive data is automatic for dressed quantities and constrains nothing by itself; the substantive symmetry requirement is reference equivariance at a context, which must be imposed explicitly where used. The bundle connection does dynamical work only where a vector field, row, channel, or record depends on it; otherwise it is a diagnostic of the evolved sections, and the text says so. Words such as "gauge", "physical", and "public" require a declared observable algebra: say which agents, channels, records, or external probes can resolve the distinction in question.

Covariant pullback geometry is a main construction on an explicitly regular statistical tier. Use the Fisher metric of the actual joint law; a marginal sum is its metric only under the appropriate product restriction. Passive gauge invariance does not remove dependence on the chosen connection. The collective normal equation determines a connection class modulo infinitesimal isotropy, with comparison maps and weights declared; it is the shape-space pullback and the mechanical connection of the declared comparison data, and it is singular where isotropy jumps. Fixed Markov channels contract Fisher information, whereas moving channels include their own derivative. Vanishing horizontal defect is sufficient, not necessary, for pullback contraction. Dynamical, response, and collective-connection inheritance are separate conditions. The determinant/Lorentz and circle construction is an optional example with extra data; positive Fisher geometry does not create indefinite signature. Do not identify sample reference coordinates with the contextual base or infer selection and persistence from an available full-rank first jet.

## Scope of the existing constructions

All constructions in chapters 6 to 12 concern the reciprocal law potential (or, in chapter 6, a nonsmooth modification of it) and must say so.

The finite-time fusion construction uses a nonsmooth square-root-KL interaction on a restricted lognormal family; its links are pure gauge and every datum synchronizes completely in finite time. Do not transfer its conclusions to the original smooth KL dynamics or to internally heterogeneous parents.

The smooth conditional-spectrum construction uses fixed independent private reference factors and receiver conditional reconstruction channels. It is an exact general-law state completion in which the private sector acts on the public flow as open-loop forcing, not a finite-parameter closure or a theorem for every channel; its general-space form needs the stated regularity hypotheses. Its marked version preserves only the declared feedback protocols. The smooth erasure example uses an engineered flat channel gate and a finite product preparation. Its heterogeneity is transient, and its empirical parent keeps all public child states. Do not describe this as discovered sufficient interfaces, compression of those public states, persistent heterogeneous organization, or recursive smooth formation.

The collective-phase projection uses the original Gibbs-reduced KL potential, identity channels, a fixed coupled evaluator, and full bounded log-density charts near a stable common reference; it is a Banach-chart instance of asymptotic phase. Ordinary fresh child sampling fails to factor through the phase. Do not call the projection an inherited complete interface, a selected hierarchy, persistent heterogeneous organization, or recursive formation. The Gibbs identifiability theorem applies to unrestricted identity-channel probes; for a fixed channel it identifies only the channelled laws.

The recursive boundary-closure theorem uses a supplied binary tree, public sign partitions, and arbitrary bounded positive profiles over a general base. It is an equivariant symmetry reduction: the discarded public partition data are invisible to the fine energy, metric, and admitted records, and they also decay. Retain the nested unordered profile tree, not a flat multiset. Its class cost is a forward/reversed pair-law KL and generally differs from KL to the averaged source. Fresh uniform-descendant samples and common base-record conditioning descend; shared-child reuse and unrestricted external probes do not. Phase losses are continuous in total variation but have no Fisher tangent. Do not claim endogenous membership selection, arbitrary regrouping, a fixed-size parent, or smooth dimensional compression on an open law chart.

The Wishart construction uses common-precision matrix Wishart laws, context-constant individual shapes, a constant model-independent evaluator, product recognition, global moment-dependent normalized channels, exact Gibbs rows, and a fixed threshold membership rule. Its membership schedule is the centroid dendrogram of the initial belief shapes and is independent of the coupling; every result holds at zero coupling. It is therefore a two-stage selection with exact inheritance, not a recursive formation in the sense of the formation standard. Preserve the complete channel derivative and the primitive population count at every join. The lost model-shape coordinate is dynamically inert, and the inherited contextual geometry decays.

The open-law construction uses six agents on bit-circle sample spaces, fixed bit-preserving/Haar-angle channels, uniform proper evaluators, all-source priors including self, exact Gibbs rows, and unit Fisher mobility. It proves two selected stages on a full open positive-law neighborhood over a finite horizon. Selection reads only bits; the angular quotient descends for every partition at every time, and the handoff loss is created by the response interface, which reads angles in a frame held by an external querier. In the proof regime the membership history is reproduced by the null flow, so the theorem does not establish interaction necessity there; state the control. Its exact inherited vector field includes moving-canonicalization terms; do not replace it with the Fisher gradient on the canonical product slice. Persistence is finite-window, the witness is nonconstructive at the proof's temperatures, and the curvature comes from the declared contextual family. No newly created redundancy, generic initialization result, global phase-root representative, fixed-size parent, or unlimited recursion is established.
