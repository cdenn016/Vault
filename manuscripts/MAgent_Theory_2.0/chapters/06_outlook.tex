\chapter{Beyond the Existence Constructions}
\label{ch:outlook}

The theory distinguishes three objectives for interacting agents. In the global picture one normalized joint covers the contents and records of every agent, and local updates are coordinate descent of one free energy (\cref{prop:dyn-global-local}). In the per-agent picture each agent has its own generative model in which its own evaluator and its peers' communicated laws compete as sources of prior information (\cref{prop:dyn-per-agent}). The reciprocal law potential is a third object, and \cref{prop:dyn-no-global-vfe} shows that it is not the free energy of any fixed normalized model in which the agent laws are recognition laws. Every construction of \cref{ch:fusion} to \cref{ch:open-formation} concerns the reciprocal potential or, in \cref{ch:fusion}, a nonsmooth modification of it. \Cref{ch:per-agent-inheritance} separately derives the own-block forces and null control of normalized per-agent examples. It establishes persistent interaction-dependent grouping and a conditional compressive parent; it does not transfer the earlier reciprocal theorems wholesale.

Measured against the formation standard \cref{inh:recursive-formation}, no construction in this manuscript is yet a recursive formation under the smooth potential. The Wishart construction of \cref{ch:conditional-formation} selects two parents and then their union with exact original-time inheritance, but its membership schedule is the centroid dendrogram of the initial belief shapes and does not depend on the coupling; it is a two-stage selection with exact inheritance. The six-agent construction of \cref{ch:open-formation} admits a full open neighborhood of positive laws and inherits a moving-representative flow, but in the regime of its proof the null flow reproduces its membership history, and its handoff loss is created by the response interface rather than by the selection. Both smooth constructions supply their compression architectures. Their quotients exist before membership occurs, so the dynamics selects when a description is used without creating a new operational equivalence. The nonsmooth fusion construction of \cref{ch:fusion} meets interaction necessity at the cost of changing the interaction, and its fused parents compress nothing. Every construction is therefore a single-stage or two-stage selection with exact inheritance, and each chapter states which clauses of the standard fail.

The supplied-architecture results retain their scopes. The profile hierarchy of \cref{ch:boundary-closure} is an equivariant symmetry reduction with a supplied tree; the data it discards are invisible to the fine energy, metric, and admitted records. The collective phase of \cref{ch:public-compression} is an asymptotic-phase projection that loses ordinary child sampling. The erasure construction of \cref{ch:smooth-erasure} keeps public child states and its heterogeneity is transient. The per-agent parent of \cref{pa:phase-parent} preserves complete admitted histories and persistent records, while \cref{pa:geometry-inheritance,pa:nonflat-window} preserve the actual collective geometry on sections with context-constant discarded phases. Its phase quotient is also supplied before grouping. Adding a future hidden-angle probe destroys that quotient, and joining already canonical parents erases no new coordinate. Response sufficiency depends on the interface: an unrestricted identity-channel Gibbs interface identifies distinct exposed laws, and child-specific probes, exact constituent-law records, or feedback can require discarded distinctions.

The collective alternative of \cref{ch:collective-local-parent} exposes every proper block through fixed relative-angle probes and permits one common physical orientation to be lost only collectively. Its reference-memory realization closes a local two-jet in the original time and preserves the actual normalized-law Fisher cost. A finite preparation generator yields a complete conditional whole-patch realization, with the full chronological record retained. The causal accumulator of \cref{lp:causal-flow} instead integrates the local angular velocity from the actual preparation time. It preserves the original native dynamics and every admitted joint history, while \cref{lp:causal-coherence} requires initial collective-phase coherence for actual geometric inheritance. That condition is not generated by the memory. \Cref{lp:initial-phase-datum} identifies the missing initial contextual phase relation for arbitrary preparations. \Cref{lp:path-memory-obstruction,lp:causal-jet-obstruction} exclude specified value-path and smooth bounded-jet causal-memory classes, in addition to the finite-order and finite global-encoding obstructions. They leave the unrestricted causal-local-parent question inconclusive. More general admissible memory mechanisms, interaction-created invisibility and a new second compressive handoff remain unresolved.

The remaining problems are more specific than an existence question. \Cref{inh:formation-open} asks for a construction that meets every clause of the standard with a derived selector and interface. The per-agent example meets interaction necessity at one stage, but still needs an interaction-selected sufficient interface and a second compressive handoff with counterfactual dependence. Under the reciprocal potential, interaction-sensitive selection requires receiver-dependent targets, since shared references with additive moment channels make every pairwise law comparison relax on a common clock. \Cref{op:dyn-three-pictures} asks how the three objectives are related and which conclusions survive a change of objective. The connection of \cref{ch:geometry} does dynamical work only where a vector field, row, channel, or record depends on it; in the present constructions it is a diagnostic of the evolved sections, and giving it a dynamical role is open. Full permutation symmetry still constrains deterministic law-only selection as in \cref{inh:selector-symmetry}. Persistent membership under the fixed per-agent record episode does not extend its geometric theorem beyond the declared finite window. None of the results establishes an indefinite spacetime metric, a thermodynamic limit, typicality under random initialization, or unbounded recursive formation.
