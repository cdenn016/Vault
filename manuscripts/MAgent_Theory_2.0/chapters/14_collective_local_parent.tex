\chapter{Collective Compression and Conditional Local Parents}
\label{ch:collective-local-parent}

This chapter concerns the per-agent objective under the Gibbs-reduced dynamics, as in \cref{ch:per-agent-inheritance}. The phase parent of \cref{pa:phase-parent} erases a distinction already invisible on each primitive port. A collective alternative transmits the second angular law between peers and admits relative-angle responses. Each proper block is then exposed by a response across its boundary, while a retention of the whole connected population can erase one common orientation. That orientation is gauge relative to every algebra declared here, so the whole-block retention fails compression (F4) of \cref{inh:recursive-formation}, and the construction is a single-stage selection with exact inheritance, not a recursive formation. This chapter constructs that quotient and a local realization with finite reference memory. A finite preparation generator gives a second realization. A causal local phase memory removes the reference evaluation when the initial collective direction is coherent across context. That preparation condition is explicit; it is not generated by the recurrence. The unrestricted local-parent question remains inconclusive, with obstructions for specified memory classes rather than every possible mechanism.

\section{A transmitted angle}

There are three agents with the normalized per-agent model of \cref{def:dyn-per-agent}. Relative to a uniform sign and two normalized Haar circles, write
\begin{align}
 q_i(z,\phi,\psi)&=Z_i^{-1}
 \exp\{l_i z/2+A_i\cdot e_\phi+zC_i\cdot e_\phi+B_i\cdot e_\psi\},
 \label{lp:law}\\
 Z_i&=I_0(|B_i|)
 \frac{e^{l_i/2}I_0(|A_i+C_i|)+e^{-l_i/2}I_0(|A_i-C_i|)}2.
 \nonumber
\end{align}
Here $e_\theta=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ and $A_i,C_i,B_i\in\R^2$. The presentation probability is
\begin{equation}
 s_i(w)=\frac{e^{n_iw/2}}{2\cosh(n_i/2)},\qquad w\in\{-1,1\}.
 \label{lp:presentation}
\end{equation}
The self evaluator is $G_i=e^{Jz\cos\phi}/I_0(J)$, with $J=\epsilon^3$, and the self presentation reference is uniform. Source priors in both sectors are $1/5$ for self and $2/5$ for each peer. Rows are reduced exactly at unit temperature, and the dynamics is the chart version of the Gibbs-reduced per-agent dynamics of \cref{def:dyn-per-agent-flow} on $\{-1,1\}\times S^1\times S^1$.

The nonself content channel copies $z,\psi$ and resamples $\phi$ from Haar measure. Define $h_0(B)=\log I_0(|B|)$ and $M(B)=\nabla h_0(B)$. The transmitted density is
\begin{equation}
 \nu_j(z,\phi,\psi)=\frac{e^{L_jz/2}}{\cosh(L_j/2)}
 \frac{e^{B_j\cdot e_\psi}}{I_0(|B_j|)},\qquad
 L_j=l_j+h_0(A_j+C_j)-h_0(A_j-C_j).
 \label{lp:peer}
\end{equation}
The sign reference is uniform, so its density differs by a factor of two from its probability mass. Presentation peers use the identity channel. Every source remains normalized.

Evidence uses the positive normalized likelihood
\begin{equation}
 \ell_{r,\lambda}(o,\omega\mid z,\phi,\psi,w)
 =\frac{e^{ro z/2}}{2\cosh(r/2)}
 \frac{e^{\lambda\cos(\omega-\phi)}}{I_0(\lambda)},
 \label{lp:likelihood}
\end{equation}
relative to sign counting measure and angular Haar measure. Every admitted finite-strength evidence transaction appends its actual outcome and leaves its likelihood in the objective. Write $H_i=\sum r o$ and $\Lambda_i=\sum\lambda e_\omega$ for its persistent coefficients, retaining the entire ordered word as well. A passive marked frame change rotates the local embeddings of these fixed lab labels and the evaluator direction. There is one marked structure group $G=S^1$ with trivial representation on the presentation sector. The common rotation of every $\psi_i$ is a separate action, outside the marked structure group. It is a symmetry of every constitutive datum: the self evaluator and the likelihood do not read $\psi$, the peer channels copy it, and the rows read only $B_j\cdot M(B_i)$ and $|B_j|$. The distinction it relates is therefore gauge relative to every observable algebra declared in this chapter (\cref{def:observable-algebra}). Only the child-level law diagnostic $\E e^{\mathrm i\Psi_i}$, which no agent, channel, record or admitted query reads, resolves it.

\propositionheading{The exact transmitted-angle field}{lp:native-field}
On the positive family \cref{lp:law}, put $m_i=\tanh(L_i/2)$ and $t_i=\E_{q_i}[Z\cos\Phi]$. The content Gibbs weights and own-block Fisher equations are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 w_{ii}&=\frac{e^{Jt_i}}{5I_0(J)},&
 w_{ij}&=\frac{2e^{L_jm_i/2+B_j\cdot M(B_i)}}
 {5\cosh(L_j/2)I_0(|B_j|)},\\
 \beta_{ij}&=w_{ij}/\sum_r w_{ir},&& j\ne i,
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:weights}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot l_i&=-l_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}L_j+H_i,&
 \dot A_i&=-A_i+\Lambda_i,\\
 \dot C_i&=-C_i+\beta_{ii}Je_1,&
 \dot B_i&=-B_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}B_j,\\
 \dot n_i&=-n_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\gamma_{ij}n_j.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:field}
\end{equation}
Here $\gamma$ is the original presentation Gibbs row. Every equation uses the original inference time.
\begin{proof}
Let $S_i=\E_{q_i}\log q_i$. The self cost is $S_i-Jt_i+\log I_0(J)$ and the peer cost is
$S_i-L_jm_i/2+\log\cosh(L_j/2)-B_j\cdot M(B_i)+h_0(B_j)$.
Their common entropy cancels from the content row, giving \cref{lp:weights}. The Gibbs envelope removes derivatives of the optimizing row from the own-objective first variation. The centered logarithmic Fisher velocity is a linear combination of $z,e_\phi,ze_\phi,e_\psi$. Matching these coefficients gives \cref{lp:field}. Their covariance is positive definite: a linear combination constant under a positive density has zero nonconstant Fourier and sign coefficients. This gives tangency of the chart field to the family. The evaluator and likelihood have bounded log densities, the logarithm of the peer output density $E_\lambda[e^{f_j}\mid Z,\Psi]/E_\lambda e^{f_j}$ is a $2$-Lipschitz function of the sender's log density $f_j$ in the supremum norm, and the Gibbs rows are smooth in bounded costs, so the sufficient conditions stated after \cref{def:dyn-per-agent-flow} hold and the chart flow has unique solutions. The family is therefore invariant, and \cref{lp:field} is its exact Fisher velocity, without a Euclidean approximation or source-role forces from other agents' objectives.
\end{proof}

With the initial record pattern $(+,+,-)$ held fixed, this model has the grouping of \cref{pa:angular-grouping}, for the selector family $S_c$ of \cref{pa:selector} applied to the public odds $L_i$ and the same constant sets. Throughout, $0<\epsilon\leq1/100$ is below the threshold of that theorem, $|A_i(0)|\leq1/20$, $|C_i(0)|\leq\epsilon^3$ and $\delta\max_i|b_i|\leq1/20$, so that the null box below contains the initial set. To see why the transmitted angle does not invalidate the argument, take $B_i=\delta b_i$ with $b_i$ in a compact pointed cone and nonzero. Relative to the common term, the extra peer log weight is $-\KL(\mathrm{vM}(B_i)\Vert\mathrm{vM}(B_j))$ and the self log weight gains $-\KL(\mathrm{vM}(B_i)\Vert H)$, so the new term in the row logarithm is $O_{C^1}(\delta^2)$ on bounded scaled charts. With $u=l/\epsilon$ and $C/\epsilon^3$ scaled, the public limiting field is
\begin{equation}
 \dot u=\left(-\frac75I+\frac25\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\right)u
 +(1,1,-1)^\top+O_{C^1}(\epsilon^2+\delta^2),\qquad
 u_*=(15/7,15/7,5/7).
 \label{lp:selection-limit}
\end{equation}
The remaining scaled blocks have a symmetric negative-definite limiting derivative. For a compact constant set $\mathsf C\subset(1/8,1/3)$, a sufficiently small attraction ball retains positive public signs and pairwise magnitude ratios above $\max\mathsf C$, since the smallest equilibrium ratio is $1/3$. Initial $u$ near $(4,1/2,-1)$ has strict singleton margins. Finite-time dependence followed by the attracting-ball estimate gives a common entrance time $T$ and persistence with that initial episode fixed.

The label-preserving null deletes nonself costs while retaining their source labels, priors and row entropy. Its surviving self weight is not one. On $|l_i|\le12\epsilon$, $|A_i|\le1/10$, $|C_i|\le2\epsilon^3$, $|B_i|\le1/10$, the self KL bound of \cref{pa:null-bound} still gives $1/9\le\beta_{ii}^0\le1/5$. Its equations include $\dot l_i^0=-\beta_{ii}^0l_i^0+\epsilon o_i$ and $\dot B_i^0=-\beta_{ii}^0B_i^0$. The signed strip $1/4\le o_i l_i^0/\epsilon\le12$ is invariant, while $|L_i-l_i|<\epsilon/8$. The renormalized null has self weight one and the same equations with $\beta^0_{ii}=1$, and the same bounds hold along it. Thus the third public sign remains negative under both nulls and the full block is never selected. For constants in $(1/8,1/3)$, (F5) therefore holds against both nulls. For constants in $(1/3,1)$ the proof of \cref{pa:selector-threshold} applies with a remainder of order $\epsilon^2+\delta^2$: the full flow and both nulls select only the pair $\{1,2\}$, and (F5) fails. For constants in $(1/8,1/3)$ the frozen-environment control reproduces the full partition sequence, and under every constant the full flow, but neither null, reverses agent $3$'s public sign against its record. Sample the same state graph at fixed census times $0,T$, retaining both partition labels and times. Strict uniform margins make those outputs constant on the chosen contextual patch. No continuously reported parameter-dependent crossing time is inserted into a differentiable statistical experiment.

\section{A collective rather than primitive quotient}
\label{sec:lp-collective}

Declare the protocol before the handoff. It admits the public, marked-lab and presentation queries, original normalized evidence transactions and source-label readouts, and relative-angle queries on the complete graph. A joint relative query draws each involved primitive angle once and returns all requested differences. Shared vertices share samples; triangle outputs obey their cycle relation. Every transaction draws fresh primitive samples, and no sample is reused by a later transaction. A diagnostic appends history without becoming an assimilated likelihood. Policies are Borel kernels, independent of context, from the complete history, fixed lab data and optional independent randomization to admitted actions and waiting times, and every record density is jointly Borel in the outcome and the state (\cref{def:adaptive-history}). For the collective geometry the expected number of transactions in $[0,t]$ is bounded on compact contextual patches; without this bound the history experiment can fail to be differentiable in quadratic mean (\cref{geo:dqm}). No absolute $\psi$ response is admitted at any stage. In the terms of \cref{inh:protocol-classes}, $A_I$ consists of these queries and transactions addressed to a block through channels that read it as a whole, and $B_I$ of the same queries applied to individual members, or to sets of them, before the handoff, together with every channel, evaluator and record kernel; neither depends on the retention.

For $\zeta_i=[I_1(|B_i|)/I_0(|B_i|)]e^{\mathrm i\arg B_i}$, the pair moment is
\begin{equation}
 c_{ij}=\E e^{\mathrm i(\Psi_i-\Psi_j)}=\zeta_i\overline{\zeta_j},\qquad
 |\zeta_i|=\sqrt{\frac{|c_{ij}||c_{ik}|}{|c_{jk}|}}.
 \label{lp:relative-identification}
\end{equation}
The function $I_1(b)/I_0(b)$ has strictly positive derivative $\operatorname{Var}_b(\cos\Psi)$ for $b>0$. Thus the three pair laws identify the tuple up to one common rotation. With an exterior nonzero $\zeta_j$ fixed, $c_{ij}/\overline{\zeta_j}$ identifies every interior vector. Public and marked law queries identify $l,A,C$, and presentation queries identify $n$. The same erasure cannot therefore be performed independently on a proper block with its boundary retained. This is identification from exact response laws, not estimation from finitely many samples. The common symmetry preexists grouping; no interaction-created invisibility is asserted.

The whole-block retention erases only the common $\psi$ rotation, and every element of $B_I$, the joint relative queries included, is invariant under it at every time. That distinction is gauge relative to $B_I$, so the retention fails (F4) of \cref{inh:recursive-formation}. Its fibers are orbits of a symmetry of every constitutive datum and admitted kernel, and the separation they carry fades by the bound after \cref{lp:cone}. What the relative queries establish is that no proper block can be compressed with its boundary retained.

\section{Finite reference memory and closed local jets}

Let $x(c)=(l_i,A_i,C_i,B_i,n_i)_i\in\R^{24}$ be a $C^2$ section on a compact contextual patch, and fix $c_0$ before retention. At every context the same realized lab history is applied to \cref{lp:field}. Suppose the initial $B_i(c)$ lie in a common open pointed cone, with its rotational union admitted. For a vector $v$ positive on that cone,
\begin{equation}
 v\cdot B_i(c,t)\ge e^{-t}v\cdot B_i(c,0)>0.
 \label{lp:cone}
\end{equation}
This follows by variation of constants in the cooperative $B$ equation. Finite evidence words preserve it. The other natural parameters have finite-interval bounds from \cref{lp:field}, so every finite admitted branch remains regular. The same equation gives an upper bound. Since $\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}=1-\beta_{ii}$, an agent attaining $\max_j|B_j|$ has $\frac{d}{dt}|B_i|\leq-\beta_{ii}|B_i|$, so
\begin{equation}
 \max_i|B_i(t)|\leq\exp\Bigl(-\int_0^t\min_i\beta_{ii}\Bigr)\max_i|B_i(0)|
 \label{lp:decay}
\end{equation}
on every branch, because no admitted likelihood reads $\psi$. The transmitted sector therefore fades, and with it the relative moments, the anchor $S$ and the information that the common rotation carries: the quotient below erases an orientation of a sector that becomes informationless as $t\to\infty$, and its noninjectivity, like the nonflatness below, is significant only on finite windows.

Write $x_\circ=x(c_0)$, $S=\sum_i B_i(x_\circ)$ and
\begin{equation}
 U(S)=\frac1{|S|}\begin{pmatrix}S_x&S_y\\-S_y&S_x\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 y=\mathsf R_Ux(c),\qquad \widehat x_\circ=\mathsf R_Ux_\circ,
 \label{lp:retention}
\end{equation}
where $\mathsf R_U$ rotates only the $B$ blocks. This smooth formula uses no angle branch. A whole-section retention applies this one $U$ at every $c$ and removes precisely one context-constant common angle. The local realization below retains only the data required at $c$ and the reference.

\theoremheading{Conditional evaluation-local realization}{lp:local-realization}
Retain $j_c^2y$, the full finite native tuple $\widehat x_\circ$, all primitive ports, constitutive data, complete ordered history, census records and persistent likelihood coefficients. If record embeddings vary with context, retain their known two-jets. Give the past-law experiment its actual compatible initialization. These data have an autonomous original-time evolution and determine the exact local two-jet and every admitted future joint response/update kernel at $c$. They preserve the actual collective Fisher cost on the declared regular experiment. Initialization uses reference-context data and is not a rule from the germ at $c$ alone.
\begin{proof}
Let $V(x,c,h)$ denote \cref{lp:field}, let $V_S=\sum_i V_{B_i}(\widehat x_\circ,c_0,h)$, and let $\mathcal J$ act by $J_2=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ on each $B$ block and by zero elsewhere. Set
\begin{equation}
 \Omega(\widehat x_\circ,h)=\frac{\det(S,V_S)}{|S|^2},\qquad
 W(y,c,h;\widehat x_\circ)=V(y,c,h)-\Omega\mathcal Jy.
 \label{lp:omega}
\end{equation}
Equivariance and $\dot U=-\Omega J_2U$ give
\begin{equation}
 \dot y=W(y,c,h;\widehat x_\circ),\qquad
 \dot{\widehat x}_\circ=V(\widehat x_\circ,c_0,h)-\Omega\mathcal J\widehat x_\circ.
 \label{lp:closed-reference}
\end{equation}
The canonical anchor stays on $S_y=0,S_x>0$. The subtraction is the derivative of the retention, not a separately fitted parent force. This is the exact vector-field intertwining criterion of \cref{inh:vector-square}, an instance of exact lumping \citep{HorstmeyerAtay2016}. The anchor $U(S)$ is a slice with phase condition $S_y=0$ in the method of slices \citep{RowleyEtAl2003,BudanurEtAl2015}, and \cref{lp:closed-reference} is its in-slice equation with the phase condition evaluated at the reference context, as in \cref{open:anchor} and \cref{open:moving-flow}; the cone estimate keeps the flow away from the slice border $S=0$. What is new here is a common rotation exposed against every proper block, the per-agent field, and evaluation-local jets.

At fixed $t,h$ and prepared section, the reference is held fixed under evaluation differentiation in $c$. For contextual indices $r,s$,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot y_r&=W_y y_r+W_r,\\
 \dot y_{rs}&=W_y y_{rs}+W_{yy}[y_r,y_s]
 +W_{yr}y_s+W_{ys}y_r+W_{rs}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:jet-flow}
\end{equation}
The explicit partials hold $y,\widehat x_\circ,h$ fixed. Every state dependence of the Gibbs rows is differentiated. Since the native field reads no neighboring contexts or contextual derivatives, no third jet enters. Evidence adds the same saved likelihood at both evaluations without resetting either law. Diagnostics change only history. For a two-dimensional base the local law jet has $24(1+2+3)=144$ coordinates, plus 24 reference coordinates before constraints. The full ordered history remains unbounded in length; this is not a fixed-memory claim.

The actual parent laws are the normalized products of \cref{lp:law} in the $y$ coordinates, together with their original presentation and Gibbs-label factors. The reference is deterministic internal memory, not an extra independent sample or agent. The common $\psi$ rotation preserves every joint relative response, every other response and its actual next retained state. Equal histories therefore supply the same next action and wait law. Induction over the joint transaction kernels proves all admitted finite adaptive histories, including response/next-state dependence.

For an unconditional history/current experiment, retain an initial local two-jet and initial reference tuple in one canonical frame as a replay seed, together with the full protocol and chronological word. Applying the original normalized kernels defines its actual history law $\Pi_c$. A current tuple and one observed word alone do not determine that past probability. Conditioning on the initial public pattern retains its positive probability normalizer. No distribution over law configurations is introduced.

Finally, for fixed $t,h$, rotating the fresh transmitted-angle samples by $U(S(x_\circ^h(t)))$ is independent of the evaluation coordinate $c$. The joint map $T(h,Y)=(h,T_hY)$ preserves Haar measure and commutes with the marked action. Stored marked outcomes are fixed lab labels; their local direction embeddings transform. Hence, for
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal P_{c,t}(dh,dY)=\Pi_c(dh)Q_{c,t}^{h}(dY),\qquad
 \overline{\mathcal P}_{c,t}=T_*\mathcal P_{c,t},
 \label{lp:actual-experiment}
\end{equation}
the contextual score and marked orbit score are carried isometrically. For every $X,\xi$,
\begin{equation}
 \overline{\mathcal C}(X,\xi)
 =\E_{\overline{\mathcal P}}(\overline s_X+\overline\ell_\xi)^2
 =\E_{\mathcal P}(s_X+\ell_\xi)^2=\mathcal C(X,\xi).
 \label{lp:cost}
\end{equation}
This proves actual-law cost inheritance before minimization, including history and label scores. It gives the same minimizing affine class, isotropy and minimized tensor. On a locally free patch their connection and curvature agree.
\end{proof}

The retained reference contains no inverse rotation: $U(R_\alpha S)R_\alpha=U(S)$, so simultaneous rotation changes none of the canonical local, reference or seed data. It still changes the excluded child absolute-angle diagnostic. The reference retains the phase difference between $c$ and $c_0$, not the discarded global angle. For $B_i(c)=b_i e_{\chi(c)}$, the actual canonical density contains $b_i e_{\chi(c)-\chi(c_0)}$, and its $\psi$-phase information is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i b_i\frac{I_1(b_i)}{I_0(b_i)}(d\chi)^2>0.
 \label{lp:phase-information}
\end{equation}
Pointwise canonicalization would delete this term. Copying it into a stored tensor would not restore it to a normalized parent law.

A local initial two-jet has an actual law realization: use its degree-two natural-parameter polynomial near the evaluation point and then evolve that preparation by the exact pointwise field, without truncating the evolved density. Its two-jet agrees with the original there throughout the common finite regular interval by \cref{lp:jet-flow}. This supplies actual law germs and their connection curvature, but does not reconstruct the unknown original section away from that point. Compatible initial data over all contexts remain a field. Marked overlap maps preserve this compatibility because the $\psi$ canonicalization commutes with them.

For a common nonflat window take $A_1(c,0)=ae_\chi$, $A_2(c,0)=de_1$, with $a\in[a_-,a_+]$, $0<a_-<a_+<1/20$ and $0<d<1/20$ independent of $\epsilon,\delta$, and small noncollinear cone vectors $B_i=\delta b_i$ with $\delta\max_i|b_i|\leq1/20$. Keep the initial episode fixed. At the uncanonicalized $\epsilon=\delta=0$ comparison, put $a_t=e^{-t}a$ and $\mathcal D(t)=\sum_{i\ne1}\mathcal I(e^{-t}|A_i(0)|)>0$, where $\mathcal I(x)=xI_1(x)/I_0(x)$; $\mathcal D(t)$ is the marked inertia written $B(t)$ in \cref{pa:inertias}. The same minimization as in \cref{pa:nonflat-limit} gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A_{0,t}&=-\frac{\mathcal I(a_t)}{\mathcal I(a_t)+\mathcal D(t)}d\chi,\\
 \mathcal F_{0,t}&=-\frac{\mathcal D(t)e^{-t}\mathcal I'(a_t)}{[\mathcal I(a_t)+\mathcal D(t)]^2}da\wedge d\chi,\\
 h_{aa}^{0,t}&=e^{-2t}\operatorname{Var}_{a_t}(\cos\Phi)>0,\qquad
 h_{\chi\chi}^{0,t}=\frac{\mathcal I(a_t)\mathcal D(t)}{\mathcal I(a_t)+\mathcal D(t)}>0.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:nonflat}
\end{equation}
The cross coefficient vanishes and $\mathcal I'(a_t)>0$. As in \cref{ch:per-agent-inheritance}, this curvature is prepared in the initial section, is present at $t=0$ and at zero coupling, and has the same limit along the null flows. Compact finite-window margins persist for sufficiently small positive $\epsilon,\delta$: the field is real-analytic in the state and in $(\epsilon,\delta)$, so the exact finite-time flow and its two-jet depend continuously on those parameters uniformly on a common positive tube \citep[Chapter V]{Hartman2002}, and so do the normalized history kernels and Fisher integrals. Apply continuity to the fine laws, since the canonical anchor is singular at $\delta=0$, and apply \cref{lp:cost} only at positive $\delta$. This gives the same nonflat rank-two window containing $T$. Later records preserve inheritance, but need not preserve this grouping or nonflatness.

\section{Handoff frames and finite preparation generators}

Fix the census handoff $T$ and freeze $U_T=U(S(x_\circ(T)))$ once. Then $z(c,t)=\mathsf R_{U_T}x(c,t)$ obeys $\dot z=V(z,c,h)$ for $t\ge T$, with no moving-frame term. Its actual costs still satisfy \cref{lp:cost}. Future local evolution needs no live reference, but its initialization is reference-coherent memory, not an instantaneous quotient of $x(c,t)$ alone. Recovering the continuously reanchored representative later requires the reference evolution or its accumulated phase.

\propositionheading{Conditional finite preparation-and-history realization}{lp:generator}
Suppose the prepared section has a known finite $C^2$ generator $x_0(c)=f(c;p)$, with $p\in P\subset\R^m$, equivariant under the common $\psi$ rotation and with nonzero reference anchor. Retain its canonical coefficients $p_*$, the full chronological history and original time. Then the current contextual laws and their two-jets have an exact whole-patch realization using these data, with all the preceding operational and geometric inheritance properties.
\begin{proof}
Let $\mathcal E_{t,h}(c)$ be the chronological composition of the unique native flows and original fixed-record coefficient appends. Evaluate
\begin{equation}
 x_t^h(c;p_*)=\mathcal E_{t,h}(c)f(c;p_*).
 \label{lp:generator-evaluation}
\end{equation}
Between transactions $\dot p_*=0$, $\dot h=0$ and $\dot t=1$; cached local values satisfy the native field and \cref{lp:jet-flow}. At a transaction append its actual outcome and likelihood, if it is evidence. The original normalized response/update kernels evaluated on \cref{lp:generator-evaluation} generate its full path law. Physical equivariance removes exactly the common seed orientation; it is not stored in the canonical seed. Evaluate the same generator at $c_0$ if a moving representative is wanted. The sample rotation is again independent of evaluation $c$, proving \cref{lp:cost}. No projection onto a finite basis of evolved sections is used.
\end{proof}

For a nonempty family, take the marked patch used in \cref{lp:nonflat}, constant initial $l_i/\epsilon$ near $(4,1/2,-1)$, $C_i=0$, small fixed presentation laws and three context-constant noncollinear $b_i$ in the cone, with their rotational union. These are finite preparation coefficients, relatively open in their parameter manifold. They are not an open set of arbitrary $C^2$ functions. The same persistent grouping and nonflat window apply. The full word remains retained, and evaluation at a new context may require replay of its entire native history. Finite seed dimension gives neither constant evaluation cost nor bounded record memory.

\section{A causal local phase memory}

The reference frame can be replaced by an accumulated local phase on a restricted preparation family. Keep the same field $V$, common $\psi$ action $\mathsf R$, generator $\mathcal J$ and cone domain. At the actual initialization time $t_*$ define, using only the value at the evaluation context,
\begin{equation}
 S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x)=\sum_i B_i(x),\qquad
 \bar x=\mathsf R_{U(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x))}x.
 \label{lp:local-slice}
\end{equation}
The sum of the $B$ blocks of $\bar x$ lies on the positive first axis. Unlike the reference tuple, this slice uses no value at $c_0$. Define its local angular velocity by
\begin{equation}
 \omega_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x,c,h)
 =\frac{\det(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x),\sum_iV_{B_i}(\bar x,c,h))}
 {|S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x)|^2}.
 \label{lp:local-angular-velocity}
\end{equation}
It uses the actual native Gibbs rows, including their dependence on the local public and marked laws. It is not a function of the sum alone.

\propositionheading{Closed causal reconstruction in the original time}{lp:causal-flow}
Starting at $t_*$ with $\vartheta=0$, evolve
\begin{equation}
 \dot{\bar x}=W_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x,c,h)
 :=V(\bar x,c,h)-\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\mathcal J\bar x,
 \qquad \dot\vartheta=\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}.
 \label{lp:causal-system}
\end{equation}
The actual parent laws are the normalized native laws at
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde x=\mathsf R_{R_\vartheta}\bar x.
 \label{lp:causal-readout}
\end{equation}
They satisfy $\dot{\widetilde x}=V(\widetilde x,c,h)$ on every regular finite branch. Their local two-jet, together with that of $\vartheta$, closes at order two. Every original finite adaptive joint response/update history, persistent likelihood and census record is preserved, with a compatible actual preparation and past-law initialization.
\begin{proof}
The cone estimate \cref{lp:cone} keeps $S_{\mathrm{loc}}$ nonzero. Differentiating its canonicalizing matrix gives $\dot U=-\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}J_2U$. Physical equivariance therefore gives \cref{lp:causal-system}. Differentiating the actual readout cancels its rotating-coordinate term:
\begin{equation}
 \dot{\widetilde x}
 =\mathsf R_{R_\vartheta}(\dot{\bar x}+\dot\vartheta\mathcal J\bar x)
 =\mathsf R_{R_\vartheta}V(\bar x,c,h)
 =V(\widetilde x,c,h).
 \label{lp:causal-cancellation}
\end{equation}
Thus no independently fitted force or time change is used. Uniqueness, with the canonical initial value, gives
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde x(c,t)=\mathsf R_{U(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*)))}x(c,t).
 \label{lp:frozen-local-initial-frame}
\end{equation}
The memory may equivalently be an $\mathrm{SO}(2)$ variable satisfying $\dot g=\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}J_2g$, $g(t_*)=1$, so no global angle branch is required. The pair \cref{lp:causal-system} is the in-slice equation and the reconstruction equation of the freezing method with phase condition $S_y=0$ \citep{BeynThummler2004,RowleyMarsden2000}, and $(\bar x,\vartheta)$ are coordinates of the native solution started from the pointwise-canonicalized preparation, as \cref{lp:frozen-local-initial-frame} records. The remaining conclusions follow from equivariance of $V$ and invariance of the transaction kernels under a constant rotation.

For explicit contextual derivatives $a,b$ and state derivative $D$, the slice jets obey the prolongation \cref{lp:jet-flow} with $W_{\mathrm{loc}}$ in place of $W$. The phase jets, initialized to zero, obey
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot\vartheta_a&=D\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\bar x_a+(\omega_{\mathrm{loc}})_a,\\
 \dot\vartheta_{ab}&=D\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\bar x_{ab}
 +D^2\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}[\bar x_a,\bar x_b]\\
 &\quad+D(\omega_{\mathrm{loc}})_a\bar x_b
 +D(\omega_{\mathrm{loc}})_b\bar x_a+(\omega_{\mathrm{loc}})_{ab}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:causal-phase-jets}
\end{equation}
All explicit partials hold $\bar x,h$ fixed. In contrast to the fixed-reference derivative, the contextual derivatives of $\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}$ enter. The field has contextual order zero, so no third jet is needed. In each $\psi$ block the actual law derivatives are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \widetilde B_{i,a}&=R_\vartheta(\bar B_{i,a}+\vartheta_aJ_2\bar B_i),\\
 \widetilde B_{i,ab}&=R_\vartheta\bigl(\bar B_{i,ab}
 +\vartheta_aJ_2\bar B_{i,b}+\vartheta_bJ_2\bar B_{i,a}\\
 &\qquad+\vartheta_{ab}J_2\bar B_i-\vartheta_a\vartheta_b\bar B_i\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:causal-law-jets}
\end{equation}
Every row and embedding derivative is included. Actual slice-valued initial families realize compatible initial jets, and their native evolution realizes these law germs.

At each fixed $c$, \cref{lp:frozen-local-initial-frame} is a time-independent common $\psi$ rotation. All original transaction kernels are invariant under it, including the entire joint relative response with one sample per involved primitive vertex. Induction on responses, waits and updates proves equality of every admitted adaptive joint history. Evidence appends its actual persistent likelihood without jumping the law; diagnostics append history without assimilation. The laws, slice and phase memory are continuous at these transactions. Both original census records remain retained. A local canonical preparation seed and full chronological word, or their compatible initialized past-law experiment, supply the unconditional history law. They do not introduce a prior over prepared sections or an independently sampled memory factor.
\end{proof}

The recurrence implements a frozen local initial frame, whereas the earlier handoff construction froze a reference frame. Retain its actual accumulated value at a later census $T$. Initializing $\vartheta=0$ afresh at $T$ instead changes \cref{lp:frozen-local-initial-frame} to the frame at $T$; its compatibility with the original preparation is not automatic. Neither existing records nor elapsed phase memory may be reset. One finite state per context and its two-jet are still distinct from a finite global encoding of an unknown contextual function, and the complete word has unbounded length.

\corollaryheading{Actual geometric inheritance under initial coherence}{lp:causal-coherence}
In addition to \cref{lp:causal-flow}, suppose the actual preparation satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*))}{|S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*))|}
 =e_*\in S^1\qquad\text{for every }c\in\mathcal C,
 \label{lp:initial-coherence}
\end{equation}
where $e_*$ is independent of subsequent history and its absolute angle is not retained. The causal parent preserves the actual complete Fisher cost \cref{lp:cost}, hence the minimizing marked connection classes, isotropy, tensor and curvature. It removes one common $\psi$ orientation after all memory is counted. There is a nonempty family satisfying this condition with the grouping of \cref{pa:angular-grouping}, under the constant sets reported there, and a positive-rank nonflat window. The condition is sufficient; it is not shown to be necessary.
\begin{proof}
The hypothesis is exactly the condition that makes the sample map independent of context, so the conclusion is that of \cref{lp:cost} and of \cref{pa:cost-isometry}. Every local initialization in \cref{lp:local-slice} applies the same matrix $U_*$ under \cref{lp:initial-coherence}. The map of current samples in \cref{lp:frozen-local-initial-frame} is consequently independent of $c,t,h$, preserves Haar measure, leaves history and numerical lab labels fixed, and commutes with the marked action. Differentiating its normalized joint density proves \cref{lp:cost} for every contextual tangent and marked comparison velocity, including history and public-pattern normalization scores. The parent samples the laws at $\widetilde x$; the phase derivatives in \cref{lp:causal-law-jets} therefore enter its actual scores. Sampling only the slice laws and attaching a stored tensor would not give this equality.

Take the marked family of \cref{lp:nonflat} with context-constant initial $B_i=\delta b_i$ in the pointed cone, allowing noncollinear $b_i$. It satisfies \cref{lp:initial-coherence}; the finite-window continuity argument after \cref{lp:nonflat} then supplies positive rank and nonzero marked curvature for small positive $\epsilon,\delta$. The weak initial record episode is retained. The equality of actual costs transfers that same window. Later arbitrary evidence preserves exact dynamical and operational inheritance on each regular finite branch, while it need not preserve grouping or nonflatness.

The initial slice, its canonical replay seed, generated phase difference and complete history are invariant under a constant common $\psi$ rotation of the original preparation. Conversely, equal canonical initial fields under \cref{lp:initial-coherence} determine preparations differing by one context-constant rotation. The erased angle is therefore not restored by memory. Relative probes still expose every proper block against its exterior; no independently invisible primitive factor is added.
\end{proof}

Initial coherence is a preparation restriction, not a consequence of causal updating or an open condition among arbitrary contextual sections. For a general preparation write locally $\alpha_0(c)=\arg S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*))$. The actual sample map now depends on $c$. At corresponding samples its contextual score is
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde s_X(T_0Y)=s_X(Y)-(X\alpha_0)\ell_\psi(Y),
 \label{lp:initial-phase-score-defect}
\end{equation}
where $\ell_\psi$ is the score of positive common rotation of the $B_i$. Indeed $\psi_{\mathrm{parent}}=\psi_{\mathrm{fine}}-\alpha_0$ and $\ell_\psi=-\sum_i\partial_{\psi_i}\log q_i$. Thus operational history equality alone has not preserved the actual geometry; \cref{lp:phase-field-defect} below gives the exact defect. With $\alpha(c,t)=\arg S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t))$ locally,
\begin{equation}
 d_c\alpha(c,t)=d_c\alpha_0(c)
 +d_c\int_{t_*}^{t}\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}(c,s)ds.
 \label{lp:initial-phase-datum}
\end{equation}
Temporal integration supplies the second term, not the initial contextual one-form. This exact $\psi$-phase derivative is distinct from the marked minimizing connection, which can remain nonflat. No context-relocation action is part of the fixed protocol.

\lemmaheading{History decomposition and phase-field parents}{lp:phase-field-defect}
For the experiment \cref{lp:actual-experiment} with an admitted protocol, write $s^\Pi_X$ for the history score and $s^Q_X,\ell^Q_\xi$ for the conditional contextual and marked orbit scores given the history. Then
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C(X,\xi)=\E_\Pi(s^\Pi_X)^2+\E_\Pi\E_{Q^H}(s^Q_X+\ell^Q_\xi)^2,
 \label{lp:history-decomposition}
\end{equation}
so the minimizing marked velocity is a ratio of history averages, $-\E_\Pi\E_{Q^H}[s^Q_X\ell^Q_1]/\E_\Pi\E_{Q^H}(\ell^Q_1)^2$, and not an average of branchwise minimizers. Let a parent sample the native laws at the configuration obtained by rotating every $B_i$ by $-\Theta^H(c)$, where $\Theta^H$ is smooth in $c$ for each history. It has the same minimizing marked connection class and isotropy as the fine experiment, and its minimized tensor satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \bar h(X,X)-h(X,X)=\E_H\bigl[(X\Theta^H)^2I^H-2(X\Theta^H)b^H(X)\bigr],
 \label{lp:phase-defect-identity}
\end{equation}
with $I^H=\sum_i\mathcal I(|B_i^H|)$, $b^H(X)=\sum_i(X\arg B_i^H)\mathcal I(|B_i^H|)$ and $\mathcal I(x)=xI_1(x)/I_0(x)$.
\begin{proof}
Conditional scores have conditional mean zero given the history, so their cross terms with $s^\Pi_X$ vanish, and the history law has no marked orbit score because stored outcomes are fixed lab labels. This gives \cref{lp:history-decomposition}. Every admitted kernel is invariant under a common $\psi$ rotation, so the parent has the history law of the fine experiment, as in \cref{lp:phase-field-history}. At corresponding samples its conditional contextual score is $s^Q_X-(X\Theta^H)\ell_\psi$. Conditionally on the history each $\psi_i$ is independent of the marked, public and presentation factors and of the source labels, so $\ell_\psi$ is orthogonal to every marked orbit score, and the von Mises identities give $\E[s^Q_X\ell_\psi\mid H]=b^H(X)$ and $\E[\ell_\psi^2\mid H]=I^H$. Expanding the square, the part that depends on $\xi$ is unchanged, which proves the statements about the connection class and isotropy, and the minima differ by \cref{lp:phase-defect-identity}.
\end{proof}

The causal parent is the case $\Theta=\alpha_0$, independent of the history. Its connection class is therefore inherited exactly for every preparation, without the coherence hypothesis, and only its minimized tensor can change. The defect \cref{lp:phase-defect-identity} vanishes at a given time when $X\alpha_0=2b(X)/I$, so the identity does not show that \cref{lp:initial-coherence} is necessary at a single time; whether it is necessary on a time interval in the exact model is not established.

\remarkheading{The obstructions refer to the declared marked group}{lp:group-dependence}
Every datum of this model is invariant under the common $\psi$ rotation, so the group $S^1_{\mathrm{marked}}\times S^1_\psi$ acting on $(\phi,\psi)$ is an admissible declaration for \cref{geo:collective}. With it $\ell_\psi$ is an orbit score. A context-dependent common rotation changes the fine contextual score by a multiple of $\ell_\psi$, which the $\psi$ component of the comparison velocity absorbs: the minimized tensor is unchanged, and only the $\psi$ component of the minimizing velocity shifts by the phase gradient. Under the enlarged group pointwise canonicalization preserves the minimized tensor, and the score defect \cref{lp:initial-phase-score-defect}, the coherence hypothesis and the obstructions below lose their force. The obstructions are therefore statements about the marked group declared in this chapter, which leaves horizontal a phase field that no agent, channel or admitted query resolves; the construction gives no internal reason to prefer the smaller group.

\section{Scoped obstructions to stronger locality}

The reference dependence cannot be removed by merely storing its sum. For $B_1=B_3=be_1$, $B_2=be_2$, $A=C=0$, $l_1=l_3=0$ and $l_2=\varpi$, put
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 s=\frac1{5I_0(J)},\quad k=\frac2{5I_0(b)},\quad E=e^{bI_1(b)/I_0(b)},\\
 u=\operatorname{sech}(\varpi/2),\qquad N=s+kE+ku.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{lp:sum-example}
\end{equation}
The exact rows give
\begin{equation}
 \Omega=\frac15\left(\frac{4ku-2kE}{N}-\frac{2k}{s+2k}\right),\qquad
 \frac{d\Omega}{du}=\frac{4ks+6k^2E}{5N^2}>0.
 \label{lp:sum-failure}
\end{equation}
Thus $S=(2b,b)$ stays fixed while its angular velocity changes. Also, for $c\ne c_0$, a section can be changed only near $c_0$ without changing its germ at $c$, yet its canonical value at $c$ changes. These facts exclude sum-only closure and germ-only computation of this representative, not all alternative parent realizations.

\definitionheading{Rich preparation class}{lp:rich-class}
Fix a compact contextual patch, a finite record word and the window of \cref{lp:nonflat}. The rich class $\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$ consists of the smooth sections $x_0$ of initial native parameters in $\R^{24}$ whose $B_i$ lie in a common open pointed cone, with its rotational union admitted, and that satisfy the strict census and window margins of \cref{lp:nonflat} with the parameter ranges stated there. It is open in the $C^2$ topology within the smooth sections, and it is closed under $x_0\mapsto x_0+\tau\chi P$ for supported polynomial bumps $\chi P$ of every order and sufficiently small $\tau$, and under $x_0\mapsto\mathsf R_{\kappa}x_0$ for smooth $\kappa$ of sufficiently small $C^2$ norm. The coherent family of \cref{lp:causal-coherence}, the generator families of \cref{lp:generator} and the real-analytic sections are smooth but violate one of the two closure properties.

\theoremheading{Finite differential-order obstruction}{lp:jet-obstruction}
Consider sections in $\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$ (\cref{lp:rich-class}), which allows independent finite-jet variations. Suppose the parent's actual law is a smooth rule $\bar p(c)=F(c,j_c^k x,h)$ of some finite order. Local native-history summaries may be included if they are themselves finite-jet functions on the fixed-history stratum. There is no separately retained phase-bearing field or reference-context memory. If the actual minimizing marked connection classes and minimized tensors agree for every section in that class, then the actual-law rule is independent of positive-order jets. It cannot also erase the common $\psi$ angle while preserving that geometry.
\begin{proof}
For $k\ge1$ fix a $k$-jet and vary the next jet in a contextual direction $X\ne0$. Contraction of symmetric $(k+1)$-tensors with $X$ is onto symmetric $k$-tensors; a supported polynomial primitive realizes each variation. At the point the parent law, orbit map $\bar J$, fine first score and fine minimizing class are fixed. The parent tangent has the form $\bar u_X(\tau)=\bar u_X(0)+\tau w$. Choose one representative $A_f(X)$ of the common minimizing class. Equality of tensors requires
\begin{equation}
 \|\bar u_X(0)+\bar J A_f(X)+\tau w\|_{\bar g}^2
 =h_f^{\min}(X,X)
 \label{lp:highest-jet}
\end{equation}
on an interval. Its quadratic coefficient is $\bar g(w,w)$, so positivity forces $w=0$. Descending in derivative order removes all positive-order dependence of the actual law.

For random histories make the argument in the joint $L^2$ space. Vary the highest initial derivative while holding lower initial jets fixed. The history law and fine first scores stay fixed, and the highest current variation is transported by the invertible fixed-record native-flow derivative on each regular branch. If the parent generates its own history law from its laws at past transactions, its history score also varies affinely, by a history function $w^{\mathrm{hist}}(H)$ of mean zero; it is orthogonal to the conditional score variation $w_H$ in the joint $L^2$ space, so the quadratic coefficient is $\E|w^{\mathrm{hist}}|^2+\E_H\|w_H\|^2$, which must vanish, and both terms vanish. A finite basis of initial variations gives derivative independence almost surely on the actual history experiment. Smooth positive kernels give its full-support continuity extension where those hypotheses hold. No conditional equality is inferred merely from an equality of averaged minima.

An order-zero phase-erasing rule has zero derivative along a common rotation of the $B_i$. The fine score in that direction has squared norm $\sum_i|B_i|I_1(|B_i|)/I_0(|B_i|)>0$. It is orthogonal to the marked orbit, which acts on $\phi$, and the relative-history and row laws are unchanged. Its fine horizontal norm is therefore positive while the parent norm is zero. Inside a nonflat section patch one may instead hold its marked derivatives fixed and vary that $\psi$-phase derivative; the positive quadratic term gives the same contradiction.
\end{proof}

This theorem applies to the smooth subset of the admitted $C^2$ class. It does not assume that every $C^2$ section has arbitrarily high derivatives. Independent jet variation and equality of the connection classes are necessary hypotheses of the proof: tensor equality alone could hide a changing marked-orbit tangent. The result is not an application of fixed-statistic Fisher contraction to a moving law map; the latter has additional derivative terms, as in \cref{geo:moving-channel}. Score sufficiency for a fixed statistic is a separate statement \citep{Pollard2013}. Finite reference memory and constrained generator families are outside the theorem.

\propositionheading{One regular finite encoding cannot hold arbitrary profiles}{lp:profile-obstruction}
On a $C^2$-open class of sections, no single smooth state of dimension $m$ can reproduce every direct public response law at every $c$, if retention and response evaluation are $C^1$ on finite-dimensional smooth section variations and all finite smooth memory is counted in $m$.
\begin{proof}
On one retained-history stratum choose $m+1$ disjoint contextual neighborhoods and points $c_j$ in them. Perturb $l_1$ by $\sum_j a_j f_j(c)$ with $f_j(c_j)=1$ and disjoint supports. At a regular finite time these current perturbations have admitted predecessors by local invertibility of the fixed-record flow. Strict census and window margins persist for small coefficients. The Jacobian of the $m+1$ direct sign probabilities is diagonal with positive entries, since $\Pr(Z=1)=\operatorname{sigmoid}(L_1)$ and $\partial L_1/\partial l_1=1$. Its rank is $m+1$. Factoring through an $m$-dimensional state would bound the rank by $m$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

This is a statement about one encoding of an indexed contextual experiment, not the addition of context-relocation actions to the protocol. It excludes neither one initialized local germ, compatible local fields, a finite generator family, nonfinite memory nor irregular encodings. No construction of this chapter uses such an encoding: each keeps a state per context or a generator restricted by hypothesis. The proposition is a dimension count that marks the boundary of those realizations, not an obstruction to any of them.

\theoremheading{Order-zero obstruction for rotation-invariant value-path retentions}{lp:path-memory-obstruction}
On the preparation class $\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$, let a strictly local causal retention read the entire value path $x(c,s)$, $t_*\le s\le t$, all actual records, persistent evidence, censuses and fixed templates. It reads no contextual derivatives, neighboring or reference contexts, or initialized contextual phase field. Require the complete retained state, including every memory and seed, to be invariant under a constant common $\psi$ rotation, and let the actual parent laws depend only on that state and fixed data. Such a retention cannot universally preserve the actual minimized Fisher tensor. There is no bound on its temporal horizon or memory dimension. The conclusion also holds for a regular stochastic retained-state kernel with this invariance and phase-independent randomization.
\begin{proof}
For any smooth time-independent $\kappa(c)$, pointwise equivariance gives
\begin{equation}
 x^{\kappa,h}(c,t)=\mathsf R_{R_{\kappa(c)}}x^h(c,t),\qquad
 \Pi_c^\kappa=\Pi_c.
 \label{lp:phase-field-history}
\end{equation}
The second identity follows by coupling the original invariant transaction kernels and policy randomness; records and censuses coincide. At each fixed $c$ the full local value paths differ by one constant rotation. Local rotation-invariant retention therefore gives identical parent law families for all $\kappa$, even with the complete temporal path available.

Choose $\kappa(c_*)=0$ and $X\kappa(c_*)=\lambda$. The fine contextual score becomes $s_X+\lambda\ell_\psi$. The score $\ell_\psi$ is orthogonal to every marked orbit score: conditional on history the $\psi$ factors are independent of the marked factors, have mean-zero scores, and neither the history nor the rows have a common-phase score. Its joint norm is
\begin{equation}
 I_\psi:=\|\ell_\psi\|^2
 =\E_H\sum_i|B_i^h|\frac{I_1(|B_i^h|)}{I_0(|B_i^h|)}>0.
 \label{lp:physical-phase-inertia}
\end{equation}
The minimizing marked class at $c_*$ is unchanged. With $s_X^{\mathrm{hor}}=s_X+\ell_{A_f(X)}$, the fine minimized cost is
\begin{equation}
 h_f^\kappa(X,X)=h_f(X,X)
 +2\lambda\E[s_X^{\mathrm{hor}}\ell_\psi]
 +\lambda^2I_\psi.
 \label{lp:path-memory-polynomial}
\end{equation}
It cannot be constant on an interval of $\lambda$, whereas the parent tensor is. Small supported smooth phase variations stay inside the strict common nonflat-window class and preserve the grouping exactly. This is a joint-experiment score identity; no conditional geometric equality is inferred from an averaged one.
\end{proof}

The unbounded horizon and memory dimension add no information on fixed-record branches. There the native field is an ordinary differential equation with piecewise-constant coefficients, and evidence does not jump the state, so the value path $x(c,s)$, $t_*\leq s\leq t$, is a smooth function of $(c,x(c,t),h)$. The retentions of the theorem are therefore rotation-invariant functions, possibly randomized, of the current value, the records and fixed data, and the theorem is the order-zero case of \cref{lp:jet-obstruction} without its regularity hypothesis on the retention; its content is the pointwise-canonicalization defect of \cref{lp:phase-information} and \cref{lp:initial-phase-score-defect}.

\corollaryheading{Smooth bounded-jet causal memories}{lp:causal-jet-obstruction}
Suppose a finite-dimensional deterministic memory is initialized smoothly from $c,j_c^k x(t_*)$ and the original initial records. Between transactions let $\dot m=\Gamma(m,j_c^k x,c,h)$; let record updates and the actual-law readout also be smooth functions of these data. Assume one finite order $k$ bounds initialization, evolution and readout on every branch, with no reference seed or separately supplied contextual phase field. On $\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$, such a parent cannot erase the common $\psi$ phase and inherit both the original minimizing marked affine classes and minimized tensors.
\begin{proof}
At a fixed finite word $h$, the native $k$-jet and memory form a closed smooth ordinary differential system. Its memory value is a smooth function of the initial $k$-jet. The native fixed-record flow is locally invertible on its reached regular image; record appends do not reset the law. Its jet prolongation is locally invertible as well. Hence the actual parent law has the finite-order form $F_{t,h}(c,j_c^k x(t))$. Apply \cref{lp:jet-obstruction}, including its joint-$L^2$ history argument. The chronological law remains initialized from the actual preparation; branchwise inversion does not infer an unconditional past law from a current snapshot or add a reverse-time protocol.
\end{proof}

The coherent preparation family of \cref{lp:initial-coherence} does not permit the independent phase-gradient variations used in these obstructions. Its causal construction therefore coexists with them. The unrestricted question remains inconclusive: an admissible causal source of the initial contextual phase relation outside these classes, or an obstruction covering it, is still needed. Genuinely unbounded contextual-order memory, differently specified infinite-dimensional memory and stochastic mechanisms outside \cref{lp:path-memory-obstruction} are not classified by \cref{lp:causal-jet-obstruction}. Temporal records do not supply an undeclared contextual calibration channel. The common $\psi$ symmetry remains predeclared; interaction-created invisibility and a new second compressive handoff remain open. All three obstructions concern the minimizing geometry of the declared marked group (\cref{lp:group-dependence}).

\openproblemheading{Causal local parents on arbitrary preparations}{lp:local-parent-open}
Does there exist a retention that is causal and germ-local at each context, whose complete retained state is invariant under a constant common $\psi$ rotation, whose actual parent laws lie in a declared family, and which for every $x_0\in\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$ reproduces every admitted fixed-context joint history together with the minimizing marked connection classes and minimized tensors on the window, either jointly or on each record branch as declared? The answer is negative for finite-order rules (\cref{lp:jet-obstruction}), for rotation-invariant value-path retentions (\cref{lp:path-memory-obstruction}) and for bounded-jet causal memories (\cref{lp:causal-jet-obstruction}). It is positive with one reference read (\cref{lp:local-realization} and the frozen handoff frame) and on the restricted coherent and finite-generator classes (\cref{lp:causal-coherence}, \cref{lp:generator}). On real-analytic preparations over a connected patch the germ at one context determines the section, so germ locality is no restriction there, and an obstruction for germ memories must use non-analytic variations.

\section{Report against the formation standard}

The construction concerns the per-agent objective under the chart Gibbs-reduced dynamics on $\{-1,1\}\times S^1\times S^1$. Its admitted class is relatively open in the declared invariant family \cref{lp:law} with Bernoulli presentation laws, so (F1) holds in the family grade. The selector family is that of \cref{pa:angular-grouping}, with persistence as an invariant block relation while the initial records are held fixed, and the rules $A_I$ and $B_I$ are those of the protocol of \cref{sec:lp-collective}. The whole-block retention with reference memory satisfies (F3) by \cref{lp:local-realization}. Compression (F4) fails: the retention erases only the common $\psi$ rotation, which is gauge relative to $B_I$; its fibers are orbits of a symmetry of every datum and admitted kernel, and the separation fades by \cref{lp:decay}. Interaction necessity (F5) holds against both nulls for every compact constant set inside $(1/8,1/3)$ and fails for constant sets that meet $(1/3,1)$; the frozen-environment control reproduces the partition sequence, and the selector-free discrepancy is the reversal of agent $3$'s public sign. There is one selection stage, so (F6a) fails and no counterfactual class is declared; the amplifying grade is not examined. The construction is a single-stage selection with exact inheritance, and it is not a recursive formation.
