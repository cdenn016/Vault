\chapter{Collective Compression and Conditional Local Parents}
\label{ch:collective-local-parent}

The phase parent of \cref{pa:phase-parent} erases a distinction already invisible on each primitive port. A collective alternative transmits the second angular law between peers and admits relative-angle responses. Each proper block is then exposed by a response across its boundary, while the whole connected population can lose one common physical orientation. This chapter constructs that quotient and a local realization with finite reference memory. A second realization uses a finite preparation generator and the complete record word. Neither establishes a strictly local compressive parent for arbitrary contextual sections. The obstructions proved below concern specified regular representations, rather than every possible memory mechanism.

\section{A transmitted physical angle}

There are three agents with the normalized per-agent model of \cref{def:dyn-per-agent}. Relative to a uniform sign and two normalized Haar circles, write
\begin{align}
 q_i(z,\phi,\psi)&=Z_i^{-1}
 \exp\{l_i z/2+A_i\cdot e_\phi+zC_i\cdot e_\phi+B_i\cdot e_\psi\},
 \label{lp:law}\\
 Z_i&=I_0(|B_i|)
 \frac{e^{l_i/2}I_0(|A_i+C_i|)+e^{-l_i/2}I_0(|A_i-C_i|)}2.
 \nonumber
\end{align}
Here $e_\theta=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ and $A_i,C_i,B_i\in\R^2$. The presentation probability is
\begin{equation}
 s_i(w)=\frac{e^{n_iw/2}}{2\cosh(n_i/2)},\qquad w\in\{-1,1\}.
 \label{lp:presentation}
\end{equation}
The self evaluator is $G_i=e^{Jz\cos\phi}/I_0(J)$, with $J=\epsilon^3$, and the self presentation reference is uniform. Source priors in both sectors are $1/5$ for self and $2/5$ for each peer. Rows are reduced exactly at unit temperature.

The nonself content channel copies $z,\psi$ and resamples $\phi$ from Haar measure. Define $h_0(B)=\log I_0(|B|)$ and $M(B)=\nabla h_0(B)$. The transmitted density is
\begin{equation}
 \nu_j(z,\phi,\psi)=\frac{e^{L_jz/2}}{\cosh(L_j/2)}
 \frac{e^{B_j\cdot e_\psi}}{I_0(|B_j|)},\qquad
 L_j=l_j+h_0(A_j+C_j)-h_0(A_j-C_j).
 \label{lp:peer}
\end{equation}
The sign reference is uniform, so its density differs by a factor of two from its probability mass. Presentation peers use the identity channel. Every source remains normalized.

Evidence uses the positive normalized likelihood
\begin{equation}
 \ell_{r,\lambda}(o,\omega\mid z,\phi,\psi,w)
 =\frac{e^{ro z/2}}{2\cosh(r/2)}
 \frac{e^{\lambda\cos(\omega-\phi)}}{I_0(\lambda)},
 \label{lp:likelihood}
\end{equation}
relative to sign counting measure and angular Haar measure. Every admitted finite-strength evidence transaction appends its actual outcome and leaves its likelihood in the objective. Write $H_i=\sum r o$ and $\Lambda_i=\sum\lambda e_\omega$ for its persistent coefficients, retaining the entire ordered word as well. A passive marked frame change rotates the local embeddings of these fixed lab labels and the evaluator direction. There is one marked structure group $G=S^1$ with trivial representation on the presentation sector. The common physical rotation of $\psi$ is a separate nongauge action.

\propositionheading{The exact transmitted-angle field}{lp:native-field}
On the positive family \cref{lp:law}, put $m_i=\tanh(L_i/2)$ and $t_i=\E_{q_i}[Z\cos\Phi]$. The content Gibbs weights and own-block Fisher equations are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 w_{ii}&=\frac{e^{Jt_i}}{5I_0(J)},&
 w_{ij}&=\frac{2e^{L_jm_i/2+B_j\cdot M(B_i)}}
 {5\cosh(L_j/2)I_0(|B_j|)},\\
 \beta_{ij}&=w_{ij}/\sum_r w_{ir},&& j\ne i,
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:weights}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot l_i&=-l_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}L_j+H_i,&
 \dot A_i&=-A_i+\Lambda_i,\\
 \dot C_i&=-C_i+\beta_{ii}Je_1,&
 \dot B_i&=-B_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}B_j,\\
 \dot n_i&=-n_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\gamma_{ij}n_j.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:field}
\end{equation}
Here $\gamma$ is the original presentation Gibbs row. Every equation uses the original inference time.
\begin{proof}
Let $S_i=\E_{q_i}\log q_i$. The self cost is $S_i-Jt_i+\log I_0(J)$ and the peer cost is
$S_i-L_jm_i/2+\log\cosh(L_j/2)-B_j\cdot M(B_i)+h_0(B_j)$.
Their common entropy cancels from the content row, giving \cref{lp:weights}. The Gibbs envelope removes derivatives of the optimizing row from the own-objective first variation. The centered logarithmic Fisher velocity is a linear combination of $z,e_\phi,ze_\phi,e_\psi$. Matching these coefficients gives \cref{lp:field}. Their covariance is positive definite: a linear combination constant under a positive density has zero nonconstant Fourier and sign coefficients. This is an invariant family of the own-law Fisher field, without a Euclidean approximation or source-role forces from other agents' objectives.
\end{proof}

With the initial record pattern $(+,+,-)$ held fixed, this model has the interaction-dependent grouping window of \cref{pa:angular-grouping}. To see why the transmitted angle does not invalidate that argument, take $B_i=\delta b_i$ with $b_i$ in a compact pointed cone and nonzero. The new term in the row logarithm is $O_{C^1}(\delta^2)$ on bounded scaled charts. With $u=l/\epsilon$ and $C/\epsilon^3$ scaled, the public limiting field is
\begin{equation}
 \dot u=\left(-\frac75I+\frac25\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\right)u
 +(1,1,-1)^\top+O_{C^1}(\epsilon^2+\delta^2),\qquad
 u_*=(15/7,15/7,5/7).
 \label{lp:selection-limit}
\end{equation}
The remaining scaled blocks have a symmetric negative-definite limiting derivative. A sufficiently small attraction ball retains positive public signs and pairwise magnitude ratios above $1/4$, since the smallest equilibrium ratio is $1/3$. Initial $u$ near $(4,1/2,-1)$ has strict singleton margins. Finite-time dependence followed by the attracting-ball estimate gives a common entrance time $T$ and persistence with that initial episode fixed.

The prescribed null deletes nonself costs while retaining their source labels, priors and row entropy. Its surviving self weight is not one. On $|l_i|\le12\epsilon$, $|A_i|\le1/10$, $|C_i|\le2\epsilon^3$, $|B_i|\le1/10$, the self KL bound of \cref{pa:null-bound} still gives $1/9\le\beta_{ii}^0\le1/5$. Its equations include $\dot l_i^0=-\beta_{ii}^0l_i^0+\epsilon o_i$ and $\dot B_i^0=-\beta_{ii}^0B_i^0$. The signed strip $1/4\le o_i l_i^0/\epsilon\le12$ is invariant, while $|L_i-l_i|<\epsilon/8$. Thus the third public sign remains negative under the null and the full block is never selected. Sample the same state graph at fixed census times $0,T$, retaining both partition labels and times. Strict uniform margins make those outputs constant on the chosen contextual patch. No continuously reported parameter-dependent crossing time is inserted into a differentiable statistical experiment.

\section{Collective rather than primitive physical loss}

Declare the protocol before the handoff. It admits the public, marked-lab and presentation queries, original normalized evidence transactions and source-label readouts, and relative-angle queries on the complete graph. A joint relative query draws each involved primitive angle once and returns all requested differences. Shared vertices share samples; triangle outputs obey their cycle relation. Transactions use fresh samples unless reuse is explicitly recorded. A diagnostic appends history without becoming an assimilated likelihood. Policies use the complete history, fixed lab data and optional independent randomization. No absolute $\psi$ response is admitted at any stage.

For $\zeta_i=[I_1(|B_i|)/I_0(|B_i|)]e^{\mathrm i\arg B_i}$, the pair moment is
\begin{equation}
 c_{ij}=\E e^{\mathrm i(\Psi_i-\Psi_j)}=\zeta_i\overline{\zeta_j},\qquad
 |\zeta_i|=\sqrt{\frac{|c_{ij}||c_{ik}|}{|c_{jk}|}}.
 \label{lp:relative-identification}
\end{equation}
The function $I_1(b)/I_0(b)$ has strictly positive derivative $\operatorname{Var}_b(\cos\Psi)$ for $b>0$. Thus the three pair laws identify the tuple up to one common rotation. With an exterior nonzero $\zeta_j$ fixed, $c_{ij}/\overline{\zeta_j}$ identifies every interior vector. Public and marked law queries identify $l,A,C$, and presentation queries identify $n$. The same erasure cannot therefore be performed independently on a proper block with its boundary retained. This is identification from exact response laws, not estimation from finitely many samples. The common symmetry preexists grouping; no interaction-created invisibility is asserted.

\section{Finite reference memory and closed local jets}

Let $x(c)=(l_i,A_i,C_i,B_i,n_i)_i\in\R^{24}$ be a $C^2$ section on a compact contextual patch, and fix $c_0$ before retention. At every context the same realized lab history is applied to \cref{lp:field}. Suppose the initial $B_i(c)$ lie in a common open pointed cone, with its rotational union admitted. For a vector $v$ positive on that cone,
\begin{equation}
 v\cdot B_i(c,t)\ge e^{-t}v\cdot B_i(c,0)>0.
 \label{lp:cone}
\end{equation}
This follows by variation of constants in the cooperative $B$ equation. Finite evidence words preserve it. The other natural parameters have finite-interval bounds from \cref{lp:field}, so every finite admitted branch remains regular.

Write $x_\circ=x(c_0)$, $S=\sum_i B_i(x_\circ)$ and
\begin{equation}
 U(S)=\frac1{|S|}\begin{pmatrix}S_x&S_y\\-S_y&S_x\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 y=\mathsf R_Ux(c),\qquad \widehat x_\circ=\mathsf R_Ux_\circ,
 \label{lp:retention}
\end{equation}
where $\mathsf R_U$ rotates only the $B$ blocks. This smooth formula uses no angle branch. A whole-section retention applies this one $U$ at every $c$ and removes precisely one context-constant physical angle. The local realization below retains only the data required at $c$ and the reference.

\theoremheading{Conditional evaluation-local realization}{lp:local-realization}
Retain $j_c^2y$, the full finite native tuple $\widehat x_\circ$, all primitive ports, constitutive data, complete ordered history, census records and persistent likelihood coefficients. If record embeddings vary with context, retain their known two-jets. Give the past-law experiment its actual compatible initialization. These data have an autonomous original-time evolution and determine the exact local two-jet and every admitted future joint response/update kernel at $c$. They preserve the actual collective Fisher cost on the declared regular experiment. Initialization uses reference-context data and is not a rule from the germ at $c$ alone.
\begin{proof}
Let $V(x,c,h)$ denote \cref{lp:field}, let $V_S=\sum_i V_{B_i}(\widehat x_\circ,c_0,h)$, and let $\mathcal J$ act by $J_2=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ on each $B$ block and by zero elsewhere. Set
\begin{equation}
 \Omega(\widehat x_\circ,h)=\frac{\det(S,V_S)}{|S|^2},\qquad
 W(y,c,h;\widehat x_\circ)=V(y,c,h)-\Omega\mathcal Jy.
 \label{lp:omega}
\end{equation}
Equivariance and $\dot U=-\Omega J_2U$ give
\begin{equation}
 \dot y=W(y,c,h;\widehat x_\circ),\qquad
 \dot{\widehat x}_\circ=V(\widehat x_\circ,c_0,h)-\Omega\mathcal J\widehat x_\circ.
 \label{lp:closed-reference}
\end{equation}
The canonical anchor stays on $S_y=0,S_x>0$. The subtraction is the derivative of the retention, not a separately fitted parent force. This is the exact vector-field intertwining criterion of \cref{inh:vector-square}, an instance of exact lumping \citep{HorstmeyerAtay2016}.

At fixed $t,h$ and prepared section, the reference is held fixed under evaluation differentiation in $c$. For contextual indices $r,s$,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot y_r&=W_y y_r+W_r,\\
 \dot y_{rs}&=W_y y_{rs}+W_{yy}[y_r,y_s]
 +W_{yr}y_s+W_{ys}y_r+W_{rs}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:jet-flow}
\end{equation}
The explicit partials hold $y,\widehat x_\circ,h$ fixed. Every state dependence of the Gibbs rows is differentiated. Since the native field reads no neighboring contexts or contextual derivatives, no third jet enters. Evidence adds the same saved likelihood at both evaluations without resetting either law. Diagnostics change only history. For a two-dimensional base the local law jet has $24(1+2+3)=144$ coordinates, plus 24 reference coordinates before constraints. The full ordered history remains unbounded in length; this is not a fixed-memory claim.

The actual parent laws are the normalized products of \cref{lp:law} in the $y$ coordinates, together with their original presentation and Gibbs-label factors. The reference is deterministic internal memory, not an extra independent sample or agent. Common physical rotation preserves every joint relative response, every other response and its actual next retained state. Equal histories therefore supply the same next action and wait law. Induction over the joint transaction kernels proves all admitted finite adaptive histories, including response/next-state dependence.

For an unconditional history/current experiment, retain an initial local two-jet and initial reference tuple in one canonical frame as a replay seed, together with the full protocol and chronological word. Applying the original normalized kernels defines its actual history law $\Pi_c$. A current tuple and one observed word alone do not determine that past probability. Conditioning on the initial public pattern retains its positive probability normalizer. No distribution over law configurations is introduced.

Finally, for fixed $t,h$, rotating the fresh private samples by $U(S(x_\circ^h(t)))$ is independent of the evaluation coordinate $c$. The joint map $T(h,Y)=(h,T_hY)$ preserves Haar measure and commutes with the marked action. Stored marked outcomes are fixed lab labels; their local direction embeddings transform. Hence, for
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal P_{c,t}(dh,dY)=\Pi_c(dh)Q_{c,t}^{h}(dY),\qquad
 \overline{\mathcal P}_{c,t}=T_*\mathcal P_{c,t},
 \label{lp:actual-experiment}
\end{equation}
the contextual score and marked orbit score are carried isometrically. For every $X,\xi$,
\begin{equation}
 \overline{\mathcal C}(X,\xi)
 =\E_{\overline{\mathcal P}}(\overline s_X+\overline\ell_\xi)^2
 =\E_{\mathcal P}(s_X+\ell_\xi)^2=\mathcal C(X,\xi).
 \label{lp:cost}
\end{equation}
This proves actual-law cost inheritance before minimization, including history and label scores. It gives the same minimizing affine class, isotropy and minimized tensor. On a locally free patch their connection and curvature agree.
\end{proof}

The retained reference contains no inverse rotation: $U(R_\alpha S)R_\alpha=U(S)$, so simultaneous rotation changes none of the canonical local, reference or seed data. It still changes the excluded child absolute-angle diagnostic. The reference retains the phase difference between $c$ and $c_0$, not the discarded global angle. For $B_i(c)=b_i e_{\chi(c)}$, the actual canonical density contains $b_i e_{\chi(c)-\chi(c_0)}$, and its private phase information is
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i b_i\frac{I_1(b_i)}{I_0(b_i)}(d\chi)^2>0.
 \label{lp:phase-information}
\end{equation}
Pointwise canonicalization would delete this term. Copying it into a stored tensor would not restore it to a normalized parent law.

A local initial two-jet has an actual law realization: use its degree-two natural-parameter polynomial near the evaluation point and then evolve that preparation by the exact pointwise field, without truncating the evolved density. Its two-jet agrees with the original there throughout the common finite regular interval by \cref{lp:jet-flow}. This supplies actual law germs and their connection curvature, but does not reconstruct the unknown original section away from that point. Compatible initial data over all contexts remain a field. Marked overlap maps preserve this compatibility because the private canonicalization commutes with them.

For a common nonflat window take $A_1(c,0)=ae_\chi$, $A_2(c,0)=de_1$, with $a,d$ in positive compact ranges independent of $\epsilon,\delta$, and small noncollinear cone vectors $B_i=\delta b_i$. Keep the initial episode fixed. At the uncanonicalized $\epsilon=\delta=0$ comparison, put $v=e^{-t}a$ and $D(t)=\sum_{i\ne1}\mathcal I(e^{-t}|A_i(0)|)>0$, where $\mathcal I(v)=vI_1(v)/I_0(v)$. The same minimization as in \cref{pa:nonflat-limit} gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal A_{0,t}&=-\frac{\mathcal I(v)}{\mathcal I(v)+D(t)}d\chi,\\
 \mathcal F_{0,t}&=-\frac{D(t)e^{-t}\mathcal I'(v)}{[\mathcal I(v)+D(t)]^2}da\wedge d\chi,\\
 h_{aa}^{0,t}&=e^{-2t}\operatorname{Var}_{v}(\cos\Phi)>0,\qquad
 h_{\chi\chi}^{0,t}=\frac{\mathcal I(v)D(t)}{\mathcal I(v)+D(t)}>0.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{lp:nonflat}
\end{equation}
The cross coefficient vanishes and $\mathcal I'(v)>0$. Compact finite-window margins persist for sufficiently small positive $\epsilon,\delta$: the exact finite-time flow, its two-jet, normalized history kernels and Fisher integrals depend continuously on those parameters on a common positive tube. Apply continuity to the fine laws, since the canonical anchor is singular at $\delta=0$, and apply \cref{lp:cost} only at positive $\delta$. This gives the same nonflat rank-two window containing $T$. Later records preserve inheritance, but need not preserve this grouping or nonflatness.

\section{Handoff frames and finite preparation generators}

Fix the census handoff $T$ and freeze $U_T=U(S(x_\circ(T)))$ once. Then $z(c,t)=\mathsf R_{U_T}x(c,t)$ obeys $\dot z=V(z,c,h)$ for $t\ge T$, with no moving-frame term. Its actual costs still satisfy \cref{lp:cost}. Future local evolution needs no live reference, but its initialization is reference-coherent memory, not an instantaneous quotient of $x(c,t)$ alone. Recovering the continuously reanchored representative later requires the reference evolution or its accumulated phase.

\propositionheading{Conditional finite preparation-and-history realization}{lp:generator}
Suppose the prepared section has a known finite $C^2$ generator $x_0(c)=f(c;p)$, with $p\in P\subset\R^m$, equivariant under the common physical rotation and with nonzero reference anchor. Retain its canonical coefficients $p_*$, the full chronological history and original time. Then the current contextual laws and their two-jets have an exact whole-patch realization using these data, with all the preceding operational and geometric inheritance properties.
\begin{proof}
Let $\mathcal E_{t,h}(c)$ be the chronological composition of the unique native flows and original fixed-record coefficient appends. Evaluate
\begin{equation}
 x_t^h(c;p_*)=\mathcal E_{t,h}(c)f(c;p_*).
 \label{lp:generator-evaluation}
\end{equation}
Between transactions $\dot p_*=0$, $\dot h=0$ and $\dot t=1$; cached local values satisfy the native field and \cref{lp:jet-flow}. At a transaction append its actual outcome and likelihood, if it is evidence. The original normalized response/update kernels evaluated on \cref{lp:generator-evaluation} generate its full path law. Physical equivariance removes exactly the common seed orientation; it is not stored in the canonical seed. Evaluate the same generator at $c_0$ if a moving representative is wanted. The sample rotation is again independent of evaluation $c$, proving \cref{lp:cost}. No projection onto a finite basis of evolved sections is used.
\end{proof}

For a nonempty family, take the marked patch used in \cref{lp:nonflat}, constant initial $l_i/\epsilon$ near $(4,1/2,-1)$, $C_i=0$, small fixed presentation laws and three context-constant noncollinear $b_i$ in the cone, with their rotational union. These are finite preparation coefficients, relatively open in their parameter manifold. They are not an open set of arbitrary $C^2$ functions. The same persistent grouping and nonflat window apply. The full word remains retained, and evaluation at a new context may require replay of its entire native history. Finite seed dimension gives neither constant evaluation cost nor bounded record memory.

\section{Scoped obstructions to stronger locality}

The reference dependence cannot be removed by merely storing its sum. For $B_1=B_3=be_1$, $B_2=be_2$, $A=C=0$, $l_1=l_3=0$ and $l_2=v$, put
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 s=\frac1{5I_0(J)},\quad k=\frac2{5I_0(b)},\quad E=e^{bI_1(b)/I_0(b)},\\
 u=\operatorname{sech}(v/2),\qquad D=s+kE+ku.
 \end{gathered}
 \label{lp:sum-example}
\end{equation}
The exact rows give
\begin{equation}
 \Omega=\frac15\left(\frac{4ku-2kE}{D}-\frac{2k}{s+2k}\right),\qquad
 \frac{d\Omega}{du}=\frac{4ks+6k^2E}{5D^2}>0.
 \label{lp:sum-failure}
\end{equation}
Thus $S=(2b,b)$ stays fixed while its angular velocity changes. Also, for $c\ne c_0$, a section can be changed only near $c_0$ without changing its germ at $c$, yet its canonical value at $c$ changes. These facts exclude sum-only closure and germ-only computation of this representative, not all alternative parent realizations.

\theoremheading{Finite differential-order obstruction}{lp:jet-obstruction}
Consider a rich smooth open class of sections in the positive chart, allowing independent finite-jet variations. Suppose the parent's actual law is a smooth rule $\bar p(c)=F(c,j_c^k x,h)$ of some finite order. Local native-history summaries may be included if they are themselves finite-jet functions on the fixed-history stratum. There is no separately retained phase-bearing field or reference-context memory. If the actual minimizing marked connection classes and minimized tensors agree for every section in that class, then the actual-law rule is independent of positive-order jets. It cannot also erase the common physical $\psi$ angle while preserving that geometry.
\begin{proof}
For $k\ge1$ fix a $k$-jet and vary the next jet in a contextual direction $X\ne0$. Contraction of symmetric $(k+1)$-tensors with $X$ is onto symmetric $k$-tensors; a supported polynomial primitive realizes each variation. At the point the parent law, orbit map $\bar J$, fine first score and fine minimizing class are fixed. The parent tangent has the form $\bar u_X(\tau)=\bar u_X(0)+\tau w$. Choose one representative $A_f(X)$ of the common minimizing class. Equality of tensors requires
\begin{equation}
 \|\bar u_X(0)+\bar J A_f(X)+\tau w\|_{\bar g}^2
 =h_f^{\min}(X,X)
 \label{lp:highest-jet}
\end{equation}
on an interval. Its quadratic coefficient is $\bar g(w,w)$, so positivity forces $w=0$. Descending in derivative order removes all positive-order dependence of the actual law.

For random histories make the argument in the joint $L^2$ space. Vary the highest initial derivative while holding lower initial jets fixed. The history law and fine first scores stay fixed, and the highest current variation is transported by the invertible fixed-record native-flow derivative on each regular branch. The quadratic coefficient is $\E_H\|w_H\|^2$, which must vanish. A finite basis of initial variations gives derivative independence almost surely on the actual history experiment. Smooth positive kernels give its full-support continuity extension where those hypotheses hold. No conditional equality is inferred merely from an equality of averaged minima.

An order-zero phase-erasing rule has zero derivative along a common physical rotation of the $B_i$. The fine score in that direction has squared norm $\sum_i|B_i|I_1(|B_i|)/I_0(|B_i|)>0$. It is orthogonal to the marked orbit, which acts on $\phi$, and the relative-history and row laws are unchanged. Its fine horizontal norm is therefore positive while the parent norm is zero. Inside a nonflat section patch one may instead hold its marked derivatives fixed and vary that physical phase derivative; the positive quadratic term gives the same contradiction.
\end{proof}

This theorem applies to the smooth subset of the admitted $C^2$ class. It does not assume that every $C^2$ section has arbitrarily high derivatives. Independent jet variation and equality of the connection classes are necessary hypotheses of the proof: tensor equality alone could hide a changing marked-orbit tangent. The result is not an application of fixed-statistic Fisher contraction to a moving law map; the latter has additional derivative terms, as in \cref{geo:moving-channel}. Score sufficiency for a fixed statistic is a separate statement \citep{Pollard2013}. Finite reference memory and constrained generator families are outside the theorem.

\propositionheading{One regular finite encoding cannot hold arbitrary profiles}{lp:profile-obstruction}
On a $C^2$-open class of sections, no single smooth state of dimension $m$ can reproduce every direct public response law at every $c$, if retention and response evaluation are $C^1$ on finite-dimensional smooth section variations and all finite smooth memory is counted in $m$.
\begin{proof}
On one retained-history stratum choose $m+1$ disjoint contextual neighborhoods and points $c_j$ in them. Perturb $l_1$ by $\sum_j a_j f_j(c)$ with $f_j(c_j)=1$ and disjoint supports. At a regular finite time these current perturbations have admitted predecessors by local invertibility of the fixed-record flow. Strict census and window margins persist for small coefficients. The Jacobian of the $m+1$ direct sign probabilities is diagonal with positive entries, since $\Pr(Z=1)=\operatorname{sigmoid}(L_1)$ and $\partial L_1/\partial l_1=1$. Its rank is $m+1$. Factoring through an $m$-dimensional state would bound the rank by $m$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

This is a statement about one encoding of an indexed contextual experiment, not the addition of context-relocation actions to the protocol. It excludes neither one initialized local germ, compatible local fields, a finite generator family, nonfinite memory nor irregular encodings. The conditional constructions therefore coexist with both obstructions. A strictly germ-local actual-law realization of the unrestricted section class with a more general admissible memory mechanism remains unresolved; failure of the stated finite-jet class does not prove general impossibility. The common symmetry remains predeclared, and no new second compressive handoff is established.
