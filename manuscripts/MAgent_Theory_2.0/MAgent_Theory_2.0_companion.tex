\bigskip
This companion develops statistical agents, their interactions, and inherited organization through examples that can be calculated by hand. It assumes familiarity with probability, linear algebra, and differential equations. Coordinate covariance, predictive communication, and exact inheritance lead to a specified two-stage fusion mechanism. Its assumptions delimit the result; the surrounding theory permits general probability laws.
