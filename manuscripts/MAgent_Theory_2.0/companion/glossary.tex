\chapter{Notation in Physical Terms}
\begingroup
\small\setstretch{0.94}

\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.19\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.75\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning and role\\
\midrule\endhead
\bottomrule\endfoot
$k,m$ & A represented state possibility and a model presentation. Neither symbol by itself denotes a probability law.\\
$q_i,s_i$ & The agent's belief about represented contents (its perception) and its belief over generative models. They can evolve in coupled equations.\\
$\zeta_i$ & The joint law of represented state and model contents; $q_i\otimes s_i$ is a declared product restriction.\\
$g^F,\ell_v$ & Fisher distinguishability on a regular statistical family and the score describing one infinitesimal change.\\
$D^\omega\zeta,h^\omega_\zeta$ & Statistical change after contextual transport is accounted for, and the resulting tensor on the context base.\\
$A^F,\mathfrak k$ & The collectively minimizing connection class and the metric measuring common infinitesimal frame motions.\\
$G_i(m,dk)$ & An evaluator assigning state predictions to each model presentation. Averaging gives $s_iG_i$.\\
$r_i,\ell_i,P_i$ & Presentation prior, record likelihood, and the normalized generative model $r_iG_i\ell_i$. A model law predicts records only through $G_i$ and $\ell_i$.\\
Species & Agents that share a generative model up to a change of frame. They may still hold different beliefs.\\
Three objectives & A global free energy of one normalized joint over all agents; per-agent free energies in which peers' laws are data; the reciprocal law potential, which moves sources toward their receivers. With fixed rows on finite alphabets the potential is not the free energy of a finite normalized model when an attended edge is an identity channel, nor, with fixed input-dependent channels, of a model whose recognition is the product of the agent laws; outside these hypotheses exact identifications exist.\\
Observable algebra & The declared functions of the configuration that agents, channels, records, or a declared external protocol can read. A distinction is gauge relative to an algebra when no element separates it at any time along the dynamics; words such as physical or public are used only relative to a named algebra.\\
$\mathcal F_i$ & Free energy of agent $i$'s own generative model. In the per-agent picture its own evaluator and its peers' channelled laws compete as sources of prior.\\
$x\in\mathsf S$ & A complete configuration of agents, including their laws and the memory and interfaces read by the dynamics.\\
$\rho_i:\mathsf S_i\to\mathsf O_i$ & A readout of an internal distinction. Its value is a record.\\
$\Gamma_{ij}$ & A normalized interaction transition on complete configurations. It induces record probabilities through the readout.\\
$U_i^a$ & An invertible frame map for sector $a=q,s$. Changing frame changes the description.\\
$T_j^a,O_i^a$ & Sending and receiving interfaces. Their product can discard directions.\\
$L_{ij}^a,\Theta_{ij}^a$ & A reference generator and its framed exponential interaction. The generator may be singular while its exponential is invertible.\\
$\beta_{ij},\gamma_{ij}$ & Belief-sector and model-sector source weights in normalized rows.\\
$\mathcal E$ & The reciprocal law potential on agent laws. Its gradient dynamics requires a metric, and every edge pushes on both its receiver and its source.\\
$\mathcal F$ & Variational free energy for a specified normalized probabilistic model and conditioned record.\\
$Q_xC$ & A law of compressed represented contents. It does not specify a parent update.\\
$R(x),F^c$ & A complete retained state and its inherited update. They must satisfy $R\circ F=F^c\circ R$, with $\circ$ composing maps in function order.\\
$\Phi^0_{\mathrm{lp}},\Phi^0_{\mathrm{rn}}$ & The two null flows: every peer cost is deleted, with the peer labels kept, so that a self source keeps a Gibbs weight below one, or with them removed, so that a self source has weight one. They are the control experiments for selection and coincide when no self cost is nonzero.\\
$\Phi^{\mathrm{fr}}$ & The frozen-environment control: nothing is deleted, but every other agent's law is held at its initial value. It asks whether the selection already follows from each agent's response to the others' initial laws.\\
$P,P^0$ & Partition sequences selected along the flow and along a null flow, without event times and up to the order of merges of disjoint blocks.\\
$S_c,\mathsf C$ & A selector family and its set of constants, both fixed before the flows are analyzed. Verdicts are reported over the whole set.\\
$A_I,B_I$ & In the formation standard, the protocols a parent must answer as a block and the probes that can address its members before the handoff. Compression asks that some probe of $B_I$ see the erased distinction.\\
Counterfactual class & The declared set of actual and counterfactual handoff states against which the second stage must be shown to depend on the first.\\
(F5) & Interaction necessity: $P\neq P^0$ at every point of a nonempty open set of admitted data, against both nulls and for every constant of the declared set. A split verdict between the nulls fails, and a shift of event times alone does not count.\\
Two-stage selection & Short for two-stage selection with exact inheritance: selection, inheritance, and a second selection among parents, with at least one of compression, interaction necessity, or counterfactual dependence of the second stage shown to fail. It is not a formation, and the failed clauses are stated.\\
Single-stage selection & Selection and inheritance with one selection stage, so the recursion clause fails.\\
Undetermined & A construction with a clause neither established nor shown to fail; it is not shown to be a recursive formation, and the open clause is named.\\
$W_2(\nu,\mathsf P)$ & A Wishart law of positive-definite matrix contents, with real shape $\nu>1/2$ and precision $\mathsf P$. It is not a Gaussian closure.\\
$\alpha_i,\eta_i; A_I,B_I,n_I$ & Individual belief and model shapes, their block sums, and the number of primitive children retained by a parent.\\
$B^q_{IJ},B^s_{IJ}$ & Native source masses between current blocks, averaged over receivers with primitive-count weights.\\
$\mathsf H_I,\phi_I$ & Relative constant angular offsets discarded by a block, and its retained local mean phase at the contextual anchor.\\
$\ell_\psi,\mathcal X_{\mathrm{rich}}$ & The score of the common $\psi$ rotation, which is gauge relative to every declared algebra of the collective construction, and the rich preparation class on which its memory obstructions are stated.\\
$\mathcal R_{\mathrm{glob}}$ & The part of the regular branch of contextual alignments on which every transport is the unique global minimizer; only there is the reduced contextual cost the minimized one.\\
$X,H_X$ & A collective message matrix and $H_X=XX^T$. The displacement connection it defines preserves the fiber metric $H_X^{-1}$, so its holonomy is orthogonal for that metric.\\
$\mathcal C,B,F$ & The context base, a connection one-form, and its curvature. These require a rule for relating neighboring contexts. The symbol $F$ also denotes a discrete update in the inheritance chapter.\\
