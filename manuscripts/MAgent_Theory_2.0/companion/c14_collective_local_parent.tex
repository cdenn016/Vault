\chapter{A Collective Phase and Its Local Realization}
\label{cf:collective-local-parent}

Three circular laws can hide one common orientation without any child hiding an independent orientation. Each child communicates its angle law, and a query compares two sampled angles. Rotating one child changes such a response; rotating all three preserves every joint difference. A parent for the whole group can therefore erase one common angle even though no proper block can do so with its exterior retained. The symmetry belongs to the declared interface before grouping, and the interaction does not create it. No agent, channel, record or admitted query reads that common angle, so it is gauge relative to every observable algebra the construction declares, and the whole-group parent fails the compression clause of the formation standard. The construction is a single-stage selection with exact inheritance.

This quotient admits exact local evolution with a finite reference configuration and the complete history. A finite preparation generator gives another exact realization on a restricted family. A local memory can instead accumulate the change of the common phase from the actual preparation time. It preserves the geometry when the initial collective direction is coherent across context. That condition is assumed, not created by waiting or recording. The unrestricted causal-local-parent question remains inconclusive; the obstructions below concern specified memory classes.

\section{The transmitted-angle model}

Each child's law has a public sign $z$, marked angle $\phi$ and transmitted angle $\psi$:
\begin{equation}
 q_i=Z_i^{-1}\exp\{l_i z/2+A_i\cdot e_\phi+zC_i\cdot e_\phi+B_i\cdot e_\psi\},
 \qquad e_\theta=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta).
 \label{clp:law}
\end{equation}
Relative to a uniform sign and two Haar circles its normalizer is
\begin{equation}
 Z_i=I_0(|B_i|)
 \frac{e^{l_i/2}I_0(|A_i+C_i|)+e^{-l_i/2}I_0(|A_i-C_i|)}2.
 \label{clp:normalizer}
\end{equation}
Its presentation law is Bernoulli with log odds $n_i$. The self evaluator is $e^{\epsilon^3z\cos\phi}/I_0(\epsilon^3)$, uniform in $\psi$. Peer content channels copy the sign and the transmitted angle $\psi$ and resample the marked angle. The transmitted sign has log odds
\begin{equation}
 L_i=l_i+\log I_0(|A_i+C_i|)-\log I_0(|A_i-C_i|).
 \label{clp:public}
\end{equation}
All sources remain normalized. The per-agent model and own-block Fisher update are those of \cref{cf:per-agent-inheritance}, with self prior $1/5$, each peer prior $2/5$ and exact unit-temperature Gibbs rows.

The positive normalized record likelihood is
\begin{equation}
 \ell_{r,\lambda}(o,\omega\mid z,\phi)
 =\frac{e^{ro z/2}}{2\cosh(r/2)}
 \frac{e^{\lambda\cos(\omega-\phi)}}{I_0(\lambda)}.
 \label{clp:record}
\end{equation}
Evidence appends its actual outcome and keeps its likelihood in the objective. Its coefficients are $H_i=\sum ro$ and $\Lambda_i=\sum\lambda e_\omega$, with the entire ordered word also retained. A diagnostic prediction appends its result without becoming a likelihood. These operations remain distinct.

With $m_i=\tanh(L_i/2)$, $M(B)=\nabla\log I_0(|B|)$ and $t_i=\E[Z\cos\Phi]$, the content row is proportional to
\begin{equation}
 w_{ii}=\frac{e^{\epsilon^3t_i}}{5I_0(\epsilon^3)},\qquad
 w_{ij}=\frac{2e^{L_jm_i/2+B_j\cdot M(B_i)}}
 {5\cosh(L_j/2)I_0(|B_j|)}.
 \label{clp:row}
\end{equation}
The exact own-block field is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot l_i&=-l_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}L_j+H_i,&
 \dot A_i&=-A_i+\Lambda_i,\\
 \dot C_i&=-C_i+\beta_{ii}\epsilon^3e_1,&
 \dot B_i&=-B_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}B_j,\\
 \dot n_i&=-n_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\gamma_{ij}n_j.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{clp:field}
\end{equation}
These are Fisher equations on an invariant exponential family. The relative orientations affect source competition through $B_j\cdot M(B_i)$, and every derivative of the field retains that dependence. No source-role derivative of another agent's objective is inserted.

For $B_i=\delta b_i$ with small positive $\delta$ and noncollinear cone vectors, the added row terms are $O(\delta^2)$. The scaled public field remains a small smooth perturbation of the stable system with equilibrium $(15/7,15/7,5/7)$. Connect same-sign public odds when the smaller-to-larger magnitude ratio exceeds $1/4$. Initial odds near $\epsilon(4,1/2,-1)$ give singleton blocks; a common finite census gives the persistent full block with the initial $(+,+,-)$ episode fixed. In the prescribed null, deleting peer costs retains source labels, priors and entropy. Its self weight lies between $1/9$ and $1/5$ on the invariant box, and the third public sign stays negative. The renormalized null, with self weight one, keeps the same signs. Both fixed census times and labels are retained. Later evidence can change grouping while still having exact inherited dynamics. As in \cref{cf:per-agent-inheritance}, the comparison with the nulls holds for thresholds between $1/8$ and $1/3$ and fails above $1/3$, and the frozen-environment control already reproduces the grouping.

\section{Why the loss is collective}

The fixed protocol admits public, marked-lab, presentation and source-label queries, normalized evidence transactions and relative-angle responses on the complete graph. For a joint relative query, sample each involved child's $\psi$ once and return all requested differences. Shared vertices share samples. The first pair moment is
\begin{equation}
 c_{ij}=\E e^{\mathrm i(\Psi_i-\Psi_j)}=\zeta_i\overline{\zeta_j},\qquad
 \zeta_i=\frac{I_1(|B_i|)}{I_0(|B_i|)}e^{\mathrm i\arg B_i}.
 \label{clp:relative}
\end{equation}
For three nonzero laws, $|\zeta_i|^2=|c_{ij}||c_{ik}|/|c_{jk}|$. The Bessel ratio is strictly increasing because its derivative is a positive circular variance. Thus all concentrations and relative directions are identified, leaving one common orientation. With an exterior law fixed, $c_{ij}/\overline{\zeta_j}$ instead identifies the interior law itself. This is identifiability from exact response laws, not a finite-sample reconstruction method.

A triangle query uses three primitive samples, not independently sampled edge outputs. Its differences retain their cycle constraint. Every accessible label, wait, record and response/update dependence is kept. Absolute $\psi$ responses are excluded before and after selection. Adding one would defeat the quotient. No existing probe is deleted at handoff, and every transaction uses fresh samples.

The same exclusion decides the compression clause. Every admitted probe, the joint relative queries included, is invariant under a common rotation of all three angles, so the erased common orientation is gauge relative to the probes available before the handoff. It is resolved only by the child-level moment $\E e^{\mathrm i\Psi_i}$, which nothing in the population reads. The transmitted sector also fades: the cooperative $B$ equation gives $\frac{d}{dt}\max_i|B_i|\leq-\min_i\beta_{ii}\max_i|B_i|$, so the information carried by the common orientation decays exponentially.

\section{Finite reference memory closes the local laws}

Context $c$ indexes a prepared statistical family and is distinct from inference time $t$. Fix $c_0$ before retention. Let $x(c)=(l_i,A_i,C_i,B_i,n_i)_i$ and $x_\circ=x(c_0)$, with $S=\sum_iB_i(x_\circ)$. Rotate only the $\psi$ blocks $B_i$ by
\begin{equation}
 U(S)=\frac1{|S|}\begin{pmatrix}S_x&S_y\\-S_y&S_x\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 y=\mathsf R_Ux(c),\qquad \widehat x_\circ=\mathsf R_Ux_\circ.
 \label{clp:reference}
\end{equation}
The same matrix is used at every evaluation context. Cone projections obey $v\cdot B_i(t)\ge e^{-t}v\cdot B_i(0)>0$, so the anchor stays nonzero on every finite admitted branch. No angle branch is chosen.

The reference sum or angle alone need not close its own motion. Two reference tuples can share $S$ and have different public laws, hence different Gibbs rows and angular velocities. Keeping the full native reference tuple is a sufficient finite choice, not a proved minimal one. Let $V$ denote \cref{clp:field} and let $\mathcal J$ rotate each $B$ block through $\pi/2$. Exact differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \Omega&=\frac{\det(S,\dot S)}{|S|^2},\\
 \dot y&=V(y,c,h)-\Omega\mathcal Jy,\\
 \dot{\widehat x}_\circ&=V(\widehat x_\circ,c_0,h)-\Omega\mathcal J\widehat x_\circ.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{clp:closed}
\end{equation}
The time is unchanged. The subtraction is the velocity of the chosen representative, not a new parent force. This is the exact-lumping chain rule \citep{HorstmeyerAtay2016}, and the anchor is a slice in the method of slices \citep{RowleyEtAl2003}.

Retain the local two-jet, reference tuple, persistent coefficients and full history. The reference at fixed $c_0$ is held fixed under evaluation differentiation. With $W=V-\Omega\mathcal Jy$,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \dot y_r&=W_y y_r+W_r,\\
 \dot y_{rs}&=W_y y_{rs}+W_{yy}[y_r,y_s]
 +W_{yr}y_s+W_{ys}y_r+W_{rs}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{clp:jets}
\end{equation}
The explicit partials hold the local value, reference and word fixed. No third jet enters because the native field reads no contextual derivatives or neighboring contexts. Evidence updates the same likelihood at both evaluations; diagnostics only append outcomes. For a two-dimensional base the local law two-jet has 144 coordinates, plus 24 reference coordinates before constraints. The record word remains unbounded in length.

This is exact local evolution with finite reference memory, not initialization from the germ at $c$ alone. One local two-jet also cannot recover an unknown function at all other contexts. A quadratic initial natural-parameter polynomial realizes the actual local law germ and its derivatives; evolving it exactly preserves that jet at the evaluation point, without asserting equality away from it.

\section{The normalized laws carry the geometry}

The parent samples the canonical normalized recognition laws, presentations and original source-label factors. The reference is unsampled deterministic memory. Sampling it as another independent factor would generally change orbit inertia. To include the past, initialize the history law from the original normalized kernels and a compatible preparation seed; an arbitrary snapshot plus an observed word does not specify that word's probability. Retain the public-pattern conditioning normalizer.

At fixed $t,h$, rotating fresh $\psi$ samples by the reference matrix is independent of evaluation $c$. The actual experiment is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal P_{c,t}(dh,dY)=\Pi_c(dh)Q_{c,t}^h(dY).
 \label{clp:experiment}
\end{equation}
A passive marked frame rotates fresh $\phi$ and local embeddings of evaluator and record directions. Stored numerical lab labels stay fixed; presentations have the trivial representation of the same marked group. This action commutes with the reference rotation of $\psi$. Thus the actual contextual and marked orbit scores are carried isometrically, giving
\begin{equation}
 \E_{\mathcal P}(s_X+\ell_\xi)^2
 =\E_{\overline{\mathcal P}}(\overline s_X+\overline\ell_\xi)^2.
 \label{clp:cost}
\end{equation}
The minimizing connection class, isotropy, minimized tensor and locally free curvature agree. The same invariant joint transaction kernels prove every admitted finite adaptive response/next-state history by successive conditioning. There is no replacement by separate marginals or independent edge outputs.

For $B_i(c)=b_i e_{\chi(c)}$, the actual canonical law contains $b_i e_{\chi(c)-\chi(c_0)}$. It keeps the $\psi$-phase information
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i b_i\frac{I_1(b_i)}{I_0(b_i)}(d\chi)^2>0.
 \label{clp:missing-score}
\end{equation}
Erasing phase separately at each context would delete this term. Reference memory works because that dependence remains in the normalized density, not because a tensor is stored separately. The erased global angle cannot be recovered from the canonical reference and seed, since a simultaneous original rotation changes none of them.

The common nonflat window uses $A_1=ae_\chi$, $A_2=de_1$ with small $a,d<1/20$ and the same finite grouping entrance time. At $\epsilon=\delta=0$, its marked connection, curvature and positive rank-two tensor are those in \cref{cp:curvature}, where $B(t)$ denotes the marked inertia of the other children, not the $\psi$ parameter $B_i$ of this chapter. As there, the curvature is prepared in the initial section and is present at time zero and along the null flows. Its compact-window margins persist for sufficiently small positive $\epsilon,\delta$ by smooth dependence of the fine laws, jets and normalized history experiment. The $\psi$ anchor is applied only at positive $\delta$, and \cref{clp:cost} transfers the geometry exactly. Later evidence can change that window's geometry.

\section{Memory can accumulate a change of phase}

A rotating coordinate frame and the actual law it describes must be distinguished. At each context let $S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x)=\sum_iB_i(x)$ and put $\bar x=\mathsf R_{U(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x))}x$, using the same matrix as in \cref{clp:reference} but evaluated locally. Its sum points along the first axis. The local laws determine their own angular velocity,
\begin{equation}
 \omega_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x,c,h)
 =\frac{\det(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x),\sum_iV_{B_i}(\bar x,c,h))}
 {|S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\bar x)|^2}.
 \label{clp:local-velocity}
\end{equation}
The cone bound keeps the denominator nonzero. The public and marked coordinates enter through the original Gibbs rows, so the collective sum alone still does not determine this velocity. No absolute-angle query is added.

Start a scalar memory at the actual initialization time $t_*$ and use
\begin{equation}
 \dot{\bar x}=V(\bar x,c,h)-\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\mathcal J\bar x,
 \qquad\dot\vartheta=\omega_{\mathrm{loc}},\qquad\vartheta(t_*)=0.
 \label{clp:causal-memory}
\end{equation}
The parent samples the normalized native laws at $\widetilde x=\mathsf R_{R_\vartheta}\bar x$. Their derivative is
\begin{equation}
 \dot{\widetilde x}
 =\mathsf R_{R_\vartheta}(\dot{\bar x}+\dot\vartheta\mathcal J\bar x)
 =V(\widetilde x,c,h).
 \label{clp:causal-native-flow}
\end{equation}
The accumulated phase cancels the rotating-coordinate velocity. The actual laws obey the original own-block Fisher equations in the original time; no parent force is fitted. The angle memory records a change, and can be represented by a rotation matrix if a real angle branch is inconvenient.

The native flow and its reconstruction give
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde x(c,t)=\mathsf R_{U(S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*)))}x(c,t).
 \label{clp:causal-frozen-frame}
\end{equation}
This is a frozen local initial frame implemented causally, rather than a continuously supplied reference frame. In the language of the method of slices, \cref{clp:causal-memory} is the in-slice equation together with the reconstruction equation for the group variable \citep{RowleyMarsden2000}, so the accumulator is a choice of coordinates for the native solution and its properties follow from rotation equivariance. At a fixed context its rotation is constant throughout the trajectory. Consequently the same joint relative samples, source labels, public and marked-lab responses, evidence updates and waits generate every original adaptive history. Each primitive angle is still sampled once per joint query. Persistent likelihoods and both census records are retained. A diagnostic result stays distinct from assimilated evidence.

For geometry retain the local two-jet, the corresponding phase jet, the known coefficient jets, and the complete chronological word. Unlike a reference fixed at $c_0$, $\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}$ depends on evaluation context. Its derivative contributes
\begin{equation}
 \dot\vartheta_a=D\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}\bar x_a+(\omega_{\mathrm{loc}})_a.
 \label{clp:causal-phase-derivative}
\end{equation}
Differentiating once more closes at order two because both fields in \cref{clp:causal-memory} read only the local law value and fixed data. These derivatives enter the sampled $B_i(\widetilde x)$; a phase stored only beside the slice density would not restore its Fisher score. The memory is unsampled internal data, not an extra independent experiment.

The precise sufficient preparation condition is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*))}{|S_{\mathrm{loc}}(x(c,t_*))|}
 =e_*\in S^1\qquad\text{for all }c.
 \label{clp:initial-coherence}
\end{equation}
Its absolute direction is not retained. Each site then applies the same initial matrix without consulting another context. In \cref{clp:causal-frozen-frame} the sample shift is independent of $c,t,h$, commutes with the marked action and preserves Haar measure. This proves the complete actual-law cost equality \cref{clp:cost}, including history and conditioning scores. The parent therefore has the same minimizing connection, tensor and curvature. Context-constant initial noncollinear cone vectors $B_i=\delta b_i$, together with $A_1=ae_\chi$ and $A_2=de_1$, give the same rank-two nonflat finite window already described. Arbitrary later evidence preserves exact inheritance but need not preserve nonflatness or membership.

All canonical initial data, phase differences, replay seed and records are invariant under one common original rotation. Within \cref{clp:initial-coherence}, equal canonical initial fields differ by only that constant angle. The lost common orientation is not recovered through memory, while every proper block remains exposed to its exterior. This introduces no independently invisible child factor.

The start time matters. Carry the actual accumulator from $t_*$ through the selected census $T$. Setting it to zero anew at $T$ freezes the frame at $T$, whose direction need not remain context-constant even from a coherent initial preparation. No old record or memory is reset. To reproduce the probability law of the past, initialize the original normalized chronological experiment, or retain its canonical local preparation seed and replay its kernels. One observed word is not its own probability law. This remains a local field with an unbounded record, rather than one finite state storing an arbitrary contextual function.

For arbitrary preparations let $\alpha_0(c)$ be the initial direction angle. The actual score at corresponding samples is
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde s_X(T_0Y)=s_X(Y)-(X\alpha_0)\ell_\psi(Y).
 \label{clp:causal-score-defect}
\end{equation}
The moving sample map has deleted the contextual score of the common $\psi$ phase. Temporal integration supplies only
\begin{equation}
 d_c\alpha(c,t)=d_c\alpha_0(c)
 +d_c\int_{t_*}^t\omega_{\mathrm{loc}}(c,s)ds.
 \label{clp:causal-initial-datum}
\end{equation}
The missing datum is $d_c\alpha_0$, which \cref{clp:initial-coherence} sets to zero. Causal accumulation does not establish that condition. This exact phase derivative concerns $\psi$; it is different from the nonflat marked connection acting on $\phi$. The defect it causes is explicit: the connection class is still inherited for every preparation, and the minimized tensor changes by $(X\alpha_0)^2I-2(X\alpha_0)b(X)$, where $I$ is the $\psi$-phase inertia and $b(X)$ its pairing with the contextual phase gradients of the $B_i$. That defect can vanish at a single time without coherence, so coherence is a sufficient condition, not a proved necessary one.

The obstruction also depends on the declared group. Every datum is invariant under the common $\psi$ rotation, so the comparison group could include it. Then the score of that rotation is an orbit score, the comparison velocity absorbs a phase gradient, and pointwise canonicalization keeps the minimized tensor. The geometric statements of this chapter concern the marked group alone.

\section{Finite generators and scoped obstructions}

Freeze the reference frame once at census $T$, and the canonical local law thereafter follows the original field without a moving-frame term. This removes the live reference from future local evolution while keeping its coherent handoff information. It does not make initialization pointwise.

Alternatively, suppose the prepared section has a known finite generator $x_0(c)=f(c;p)$. Retain its coefficients canonical for the common $\psi$ rotation, the full chronological history and original time. Compose the original native flows and likelihood appends to evaluate the current law at any context. The normalized response kernels generate the same path law, and the same sample-coordinate argument proves \cref{clp:cost}. No evolved section is projected back onto a finite ansatz. The marked patch above, constant initial public odds and context-constant noncollinear $b_i$ give an explicit family inside the grouping window. It is a restricted preparation family, not an open set of arbitrary contextual functions. Replay work and word length may grow.

A derivative-only repair faces a stronger obstruction than one failed truncation. Suppose a smooth finite-order rule maps $j^k x$ and history to an actual parent law, with no separate phase-bearing reference memory. Require equality of both the minimizing connection classes and tensors for every section in the rich class of the main text: smooth sections satisfying the cone condition and the strict grouping and window margins, open in the $C^2$ topology, and closed under small supported polynomial bumps and small context-dependent common rotations. Hold the $k$-jet fixed and vary the next jet. The fine first score is fixed, while the parent tangent becomes $u+\tau w$. A fixed common minimizing representative would require
\begin{equation}
 \|u+\overline J A_f+\tau w\|^2=h_f^{\min}
 \label{clp:obstruction}
\end{equation}
on an interval. Its quadratic coefficient is the positive norm of $w$, hence $w=0$. Repeating by descending order removes all derivative dependence from the actual law. For random histories the coefficient is $\E_H\|w_H\|^2$ in the joint $L^2$ experiment; conditional equality is not assumed from averaged equality. The remaining order-zero phase quotient loses \cref{clp:missing-score}. The proof uses independent smooth jets and equal connection classes. It does not exclude reference memory or constrained generator families.

A different rank argument excludes one smooth finite state encoding every direct response of an arbitrary open section class over the entire patch. More disjoint public-law perturbations than state dimensions give a response Jacobian of rank too large to factor through that state. This is about one global encoding of an indexed experiment, not an added context-relocation action. It excludes neither a field of local states nor a restricted finite generator.

Keeping more temporal history cannot by itself supply an arbitrary initial contextual phase. On a fixed record branch the past values are a smooth function of the current value, because the native flow is invertible and evidence does not jump the state, so a value-path retention carries no more information than the current value; the result below is the order-zero case of the jet argument. To see it directly, rotate the whole initial block at each $c$ by a smooth angle $\kappa(c)$ held constant in time. The native local trajectory rotates by the same angle, while every original history law, persistent coefficient and census result is unchanged. A strictly local retention of the value path that erases constant rotations must therefore give the same complete retained state at each $c$. This remains true with the entire temporal path available and no bound on memory dimension, which on these branches adds no information. Derivatives, neighboring contexts and an initialized contextual phase field are excluded from this particular statement.

At a point with $\kappa=0$ and $X\kappa=\lambda$, the fine contextual score gains $\lambda\ell_\psi$. Conditional independence of the $\psi$ factors makes this score orthogonal to the marked orbit, and
\begin{equation}
 \|\ell_\psi\|^2
 =\E_H\sum_i|B_i^h|\frac{I_1(|B_i^h|)}{I_0(|B_i^h|)}>0.
 \label{clp:causal-positive-score}
\end{equation}
The fine minimized cost therefore contains a positive $\lambda^2$ coefficient, whereas the parent actual family is unchanged. They cannot agree on an interval of phase-gradient variations. Small variations preserve the original strict nonflat window. This excludes rotation-invariant local value-path memory for the rich preparation class. Regular stochastic retention kernels with the same complete invariance and phase-independent randomization obey the same argument.

There is also a class result for smooth finite-dimensional deterministic memories reading a uniformly bounded contextual jet. Suppose initialization, evolution, record updates and actual-law readout use $j^k x$ for one finite $k$, with no supplied reference or contextual phase field. On a fixed finite record branch the augmented jet-memory ODE closes. Its memory is a smooth function of the initial jet. The native fixed-record flow and its jet prolongation are locally invertible on their reached regular image, so the parent actual law is a smooth function $F_{t,h}(c,j^k x(t))$. The positive-norm argument in \cref{clp:obstruction} applies when both the original minimizing affine classes and tensors must agree. Thus adding arbitrarily many temporal recurrence steps within this smooth bounded-jet class does not evade that obstruction. The past-law initialization and joint-$L^2$ qualifications remain necessary.

Initial coherence restricts the phase-gradient variations, so its causal construction does not contradict either obstruction. Whether a causal, germ-local, geometrically faithful parent exists on arbitrary preparations of the rich class is posed as an open problem in the main text; it is answered with one reference read and on restricted preparation classes. Unbounded contextual-order memories, differently specified infinite-dimensional memories and stochastic mechanisms outside the value-path theorem have not all been classified. Any proposed calibration must come from information allowed by the original protocol, which has no context-relocation action. Interaction-created invisibility and a second compressive handoff remain open.
