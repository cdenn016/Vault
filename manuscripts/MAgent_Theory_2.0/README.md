# MAgent Theory 2.0

The main entry point is `MAgent_Theory_2.0.tex`. Its chapters and appendices contain the core constructions and proofs. `MAgent_Theory_2.0_supplement.tex` builds the separate supporting document. `MAgent_Theory_2.0_companion.tex` builds the self-contained physicist's companion; its explanatory chapters and editable TikZ figures are under `companion/`. All use the local style, preamble, and bibliography, with no external manuscript inputs. Scientific scope and editing conventions are in `AUTHORING.md`.

From this directory, compile sequentially with TeX Live:

```powershell
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -file-line-error -outdir=build MAgent_Theory_2.0.tex
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -file-line-error -outdir=build MAgent_Theory_2.0_supplement.tex
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -file-line-error -outdir=build MAgent_Theory_2.0_companion.tex
Copy-Item -LiteralPath build/MAgent_Theory_2.0.pdf -Destination MAgent_Theory_2.0.pdf
Copy-Item -LiteralPath build/MAgent_Theory_2.0_supplement.pdf -Destination MAgent_Theory_2.0_supplement.pdf
Copy-Item -LiteralPath build/MAgent_Theory_2.0_companion.pdf -Destination MAgent_Theory_2.0_companion.pdf
```

## Current state (2026-10-29)

Agents hold beliefs and generative models. `01_foundations` defines the generative model of an agent (a presentation prior, an evaluator as prior over contents, and a record likelihood with a declared density and reference measure), its variational free energy, a shared generative model up to frame (a species), and the observable algebras relative to which a distinction is called gauge (`def:observable-algebra`). Codes and languages are not components of the generative model. `03_dynamics` keeps three objectives apart: a global free energy over all agents, whose local free energies give coordinate descent of one functional; per-agent free energies, in which each agent's own model and its peers' communicated laws are competing mixture sources; and the reciprocal law potential, whose flow also moves sources toward receivers. Under Gaussian references, affine evaluators, linear-Gaussian channels and log-quadratic records both free-energy flows are tangent to the Gaussian product family, while the reciprocal flow leaves it (`prop:dyn-gaussian-closure`, `prop:dyn-gaussian-noninvariance`). `prop:dyn-no-global-vfe` proves, with fixed positive rows on finite alphabets, that the reciprocal potential is not an affine function of the free energy of any finite normalized model under any recognition rule when an attended edge is an identity channel, and that with fixed input-dependent channels it is not, on any open set, an affine function of the free energy of a model with product recognition by the agent laws; constant-output channels give an exact identification. `rem:dyn-no-global-scope` records the exact identifications outside these hypotheses (countable auxiliary latents, Gaussian-copula recognition on atomless spaces, constant channels) and what is not established (the Gibbs-reduced potential, law-dependent channels, charts). No objective is declared primary, and relating the three is an open problem (`op:dyn-three-pictures`).

The prediction chapter and the conditional-memory appendix integrate a general-law cue/result generative model with correlated model/content recognition. They include the exact local free-energy identity, Gaussian conditioning, signed cross-prediction response, posterior Fisher tensor, projection loss, a later-readout counterexample, frame and shared-model conditions, and a bounded sequential filtering construction. Exact local conditioning and output projection remain distinct from the product-family population flows; a common objective for arbitrary repeated communication and a new inheritance or formation result are not established.

The interacting-memory appendix extends this construction to two agents with a common preparation, private records, a shared transcript, and posterior-sampling messages. It derives the actual finite-history likelihood, sender invariance, receiver assimilation, full and reduced free-energy identities, and an exact joint-private-record retained state with fixed reconstruction. The joint private-record posterior is a product tilt of the fixed preparation coupling (`imem:product-tilt`): each message multiplies its speaker's factor, the cross-agent dependence never changes, and with the preparation fixed the two marginals determine the posterior. The four weights of the binary example are therefore a redundant form of two changing numbers. Affine singleton-presentation Gaussian banks close exactly, but multiple presentations need not remain a finite Gaussian mixture, and the first posterior is already not one. The appendix is positioned against agreement theorems and Bayesian dialogue. The assumptions supply the cross-agent relation and communication policy; new measurements, model adaptation, minimal retention, asymptotic agreement of repeated dialogue, and formation remain open.

`05_inheritance` states the formation standard `inh:recursive-formation` with clauses (F1) to (F6): an admitted class open in the chart topology (full grade) or relatively open in a declared invariant family (family grade); selection by a selector family whose constants are fixed in advance and range over sets with nonempty interior; inheritance for declared parent protocol and child-level probe classes (`inh:protocol-classes`); compression, which asks that some declared child-level probe resolve the erased distinction on the reachable post-handoff set; interaction necessity, pointwise on a nonempty open set against both the label-preserving and the renormalized null (`inh:null-flow`), for every declared constant, with the frozen-environment control reported beside it; and a second stage that is selected under the inherited dynamics and does not form from a set with nonempty interior of a declared counterfactual class (`inh:counterfactual-class`). A split verdict between the two nulls counts as failing. A construction meeting selection, inheritance and the second selection but shown to fail compression, interaction necessity or counterfactual dependence is a two-stage selection with exact inheritance; a clause neither established nor refuted leaves the construction undetermined, not shown to be a recursive formation. Agents that do not interact fail interaction necessity (`inh:no-interaction-null`), and the anchored linear consensus `inh:anchored-consensus` calibrates the standard: it passes interaction necessity and fails the counterfactual clause.

No construction in the manuscript is proved to satisfy every clause. Every construction of `06_fusion` to `12_open_formation` concerns the reciprocal potential (in `06_fusion`, a nonsmooth modification of it), and those of `13_per_agent_inheritance` and `14_collective_local_parent` concern the per-agent free energies. The fusion construction of `06_fusion` is a two-stage selection with exact inheritance: compression fails because fused children are identical; it meets the interaction-necessity clause only through its exact-equality selector, since on the common-offset family its coalescence order is the normalized-centroid linkage of the initial data for every coupling (`fus:ncl`) and a threshold selector gives the same sequence along the nulls; and its second stage forms from every counterfactual handoff. `07_smooth_closure`, `09_public_compression` and `10_boundary_closure` are retention constructions with no selection claim; in `10_boundary_closure` the two nulls coincide and preserve the same family and interface, so the exact hierarchy is a property of the prepared symmetry. The gated erasure construction of `08_smooth_erasure` is a single-stage selection whose interaction necessity is undetermined: its first and last partitions agree with the nulls and its intermediate partitions are not determined. The Wishart construction of `11_conditional_formation` is a two-stage selection with exact inheritance: its schedule is the centroid dendrogram of the initial shapes at every coupling and is reproduced by the renormalized null and the frozen-environment control, the label-preserving null has agent-dependent clocks at zero coupling and integrations indicate a different schedule on an open set, so the verdict is split and fails, and the union forms from every counterfactual handoff. The six-agent construction of `12_open_formation` establishes selection, exact inheritance and compression on an open class over a finite window, but neither interaction necessity nor counterfactual dependence; integrations of the limit field indicate that the nulls form the same blocks, its block relations are transient, and it is not shown to be a recursive formation. In `13_per_agent_inheritance` the persistent grouping, whose small-signal limit is the anchored opinion pooling of Friedkin and Johnsen, passes interaction necessity against both nulls for ratio thresholds in (1/8, 1/3) and fails above 1/3, and the frozen-environment control reproduces it; the angular parent quotients a phase that is gauge relative to every declared algebra, so compression fails and the construction is a single-stage selection with exact inheritance. The collective construction of `14_collective_local_parent` erases one common orientation, also gauge relative to every declared algebra, and is likewise a single-stage selection with exact inheritance.

The collective/local-parent chapter also gives exact local two-jet evolution with finite reference-state memory, actual normalized-law Fisher-cost inheritance, a conditional finite preparation-and-history realization, and a causal local phase accumulator whose actual normalized laws follow the original-time native field and preserve every admitted finite adaptive joint history. Exact geometric inheritance is proved under initial collective-phase coherence across context, a sufficient condition not shown to be necessary; the memory does not generate it. Its obstructions, an order-zero obstruction for rotation-invariant value-path retentions and one for smooth finite-dimensional memories reading a uniformly bounded contextual jet on the declared rich preparation class, refer to the declared marked structure group and lose their force when the common rotation is added to it (`lp:group-dependence`). The unrestricted causal-local-parent question remains INCONCLUSIVE.

The contextual-coupling chapter `15_contextual_coupling` adds an auxiliary alignment energy to the reciprocal potential on declared finite-dimensional regular families. The finite regular-branch theorem holds on the regular critical set, and the reduced cost is the minimized cost only on its globally minimizing part; for general linear frames of dimension at least three the branch hypothesis fails at every consensus context. The continuum local Sobolev theorem on a supplied flat torus now needs shape in H^s with s > d/2 + 1 and frame and row log odds in H^(s-1). No locally gauge-invariant eliminated energy diffuses frames. Global regularity, orbit-type changes, independent row/link/frame smoothing, and graph refinement remain open.

The base-transport chapter `16_link_base_transport` constructs base transport once comparison labels, columns, weights and a metric are declared. Its rectangular selector is a connection on the general linear frame bundle with holonomy orthogonal for the induced metric; the curved local-alignment example uses reciprocal rotation links with a declared bi-invariant metric and has Abelian holonomy. Link-only alignment needs an invariant target metric, which conjugation targets of the general linear group never admit. These selectors are kinematic, need not equal the law-Fisher selector, and give no new dynamical or formation theorem.

The communication, link-dynamics and curvature-hierarchy chapters `17_communication_links`, `18_link_dynamics` and `19_curvature_hierarchy` contain the supported results of Items 1 to 3. Identification of dictionaries modulo code stabilizers assumes a known code. The link-dynamics chapter gives the coupled Gaussian reciprocal Fisher equations, global forward existence with convergence of each trajectory to one stationary point, and an exact three-agent stability threshold; for its reference data the local critical mode is a subcritical pitchfork, and the sign of the quartic coefficient depends on those data. Its per-agent link model is a record-likelihood model distinct from the mixture-source model of `03_dynamics`, and source-role stationarity has an EM fixed-point form. The induced curvature cost is a quadratic cost of an affine coded residual; its cohomological part is stated in the small-noise limit at fixed anchors and, within the tangent model only, in the weak-anchor limit, where the omitted log-determinant dominates; which regime defines it is open. The standalone working documents `Item_1_communication_links.tex`, `Item_2_link_dynamics.tex` and `Item_3_curvature_hierarchy.tex` carry status notes: the chapters are authoritative for every shared statement and label, and later chapter refinements are listed there rather than repeated.

The working document `Item_7_formation_feedback.tex` is not part of the main manuscript and is not cited by it. Its twelve-agent shared-label instance is not shown to be a recursive formation, and its classification is undetermined. Fixed-partition exact inheritance in Gaussian families of the sufficient statistic is proved; both null histories are proved on the whole admitted class (the label-preserving null never selects, the renormalized null selects only the block of all twelve agents); the gap-equation roots and the saddle-node are certified by interval arithmetic; the first-stage history of the shared-label flow rests on fixed-step integration with explicit margins. Open are (F2) and (F5) over a declared constant set, inheritance (F3) of the hybrid process with candidate clocks and merges, second-stage compression (F4), and (F6b) against a declared counterfactual class; widened initial states examined so far give uniting handoffs. The frozen-environment control selects the four groups and no union. The earlier convex no-go is restricted to all-time persistence: for the finite-dwell selector a strictly convex Gaussian hierarchy can meet (F6b). This status agrees with the statement in the abstract that no construction is proved to satisfy every clause.
