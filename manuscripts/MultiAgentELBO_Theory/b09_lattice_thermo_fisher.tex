\chapter{Thermodynamic Fisher Geometry of Retained Ensembles}
\label{ch:thermodynamic-fisher-draft}

This chapter asks whether the Fisher tensors of the native lattice experiment
have volume densities that are differentiable in the source parameters, and
whether a fixed blocking word preserves them. The answer is affirmative on
compact positive high-temperature source boxes: the native pressure is $C^4$
and its Fisher density $C^2$ (\cref{thm:rg-thermo-native-cfour-pressure,prop:rg-thermo-native-ctwo-metric}),
and the finite free law pushed along a fixed word has a $C^2$ Fisher density
per coarse cell, equal to that of the infinite-law marginals although the
finite laws differ (\cref{thm:rg-thermo-native-free-push-ctwo}). With these
branch inputs the finite-mixture Fisher asymptotics of
\cref{sec:cg-retained-label-asymptotics} in the main manuscript hold for the
lattice with $N=L^2$ cells, in the strict phase, at a tie, and in the
reciprocal-volume window (\cref{cor:rg-thermo-native-label-limits}). These
statements refine the label rates of \cref{ch:geometry-pressure}; their
proofs, and the lattice-side label results that accompany them, are in
\cref{app:thermodynamic-fisher-draft-proofs}. Every Fisher tensor here
belongs to the complete-data experiment that observes the label and the
configuration (\cref{sec:rg-thermo-finite-experiment}), and no result uses
one as the metric of a dynamics.

The chapter keeps the lattice notation of \cref{ch:constructive-spatial-rg}
and adds the following. The square $\Lambda_L$ has $N=L^2$ cells, and a fixed
word of total linear factor $B$ maps $B^2N$ fine cells to $N$ coarse cells.
The source point in natural coordinates is $\mathsf g=(h,j)$, with
$b=\tanh h$ and $t=\tanh j$; a statistical parameter $\theta$ ranges over an
open $\Theta\subset\R^d$, source maps are $\mathsf g_\gamma:\Theta\to\R^2$, and
$\mathcal K$ denotes a compact parameter or source set. The natural
log-partition density is $\mathsf A$, its Hessian $\mathsf H$, and
$\mathcal I_B$ is the Fisher density per coarse cell of a push along a word
of factor $B$. The bulk log-mass densities $\mathsf s_\gamma$, tie slopes
$\mathsf v_\gamma$, branch Fisher densities $\mathcal I_\gamma$, limiting label
law $\bar{\mathsf p}$, and window coordinate $\mathsf w$ are those of
\cref{def:rg-thermo-label-family} and the statements that follow it.

The chapter and its proof appendix do not claim a solution of a
self-consistent curvature action, a regular inverse at unbounded depth, a
critical or continuum limit, a simultaneous limit in depth and volume, or a
derivative theorem for an arbitrary positive likelihood or for the free law
of a canonical coarse interaction. The constructions that would settle the
first four are part of
\cref{open:rg-spatial-generality}, and the typing of the lattice sites is
\cref{open:rg-lattice-typing}. \status{NOT-CLAIMED}

\section{Source box, carriers, and fixed words}
\label{sec:rg-thermo-native-families}

\hypothesisheading{Compact native source box}
{hyp:rg-thermo-native-source-box}
Fix a compact rectangle $\mathcal K$ inside $h>0$,
$0<j<\operatorname{atanh}(1/12)$, and an enlarged rectangle
$\widehat{\mathcal K}=[h_-,h_+]\times[0,j_+]$, with $h_->0$ and
$0<j_+<\operatorname{atanh}(1/12)$, whose positive-$j$ interior contains
$\mathcal K$. Choose $\tau>0$ with $12\tanh(j_+)e^\tau<1$ and put
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm q=12\tanh(j_+)e^\tau,\qquad
 \mathrm c_1=\frac{16\mathrm q}{1-\mathrm q},\qquad
 \rho_* =\tanh(h_++2j_+),\qquad d_*=1-\rho_*>0.
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-compact-constants}
\end{equation}
These are the constants of \cref{thm:rg-spatial-constrained-specification}
at the plaquette strength $\tanh j_+$, fixed on $\widehat{\mathcal K}$. The
edge $j=0$ of $\widehat{\mathcal K}$ is used only for one-sided continuation
and for the product control. The hypothesis excludes nonpositive fields,
plaquette couplings with $j\geq\operatorname{atanh}(1/12)$, and noncompact
source sets, and it is standing for this chapter and
\cref{app:thermodynamic-fisher-draft-proofs}.

\definitionheading{Native free law and its exact pushes}
{def:rg-thermo-native-families}
On $\Lambda_L$ retain all $2N$ signs $z_{x,a}$, $a=1,2$, write
$P_x=z_{x,1}z_{x+e_1,2}z_{x+e_2,1}z_{x,2}$ for the plaquette products,
$x\in\{0,\ldots,L-2\}^2$, and let $r_L(\mathsf g,z)$ be the raw factor
\cref{eq:rg-pressure-native-factor}. The finite free law is
\begin{equation}
 \nu^{\mathrm f}_{L,\mathsf g}(z)=\frac{4^{-N}r_L(\mathsf g,z)}{Z_L(\mathsf g)},
 \qquad Z_L(\mathsf g)=\sum_z4^{-N}r_L(\mathsf g,z),
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-free-factor}
\end{equation}
and $\mu_{\mathsf g}$ is the unique infinite native Gibbs law of
\cref{thm:rg-spatial-source-law}. For a fixed integer $B\geq1$, retain the
paths $\mathcal P_B(x,a)=\{(Bx+ke_a,a):0\leq k<B\}$, complete them to a
partition of the fine edges with unused singleton blocks, and let $C_B$
record their parities, as in \cref{def:rg-spatial-path-channel}. The three
families are
\begin{equation}
 P^{\mathrm f}_{B,L,\mathsf g}=(C_B)_\#\nu^{\mathrm f}_{BL,\mathsf g},\qquad
 \mu_{B,\mathsf g}=(C_B)_\#\mu_{\mathsf g},\qquad
 \mu_{B,L,\mathsf g}=\mu_{B,\mathsf g}|_{\Lambda_L},
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-three-families}
\end{equation}
each with its own normalized reference. A coarse translation by $x$ is a fine
translation by $Bx$, and local limits use the centered squares of
\cref{def:rg-pressure-native}. A fixed finite word of integer factors with
total linear factor $B$ acts by $C_B$ on the same fine square, by
\cref{prop:rg-spatial-path-square}, and is fixed throughout every volume
limit. Restricted parity conditionals use the product of uniform references
on their prescribed fibers, and unrevealed paths and unused singleton blocks
remain unconstrained.

\section{Volume limits and exact pushforward}
\label{sec:rg-thermo-draft-limit-organization}

The finite free laws, their pushes along a fixed word, and the marginals of
the infinite law share their limiting Fisher densities but not their finite
laws. The statements below keep the three families apart.

\theoremheading{Native free pressure in \(C^4\)}
{thm:rg-thermo-native-cfour-pressure}
Define the natural statistics and pressures by
\begin{align}
 \mathsf T_{h,L}&=\sum_{x\in\Lambda_L}\sum_{a=1}^2z_{x,a},\qquad
 \mathsf T_{j,L}=\sum_{x\in\{0,\ldots,L-2\}^2}P_x,\qquad
 \mathsf A_L(\mathsf g)=\frac1N\log\E_{\mathrm{ref}}
                          e^{h\mathsf T_{h,L}+j\mathsf T_{j,L}},
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-natural-pressure}\\
 \mathfrak p_L(\mathsf g)&=\frac1N\log Z_L(\mathsf g).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-label-natural-pressure}
\end{align}
There are $C^4$ functions $\mathsf A$ and $\mathfrak p$ on the domain $h>0$,
$0<j<\operatorname{atanh}(1/12)$ such that $\mathsf A_L\to\mathsf A$ and
$\mathfrak p_L\to\mathfrak p$ in $C^4(\mathcal K)$ for every compact source
box of \cref{hyp:rg-thermo-native-source-box}, with the finite and limiting
conversions
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \mathfrak p_L&=\mathsf A_L-2\log\cosh h-\frac{(L-1)^2}{N}\log\cosh j,\\
 \mathfrak p&=\mathsf A-2\log\cosh h-\log\cosh j.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-pressure-conversion}
\end{equation}
\status{ESTABLISHED}

\propositionheading{Native Fisher density and a source pullback}
{prop:rg-thermo-native-ctwo-metric}
The finite free family satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac1N I(\nu^{\mathrm f}_{L,\mathsf g})=\nabla_{\mathsf g}^2\mathsf A_L(\mathsf g)
 \longrightarrow \mathsf H(\mathsf g):=\nabla_{\mathsf g}^2\mathsf A(\mathsf g)
 \quad\text{in }C^2(\mathcal K),
 \qquad
 \mathsf H=\nabla_{\mathsf g}^2\mathfrak p+
      \operatorname{diag}(2\operatorname{sech}^2h,
                              \operatorname{sech}^2j).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-fisher-pressure}
\end{equation}
If $\mathsf g:\Theta\to\R^2$, $\mathsf g(\theta)=(h(\theta),j(\theta))$, is $C^4$ and
sends a neighborhood of a compact parameter set into an admitted source box,
then
\begin{equation}
 \frac1N I(\nu^{\mathrm f}_{L,\mathsf g(\theta)})
 =D\mathsf g(\theta)^\top\nabla_{\mathsf g}^2\mathsf A_L(\mathsf g(\theta))D\mathsf g(\theta)
 \longrightarrow
 D\mathsf g(\theta)^\top\mathsf H(\mathsf g(\theta))D\mathsf g(\theta)
 \quad\text{in }C^2,
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-source-pullback}
\end{equation}
whereas the Hessian of $\mathsf A_L\circ\mathsf g$ differs from this pullback by
$\sum_{i=1}^2(\partial_i\mathsf A_L\circ\mathsf g)\nabla^2_\theta\mathsf g_i$, and the
Hessian of the raw pressure differs from $\mathsf H$ by the diagonal term
above. \status{ESTABLISHED}

In natural coordinates the native law is an exponential family, so its
Fisher density is the Hessian of the log-partition density, the Hessian
structure of exponential families \citep[Ch.~3]{AmariNagaoka2000} and the
thermodynamic metric of \citet{Crooks2007}.

\theoremheading{Exact pushed-free Fisher density}
{thm:rg-thermo-native-free-push-ctwo}
For every fixed integer $B\geq1$ and compact positive source box,
\begin{equation}
 \frac1N I(P^{\mathrm f}_{B,L,\mathsf g})
 \longrightarrow\mathcal I_B(\mathsf g):=
          \E_{\mu_{B,\mathsf g}}\mathsf I_{\mathcal P}(\mathsf g,Y)
 \quad\text{in }C^2(\mathcal K),
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-free-push-limit}
\end{equation}
where $\mathsf I_{\mathcal P}$ is the Fisher matrix of the four-state
predictive row of a coarse cell with the complete lexicographic past
$\mathcal P$ of the coarse configuration $Y$ prescribed and every future path
free. This is the density
\cref{eq:rg-spatial-fisher-density} of the infinite-law marginals, although
the finite laws of the two limits need not agree, and at $B=1$
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal I_1=\mathsf H=\nabla_{\mathsf g}^2\mathsf A.
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-native-identity-density}
\end{equation}
\status{ESTABLISHED}

For $B>1$ the push need not be a two-statistic exponential family, and
$\mathcal I_B$ is not identified with the Hessian of a native or canonical
coarse pressure.

\propositionheading{Branch inputs from the native theorems}
{prop:rg-thermo-label-native-map}
Let each $\mathsf g_\gamma:\Theta\to\R^2$,
$\mathsf g_\gamma(\theta)=(h_\gamma(\theta),j_\gamma(\theta))$, be $C^4$ on a
neighborhood of a compact $\mathcal K\subset\Theta$ and map it into a common
compact admitted source box, and let $p_{\gamma,L,\theta}=\nu^{\mathrm f}_{L,\mathsf g_\gamma(\theta)}$.
Then the branch raw pressure
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak p_{\gamma,L}:=\frac1N\log Z_{\gamma,L}
 =\mathsf A_L\circ\mathsf g_\gamma-2\log\cosh h_\gamma
   -\frac{(L-1)^2}{N}\log\cosh j_\gamma
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-label-raw-pressure}
\end{equation}
converges in $C^4(\mathcal K)$ to a limit $\mathfrak p_\gamma$, and the branch
Fisher densities satisfy
\begin{align}
 \frac1NI(p_{\gamma,L})
 &=(D\mathsf g_\gamma)^\top
       (\nabla^2_{\mathsf g}\mathsf A_L\circ\mathsf g_\gamma)D\mathsf g_\gamma,
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-label-native-pullback}\\
 \frac1NI(p_{\gamma,L})
 &\longrightarrow\mathcal I_\gamma
 =(D\mathsf g_\gamma)^\top(\mathsf H\circ\mathsf g_\gamma)D\mathsf g_\gamma
 \quad\text{in }C^2(\mathcal K),
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-draft-native-density}
\end{align}
with bounded $C^2$ norms. For the push along a fixed word of total factor
$B$, from $B^2N$ fine cells to $N$ coarse cells,
\begin{align}
 I_\theta(p_{\gamma,BL}C_B)
 &=(D\mathsf g_\gamma)^\top
 I_{\mathsf g}(P^{\mathrm f}_{B,L,\mathsf g_\gamma(\theta)})D\mathsf g_\gamma,
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-label-push-pullback}\\
 \frac1N I_\theta(p_{\gamma,BL}C_B)
 &\longrightarrow (D\mathsf g_\gamma)^\top
      (\mathcal I_B\circ\mathsf g_\gamma)D\mathsf g_\gamma
 \quad\text{in }C^2(\mathcal K).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-label-push-input}
\end{align}
No rank assumption is needed for these semidefinite pullbacks.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

\corollaryheading{Lattice instances of the finite-mixture asymptotics}
{cor:rg-thermo-native-label-limits}
Let the branches be the free laws of \cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-native-map},
with raw label weights satisfying \cref{eq:rg-thermo-label-raw-growth}, in
either normalization mode, and let $\mathsf s_\gamma$ be the bulk limits of
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-representatives}. Write $P_{L,\theta}$ for the
labeled law on $\Lambda_L$.
If a label $\gamma_*$ has a uniform positive gap in $\mathsf s_\gamma$ on
$\mathcal K$, then
\begin{equation}
 \frac{I(P_{L,\theta})}{N}\longrightarrow\mathcal I_{\gamma_*}(\theta)
 \quad\text{in }C^2(\mathcal K).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-draft-strict-density}
\end{equation}
At a tie $\theta_0$, if $W_{\gamma,L}(\theta_0)\to w_\gamma$ and
$\mathsf v_\gamma=\nabla\mathsf s_\gamma(\theta_0)$, then
\begin{equation}
 \frac{I(P_{L,\theta_0})}{N^2}
 \longrightarrow \operatorname{Cov}_{w}(\mathsf v_\gamma).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-draft-tie-density}
\end{equation}
If the center ratios of \cref{eq:rg-thermo-label-center-ratios} converge to
positive $\kappa_\gamma$ on the tied set $S$, then in
$\theta=\theta_0+\mathsf w/N$ the label law and the Fisher tensor satisfy,
in $C^3$ and $C^2$ on compact $\mathsf w$-sets,
\begin{align}
 \bar{\mathsf p}_\gamma(\mathsf w)
 &=\frac{\kappa_\gamma e^{\mathsf w\cdot\mathsf v_\gamma}}
         {\sum_{\gamma'\in S}\kappa_{\gamma'}e^{\mathsf w\cdot\mathsf v_{\gamma'}}},
 \qquad \gamma\in S,
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-draft-window-weights}\\
 N^{-2}I(P_{L,\theta_0+\mathsf w/N})
 &\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cov}_{\bar{\mathsf p}(\mathsf w)}(\mathsf v_\gamma).
 \label{eq:rg-thermo-draft-window-metric}
\end{align}
The same conclusions hold for the pushes along a fixed word of total factor
$B$ in the coarse volume $N$, with the branch densities of
\cref{eq:rg-thermo-label-push-input} and with $\mathsf s_\gamma$ and
$\mathsf v_\gamma$ replaced by $B^2\mathsf s_\gamma$ and $B^2\mathsf v_\gamma$,
and for the selected posterior of the record of
\cref{hyp:rg-pressure-positive-record} under the sensor hypotheses of
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-representatives}, with its posterior
representatives and the shifted branches.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

\noindent\emph{Proof.}
\Cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-representatives} gives the growth hypothesis
\cref{eq:rg-thermo-label-effective-growth} with bulk limits $\mathsf s_\gamma$
in both modes, and \cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-native-map} gives $C^2$
convergence of the branch densities, hence
$I(p_{\gamma,L})(\theta_0)=O(N)$. With $N=L^2$ these are the hypotheses of
\cref{thm:rg-thermo-label-strict}, of \cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-tie-scale},
and, with the center ratios, of \cref{thm:rg-thermo-label-crossover}, whose
conclusions are the three displays. For a push, the label weights are those
of the fine volume $B^2N$, so the representatives divided by $N$ converge to
$B^2\mathsf s_\gamma$ in $C^3$ with the same winner and center ratios, and
\cref{eq:rg-thermo-label-push-input} supplies the branch densities. For the
posterior, the same proposition supplies the posterior representatives and
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-native-map} applies to the shifted source maps.
$\square$

The strict density is a semimetric unless it has a positive lower bound on
the chart in use; on such charts its inverse, Levi-Civita connection, and
curvature converge by \cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-regular-geometry}, and
\cref{cor:rg-thermo-native-regular-charts} exhibits nonempty charts. A native
pullback has rank at most two by
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-native-finite-energy-floor}. The order-$N^2$ tie
information is information about the label: in the native realization of
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-native-witness} the experiment that forgets the
label has zero Fisher information, in line with
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-finite-missing-information}. Residual log-weight
gradients and rare labels enter before score squaring
(\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-residual-scales} and
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-tie-counterexamples}). The reciprocal-volume scale
and the logistic label law have the form of the finite-size rounding at a
first-order phase transition, where
\citet[Thm.~3.2]{BorgsKotecky1990} derive, for two coexisting phases, a
hyperbolic-tangent crossover of width of order $N^{-1}$ in the field from the
lattice model itself; here the phase mixture is
prescribed through the effective log masses, which makes the statement
elementary. Parameter-independent comparison kernels between the window
experiment and its limit, its identifiable quotient, and a moving center for
a transverse two-label tie are
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-label-comparison-kernels,prop:rg-thermo-label-quotient,thm:rg-thermo-label-moving-center}.
Along a fixed word an extensive density carries the factor $B^2$ and a
density normalized by squared volume the factor $B^4$, provided the
denominator, the parameter coordinate, and the observed experiment agree on
both sides (\cref{prop:rg-thermo-account-superextensive}). Prescribed label
actions change the raw weights, and their relative center values, volume
orders, and derivative bounds govern the resulting experiments by
\cref{prop:rg-thermo-account-action-growth,prop:rg-thermo-account-action-ratios};
curvature actions keep the types of \cref{def:rg-geometry-curvature-factor}.
