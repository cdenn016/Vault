## Verdict

Algorithm 1 is an exact description of the forward computation that runs under the archived configuration. An independent float64 implementation written only from the manuscript formulas reproduces the code to a relative error of 1.2e-6 in the final mean and 5.3e-5 in logits on 24 windows, and the current source reproduces the run's own saved per-layer diagnostics to six significant digits at layer 0. All eight items the coordinator asked about agree with the text (table in the item-by-item trace below). The discrepancies are about what the evaluated model does with that computation, and about what the text omits. The main one is that the explicit attention residual is numerically inert in the trained model: the message exceeds the pre-attention state by three to four orders of magnitude, and deleting the residual changes cross-entropy by less than 4e-4 nats (lead L4 confirmed, but with a corrected comparator). Second, the routing covariance is predominantly generated by the frames, because an unreported weight decay on the log-variance table drove the stored variances to about 1. Third, the linked code no longer contains the selectors Section 5.2 cites or the mean-only and dot-product control Section 6.4 proposes, and it cannot load the archived configuration.

## Findings

### 1. The explicit attention residual is numerically inert in the evaluated model
**Location:** Main eq:relational_predictive_residual (886) and its gloss (889); Table 2 row "Predictive residual" (1085); Algorithm 1 line 1117; Sec. 5.2 (1132, "the additive attention residual"); Sec. 6.1 (1173); Sec. 6.4 (1183).
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:**
- *Code.* mu_att = mu + m with coefficient exactly 1 (`965718d relational.py:124-126`; VFE_3.0 HEAD `relational.py:133-135`), followed by the shared LayerNorm and the MLP (`965718d block.py:232,237`).
- *Executed.* This used the trained weights on 24 windows of 128 tokens, all ran through current source, with 12 test and 12 validation windows. The per-token ratio of norms of m_i to mu_i has median 2.1e4, 5.9e3, 5.3e3, and 1.8e3 in layers 0 to 3, and its minimum over all 3,072 tokens is 693. The residual changes the LayerNorm output by at most 1.8e-3 in relative terms. Setting mu_att = m in all four layers changes full-vocabulary CE by -8.7e-6 nats on average (extremes -3.4e-4 and +2.6e-4). Rescaling every O_h by 0.5 or by 2 changes CE by at most 2.6e-4, so the overall scale of the message is a flat direction of the trained model.
- *Mechanism.* The comparison and content maps train with weight decay 0 (`965718d train.py:365`), and LayerNorm is invariant to the scale of its input. Once m dominates mu, the loss barely depends on the scale of O_h T_h.
- *Records.* The recorded max singular value of O_h T_h grows from 1 at initialization (identity block placement) to 43 at step 1200, 663.6 at 12000, and 4578 at 240000 (`metrics.csv`). `parameter_motion.json` shows 36 to 48 times norm growth of T and O. At step 1200, 0.5% of training, the last-layer message already had a per-token RMS of 16640/sqrt(128), about 1.5e3.
- *What remains.* In the evaluated model, u_i = LN(U_i sum_h O_h a_i^h) up to a relative 2e-3. Token identity reaches the block through the receiver frame U_i, the query row of the routing, and the routed self-term (mean beta_ii 0.09 to 0.13 under causal_alibi, which includes the self position). It does not arrive through the additive mu_i.

**Fix:** Add to Sec. 5.2: "In the trained model the attention message dominates the pre-attention state (median per-token norm ratio 1.8x10^3 to 2.1x10^4 across layers). Deleting mu_i from mu^att changes cross-entropy by less than 4x10^-4 nats on 24 sampled windows, so at the final weights the layer acts as u_i = LN(m_i). The residual role earlier in training was not measured." Reword the Table 2 row and the residual-necessity sentences at 1173 and 1183 to cite this. Add a placeholder figure plotting the residual-to-message norm ratio over training together with a no-residual retrain.
**Falsifies:** A replay of the original bf16 GPU path in which residual removal changes CE by at least 1e-2 nats. Alternatively, evidence that the dirty historical source normalized reference coordinates or rescaled m before LN; this is unlikely, since current source reproduces the saved layer-0 diagnostics to six digits. The finding is about the final weights; it does not show the residual is unnecessary for training.
**Status:** The residual is D (a definitional architectural choice). Its functional effect is E and unreported. The prose presents it as an operative component of the recorded run and leaves "residual necessity" open when the saved model already shows it carries no measurable signal at the end of training.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new; tests lead L4 and corrects its comparator (see Leads tested).

### 2. The routing covariance is mostly frame-generated; the stored variances were decayed to about 1 by an unreported log-variance weight decay
**Location:** Main 1108, 1111, 1134 ("The stored beliefs are diagonal Gaussians..."), 1171. Config `sigma_init=15`, `sigma_weight_decay=0.04`.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:**
- *`sigma_init` is a variance.* The table stores log-variance initialized at log 15 (`965718d prior_bank.py:1014,1022`), and encoding exponentiates it (`965718d numerics.py:104`, `exp(clamp(log, max=80)).clamp(min=eps)`). So sigma^2 = 15 initially, sigma about 3.87.
- *Decay.* The sigma-role optimizer group carries decoupled AdamW decay 0.04 (`965718d train.py:309,324`). `weight_norm_sigma` falls from 9277 at step 1200 to 241 at step 240000 (`metrics.csv`). In the checkpoint (executed), the RMS log-variance over all 12.9M entries is 0.067, and 84.6 percent of entries lie within 10 percent of unit variance. Variances of sampled tokens span 0.52 to 1.93, median 1.05. `research.json` reports `sigma_trace_cv=0.047`.
- *Consequence.* C_i = U_i^-1 Sigma_i U_i^-T is approximately (U_i^T U_i)^-1.
- *Executed interventions (frozen final weights).* Routing with C_i replaced by I_K, which removes every covariance term while keeping means and S, raises CE by +0.325 nats (8 windows, range +0.11 to +0.57). Routing with the stored Sigma_i replaced by I while keeping frames raises it by +0.024 plus or minus 0.024 nats (24 windows). On the paired windows that is 9 percent (at most 18 percent) of the full covariance effect.

**Fix:** In Sec. 5.2 report `sigma_init` as an initial variance of 15, the 0.04 decoupled decay on log sigma^2, and the final variance distribution. State that the routing covariance of the reported model is predominantly the frame metric (U_i^T U_i)^-1 with near-unit stored scales. Add both interventions, or a placeholder for them, to the planned Gaussian versus mean-only routing figure.
**Falsifies:** Historical source showing the sigma group was not decayed; the `weight_norm_sigma` trajectory would then need another cause. Alternatively, a bf16 GPU replay giving a materially larger Sigma to I share.
**Status:** E. The manuscript is silent. Readers cannot tell that the Gaussian covariance used by the router is mostly a learned per-(token, position) frame metric rather than stored variances.
**Novelty vs prior review:** extends R3 (decays) and R6/C7 with a measured consequence; tests L3.

### 5. The decoder prior is underspecified and redundant with the learned bias; decode_tau is inert
**Location:** Main eq:predictive_linear_decoder (953-959), Algorithm 1 line 1121. Config `decode_unigram_prior=true`, `unigram_kappa=1.0`, `decode_bias=true`, `decode_tau=0.008`.
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:**
- *kappa_uni.* It is `unigram_kappa`=1.0, stored in a fixed buffer and never learned (`965718d prior_bank.py:1267`).
- *p_uni.* It is the add-one-smoothed unigram of the training token stream, filled once before training (`965718d prior_bank.py:1244`; `train.py:2080`).
- *Redundancy with b_out.* b_out is learned from zero with weight decay 0 (`965718d train.py:338`); `parameter_motion.json` shows its norm moving from 0 to 626. Because b_out is a free per-class bias, the kappa_uni log p_uni term only offsets its initialization: z = W mu + (b_out + log p_uni).
- *decode_tau.* The linear decoder discards the decode temperature (`965718d prior_bank.py:2946-2948`), and the constructor warns "decode_tau=0.008 is inert when decode_mode='linear'" (executed). `decode_log_scale` is therefore a dead parameter; it is included in the 456,654,162 count.

**Fix:** After eq:predictive_linear_decoder, add: "p_uni is the add-one-smoothed unigram frequency of the training stream and kappa_uni = 1 is fixed. Because b_out is learned without decay, this term acts as an initialization offset for b_out." Note that decode_tau is unused by the linear decoder.
**Falsifies:** Historical source in which kappa_uni is learnable or b_out is absent.
**Status:** D, underspecified. No correctness issue.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new (the brief's settled list covers only the dead decode_log_scale).

### 6. The implementation fixes H = B and r_h = s_h = d_b, and keys the temperature to the frame-block dimension
**Location:** Main 366-373, 1036, 1053, 1100 ("tau_h = sqrt(32)"), 1185.
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:**
- *Ranks and head count.* The relational operators set score and content rank to the gauge-block dimension d, and the head count to the number of blocks (`965718d relational_operators.py:62-63,68`; archived shapes (8,32,256) and (8,256,32)).

## Extensions of prior R3 (not re-raised; new specifics only)

The following optimizer and initialization facts extend R3. Anchors are `965718d train.py` and `prior_bank.py` unless stated.

- **Per-role clipping.** The executed clipping is per role only, with one L2 bound of 1.0 for each of `mu`, `sigma`, and `phi` (`train.py:1006-1025`). The `mu` role is a catch-all that clips jointly the mean table, W_out, b_out, all four MLPs, all 16 relational maps, and the LayerNorm affine. `weight_norm_mu` in `metrics.csv` is the norm of that aggregate (`train.py:968-974`), not of the embedding table.
- **Weight decays.** The groups are:
  - mean table and W_out: 0.025;
  - log-variance table: 0.04 (Finding 2);
  - token and positional frame tables: 0.05;
  - MLP: 0.01;
  - relational maps, b_out, and LayerNorm affine: 0.
- **Initialization-only scales.** `mu_init_std=0.065`, `phi_scale=0.035`, and `pos_phi_scale=0.02` are standard deviations of the initial mean, token-frame, and positional-frame tables (`prior_bank.py:1020,1025`; `model.py:593`). None is a forward multiplier: encoding reads the frame table directly, and the learned positional builder ignores its `scale` argument.
- **Frame projection coverage.** The post-update frame projection of R3 covers both the 50,257 token rows and the 128 positional rows (`phi_chart_total_rows=50385`).

## Leads tested

- **L1 (unitary-gauge normal form).** CONFIRMED at the code level, with a quantification.
  - *Where frames enter.* Frames enter exactly at:
    - (a) xi = U^-1 mu in routing and content;
    - (b) the static C_i;
    - (c) the local-coordinate LayerNorm and MLP, applied to U_i times the reference-space message;
    - (d) W_out acting on local mu^(L).
    Nothing else reads phi: `mass_phi=0`, and the frame is fixed through the stack.
  - *Static C_i.* C_i is static per (token, position) because Sigma passes through unchanged and the frame is built once.
  - *Measured roles.* Removing all covariance from routing costs +0.325 nats, and about 91 percent of that effect remains when only the stored variances are set to I, so role (b) is carried by the frames. With Finding 1, role (c) is effectively u_i = LN(U_i sum_h O_h a_i^h).
- **L2 (Kretschmann-type objection).** Outside this lens. Code premise confirmed: S_Q, S_K, T, and O are unconstrained free matrices (orthonormal-row and identity-block initialization, no intertwiner constraint).
- **L3 (static covariance as pair biases).** MODIFIED.
  - *Confirmed.* Covariance is static per (token, position).
  - *Sources.* The stored variances are near 1 because of the 0.04 log-variance decay, and the covariance-dependent terms are therefore mostly frame-generated.
  - *Size.* The terms are not negligible: removing them costs +0.325 nats on 8 windows. The stored variances contribute only +0.024 plus or minus 0.024 nats.
- **L4 (residual numerically inert).** CONFIRMED, with a corrected comparator.
  - *Displacement diagnostic.* `relational_mean_displacement_norm` is the Frobenius norm of m over one 128 x 256 window (`965718d relational.py:129`, `model.py:3164`). Per-token RMS is about 6.5e4 at layer 0.
  - *Comparator error.* `weight_norm_mu`=3024.9 is not the embedding-table norm; it aggregates every mu-role group. The actual embedding row norms have median 2.45 on sampled tokens, so the layer-0 ratio is about 2e4, larger than the lead states.
  - *Measured effect.* The LayerNorm sensitivity to the residual is at most 1.8e-3, and deleting the residual changes CE by at most 3.4e-4 nats.
- **L5 (parameter redundancy).** CONFIRMED at the code level. There is no gauge fixing, and the relational maps carry weight decay 0.
  - *Exact invariance.* The common comparison-space scale (S_Q, S_K) to (c S_Q, c S_K) leaves D exactly invariant.
  - *Additional flat direction.* The inert residual creates one more: rescaling O_h by 0.5 or by 2 changes CE by at most 2.6e-4, because LayerNorm is scale-invariant.
  - *Interpretation.* The 36 to 54 times norm growth is consistent with drift along these directions.
- **L6 to L10.** Outside this lens; not tested.
