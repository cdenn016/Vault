## Lead hypotheses from the coordinator (TEST THEM; try to refute them; do not assume them)

L1 (unitary-gauge normal form). Every quantity in the attention operator factors through the gauge-invariant reference moments (xi_i, C_i); under g_i = U_i^{-1} the operator becomes frame-free (executed residual 4e-15). The frames therefore act as dressing (Stueckelberg-type) fields, and in the REPORTED model they have exactly four functional roles: (a) factored input embedding xi^(0) = U_i^{-1} mu_p(x_i); (b) the routing covariance C_i = U_i^{-1} Sigma_p(x_i) U_i^{-T}, which, because covariance is passed through unchanged and frames are fixed through the stack, is a STATIC function of (token type, position), not of context; (c) token-and-position-dependent conjugation of the coordinate LayerNorm+MLP block, xi^(l+1) = U_i^{-1} G(U_i xi^att) with G = (id+MLP) o LN; (d) token/position-dependent decoder W_out U_i. Loop traces of routed links equal Tr(A_{i0 i1} ... A_{i_{r-1} i0}) with A_ij = sum_h beta O_h T_h, i.e. are frame-independent. Hence "lattice-gauge structure at the level of link fields" (Conclusion) may overstate: the links are gauge-covariant composite bilocal operators whose invariant content is that of the frame-free A_ij.
L2 (Kretschmann-type objection). Covariance is obtained by letting learned frames transform; interface maps are defined in a global reference space; so covariance places no constraint on S_Q, S_K, T, O (unlike steerable/gauge CNNs where frames are given geometric data and kernels must be intertwiners). Relevant literature: dressing-field method (Fournel, Francois, Lazzarini, Masson 2014 J. Math. Phys.; Attard et al. 2018 review), Kretschmann 1917 / Norton 1993.
