## Verdict in one paragraph

Every variational identity in scope is correct as stated, and every one recomputes to machine precision (table at the end). The pair is, if anything, too modest about its own variational content. Three exact results that the manuscript does not state are available at no new modeling cost and all serve the no-neural-architecture program: the Gibbs row is the exact conditional posterior of a source label at every positive temperature (including the bounded cost actually used), not only at unit temperature; the content aggregation, the unit residual, and the multi-head sum are together the exact predictive mean of a normalized mixture-of-channels Gaussian kernel whose moment-matched covariance is a principled replacement for covariance passthrough; and the evaluated linear decoder is exactly the unit-temperature Gibbs readout of a shared-covariance Gaussian template family. The one genuine consistency gap is that the main-text local mixture (Section 2.6) builds a generative factor from the sender state that the text calls a belief, while the supplement (line 59) forbids generative factors from reading recognition laws; the construction is legitimate only under a reading of the states as fixed values, which the paper never states. The closest prior art for the content-mixture reading (Movellan and Gabbur) is uncited.

## Findings

### F1. The unit-temperature restriction is unnecessary: a binary compatibility record makes the Gibbs row the exact label posterior at every positive temperature, including the capped cost
**Location:** Main 65, 719-721, 900, 1100, 1189; Supp 68-74; broader theory 05b 546-574 (normalization "remains declared model data").
**Severity:** medium (top-ranked; a strengthening of the paper's headline variational claim, not an error in it).
**Evidence:** Attach to each receiver-head pair a label $J_i^{(h)}\sim\pi_i^{(h)}$ (independent) and a binary record $o_i^{(h)}\in\{0,1\}$ with $P(o_i^{(h)}=1\mid z,J_i^{(h)}=j)=\exp[-D_{ij}^{(h)}(z)/\tau_h]$ and $P(o_i^{(h)}=0\mid z,j)=1-\exp[-D_{ij}^{(h)}(z)/\tau_h]$. Since $D\ge0$ this is a normalized kernel for every $\tau_h>0$; the implemented bounded cost $\bar D\in[0,8K]$ (Main 1100) is also nonnegative, and any merely finite cost works after the label-independent shift $D-\min_kD_{ik}$ (the shift becomes a factor $c_i(1,z)$, exactly the form Supp 68 allows). Records are conditionally independent children of the latent configuration, so the joint is normalized for any source graph, cyclic ones included (05b, Prop. "Normalization on arbitrary interaction cycles").
SymPy (S2, exact): the label posterior equals the Gibbs row identically; $\sum_j\beta_j\log[\beta_j/(\pi_je^{-D_j/\tau})]=D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\beta\|\pi)+\tau^{-1}\sum_j\beta_jD_j$ identically; the main-text objective satisfies $\mathcal A_i=\tau\mathcal F_i^{\mathrm{att}}$ identically; $\min_\beta\mathcal F_i^{\mathrm{att}}=-\log P(o_i=1\mid z)$ identically.
Brute force (N6: 3 receivers on a cyclic source graph, 4 latent states, $\tau\in\{0.25,1,\sqrt{32},10\}$, costs nonnegative, capped at 3, or arbitrary real with the shift): total mass minus one at most $8.9\times10^{-16}$; conditional posterior versus product of Gibbs rows at most $4.4\times10^{-16}$; full free energy under $Q_Z\prod_i\beta_i$ minus $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_Z\|p_Z)+\sum_i\mathcal F_i^{\mathrm{att}}(\beta_i)$ at most $2.1\times10^{-14}$; BFGS minimization of the full free energy over one row versus $\operatorname{softmax}(\log\pi-\mathbb E_{Q_Z}D/\tau)$ at most $1.8\times10^{-7}$ (optimizer tolerance). The recognition optimum differs from the $Q_Z$-average of pointwise posteriors by total variation up to 0.278, as 05b 645-651 states.
The broader theory already uses this device and does not cite it here: 07j 245-267 declares "a binary observation $E_i$ ... $\Pr(E_i=1\mid x,S_i=j)=q_0\exp[-\delta f(\cdot)]$ ... The complementary probability defines $E_i=0$, and the observed record is $E_i=1$ for every $i$," and 07j 328-335 obtains "the product of the receiver posterior rows." This does not conflict with the Research wiki result of 2026-07-27 that "a divergence-scored row at nonunit temperature is the exact coordinate of no model in the family": that family is the Gaussian link model in which the label selects the generator of the receiver state, and a nonunit temperature there needs a tempered normalizer with a log-determinant logit. The binary record lies outside that family because it generates a compatibility event, not the receiver state.
What this discharges: the supplement's "Normalization over all possible records is an additional hypothesis" (Supp 68) now has a constructive witness; the implication at Main 65 and 1189 that general temperature lacks a normalized-joint realization is removed; and the evaluated routing ($\tau_h=\sqrt{32}$, capped KL, causal ALiBi prior) is itself an exact conditional posterior, which upgrades Main 1100. What it does not discharge: Main 721 and 900 remain literally true (general $\tau$ is not the KL of the Section 2.6 mixture); Supp 74's caveat stands (the ELBO sector is $\mathcal A_i/\tau_h$, so rows with different $\tau_h$ are not independently weighted sectors of one ELBO); the record is a declared pseudo-observation whose evidence $P(o=1)$ is a partition function, not data; and if the states were latent with nondegenerate recognition, conditioning on compatibility would tilt their posterior toward agreement (the optimism described in Levine 2018, Sec. 2.3), which is irrelevant while states are held fixed during routing. A point mass on a continuous latent has infinite relative entropy to a continuous prior, so "exact posterior at deterministic states" should be phrased as conditioning on the states as data.
**Fix:** (i) Main, after line 720: "At every $\tau>0$ the same row is also an exact posterior. Attach a binary compatibility record $o_i$ with $P(o_i=1\mid J=j)=\exp(-D_{ij}/\tau)$ and $P(o_i=0\mid J=j)=1-\exp(-D_{ij}/\tau)$. Because $D_{ij}\ge0$ the kernel is normalized for every positive temperature, and $P(J=j\mid o_i=1)=\beta_{ij}^*$ at the given states. Equation~\eqref{eq:F_align_canonical_tau} is $\tau$ times the corresponding label free energy, and its minimum $-\tau\log Z_i$ is $\tau$ times the surprisal $-\log P(o_i=1)$. The record is a declared modeling event of the kind used for optimality variables in control as inference \citep{Levine2018,ToussaintStorkey2006,Kappen2012}; it is not an observed datum." (ii) Main 65, replace the unit-temperature sentence: "At every positive temperature it is also the exact conditional posterior of a source label in a normalized joint that records query-key compatibility as a binary event; at unit temperature it is additionally a coordinate minimization of a normalized mixture KL." (iii) Main 1189: "At every positive temperature the routing row is the exact conditional posterior of a source label under a normalized binary compatibility record; at unit temperature it is also the coordinate minimizer of a local Gaussian mixture KL." (iv) Main 1100, add: "The bounded cost is nonnegative, so the evaluated routing is also the exact label posterior of the binary-record joint for that cost." (v) Supp 68-74: use the display proposed under F6, which also closes prior R5. Optionally cite \citet{BissiriHolmesWalker2016}, already in `references.bib` and unused: their Eq. (7) makes $\int l d\nu+d_{\mathrm{KL}}(\nu,\pi)$ the cumulative loss minimized by the Gibbs update, with the KL term forced by coherence (Sec. 1.1) and the loss weight $w=1/\tau$ calibrated in their Sec. 3.
**Falsifies:** a nonzero residual in S2 or N6; a nonnegative cost with $\exp(-D/\tau)\notin(0,1]$ (impossible); or a reading of Supp 68 in which "normalization over all possible records" means something other than normalization of the record kernel over its record space.
**Status:** Main presents general $\tau$ as S ("a separately declared entropy-regularized extension", 1189); the exact result is D (two-line derivation). The prose deflates.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new. The prior review lists "Same local-joint KL interpretation at every temperature: Not claimed" and does not observe that a different normalized joint realizes the row at every temperature.

### F2. The Section 2.6 local mixture satisfies the supplement's own no-reading rule only if the Gaussian states are fixed values, and in that reading the Gibbs row is a restricted coordinate, not the source posterior given the query
**Location:** Main 298 and 307 ("belief and prior sections"), 688-701, 721, 824; Supp 59, 76; broader theory 05b 23-24, 62-64, 553-556.
**Severity:** medium (internal consistency between main text and supplement at the point where the variational status is claimed).
**Evidence:** In $P_i(y,J=j)=\pi_{ij}p_{K,j}(y)$ the factor $p_{K,j}=\mathcal N(S_K\xi_j,S_KC_jS_K^\top)$ is a pushforward of the sender state $q_j$, which the main text calls a belief section (298, 307). Supp 59 states that latent variables "may themselves take values in a space of probability laws; this does not permit a generative factor to read the recognition distribution being optimized," and 05b 62-64 and 553-556 require that no kernel "reads a recognition law" and that the energy "does not contain a live recognition distribution." The mixture is compatible with that rule under exactly one kind of reading: the Gaussian parameters $(\xi_j,C_j)$ are values, either latent points of the statistical fiber (05b 23-24 permits "a belief-law point") held at a point mass, or, more cleanly, structural data computed from the prefix before routing (the $X$ of Supp Eq. S1). The point-mass version has infinite relative entropy to any continuous state prior, so only the structural-data version gives a finite free energy. Under this reading the only optimized recognition variable is $\beta_i$, the local joint carries no observation record, and the receiver enters only through the query law clamped as the recognition marginal of $y$: $P_i$ itself does not contain the receiver state. Neither document states this reading; Supp 76 instead calls the mixture "a more specific local identity," although it is a different joint from the record construction of Supp 68 (an auxiliary comparison variable with a clamped recognition marginal and no record), which merely shares its unit-temperature row objective.
The clamping has a precise consequence (`jeffrey_check.py`). Over all joints $Q(J,y)$ whose $y$-marginal is fixed to $q_{Q,i}$, the minimizer of $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q\|P_i)$ is $q_{Q,i}(y)P_i(J\mid y)$, with value $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_{Q,i}\|P_{i,Y})$ (residual $1.1\times10^{-16}$; unconstrained numerical minimization agrees to $1.3\times10^{-14}$) and label marginal $\int q_{Q,i}(y)P_i(J=j\mid y)dy$, the Jeffrey (probability-kinematics) update. The Gibbs row is the minimizer only within the factorized family of Main 688. In a random discrete instance the two label laws differ by total variation 0.065 and the restricted KL is 1.152 against 0.865. Read as a routing problem, the paper's own Section 2.8 example (query $\mathcal N(0,4)$, keys $\mathcal N(0,1)$ and $\mathcal N(2,1)$, uniform prior) gives Gibbs weight 0.11920 for the second key against the Jeffrey value 0.32383. This is the same pair of optima the paper distinguishes carefully for the decoder in Section 2.8 and that 05b 645-651 distinguishes as $\beta^{Q\star}$ versus $\mathbb E_{Q_Y}\beta^P$, but it is not drawn for routing. The main text never calls $\beta$ a posterior (it says "factorized recognition law" and "coordinate minimization"), so no statement is false; the point is that the record reading of F1 is the one in which $\beta$ is a posterior given the receiver's evidence, while in Section 2.6 the receiver's information is imposed as a constraint.
**Fix:** Main, after 692: "Here the Gaussian states are fixed values at the given context: they are computed from the prefix before routing and enter the local joint as data, so $p_{K,j}$ reads a state value rather than a recognition law being optimized, and $\beta_i$ is the only recognition variable. The receiver enters only through the clamped query law. Within the factorized family the Gibbs row is the coordinate minimizer; allowing the label law to depend on $y$ gives instead $\int q_{Q,i}(y)P_i(J=j\mid y)dy$, which generally differs. The binary compatibility record of Eq.~(F1) is the reading in which the row is an exact posterior." Supp 76: replace "supplies a more specific local identity" with "supplies a different local joint with the same unit-temperature row objective." In the notation paragraph of Section 4 (near 1134), one sentence connecting this to the stored covariance being an operational state.
**Falsifies:** a passage in either document that already declares the states as data or fixed latent values; or a reading of Supp 59 under which a generative factor built from a sender belief is not "reading a recognition distribution."
**Status:** D for the identity; the variational interpretation is S and currently underspecified. Neither inflated nor deflated as an identity; the interpretation is ambiguous.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new; extends C7 (stored covariance as operational state), which the value reading makes precise.

### F3. Content aggregation, the unit residual, and the multi-head sum are the exact predictive mean of a normalized mixture-of-channels kernel; its moment-matched covariance is a principled replacement for covariance passthrough
**Location:** Main 881-900 (single-head content mixture), 937 and 1119 (passthrough), 1053 (residual "not obtained by choosing a step size"), 1069 (single-head mixture covariance), 1171, 1175-1176; Supp 76-78.
**Severity:** medium (a principled strengthening that answers part of what Main 1176 and Supp 78 leave "unresolved").
**Evidence:** Give each head an independent label $J_i^{(h)}$ and take the directed kernel
$$k_i'\mid k,J_i\sim\mathcal N\Bigl(k_i+\sum_hL^{(h)}_{iJ_i^{(h)}}k_{J_i^{(h)}},Q_i\Bigr),\qquad Q_i\succeq0,$$
This is a normalized kernel for any link rank (a degenerate Gaussian if $Q_i$ is singular), and the layer index orders the composition, so no cycle arises. With the labels distributed by the per-head Gibbs rows (the exact label posteriors of F1 given the prefix-measurable records) and state values $k_j=\mu_j$, the predictive mean is $\mu_i+\sum_{h,j}\beta^{(h)}_{ij}L^{(h)}_{ij}\mu_j=\mu_i^{\mathrm{att}}$ exactly. With independent source states $k_j\sim\mathcal N(\mu_j,\Sigma_j)$ (self edge included, as the causal support does), the moment-matched covariance is
$$\Sigma_i^{\mathrm{att}}=Q_i+\Sigma_i+\sum_h\beta_{ii}^{(h)}\bigl(L_{ii}^{(h)}\Sigma_i+\Sigma_iL_{ii}^{(h)\top}\bigr)+\sum_j\sum_{h,h'}c_{ij}^{hh'}L_{ij}^{(h)}\Sigma_jL_{ij}^{(h')\top}+\sum_h\Bigl[\sum_j\beta_{ij}^{(h)}n_{ij}^{(h)}n_{ij}^{(h)\top}-\bar n_i^{(h)}\bar n_i^{(h)\top}\Bigr],$$
with $c_{ij}^{hh}=\beta_{ij}^{(h)}$, $c_{ij}^{hh'}=\beta_{ij}^{(h)}\beta_{ij}^{(h')}$ for $h\ne h'$ (heads that pick the same source share its draw), $n_{ij}^{(h)}=L_{ij}^{(h)}\mu_j$, $\bar n_i^{(h)}=\sum_j\beta_{ij}^{(h)}n_{ij}^{(h)}$. In reference coordinates, with $M_h=O_hT_h$ and $Q_i=U_i\widetilde Q_iU_i^\top$, every frame cancels: $C_i^{\mathrm{att}}=\widetilde Q_i+C_i+\sum_h\beta_{ii}^{(h)}(M_hC_i+C_iM_h^\top)+\sum_j\sum_{h,h'}c_{ij}^{hh'}M_hC_jM_{h'}^\top+(\text{spread of }M_h\xi_j)$. SymPy (S3, scalar, two heads, self edge plus one source): mean and variance residuals exactly 0. NumPy (N7, $K=3$, two heads, three sources including the receiver, rank-one links, exhaustive label enumeration): mean residual $4.4\times10^{-16}$, covariance residual $1.8\times10^{-15}$; passive frame change of all inputs gives $g_i\Sigma_i^{\mathrm{att}}g_i^\top$ to $1.1\times10^{-14}$; the single-head formula printed at Main 1069 is reproduced to $1.1\times10^{-16}$. Passthrough keeps only the term $\Sigma_i$; in the test instance the eigenvalues of $\Sigma_i^{\mathrm{att}}-\Sigma_i$ are 2.26, 6.47, 22.87. Under passthrough $C_i$ is static per token and position; under moment matching it would depend on context through $\beta$, the source covariances, and the disagreement among routed messages, which addresses the static-covariance concern raised in the brief (L1(b), L3).
The multi-head structure is the factorial (multiple-cause) construction of factorial hidden Markov models, in which each chain selects a component and "the sum of the contributions of all the chains is the mean of a gaussian distribution" (Ghahramani and Jordan 1997, as described by Jacobs, Jiang and Tanner 2002). The label posterior stays the product of per-head Gibbs rows only because $k_i'$ is predicted rather than observed: conditioning on $k_i'$ would couple the heads by explaining away. The same condition refines the exclusivity hypothesis (F6): a latent child that reads $J$ leaves the row unchanged if it is integrated out or carried with its exact conditional in the recognition (the cancellation of Main 891-900); a mean-field factor $Q_c$ independent of $J$ would instead feed $\mathbb E_{Q_c}\log r_j(c)$ back into the row.
What this derives: the unit residual as a persistence term, the content aggregation, and the head sum as one predictive mean at any temperature, any link rank, and any number of heads; a covariance update with the correct passive law. What it does not derive: $T_h$, $O_h$, $Q_i$ (free); the identity persistence coefficient (a declared choice; $A_ik_i$ would give $A_i\mu_i+m_i$); additivity across heads and the use of one label for both the compatibility record and the content channel (declared); LayerNorm and the MLP (Supp B.2 limits); the point collapse between layers (the next layer consumes the predictive mean as a state value, an assumed-density projection of a mixture whose component count grows with depth); population cross-covariances $\Gamma_{jk}$ (Supp B.1 terms would add); and cross-entropy training, which is maximum likelihood for the point-collapsed predictor, not for this latent model.
**Fix:** Add to Section 3.1 after 900 the kernel display, the predictive-mean identity, and the covariance display above, followed by: "Covariance passthrough retains only the term $\Sigma_i$; the displayed covariance is the moment-matched alternative, which the evaluated model does not use." Revise 1176 and Supp 78 to: "For the attention sublayer, a normalized latent model exists in which routing is the exact label posterior and the content-plus-residual update is the exact predictive mean. Unresolved are the normalization and MLP maps, the point collapse between layers, and the relation of cross-entropy training to that model's evidence."
**Falsifies:** a nonzero residual in S3 or N7; a reading in which the heads' routing must be jointly (not independently) normalized; or evidence that the implemented residual is not coefficient one.
**Status:** paper: the residual is a declared architectural choice (S), the content mixture is D for one head; the kernel result is D. The prose deflates.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new (the prior review records "Normalized content cancellation selects content maps: Not claimed" but not the predictive-kernel identity or the covariance).

### F4. The closest precedent for the content-mixture reading and for the compensating key-norm prior is uncited
**Location:** Main 67 (precedents), 702-720 (entropy-regularized softmax), 891-900 (content mixture), 1013-1020 (compensating prior, adjacent to my scope).
**Severity:** medium (positioning of the paper's probabilistic reading of attention).
**Evidence (primary source, extracted text of arXiv:2010.15583v3):** Movellan and Gabbur, "Probabilistic Transformers" (2020), Eq. (2): $p_i(q,v\mid x)=\sum_j\pi_{i,j}(x)p_i(q,v\mid u_j,x)$, "where $\pi_{i,j}(x)$ is the probability of activating unit $j$ when unit $i$ is queried"; Eq. (7): $p_i(q,v\mid u_j,x)=p_i(q\mid u_j,x)p_i(v\mid u_j,x)$, that is, query and value conditionally independent given the unit, which is the paper's independent content law $r_j$; Eqs. (14): $v^{t+1}=\sum_jw^t_{i,j}\mu_j$ with responsibilities $w$ ("The $n\times n$ matrix $w$ corresponds to the Attention matrix"); Eq. (16): $\pi_{i,j}\propto e^{\frac\alpha2\|\xi_j\|^2}e^{\frac\beta2\|\mu_j\|^2}$, which "link[s] the priors of each unit to the length of the key", and as $\beta\to0$ Eqs. (20)-(21) give standard attention. Eq. (16) is the paper's compensating prior at Main 1014-1019 with $\alpha=1/\tau$ after whitening; Eq. (20) is the paper's mixture-mean readout. The content mixture of Section 3.1 and the compensating prior of Eq.~\eqref{eq:projected_key_bias_compensation} are their full-covariance, Gaussian-KL analogues, in which the query enters as a Gaussian law rather than a point. Entropy-regularized attention maps are analyzed by \citet{Niculae2017Regularized}." **Falsifies:** a citation of these works elsewhere in the pair (none found by search of both files and `references.bib`).
**Status:** the paper's claim is organizational ("the explicit organization of ... predictive content"); the mixture-mean reading itself has precedent. Neither inflated nor deflated, but unpositioned.
**Novelty vs prior review:** extends R7 (which asked for CRATE and Transformer-MGK only); tests part of lead L8.

### F5. The evaluated linear decoder is exactly the unit-temperature Gibbs readout of a shared-covariance Gaussian template family
**Location:** Main 824, 850-853, 857 ("the Gaussian-bank readout is a separate probabilistic construction"), 951-958, 1171.
**Severity:** low (an exact identity that changes how the reported readout should be described).
**Evidence:** For $\pi_v=\mathcal N(\Sigma_*w_v,\Sigma_*)$ with any $\Sigma_*\succ0$, the class-dependent part of $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\|\pi_v)$ at $q=\mathcal N(\mu,\Sigma)$ is $-w_v^\top\mu+\tfrac12w_v^\top\Sigma_*w_v$, independent of $\Sigma$. Choosing $\log\omega_v=b_{\mathrm{out},v}+\kappa_{\mathrm{uni}}\log p_{\mathrm{uni}}(v)+\tfrac12w_v^\top\Sigma_*w_v+\mathrm{const}$ makes $\operatorname{softmax}_v(\log\omega_v-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\|\pi_v))=\operatorname{softmax}_v(W_{\mathrm{out}}\mu+b_{\mathrm{out}}+\kappa_{\mathrm{uni}}\log p_{\mathrm{uni}})$ for every $q$. SymPy (S4, $K=2$, general symmetric $\Sigma$ and $\Sigma_*$): derivatives of the logit difference with respect to $(w_v,b_v)$ are exactly 0. NumPy (N8, $V=7$, $K=4$, 50 random beliefs with arbitrary covariance): maximum probability difference $3.4\times10^{-15}$. This is the Gaussian discriminant with shared covariance (Bishop 2006, Sec. 4.2.1; equation numbers not re-verified because the PDF returned HTTP 403), read at the level of the expected log. The covariance of the belief cancels, which explains exactly why the evaluated decoder reads only $\mu^{(L)}$. The paper's own 2.8 example uses shared-covariance templates, so its Gibbs value $1/(1+e^2)$ equals the plug-in posterior $p(1\mid x=\mathbb E_qX)$, which is what a linear decoder computes. In reference coordinates the natural parameter is $U_i^\top w_v$, so the implied template bank depends on the receiver frame (consistent with the paper's statement that the decoder is outside the covariance theorem).
**Fix:** Replace the last sentence of 857 with: "The linear decoder of Eq.~\eqref{eq:predictive_linear_decoder} is itself the unit-temperature Gibbs readout for the shared-covariance templates $\pi_v=\mathcal N(\Sigma_*w_v,\Sigma_*)$, with $w_v$ the $v$th row of $W_{\mathrm{out}}$ and class prior $\log\omega_v=b_{\mathrm{out},v}+\kappa_{\mathrm{uni}}\log p_{\mathrm{uni}}(v)+\tfrac12w_v^\top\Sigma_*w_v$ up to a constant, for any $\Sigma_*\succ0$; the belief covariance cancels from every logit difference. The token-bank readout differs by giving each class its own template covariance." In the Discussion, move the decoder from the list of non-variational components to this specified Gibbs readout.
**Falsifies:** a class-dependent term surviving in S4 or N8, or an implementation detail (for example a nonlinearity inside the decoder) absent from Eq. (958).
**Status:** the decoder is presented as an unrelated specified component (S); the identity is D. The prose deflates.
**Novelty vs prior review:** new.

### F6. Supplement Appendix A compresses the broader latent-label theorem with five omissions; one displayed joint repairs all of them
**Location:** Supp 68-74 against 05b 535-588 (hypotheses), 590-651 (proposition), 684-689 (temperature scaling); Main 881-898 (symbols).
**Severity:** low.
**Evidence:** (a) Product label prior: 05b Eq. (obs-attention-augmented-baseline) displays $\mathbb P^Y\prod_i\pi_i$; Supp says only "Give each source label $J_i$ a prior $\pi_i$" (prior R5; its counterexample reproduces as $(0.8529313604,0.1470686396)$ in N10). (c) Integrability: 05b 586-588 requires $\mathbb E_{Q_Y}|\log c_i|<\infty$; Supp states only "finite expected costs," so "up to a row-independent term" can be an undefined infinite constant. (e) Normalization: both Supp 68 and 05b 557 and 570-571 leave record normalization as declared data although 07j 245-267 constructs a normalized Bernoulli witness (F1). Everything else agrees: recognition $Q_Z\prod\beta_i$ versus 05b Eq. (obs-attention-recognition-factorization); pointwise versus recognition rows; and Supp 74 matches 05b 684-689 on temperature scaling. ## Relation to the no-neural-architecture program

Within this lens the pair undersells how much of its own architecture already has an exact probabilistic reading. Of the four components the paper lists as prescribed rather than derived (residual, normalization, MLP, decoder), two acquire exact readings without new parameters: the unit residual is the persistence term of a normalized predictive kernel (F3), and the linear decoder is a Gibbs readout of a shared-covariance Gaussian template family (F5). The routing is an exact label posterior at the temperature actually used (F1), the covariance handoff has a principled moment-matched replacement (F3), and a parameter-free content correction is available from the paper's own shared-latent model (F7). What remains prescribed is the normalization and MLP maps, the choice of content maps, and the point collapse between layers; those are the correct targets for the next derivation, and the paper can say so in one sentence instead of several disclaimers. The results of F1 and F3 do not depend on the sources being tokens: if some sources are persistent latent template agents (the MLP-replacement route proposed by the coordinator), routing to them is again an exact label posterior and their contribution again a predictive mean. That route's approximation properties are outside this lens and were not checked.
