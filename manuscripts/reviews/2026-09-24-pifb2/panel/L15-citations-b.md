## Findings (ranked)

### L15-1: "It from bit has remained a slogan" is false against the physics literature; the informational reconstructions of quantum theory and Fuchs's participatory realism are neither cited nor engaged
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:81 ("Where Wheeler's "it from bit" has remained a slogan ... the framework supplies the formal objects those programs lack"); :102 ("Wheeler's participatory universe ... have remained informal philosophy"); :120 (scope: "interpretive moves that have circulated as informal philosophy"); :1767 (the QBism contrast).
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** Since 1999 a line of physics papers has tried to formalize Wheeler's program directly, as quantum theory derived from rules about binary questions and information. Zeilinger (1999, Found. Phys. 29, 631) takes as his foundational principle that an elementary system carries one bit. Brukner and Zeilinger followed with a Fisher- and Shannon-information analysis (verify). Hardy (2001, quant-ph/0101012), Chiribella, D'Ariano and Perinotti (2011, PRA 84, 012311), and Masanes and Müller (2011, NJP 13, 063001) gave full reconstructions from informational axioms (all verify). Höhn (2017, Quantum 1, 38, verified) builds qubit quantum theory from "an observer O interrogating a system S with binary questions", which is Wheeler's yes-or-no picture carried out in full. Not one of these works is in references.bib; the script found no author match for Zeilinger, Hardy, Chiribella, Masanes, or a Höhn-first entry. Fuchs (2017) is in the bib and is cited at 1767, but only as a second QBism reference. Its abstract claims Wheeler's program for QBism by name: these views "have lately been termed 'participatory realism'", and the paper "highlights the influence of John Wheeler's 'law without law' on QBism's formulation" (arXiv:1601.04360v3, abstract). A foundations referee will read the paper's central novelty claim, that it gives formal content to a slogan, as ignoring the one research program that already claims to do so.
**Fix:** Delete "has remained a slogan" and "have remained informal philosophy". Replace them with a positioning sentence along these lines: "Wheeler's proposal has been formalized in quantum foundations as reconstructions of quantum theory from informational axioms [Zeilinger1999, Hardy2001, Chiribella2011, Masanes2011, Hoehn2017] and as QBism's participatory realism [Fuchs2017]. These programs recover quantum structure from an agent's questions but have no multi-agent transport law and no geometry pulled back to a base manifold. The present construction supplies those, at the price of a classical fiber." At 1767, give Fuchs 2017 one sentence of its own saying that QBism claims Wheeler's participatory mantle and naming exactly where PIFB2 departs from it (consensus as a stationary point, and a transport law between agents).
**Falsifies:** A reading of these reconstructions on which they do not formalize any part of it-from-bit. That reading is untenable for Höhn 2017, whose stated method is binary interrogation.
**Status:** The novelty claim is S (a positioning statement). As written it overstates priority.

### L15-2: The causal-cone route to Lorentzian signature has no citations, yet its main statement is a classical theorem (Hawking-King-McCarthy 1976; Malament 1977)
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1977-1981 (`sec:causal_cone_route`, register entry P5); the whole of `sec:signature_resolution` (1894-2014) contains no `\cite`.
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** The script found no citation keys anywhere in lines 1894-2014. At 1979 the text says the null cone "fixes $g$ only up to a conformal factor and does not select the dimension count". That is the content of the Hawking, King and McCarthy theorem (J. Math. Phys. 17, 174, 1976) and of Malament's theorem (J. Math. Phys. 18, 1399, 1977): for distinguishing spacetimes, the causal order fixes the conformal class of the metric (both verify). The "conformal scale" left open at 1980 is the missing volume element that causal set theory supplies by counting, "order plus number equals geometry" (Bombelli, Lee, Meyer and Sorkin, PRL 59, 521, 1987, verify). Section 1894 also calls dynamical selection of the signature open without citing earlier attempts at it, for example Greensite (Phys. Lett. B 300, 34, 1993, verified), who treats the signature as a dynamical phase and finds a Lorentzian stationary point singled out in D = 4. The "finite maximal epistemic propagation speed" postulate has a standard physics precedent: an emergent light cone from local dynamics, the Lieb-Robinson bound (Commun. Math. Phys. 28, 251, 1972, verify). Neither HKM, Malament, Sorkin, Greensite, nor Lieb-Robinson is in references.bib.
**Fix:** At 1979, after "conformal factor", add: "as it must, since by the Hawking-King-McCarthy and Malament theorems the causal order fixes a spacetime metric only up to its conformal class [HKM1976, Malament1977]; causal set theory supplies the missing scale by counting elements [Bombelli1987]." At 1980, cite Lieb-Robinson as the precedent for a finite speed emerging from local first-order dynamics. This also bears on the stated tension with parabolic natural-gradient flow, because Lieb-Robinson cones arise without any hyperbolic equation. At 1894, cite Greensite 1993 as prior work on dynamical signature selection.
**Falsifies:** A reading of 1979 in which the conformal ambiguity is meant as a new observation specific to the framework. Even under that reading the classical theorem must be cited.
**Status:** The causal route is a C/S construction under postulate P5. The prose does not inflate it, but it gives no literature for the one part that is P.

### L15-3: The time section places its clock against the wrong neighbors: Caticha's entropic time and Wootters's statistical distance are missing, thermal time is mischaracterized, and Cover and Thomas does not support the distinguishability unit
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1830 (the unit claim citing CoverThomas2006 and AmariNagaoka2000); :1835 (the list of distinguished proposals); :1840 (`eq:clock_dissipation`).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** (a) At 1835 the clock is said to differ from proposals that use "an entangled clock subsystem, an external modular flow, or a horizon thermodynamic state". The Connes-Rovelli thermal time is not external: it is the Tomita-Takesaki modular flow of the system's own statistical state (Class. Quantum Grav. 11, 2899, 1994; wiki source note `connes-rovelli-1994-thermal-time.md`). That state-determined flow is the closest conceptual relative of a clock read off the agent's belief state, so "external" inverts the relationship. (b) The literal precedent, time as bookkeeping of information updates carrying an arrow, is Caticha's entropic time (Caticha, "Entropic dynamics, time and quantum theory", J. Phys. A 44, 225303, 2011, verified: "time is introduced as a device to keep track of change"). PIFB2 cites Caticha 2019 and Johnson-Caticha 2011 elsewhere but not this paper. (c) At 1830, "distinguishable from a single observation when the Fisher-Rao length $\gtrsim 1$" is cited to Cover and Thomas and to Amari and Nagaoka. The operational source for "distance equals number of distinguishable steps" is Wootters (Phys. Rev. D 23, 357, 1981; wiki note `wootters-1981-statistical-distance.md`). Wootters was Wheeler's doctoral student, so this is also the most Wheelerian citation available for the passage. Cover and Thomas give Cramér-Rao and Pinsker bounds, not this unit. Also, $g(\delta\theta,\delta\theta)$ is the squared length, not the length. (d) The identity $(d\tau/dt)^2 = \eta(-d\mathcal{F}/dt)$ is correct (script `L15_clock_identity.py`: residual exactly 0; discrete ratio $\Delta I/((\eta/2)|\Delta\mathcal{F}|) - 1 = 9.9\times10^{-3}, 9.8\times10^{-4}, 9.8\times10^{-5}$ at $\eta = 10^{-2}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-4}$, so the correction is O($\eta$) as the text says). A statistical physicist will recognize it at once as a relative of thermodynamic length and dissipation (Salamon and Berry, PRL 51, 1127, 1983; Crooks, PRL 99, 100602, 2007; both verify) and of the Fisher-speed and entropy-production relations of Ito (PRL 121, 030605, 2018, verified). None of Caticha 2011, Wootters 1981, Salamon-Berry, Crooks, or Ito is in references.bib.
**Fix:** At 1835, replace "an external modular flow" with "the modular flow of a global statistical state [ConnesRovelli1994]". Add Caticha 2011 as the closest precedent and say what differs: the multi-agent, frame-relative clock and the meta-clock slowdown. At 1830, cite Wootters 1981 in place of CoverThomas2006 and write "squared Fisher-Rao length". After `eq:clock_dissipation`, add one sentence: "The identity is the natural-gradient analog of the thermodynamic-length relations between Fisher speed and dissipation [SalamonBerry1983, Crooks2007, Ito2018]." Optionally, add Höhn, Smith and Lock ("Trinity of relational quantum dynamics", PRD 104, 066001, 2021, verify), which ties Page-Wootters to quantum reference frames, next to PageWootters1983.
**Falsifies:** A reading of 1835 in which "external" means external to the agent. The thermal-time flow is still internal to the system's state, so the contrast would have to be restated in any case.
**Status:** The clock identity is D and correct. The positioning is S, with one mischaracterization.

### L15-4: The it-from-bit pullback (1872) and the physical readings in Part II are positioned without the direct prior art on geometry from Fisher information, emergent gravity, and objectivity by consensus
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1872 ("here that proposal is given a precise fixed-gauge realization as the ordinary pullback of the Gaussian Fisher-Rao metric"); :1893; :2072 (gravity; no citations); :2078 (macroscopic objects enforce consensus); :2129.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Caticha proposed modeling physical space as a statistical manifold whose metric is its information geometry ("The information geometry of space and time", AIP Conf. Proc. 803, 355, 2006, gr-qc/0508108, verified). He later swept that information geometry into a four-dimensional spacetime that reproduces the vacuum Einstein equations (arXiv:1909.09657, 2019, verified; the abstract says the geometry of any embedded spacelike surface is given by its information geometry). That structure, positive-definite spatial information geometry plus evolution, is the same one PIFB2 adopts in postulate P5. Matsueda (arXiv:1310.1831, 2013, verified) builds an Einstein tensor from a Fisher metric. Erdmenger, Grosvenor and Jefferson (SciPost Phys. 8, 073, 2020, verified) show that an AdS metric "is a relatively general feature" of Fisher geometries with symmetries. A referee will use that result as a caution: the Gaussian Fisher metric of 1889 is hyperbolic for generic reasons, so geometry emerging from Fisher information is not by itself evidence of physics. The gravity subsection (2072-2076) has no citations, and references.bib already holds an uncited `Carlip2014` ("Challenges for emergent gravity", Stud. Hist. Phil. Mod. Phys. 46, 200). The sentence at 2078, "macroscopic objects enforce consensus through precision dominance", with "all observers" anchored, is the classical analog of Zurek's quantum Darwinism, which explains objectivity as redundant records accessible to many observers (Zurek, Nature Phys. 5, 181, 2009, verify; wiki note `zurek-2009-quantum-darwinism.md`). Only Zurek 2003 is cited, at 2084. Müller ("Law without law: from observer states to physics via algorithmic information theory", Quantum 4, 301, 2020, verified) derives objective reality "as an asymptotic statistical phenomenon" from a first-person postulate. It is titled after Wheeler and is the closest competitor to PIFB2's objectivity as within-species consensus (2042, 2129). None of these except Carlip2014 is in references.bib.
**Fix:** At 1872, add: "Information-geometric models of physical space and spacetime were proposed by Caticha [Caticha2006, Caticha2019st], and Fisher-metric emergent-gravity constructions exist [Matsueda2013]. The present contribution is the agent-indexed family of pullbacks and their gauge dependence, not the idea of Fisher geometry as spatial geometry." Near 1889, add a clause citing Erdmenger et al. 2020 on the genericity of hyperbolic Fisher geometry. At 2072, cite Carlip2014 for the standard obstructions an emergent-gravity reading must meet. At 2078, cite Zurek 2009 as the quantum precedent for objectivity by redundancy. At 2042 or 2129, cite Müller 2020 as the closest observer-first account of emergent objectivity.
**Falsifies:** Showing that the Caticha constructions do not pull back along agent sections. That is true, and it narrows the overlap without removing the duty to cite.
**Status:** These readings are Level-3 S/C. "Precise realization" at 1872 is accurate only for the mathematics (the pullback is D). Its novelty as an it-from-bit mechanism is unpositioned.

### L15-6: The list of "empirical consequences" of gauge invariance at 2113 is physically wrong, and the cited survey does not support it
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:2113 ("gauge invariance proper ... with specific empirical consequences such as Higgs mass generation, QCD confinement, and the chirality of the weak interaction").
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The chirality of the weak interaction is an empirical input, the choice of a chiral representation of SU(2)$_L\times$U(1)$_Y$, and not a consequence of gauge invariance. Confinement is not a proven consequence of gauge invariance; it is an open problem (Wilson's criterion and the Clay problem). "Higgs mass generation" is conventionally described as spontaneous breaking of a global remnant, since Elitzur's theorem forbids spontaneous breaking of a local gauge symmetry. Norton 1993, the survey this sentence leans on, is about general covariance in general relativity and makes none of these claims. A physics referee will stop at this sentence.
**Fix:** Replace the tail with: "and between coordinate invariance and gauge invariance proper, the latter constraining the form of the action (for example, forbidding explicit gauge-boson mass terms and fixing the universality of gauge couplings)."
**Falsifies:** A source deriving weak-interaction chirality from gauge invariance alone. I know of none.
**Status:** Stated as P-level fact; incorrect as written.

### L15-8: Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics is described as relativizing states "to observers"; its defining move is that states are relative to any physical system
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1767.
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** The Rovelli 1996 abstract (quant-ph/9609002) says the theory describes "only the information that systems have about each other", and the paper denies any observer-observed distinction. **Fix:** "Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics [Rovelli1996] holds that quantum states, and the values of variables, are relative to other physical systems, any system serving as an observer, and describes only the information systems have about each other."
**Falsifies:** Not applicable; the abstract quotation settles it.
**Status:** S.
