**Evidence:** The same-time shadow is a registered framing item (ledger: "same-time shadows are forbidden by the Theory's typing prohibition"); this finding adds what the Theory has built since then and what it concludes. PIFB2 says "this bidirectional coupling realizes Wheeler's participatory loop" (1677) and "This realizes the formal feedback structure of Wheeler's self-excited circuit" (1727). The Theory now locates participation at three loci, each a sequence of fixed normalized joints (`c12` sec:obs-participatory): block coordinate ascent (`rem:full-graph-inference-loop`, tagged STANDARD: it happens "in every connected hierarchical model"), learned top priors (`prop:full-graph-aggregate-prior`), and delayed feedback (`def:full-graph-episode-joint`, `prop:full-graph-episode-joint`, ESTABLISHED). Its verdict (`c14` 149-160): these loci resemble Wheeler's proposal "without being derived from it or evidence for it", and "None of these separates the program from a hierarchical model with learned priors; formation could." The dynamic reading PIFB2 gives at 1673 ("read dynamically ... its belief and its generative model become the prior") now has an exact counterpart: in the episode-indexed joint the children's prior at episode n+1 is the filtered parent law pushed through a declared transition, `eq:full-graph-episode-predictive`. PIFB2's parent-predictive prior of `lem:shadow_mf_optimum` is that formula with a rigid-link transition and with the variational parent standing in for the filtered generative posterior.
**Fix:** In 1664-1677 replace the same-time assignment by the delayed construction: "When a parent forms at episode n, its inferred law parameterizes the children's conditional at episode n+1; the children's prior is the filtered parent law pushed through that transition (Theory, prop:full-graph-episode-joint). Same-time assignment of a posterior to a prior is not a factor of one normalized joint." Replace "realizes Wheeler's participatory loop" by "has the feedback structure Wheeler's participatory loop requires; that structure alone does not distinguish the program from hierarchical modeling with learned priors (Theory, Chapter 14), and formation by the inference would." Cite `conj:phil-contrast-formation` as the one named observation that would count against the participatory reading. This also improves fence F5 (line 114), which currently says the participatory reading "has no identified falsifying observation".
**Falsifies:** A proof that same-time assignment `p_i = Omega_{i,I}[q_I]` is a factor of one normalized joint in which no kernel reads a recognition law, or a demonstration that coordinate ascent in a generic hierarchical model lacks the bidirectional dependence PIFB2 attributes to its tower.
**Status:** S presented with "realizes" (inflation). The delayed-feedback reading is D in the Theory (ESTABLISHED there).

**Severity:** high

The strict barycenter with dispersion is the moment match of the mixture (G `prop:cg-gaussian-forward-kl-barycenter`); `L18_barycenter_parent.py` (a) reproduces it by numerical minimization (mean and covariance residuals 6.6e-9 and 6.2e-8; objective 0.5331162083 equals the G score to 2.2e-16) and shows the no-dispersion form has a strictly larger objective (gap 8.85e-2). **Fix:** At 1577 write: "Gauge covariance of the barycenter map (Theorem rg_covariance) does not make it an exact coarse-graining. The exact parent is the pushforward of the fine law under a declared coarse channel (Theory, cor. thm:rg-pointwise-parent-datum); the Gaussian barycenter with its dispersion term is the moment-matched projection of the random-selection mixture (Theory Supplement G, prop:cg-gaussian-forward-kl-barycenter), and the common-variance form used in the implementation is the image of no parameter-independent Markov kernel (prop:hist-normal-barycenter-kernel)." At 1593-1600 state that the left side of `eq:meta_agent_FE_criterion`, evaluated on exact parents, is lower by the discarded conditional information for every cluster, so the criterion needs a post-merge functional that is not monotone under refinement, which neither document supplies. Keep the M-step reading at 1602; it is `prop:full-graph-aggregate-prior` restricted to a Gaussian family.
**Falsifies:** A parameter-independent Markov kernel realizing `eq:meta_agent_sigma_impl` for all constituent covariances, or a post-merge functional for which (1595) is not satisfied by every sufficiently coarse partition.
**Status:** Barycenter existence and uniqueness is P (agrees with G). "Correct coarse-graining target" is S presented as a consequence of P.

## Physics-audience and standalone notes

At a single base point the Theory shows the only active symmetry is one global copy of G and per-agent frame changes are passive bookkeeping (`c02` physicist's summary). "Free energy" in a stat-mech sense is preserved by exact coarse-graining (`Z_{s+1} = Z_s`, PIFB2 `thm:rg_pushforward`).
