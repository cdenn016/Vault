### L04-1: "Explicit symmetry breaking via observations" names a redundancy that the observation term does not break
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:922-927 (`sec:symmetry_breaking`); also 875 ("The observation term ... breaks symmetry, preventing agents from drifting into self-consistent but false consensus") and 903 ("this gauge-fixing is the implicit content of the explicit symmetry breaking").
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** Line 925 states: "Without observation terms, the functional is invariant under the simultaneous group-level transformation $U_i\mapsto U_i g$ for all agents ... so configurations related by that action are energetically degenerate." The manuscript itself classifies this action at 341 as "the global diagonal redundancy ... the redundancy of the overall labeling of the gauge orbit": it leaves every $\Omega_{ij}=U_iU_j^{-1}$, every $q_i$, and every shadow prior invariant. The observation term $-\mathbb{E}_{q_i}[\log p(o\mid k_i)]$ contains no $U$, so it is invariant too. `L04_presence_env_symmetry.py` part C builds $\mathcal F$ = self KL against cross-scale shadow priors + reduced attention rows + a fixed Gaussian observation term and finds, for the right action $U\mapsto Ug$ *with* the observation term, $|\Delta\mathcal F| = 3.6\times10^{-14}$. The symmetry that a fixed observation model does break is a different one: the diagonal left action $U_i\mapsto hU_i$, $q_i\mapsto h_\# q_i$ (Action (b) of 3641 at constant $h$), which moves the physical consensus belief. The same script gives, for that action, $|\Delta\mathcal F| = 3.2\times10^{-14}$ without the observation term and $8.62$ with it. A physics referee reads "explicit symmetry breaking" as adding a non-invariant term to a Hamiltonian with a genuine global symmetry (distinct physical states related by the action). A redundancy cannot be broken, and "energetically degenerate" configurations related by it are the same physical state. The "false consensus" drift of line 875 is the left-action (collective belief rotation) degeneracy, not the right-action redundancy. Line 903 conflates gauge fixing (choice of orbit representative, no physical change) with explicit breaking (physical change). The environmental agents of 927 break the right action only if their frames $U_{e_k}$ are exempted from the transformation, which promotes them to fixed background fields; that should be said.
**Fix:** Rewrite 925-927 around the diagonal left action: "Without observation terms and with priors transformed together with beliefs, the functional is invariant under the global action $U_i\mapsto hU_i$, $q_i\mapsto h_\# q_i$, $p_i\mapsto h_\# p_i$ for a single $h\in G$, which rotates the collective belief configuration rigidly. A fixed observation model $p(o\mid k)$, or an environmental agent whose frame and belief are held fixed, is not transformed and therefore breaks this global symmetry explicitly, in the sense of an external field. The right translation $U_i\mapsto U_ig$ is a labeling redundancy and is not broken by any term of the functional." Delete "this gauge-fixing is the implicit content of the explicit symmetry breaking" at 903 and say instead that $\Omega_{i,e_k}=I$ declares the sensor frame to coincide with the receiving frame. At 875 replace "breaks symmetry" by "breaks the global belief-rotation symmetry explicitly".
**Falsifies:** A version of the observation term that depends on $U_i$ (not present in eq. 669/689 or in the proposition at 887-895), or a definition under which $U_i\mapsto U_ig$ also transforms $q_i$; either would make the right action physical and the current text correct.
**Status:** The invariance statements are P (checked numerically here). The claim that observations break the $U_i\mapsto U_ig$ invariance is false as written; the corrected statement is D.

### L04-2: The temperature statement at 678 is correct at row level, but it omits the value-level scaling and leans on an uncited "companion"
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:678 (status paragraph before `eq:free_energy_functional_final`); 587-592.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** C1 (row N5) flags "the PIFB2 temperature defect (678)". I checked the text. The row claim is correct: with a Gaussian link $y_i\mid y_j,J=j\sim\mathcal N(\Omega_jy_j,L_j)$ and mean-field Gaussians, matching the coordinate update to $-\mathrm{KL}(q_i\|\Omega_jq_j)/\tau$ in the sender-mean quadratic forces $L_j=\tau A_j$ with $A_j=\Omega_j\Sigma_j\Omega_j^\top$, and the residual source-dependent logit is then $-\tfrac12(1-1/\tau)\log|A_j|$. `L04_link_row.py`: after subtracting that predicted logit the residual is source-independent to spread $1.1\times10^{-12}$ ($\tau=1$), $2.6\times10^{-14}$ ($\tau=0.7$), $2.1\times10^{-14}$ ($\tau=2.3$). The log-determinant logit and the forcing of $\tau=1$ are confirmed, so the defect C1 names is a property of the construction that 678 reports correctly, not an error in the text. Three problems remain. (i) The text treats only the row's coordinate update. At value level the displayed row block $\sum_j\beta_{ij}E_{ij}+\tau\mathrm{KL}(\beta_i\|\tilde\pi_i)$ is $\tau$ times the dimensionless evidence-bound row $\mathrm{KL}(\beta_i\|\pi_i)+\tau^{-1}\sum_j\beta_{ij}E_{ij}$ (Theory `c06_attention.tex`, `eq:obs-attention-vfe`; `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex`, `prop:full-graph-gibbs-rows`: "The energy-unit form with $\tau\ne1$ is not an independently weighted sector of one evidence bound"). Because the self term and the observation term carry unit weight, no global rescaling of eq. 689 makes all three weights consistent with one bound unless $\tau=1$. This obstruction is separate from the log-determinant logit, and it is the one that changes the belief gradient. (ii) "Exact coordinate update of a fixed normalized joint provided the link covariance is set to the transported sender covariance" ties a generative parameter to the recognition covariance $\Sigma_j$. The joint is fixed only if $\Sigma_j$ is frozen at its current value for that step, which is what keeps the statement consistent with `thm:state_level_elbo_nogo` (3282). The text says "declared postulate" but not "frozen per step". (iii) "the companion exact-ELBO treatment" and "That treatment has since settled the attention row" carry no citation. A standalone reader cannot locate the result, and "settled" asserts a result the paper does not contain.
**Fix:** Replace the second half of 678 with: "On a sample space enlarged by a source label with prior $\pi_i$ and a record likelihood proportional to $\exp(-D_{ij}/\tau)$, the optimal constant recognition row is the softmax below, and the row contributes $\mathrm{KL}(\beta_i\|\pi_i)+\tau^{-1}\sum_j\beta_{ij}\mathbb{E}D_{ij}$ to the negative evidence lower bound [cite Theory, prop:obs-attention-elbo]. The row block of Eq. (689) is $\tau$ times this quantity, so for $\tau\ne1$ it is not a sector of one evidence bound alongside the unit-weight self and observation terms. For a Gaussian link, reducing the coordinate update to a divergence score further requires the link covariance to equal $\tau$ times the transported sender covariance, frozen at its current value, and at $\tau\ne1$ leaves an extra logit $-\tfrac12(1-1/\tau)\log|\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_j\Omega_{ij}^\top|$." At 587 add: "The tempered objective is not the relative entropy of the mixture model above unless $\tau=1$."
**Falsifies:** A normalized joint with unit-weight self and observation terms whose recognition row block equals $\sum\beta E+\tau\mathrm{KL}(\beta\|\pi)$ for $\tau\ne1$.
**Status:** Row-level claims D and verified. The prose does not inflate the functional (it says ansatz), but "settled" attributes an uncited result.

At 716 write "The boxed equation is the complete consensus functional ...". At 544 write "We restrict the variational family to $Q(k,z)=q_i(k)\beta(z)$ and minimize its relative entropy to $P(k,z)$"; drop "data" at 549. At 541 say "attention is the recognition-optimal source row, not the posterior source row".
**Falsifies:** A normalized model with an observed variable in the mixture construction, which the text does not supply.
**Status:** Functional: ansatz (correctly tagged at 678). The wording at 716, 544, and 541 inflates it to probabilistic status.

### L04-4: The environmental-agent proposition is stated for "any system with observation terms" but holds only for Gaussian likelihoods with full-rank linear readout
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:887-895 (Proposition, mean-gradient equivalence) and 897-911 (Construction).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The proposition opens "For any system with observation terms ... there exists a system with environmental agents ... whose mean-gradient flow agrees ... for any environmental sensory covariance $\Sigma_o>0$." The construction assumes "a Gaussian sensor with identity readout, $m_k=o_k$". For that case the checks pass (`L04_presence_env_symmetry.py` part B: mean-gradient mismatch $4.4\times10^{-10}$; covariance gradient of the KL equals $\tfrac12(\Lambda_o-\Sigma_i^{-1})$ to $2.5\times10^{-10}$; the negative log-likelihood gives $\tfrac12\Lambda_o$ to $3.6\times10^{-10}$; cross-entropy equals KL plus entropy to $9\times10^{-16}$). Outside it the claim fails. For a nonlinear readout $o=\tanh(2k)+\epsilon$ the mean gradient of $\mathbb{E}_q[-\log p(o\mid k)]$ over a grid of $\mu$ deviates from its best affine fit by $13.8$, while every Gaussian environmental agent produces an affine mean gradient $\Lambda_o(\mu-m)$, so no choice of $(m_k,\Sigma_o)$ matches it. For a linear readout $o=Hk+\epsilon$ with $\operatorname{rank}H<K_q$, the induced precision $H^\top R^{-1}H$ is singular and $q_{e_k}$ is not a normalized density, so the KL is undefined. "For any $\Sigma_o>0$" also reads as if $\Sigma_o$ were free; the equivalence needs $\Sigma_o$ equal to the sensor covariance of $\mathcal F_{\text{obs}}$.
**Fix:** Restate: "For observation terms with Gaussian likelihood $o_k=Hk+\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,R)$, and $H^\top R^{-1}H\succ0$, set $\Lambda_o=H^\top R^{-1}H$ and $m_k=\Lambda_o^{-1}H^\top R^{-1}o_k$. Then ... agree on the mean gradient; the covariance gradients differ by $-\tfrac12\Sigma_i^{-1}$; replacing the KL by the cross-entropy restores exact equality. For non-Gaussian or nonlinear likelihoods no Gaussian environmental agent reproduces the mean gradient." Title it "Proposition (Gaussian-sensor equivalence)".
**Falsifies:** A Gaussian $q_{e_k}$ reproducing the nonlinear-readout mean gradient above, which cannot exist because the KL mean gradient is affine in $\mu_i$.
**Status:** D under the Gaussian identity-readout assumption; the universal quantifier inflates it.

### L04-5: The absorbed-presence bookkeeping removes the receiver's own presence from its alignment row
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:687-688 and 694 (definition of $\tilde\pi_{ij}$); 709 ("reduces to the unweighted softmax on the interior"); 711 ("for hard masks $\chi_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$ the two bookkeepings coincide"); 852-854.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** $\tilde\pi_{ij}=\chi_i\chi_j\pi_{ij}/\sum_k\chi_i\chi_k\pi_{ik}$: the receiver presence $\chi_i(c)$ cancels between numerator and denominator (and the ratio is $0/0$ where $\chi_i=0$). The alignment integrand at 687 has no other $\chi_i$ factor, while the self term at 685 does. **Falsifies:** A convention stated elsewhere restricting the alignment integral to the support of $\chi_i$; I found none in 516-928.
**Status:** Bookkeeping error in a displayed definition (that $\chi_i$ cancels is P). It does not affect $\beta^*$ or the envelope identity.

### L04-6: A temperature learned by descending the functional collapses to hard attention
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:673 ("$\tau=\kappa\sqrt{K_q}$, with $\kappa$ a learnable scalar ... lets the network adapt the effective sharpness during training"); 2222.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** From $\mathcal F_{i,\mathrm{red}}=-\tau\log Z_i$, $\partial\mathcal F_{i,\mathrm{red}}/\partial\tau=-\log Z_i-\langle E\rangle_{\beta^*}/\tau=\mathrm{KL}(\beta^*_i\|\tilde\pi_i)\ge0$ (SymPy identity in `L04_gibbs_envelope.py`; finite differences agree with $\mathrm{KL}(\beta^*\|\pi)$ to $2\times10^{-10}$ on three random rows). This is the thermodynamic $\partial F/\partial T=-S$ with $S=-\mathrm{KL}(\beta\|\pi)\le0$. The functional is nondecreasing in $\tau$, strictly unless $\beta^*=\pi$; descending it in $\kappa$ drives $\tau\to0^+$, where $-\tau\log Z_i\to\min_jE_{ij}$ on the support (arg-min attention). The dimensionless evidence-bound row of L04-2 has the opposite pathology, $\partial(-\log Z_i)/\partial\tau=-\langle E\rangle/\tau^2\le0$, driving $\tau\to\infty$. The text does not say which objective trains $\kappa$.
**Fix:** Add after 673: "The temperature is not a variational parameter of $\mathcal F$: since $\partial(-\tau\log Z_i)/\partial\tau=\mathrm{KL}(\beta_i^*\|\tilde\pi_i)\ge0$, minimizing $\mathcal F$ over $\tau$ collapses attention to the arg-min. $\kappa$ is either fixed or trained by the external prediction loss of Section X, and that loss, not $\mathcal F$, is the objective for $\kappa$."
**Falsifies:** Learning $\kappa$ by minimizing eq. 689 without collapse; that would contradict the sign identity, which holds for every $\tau>0$.
**Status:** P (identity). The sentence at 673 is silent rather than false; the gap matters because a reader will assume "learnable" means "learned by the same functional".

### L04-7: The adaptive precision is called a MAP estimate, but the penalty is not the negative log-posterior of a normalized model
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:771 ("The stationary point ... is therefore the fixed-hyperparameter MAP precision"); 758-763; 795.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The Gamma density at 758 is normalized and the hyperparameter derivatives at 766-768 are correct. But $\alpha_iD_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_i\|p_i)$ is not $-\log$ of a likelihood in $\alpha_i$ (there is no normalizer), so $\alpha D+R(\alpha)$ is not a log-posterior. For the normalized hierarchical model $k\sim\mathcal N(\mu_p,\Sigma_p/\alpha)$ with the same Gamma prior, stationarity of $\mathbb{E}_q[-\log p(k\mid\alpha)]-\log p(\alpha)$ gives $\alpha=(c_0+K/2)/(b_0+Q)$ with $Q=\tfrac12\mathbb{E}_q[(k-\mu_p)^\top\Sigma_p^{-1}(k-\mu_p)]$ (`L04_alpha_precision.py`, SymPy). Numerically ($K=3$, $b_0=1$, $c_0=2$): the PIFB2 $\alpha^*=0.933$, the hierarchical MAP $1.646$. Line 795 concedes that "the correspondence is structural rather than exact conjugacy", which contradicts 771. What is exact, and useful to a physics reader: the reduced adaptive term is $\min_\alpha[\alpha D+b_0\alpha-c_0\log\alpha]=c_0+c_0\log((b_0+D)/c_0)$ (SymPy residual 0), a logarithmic penalty concave in $D$, which is why anchoring weakens for drifted beliefs.
**Fix:** Replace the sentence at 771 with: "The stationary point is the minimizer of the declared penalty $\alpha D+R(\alpha)$ at fixed hyperparameters. It is not the MAP precision of a normalized hierarchical Gaussian model, whose stationary point is $(c_0+K/2)/(b_0+Q)$. At the optimum the adaptive self term reduces to $c_0\log(b_0+D_{\mathrm{KL}})$ up to a constant."
**Falsifies:** A normalized joint over $(k,\alpha)$ whose $\alpha$-dependent negative log density is $\alpha D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\|p)+b_0\alpha-c_0\log\alpha$ up to $\alpha$-independent terms.
**Status:** Stationarity and envelope cancellation P (verified). "MAP" is status inflation.
