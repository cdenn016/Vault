[G4-appendix-HI-field-theory] ### G4-2: eq:beyond_mf_one_loop leaves μ, g_phys and Γ_measure undefined; with a fixed μ the remainder is O(T log T), not O(T²), and the quotient metric requires a gauge-invariant ambient metric that PIFB2's frame sector does not supply

**Location:** PIFB2.tex:3546-3554 (`eq:beyond_mf_one_loop` and the paragraph after it), 3520-3527 (`eq:beyond_mf_generating_functional`, dν_phys).

**Severity:** medium

**Evidence:** The Laplace expansion of Z_phys = ∫ e^{−A/T} dν_phys around a nondegenerate quotient saddle, with dν_phys = ρ_phys vol_{g_phys}, is

Γ[X̄] = A(X̄) + (T/2) log det(g_phys^{−1}H_phys/(2πT)) − T log ρ_phys(X̄) + O(T²).

This matches the displayed form only if μ² = 2πT_cfg and Γ_measure = −T log ρ_phys(X̄). The manuscript defines none of μ, g_phys or Γ_measure (grep finds each symbol only at 3549-3554). With μ a fixed scale the difference is −(n_phys/2) T log(2πT/μ²), where n_phys is the number of physical modes. That is an O(T log T) term, so the stated O(T_cfg²) remainder is false unless it is absorbed into Γ_measure.

Compact test (`G4_quotient_one_loop.py`, Part A). Take two K = 2 means with diagonal SO(2) redundancy and F = (|a|²−1)² + ½(|b|²−2)² + 0.8(a·b − 0.6)² + 0.6(a×b − 0.9)². Evaluate Z_phys = Z_full/vol(SO(2)) exactly by the polar change of variables on a 221³ grid. The saddle has |grad| = 4×10⁻¹⁵, a Hessian kernel exactly along the orbit generator V (|HV| = 4.5×10⁻¹⁵), and horizontal eigenvalues 3.50, 6.88, 10.97. With Γ_measure = −(T/2) log Gram, where Gram = |V|² = 2.796 is the Gram matrix of the redundancy generators in the ambient metric (the orbit-volume density), and μ² = 2πT, the residual divided by T² is −0.1525, −0.1485, −0.1465, −0.1456 and −0.1451 for T = 0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.025 and 0.0125. The remainder is therefore O(T²). With μ = 1 fixed, the residual divided by T is 3.8144 at T = 0.0125, against −(3/2)log(2πT) = 3.8162. That residual grows like T log T, as predicted. Γ_measure itself is −0.51 T here, the same order as the one-loop term, so it cannot be dropped.

Noncompact test (Part B). GL⁺(2) acts on Gaussian (μ, Σ) with the Fisher metric. The determinant of the Gram matrix of the four generators is 5.499008264463 at three random points of one orbit, so it is constant along orbits and Γ_measure is finite. The Weil quotient density of the Fisher volume (Haar normalized to Lebesgue at the identity) equals sqrt(det Gram)·sqrt(g_uu) to 10⁻¹⁰ at u = 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2.5. The one-loop quotient formula against the exact one-dimensional quotient integral gives residual/T² of 0.162 to 0.182, again O(T²). By Weil's formula the raw invariant integral equals vol_Haar(GL⁺(2)) × Z_quot = ∞, so the manuscript's "Raw Haar integration ... is not admissible" is correct (analytic, not numerically run). With the non-invariant coordinate (Lebesgue) metric, the same Gram determinant varies from 4.7×10⁻⁵ to 8.4 along one orbit, so no quotient metric or quotient density is defined without a slice. PIFB2's own frame metric (`eq:pullback_metric`, ambient Frobenius) is not invariant under the redundancy U_i → U_i g (L07-5). For GL⁺(K_q) frames, g_phys in `eq:beyond_mf_one_loop` is therefore undefined unless a right-invariant metric is adopted.

**Fix:** Replace the display and the following sentence with:
"Γ[X̄] = A_o[X̄] + (T_cfg/2) Tr log(g_phys^{−1}H_phys/(2πT_cfg)) + Γ_measure[X̄] + O(T_cfg²), with Γ_measure = −T_cfg log ρ_phys(X̄), where dν_phys = ρ_phys vol_{g_phys}. For a free action of the redundancy group by isometries of a G-invariant ambient metric g, with ν the Riemannian volume of g, the Weil quotient measure has ρ_phys = (det 𝒢_{ab})^{1/2} with 𝒢_{ab} = g(V_a, V_b) the Gram matrix of the redundancy generators, so Γ_measure = −(T_cfg/2) log det 𝒢. In a gauge-fixed calculation with slice χ = 0 the same result is (T_cfg/2) log det H_slice − T_cfg log|det FP|, where FP = ∂χ/∂θ, since det FP²/det H_slice = det 𝒢/det H_phys."
Add a hypothesis that the ambient metric and reference measure are invariant under the quotiented redundancy, and note that `eq:pullback_metric` is not.

**Falsifies:** A definition of μ with μ² = 2πT_cfg, or of Γ_measure containing the T log T term, anywhere in PIFB2. Or a redundancy that PIFB2 quotients which acts non-freely at the backgrounds used, where the formula needs orbifold corrections instead.

**Status:** D (standard Laplace expansion). The content is correct once the symbols are defined as above. As written the display is underdetermined, and its remainder claim is wrong for a T-independent μ.

[L05-variational-hierarchy] ### L05-1: The rigid-link "parent-predictive prior" double counts the constituent's evidence, and the shadow functional is not a bound for the augmented joint it is said to reduce to
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1675 (Coarse-Graining II); 3236-3257 `lem:shadow_mf_optimum`; 3259-3261 `rem:shadow_marginal_vs_mf`; 3265-3267 (Implications); 3271.
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** The marginalization identity itself is correct (L05_shadow_rigid_link: log-density residual 0, rigid-limit variance ω²S). The defect is in how it is used. Section 1675 says the shadow "emerges as the parent-predictive prior" of the augmented joint and places the framework "in continuity with" hierarchical VI. Line 3265 calls the construction "a hierarchical generative model with a specified mean-field reduction". Three executed checks contradict that reading.

(i) Double counting. I built a K = 2 instance of Definition `def:augmented_joint`: one parent, two children, GL⁺(2) transports, and Gaussian observations under each child. I compared the exact child posterior with the scheme the text uses, in which the child posterior is the shadow of the exact parent posterior times the child's own likelihood. At σ² = 0.3 the shadow scheme's mean error is 1.29e-1 and its covariance error is 3.1e-2. In the rigid limit σ² = 1e-8, the shadow scheme's precision exceeds the exact child precision by exactly R⁻¹ = diag(2.5, 1.667), which is the precision of the child's own observation. That observation enters the parent posterior and then enters the child a second time. The belief-propagation cavity message (the parent posterior with the child's own message removed) reproduces the exact child posterior to 1.1e-16 at σ² = 0.3 and to 6e-10 at σ² = 1e-8 (L05_augmented_gaussian, parts d-f). In the rigid limit the joint forces k_i = Ω_i k_P, so the exact child posterior *is* the pushforward of the exact parent posterior. Using that pushforward as a prior and multiplying in the likelihood again is exactly the double count.

(ii) Not a bound. Take the same-time shadow functional λKL(q_P‖p_top) + Σ_i[KL(q_i‖N(μ_P, Σ_P + σ²)) − E log p(o_i|k_i)] and minimize it over Gaussians at σ² = 0.05. The minimum falls below the surprisal −log p_σ(o) of the augmented joint by 12.98 nats for o = (2, −2), by 0.14 nats for concordant data o = (1, 1), and by 30.53 nats for o = (3, −3, 0.5) (part g). A variational free energy of that joint cannot go below its surprisal. The mechanism is the Jensen inequality E_{q_i}[−log ∫χ q_P] ≤ E_{q_i q_P}[−log χ]; the gap is 14.33 nats at the sample point in L05_shadow_rigid_link part 5. Retaining the parent covariance in the prior, the property Remark `rem:shadow_marginal_vs_mf` advertises, is what opens this gap.

(iii) The paper's own obstruction applies. The child-parent term KL(q_i‖Ω_{i,I}q_I), with q_I a varying factor, has the nonzero mixed third variation of Eq. `eq:consensus_third_variation`: the finite difference is 5.22046 against the predicted Var_{q_i}(g) = 5.22044 (L05_shadow_nogo). By Theorem `thm:state_level_elbo_nogo` (3282), the shadow edge therefore has no fixed state-level ELBO representation, which is the same status the paper assigns to the peer terms. Line 3271 ("does not affect the status of the cross-scale shadow ... within a well-defined hierarchical generative model") contradicts this.

**Fix:** Restate Lemma `lem:shadow_mf_optimum` as a computation of the variational predictive density, and drop "emerges", "reduction", and "continuity with the standard scheme" for the shadow. State in Section 1675 that the shadow evaluated against the full parent posterior is not a message of the augmented joint. Then name the three ELBO-consistent alternatives:
- the conditional (mean-field or ladder) coupling E_{q_I}KL(q_i‖χ(·|k_I));
- the cavity predictive ∫χ q_I^{∖i};
- the random-effects reading of L05-3.

Suggested sentence: "Evaluated against the full parent posterior, the shadow reuses each constituent's own evidence; the ELBO-consistent downward message uses the parent posterior with that constituent's contribution removed."
**Falsifies:** Either of two results would retract this finding. The first is an exact-posterior computation of the augmented joint, at σ² > 0 and with observations under the child, in which the child posterior equals shadow times likelihood with the full parent posterior. The second is a proof that the shadow functional is at least −log p_σ(o) for all o. The executed counterexamples rule out both for the model as written.
**Status:** The marginalization identity is P. The identification of the shadow as the prior of the augmented joint is S. The prose ("emerges", "derive the cross-scale shadow", "a hierarchical generative model with a specified mean-field reduction") inflates S to D.
