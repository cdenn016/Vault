[G1-meta-agent-construction-recompute] ### G1-1: The cited radius is Kendall's bound for a different objective; for the global argmin in eq:meta_agent_frame_barycenter the uniqueness claim fails below the stated π/2
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1573 (eq:meta_agent_frame_barycenter, "exists and is unique on convex geodesic balls below the curvature-dependent radius bound of Kendall, Afsari (π/2 under the unit-curvature normalization)"); 3762 ("locally unique ... π/2 ..., which also lies below the injectivity radius"); thm:rg_covariance hypothesis at 3807.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Afsari's paper separates three statements, and they govern different objectives. (i) Karcher's local bound, p. 658, eq. (1.3): "ρ < ½ min{inj M, π/(2√Δ)}". (ii) Kendall, as reported by Afsari p. 658: "if ρ < min{inj M, π/(2√Δ)}, then there exists a unique solipsistic Karcher mean". That is uniqueness for the distance *restricted to the ball* (Groisser's "solipsistic" mean). The same page adds that with the global distance, "Kendall's result yields the bound in (1.2)". (iii) Afsari, p. 657, eq. (1.2), "ρ < r_cx ≜ ½ min{inj M, π/√Δ}", and Theorem 2.1, eq. (2.1), p. 659, with conclusion on p. 660: the Lᵖ center "is a unique point and lies inside B" (1 < p ≤ ∞; ρ_{Δ,p} = r_cx for p ≥ 2). This is the global Fréchet minimizer over all of M. Eq. meta_agent_frame_barycenter is an argmin over all of G with the global d_G, so (iii) is the statement that applies. SO(3) under the bi-invariant metric is RP³ of constant curvature. Normalized to Δ = 1, its injectivity radius is π/2, so r_cx = ½ min{π/2, π} = π/4 (π/√2 in d_G, rotation angle π/2, consistent with L06-10). The printed π/2 is Kendall's solipsistic number min{inj, π/(2√Δ)} = min{π/2, π/2}, and it is not a bound for the global objective. Counterexample (`G1_radius_detector.py` (1)): frames U_j = R_z(2πj/3), j = 0,1,2, lie in the ball about I of rotation-angle radius 2π/3, i.e., unit-curvature radius π/3 < π/2. This ball satisfies Kendall's bound and is weakly convex (Afsari p. 658). The global objective has minimum value 5.84865446 = 16π²/27, attained at three distinct points (60 random starts found exactly 3; the objective on the v-orbit of one minimizer is 5.8486544599 three times). The "below the injectivity radius" clause at 3762 is also false at unit normalization, since π/2 equals inj(RP³). At 3762, "locally unique" undersells what Afsari proves, because the conclusion that applies below r_cx is global uniqueness. That global uniqueness is also what makes the proof of thm:rg_covariance at 3811 immediate. Attribution: Afsari p. 656 credits the general-manifold construction to Grove and Karcher [10] (Math. Z. 1973), with Cartan earlier on Hadamard manifolds. "The construction is Karcher's" should therefore read Grove-Karcher, with Karcher 1977 supplying the estimates.
**Fix:** Replace the radius clause at 1573 and 3762 with: "exists and is unique, as the global minimizer over G, whenever the weighted frames lie in a geodesic ball of radius ρ < r_cx = ½ min{inj G, π/√Δ} (Afsari 2011, Thm. 2.1), with Δ an upper bound on the sectional curvature; for SO(K_q) with d_G² = ‖log(U⁻¹V)‖_F² this is ρ < π/√2, a rotation angle of π/2 (Kendall 1990 obtains the larger radius min{inj, π/(2√Δ)} only for the mean defined with the distance restricted to the ball)." Delete "which also lies below the injectivity radius" and change "locally unique" to "unique". Cite Grove-Karcher 1973 for the construction.
**Falsifies:** A reading of Kendall's Theorem 7.3 under which the global-distance Fréchet minimizer is unique on balls of radius min{inj, π/(2√Δ)}. The three-minimizer SO(3) configuration would refute that reading, so the retraction condition is that the configuration fails to be three global minimizers (a lower value found elsewhere on SO(3)).
**Status:** The uniqueness statement is P once the hypothesis is corrected to Afsari's. As printed, with the π/2 hypothesis, it is false. The prose "the sharp radius bound is Kendall's and Afsari's" merges two bounds for two objectives.

[G1-meta-agent-construction-recompute] ### G1-2: The Definition of a meta-agent omits the frame-coherence hypothesis that the frame barycenter needs, and the detector cannot supply it
**Location:** PIFB2.tex:1580-1585 (Definition, conditions (1)-(3)); 1611-1613 (Γ = P·C_q·C_s); contrast 3758 ("local frame coherence on a convex normal ball of G") and 3807 ("G is compact with locally unique Karcher means").
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Every factor of Γ is built from transported divergences, which are gauge invariant, so Γ is blind to how far apart the frames U_i are. With q_j = (U_j)_# q₀ and U_j = R_z(2πj/3), the maximum transported KL is 2.2e-16 and C_q = 1.000000, so Γ = 1 with P = C_s = 1 (`G1_radius_detector.py` (1)). The cluster passes every condition of the Definition, yet eq:meta_agent_frame_barycenter has three global minimizers (G1-1), so the Definition's "constructed via the gauge-covariant averaging (meta_agent_barycenter)-(meta_agent_frame_barycenter)" is set-valued there. The observable content is unaffected, as 1575 predicts: across the three minimizers, the frame-free parent pair (m, S) = (U_I⁻¹μ_I, U_I⁻¹Σ_I U_I⁻ᵀ) varies by 1.8e-15. The same tuple is the one used in the no-equivariant-map argument at 1575.
**Fix:** Add a fourth condition to the Definition: "(4) the constituent frames at c lie in a geodesic ball of G of radius below r_cx (Afsari 2011, Thm. 2.1), so that (meta_agent_frame_barycenter) is single-valued; this condition is not detectable by Γ, which is gauge invariant." Alternatively, following 1575, state that U_I is a declared gauge convention, drop the uniqueness requirement, and say that the parent is defined by (m, S).
**Falsifies:** A proof that Γ > Γ_min forces the frames into an r_cx-ball. The Γ = 1 cyclic configuration contradicts this.
**Status:** The Definition is definitional. The implied claim that the admitted cluster determines a unique frame barycenter is unsupported (S presented as D).

[G2-appendix-theory-crosswalk] ### G2-1: Appendix I restates in weaker form what the Theory proves; most of its theorem block should become citations, but three items must stay

**Location:** PIFB2.tex:3569-3941. Labels: `eq:rg_exact_free_energy` (3637), `thm:rg_pushforward` (3776), `thm:rg_semigroup` (3785), `thm:rg_exact_closure` (3820), `thm:rg_residual` (3882), `thm:rg_residual_explicit` (3896), `thm:rg_detector_retention` (3920). Also `thm:belief_configuration_gibbs_lift` (3578), `thm:rg_covariance` (3806), `eq:rg_reduced_hessian` (3678).

**Severity:** high. Appendix I is 6,884 words, the largest appendix, and it is cited from the body nine times as `app:rigorous_rg` (1535-1863).

**Evidence:**
1. *Pushforward.* The Theory's conditional-partition formula (c11 `def:rg-measure-pair`, `thm:rg-effective-action`) is stated for any Markov kernel. It pushes a probability reference together with the evidence measure, preserves total mass (`prop:rg-measure-pair-composition`), and composes along a tower. PIFB2 instead picks a separate parent reference ν_{s+1} and a coarea density. `G2_rg_crosswalk.py` (1) sets x∈R², ν_s=N(0,I), a quadratic-plus-linear F, T_cfg=0.7 and R(x)=mean. It confirms F_{s+1}(z) = T·H^c(z) − T·log(dν^c/dν_{s+1})(z), where H^c = −log E_ν[e^{−F/T} | Z=z] and ν^c = R_#ν_s = N(0,1/2). The residual is 8.9e-16 over five z, and Z_s = Z_{s+1} = 0.480421025322 exactly. So PIFB2's renormalized energy is the Theory's effective action plus a reference-change term. That term exists only because ν_{s+1} was chosen independently. The coarea machinery, and the sentence behind registered item (b) (ledger), both disappear when the Theory's pushed reference is used.
2. *Functoriality.* `thm:rg_semigroup` is the chain case of c09 `prop:rg-scale-functoriality` (i), where "the composition law imposes no condition". The Theory also gives the case with content: non-chain posets, with the majority witness. `G2_rg_crosswalk.py` (3) reproduces that witness: pairs then triple gives 1, triples then pair gives 0. PIFB2's following remark says a coupling-space semigroup "is not established here". The Theory establishes one for Galerkin aggregation on the Gaussian interaction family (g09 `prop:grg-aggregation-semigroup`, g07 `prop:cg-gaussian-aggregation`). Script (4) confirms that the coarse blocks stay in the family and that A_{S2}A_{S1} = A_{S1S2} (3.6e-15). Aggregation is conditioning on zero internal disagreement (g07 `prop:cg-aggregation-conditioning`), not marginalization, and Kron marginalization leaves the family (g07 `prop:cg-kron-leaves-family`).
3. *Exact closure.* The constant-block case of `thm:rg_exact_closure`, with `eq:rg_app_schur_complement`, is g07 `prop:cg-normalized-schur-action`, and the Theory version also carries the source and the constant. Script (2) confirms −log∫e^{−E}dξ = ½Λ_eff x² − h_eff x + c_eff (5.8e-15). The Y-dependent (A6) case has no Theory counterpart; L06-2 treats its content.
4. *Residual and detector.* The Theory has no schematic closure bound. It has an exact residual identity (c11 `def:rg-retained-projection`, `prop:rg-interaction-residual`), generic hyperedge generation (`prop:rg-hyperedge-generation`), and the finite-network obstruction (`prop:rg-interaction-finite-network-obstruction`). It has no detector theorem. c09 `open:cg-natural-selector` records that no cost analyzed so far selects a partition, and `thm:cg-evidence-preserving-channel` records that the coarse ELBO rise is discarded information, "neither model improvement nor evidence ascent" (L18-2 draws the consequence for the licensing criterion).
5. *Holonomy residual.* PIFB2's group-level spread H_IJ (Karcher mean of Θ^{IJ}_{ij}) has a law-level replacement in c10 `thm:cg-holonomy-kl-marginal` and `prop:cg-holonomy-compact-closed-form`. For noncompact GL links with generic loop holonomy, `cor:cg-holonomy-noncompact-gaussian` shows that no Gaussian parent is admissible. L06-3 shows H_IJ is trivial in App. I's own state space; the Theory result is what a Regime II version needs.
6. *Items with no Theory replacement.* `thm:belief_configuration_gibbs_lift` is PIFB2's own (see G2-7). `thm:rg_covariance` verifies, for the Karcher and barycenter map, the equivariance that c09 `prop:cg-equivariant-channels` takes as a hypothesis. `eq:rg_reduced_hessian` is c06 `prop:obs-attention-reduced-hessian` after multiplying by τ (script `G2_repr_crosswalk.py` (3), 2.9e-9 by finite differences); the ledger already records it as a Schur complement.

**Fix:** In the physics-journal version, cut the following to one paragraph that cites the Theory: the coarea display, Actions (a)/(b), `thm:rg_pushforward`, `thm:rg_semigroup`, the constant-block part of `thm:rg_exact_closure`, the H_IJ paragraph and the finite-size-scaling paragraph. Move `ass:closure_regime`, `thm:rg_residual`, `thm:rg_residual_explicit`, `thm:rg_detector_retention` and the Fenichel paragraph to the online supplement, or cut them. Keep, in about 900 words:

**Falsifies:** A Theory statement that fails to cover the deterministic-map, proper-reference case of `eq:rg_exact_free_energy`, which script (1) instantiates. Or a body claim of PIFB2 that uses the (A6) Y-dependent closure or the explicit constants, which would force them to stay.

## Physics-audience and standalone notes

A referee at a statistical-physics or mathematical-physics venue will reject the word "renormalization" at 81 and 1804 before reading further, because nothing is rescaled and nothing flows. The status paragraph at 1681 is the right register and should become the only place RG vocabulary is used for the construction. The "scale" $s$ is a hierarchy depth, not a length. The paper should say so where $s$ is introduced (1705), and nowhere pair it with correlation lengths or scaling dimensions. The term "mass" for the stiffness $m_I$ (3713, "Internal disagreement modes are massive") is acceptable in field-theory usage but should be tied once to "inverse susceptibility of the internal modes".

With $N\sim L^d$ the conventional exponents appear as $\beta/(d\nu)$ and $1/(d\nu)$. On a graph without a dimension, the exponents in $N$ must be declared as their own quantities, as in complete-graph (Curie-Weiss) scaling. The "susceptibility" defined as $N$ times the variance of the mean cluster size $S_N$ is unusual. In percolation the susceptibility is the mean cluster size itself. The paragraph is correctly fenced as future work but should adopt the standard definitions.
