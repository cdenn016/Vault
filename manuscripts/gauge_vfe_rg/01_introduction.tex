\chapter{Scope, Claims, and Reading Map}
\label{ch:introduction}

This document develops a gauge-covariant variational theory from typed
probability objects and then asks which parts survive coarse description.  Its
ambient geometry consists of one principal gauge bundle
\(\mathscr P_G\to\mathcal C\) with structure group \(G\), and two associated law bundles
\(\mathcal E_b=\mathscr P_G\times_{\widehat\rho_b}\mathcal B_b\) and
\(\mathcal E_m=\mathscr P_G\times_{\widehat\rho_m}\mathcal B_m\).  The representations
\(\rho_b\) and \(\rho_m\) may be inequivalent and may act on fibers of
different dimensions; the hats denote their induced actions on probability
laws.  Belief and model coordinates may use different local sections of
\(\mathscr P_G\).  Their unique relative principal-frame field is a \(G\)-valued
comparison, not a map between the two associated fibers.  The declared
cross-channel
morphisms
\[
\Phi:\mathcal E_b\longrightarrow\mathcal E_m,
\qquad
\widetilde\Phi:\mathcal E_m\longrightarrow\mathcal E_b
\]
cover \(\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal C}\); they are neither principal-bundle
morphisms nor assumed inverses.  Separate connections induce same-channel
parallel transports \(\Omega_\gamma\) on \(\mathcal E_b\) and
\(\widetilde\Omega_\gamma\) on \(\mathcal E_m\).  Parallelness of either cross
map is an additional hypothesis, measured otherwise by its covariant defect.
The two channels may use different connections on the same \(\mathscr P_G\); neither a
common local frame nor an equal connection is assumed.
Independently declared graph links are denoted
\(\Theta^b_e,\Theta^m_e\) on oriented edge copies (abbreviated by endpoints
when the copy is unique), and are identified with base parallel transport
only after a curve assignment and an equality hypothesis. These
distinctions are the type contract of \Cref{ch:geometry}. \status{DEFINITION}

The primitive descriptive data are finitely many section-bearing agents, their
agent-indexed recognition laws and evaluated generative mechanisms, and normalized
kernels for the records produced at declared interactions.  Complete population laws
enter only after an explicit construction or selection.  They encode the dependence
required by evidence and collective variational free energy, but they are not additional
agents and need not be represented by any participant.  \status{DEFINITION}

The local-first architecture is
\begin{equation}
\left\{
\mathcal A^i,G_{\theta,i,D},\mathbb Q_{i,o,X},K_a
\right\}_{i\in V,a\in\mathfrak A}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\mathbb P_{\theta,V} & \text{normalized construction},\\
\mathbb Q_{V,o,X}\in\operatorname{Cpl}(\mathbb Q_{\bullet,o,X})
& \text{declared measurable selection},
\end{cases}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal L_V.
\label{eq:intro-local-to-global-architecture}
\end{equation}
The constructed joint \(\mathbb P_{\theta,V}\) and selected coupling
\(\mathbb Q_{V,o,X}\) therefore precede the local conditional and collective objectives.
\status{DEFINITION}

Under the stated absolute-continuity and integrability hypotheses, there is one exact identity,
\begin{equation}
\log p_{\theta,V}(o\mid X)
=\mathcal L_V(\mathbb Q_{V,o,X};X,o)
+\KL\left(\mathbb Q_{V,o,X}\middle\Vert
\boldsymbol\Pi_{\theta,V,o,X}\right).
\label{eq:elbo-intro-identity}
\end{equation}
It is invariant under independent belief- and model-coordinate pushforwards.
These passive re-expressions form a product of frame-coordinate changes; they
must not be confused with replacing the ambient diagonal \(G\)-gauge symmetry
by a principal \(G\times G\)-bundle.  Equality
holds exactly when the recognition law is the posterior as a measure; matching
coordinate marginals is insufficient.  Excluding the posterior makes every
pointwise gap positive, but does not by itself make the optimized infimum
positive or attained. \status{ESTABLISHED}

The logical order is therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{typed bundles and kernels}
&\longrightarrow \text{normalized joint}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{local and collective ELBOs}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{covariant informational pullbacks}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{relational inference histories}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{general coarse maps}
\longrightarrow \text{exact effective RG}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{constructive dynamical and spatial realizations}\\
&\longrightarrow \text{Gaussian realization}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The general coarse layer distinguishes three operations that are often
conflated.  A normalized Markov kernel pushes laws forward, preserves the
observation evidence when it acts only on latent variables, and contracts
relative entropy.  Energy precomposition restricts an unnormalized energy
along a sample-space map and requires a separately declared coarse reference
measure and finite normalizer.  Parameter aggregation is a conditional
closure statement inside a selected graph-exponential family.  A
renormalization further requires comparison or rescaling maps that type
different scales; without them there is only a changing-space coarse diagram,
not a fixed point problem. \status{ESTABLISHED}

For an arbitrary finite agent hypergraph, normalized interaction-record
kernels give one collective VFE and, by disintegration, a conditional VFE for
every agent or block.  A unilateral local change equals the corresponding
change of the collective VFE, so local evolution is a blockwise collective
descent.  The local objectives do not sum symmetrically because shared factors
occur in every incident coordinate objective.  A categorical source label
places attention inside the fixed joint, and a standard-Borel observation
kernel is equivalent to a message from an operational environment node.
These results are proved in \Cref{ch:local-collective-elbo}.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

A chosen connection turns the derivative of a smooth statistical section into
a vertical first jet.  Pulling the vertical Fisher and Amari tensors back by
that jet gives gauge-invariant, connection-relative tensors on the contextual
base; the Fisher tensor may be degenerate.  This tensor pullback is distinct
both from restricting a parameter manifold to a submodel and from a
dynamical-systems pullback attractor.  The subsequent relational construction
quotients regular natural-gradient solution curves by
orientation-preserving reparameterization and measures them by Fisher length.
The resulting histories and durations are information-geometric objects on a
declared configuration space. These distinctions are developed in
\Cref{ch:pullback-geometry,ch:relational-inference}.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

The construction does not identify those histories or durations with a
physical clock.
\status{NOT-CLAIMED}

The exact law-level RG of \Cref{ch:agent-network-rg} pushes the baseline,
evidence submeasure, recognition law, posterior, and joint attention-event law
through the same structural channel. Exact closure retains induced hyperedges,
simultaneous root-framed holonomy and boundary data, and path memory.
Cross-scale agent--meta kernels follow from posterior bridges, while differential beta
functions and fixed points are typed only after an explicit rescaling returns
the changing scale spaces to one common space.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

The ordered path coordinate is neither a relational inference history nor a
physical-time coordinate, and RG depth is a scale index.
\status{NOT-CLAIMED}

The constructive extensions give explicit realizations of several earlier
conditional statements. \Cref{ch:neighborhood-parents} constructs compatible
parent sections over an actual overlap cover, handles changing active agents
through joint event masses, and retains coarse connection defects.
\Cref{ch:inference-memory} gives a normalized parent-sensitive mechanism and
retained path laws with an exact finite-memory information cost. These
constructions use declared ambient laws, channels, and mechanisms; they do
not infer a joint law from independently specified singleton objectives.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

At one base point, \Cref{ch:finite-depth-formation} constructs a nonlinear
cyclic family for every prescribed finite depth. Its declared VFE-descending
diffusion, source jumps, and natural-gradient model learning produce
successive coherence episodes. Exact normalized parents retain all source
assignments and loop marks, activate from the preceding actual retained law,
and continue learning from their own laws. The weakest interaction energy,
continuous mobility, and source proposal rate stay fixed at one. Separate
bounds control conditional relative entropy, complete model scores,
continuous trajectories, and entire discrete source histories.
\Cref{thm:finite-depth-adaptive-formation} and
\Cref{app:finite-depth-formation-proofs} give the joined assumptions and proof.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

This family uses supplied interaction contrasts, restricted source support,
and prepared initial laws at positive temperatures that decrease with the
regime parameter. The finite-depth construction does not assert that source
learning creates those contrasts, that arbitrary initial laws select the
same hierarchy, or that an infinite-depth or physical-time limit exists.
\status{NOT-CLAIMED}

\Cref{ch:constructive-spatial-rg} supplies an infinite-volume
high-temperature lattice realization, complete generated interactions, a
local RG operator on an open interaction neighborhood, and a constructive
basin bridge. Its Fisher geometry is regular only on the stated fixed-depth
parameter regions. The lattice domain is a worked realization, not a
restriction imposed on the ambient gauge-variational theory.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

\Cref{ch:normalized-geometry,ch:geometry-pressure} distinguish branchwise
normalization from one global geometry factor, transport the complete
observation experiment and scalar normalization, and prove pressure and
specific-VFE limits for retained finite geometry labels. Gauge, Fisher,
and base curvature require different geometric data. Label erasure can
destroy Gibbs regularity, and a disappearing label can make KL to a limiting
joint infinite while the finite-ensemble VFE density remains finite.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

Critical universality, continuum field measures, smooth geometry across
phase ties, uniform Fisher invertibility at unbounded depth, and a
self-consistent curvature action remain separate construction obligations.
The available results do not establish autonomous agency or a physical law.
\status{OPEN}

The multivariate Gaussian enters only after that general structure.  It
realizes the smooth statistical tier in information coordinates and supplies
explicit determinant gaps, Fisher charts, matrix-weighted interaction
operators, aggregation formulas, Kron counterexamples, and generalized
spectra.  The Laplacian-plus-self-term interaction cone is a declared
subfamily, not a consequence of every linear-Gaussian directed model.
\status{HYPOTHESIS}

Within its exact domain, aggregation is closed, but closure alone does not
select that cone or a nondegenerate partition. \status{ESTABLISHED}

Unrestricted matrix-weighted Kron closure fails, whereas
the later Gaussian coarse chapter records the exact closed
congruence-diagonal domain and its qualified maximality boundary.
\status{ESTABLISHED}

The RG conclusions are deliberately narrower. Rescaling is declared rather
than derived. \status{DEFINITION}

Bi-additive Gaussian parameters are closed under additive pushforward, and an
identified homogeneous hierarchy gives an operator fixed ray, but the ray lies
on the boundary of the full-space Gaussian probability domain. \status{ESTABLISHED}

For the Gaussian operator family just described, general infinite-hierarchy
attraction and blocking-scheme independence remain open. These Gaussian
obligations are distinct from the constructive lattice results below. \status{OPEN}

Finite numerical gates test specified diagnostics and controls; they do not
prove those asymptotic claims. \status{NUMERICAL}

Identifying any repaired limiting object with a physical law remains the
semantic claim \ref{claim:physical-law-identification}; it requires a complete
probability repair, a target system, a validation criterion, and
discriminating evidence. \status{OPEN}

\section*{Epistemic contract}

Every nontrivial statement carries one of the following tags. The central
open-obligation appendix collects unresolved conjectures and problems once;
established claims remain with their hypotheses or proofs, and numerical
claims remain bound to the numerical-provenance appendix.

\begin{sciencetable}{Status taxonomy: tags used throughout.}
\label{tab:status-taxonomy}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.19\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
\toprule
\textbf{Tag} & \textbf{What it promises the reader} \\
\midrule
\status{ESTABLISHED} & Proved here, or a standard result cited to a source that has been checked. \\
\status{DEFINITION} & A declared type, construction, or convention. Nothing is being proved and the text says so. \\
\status{HYPOTHESIS} & A restriction the development adopts by choice. What it excludes, and where it is used, are stated. \\
\status{CONJECTURE} & Believed and precisely stated, but not proved. Stated sharply enough to be attacked. \\
\status{NUMERICAL} & Supported by computation only, with its measurement, seed, and control reported. Computation is not proof. \\
\status{OPEN} & Unsettled. What would settle it, and what obstructs it, are named. \\
\status{NOT-CLAIMED} & A statement the development deliberately declines to make. Declining is not refuting, and the text says which it is doing. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{sciencetable}

A gesture is either discharged by proof, calculation, or a checked source, or
converted into an open obligation with exact hypotheses.  Likewise,
\texttt{NOT-CLAIMED} is never used for a refuted statement.  The obstruction
chapter contains exact negative results; the interpretation chapter marks
readings and empirical commitments without promoting them to theorems.

\section*{A physicist's fast path}

A first reading may take the object and frame-covariance contract from
\Cref{ch:introduction,ch:geometry}, then read only the normalized-joint and
ELBO identities in \Cref{ch:probability,ch:generative,ch:elbo}.  The next pass
should read the local--collective potential theorem and interaction-label
attention in \Cref{ch:local-collective-elbo}, then the covariant pullback and
relational-history constructions in
\Cref{ch:pullback-geometry,ch:relational-inference}.  The next pass should
take the three-operation distinction, Markov coarse theorem, exact effective
hypergraph/path-space closure, and typed scale diagram in
\Cref{ch:coarsegraining,ch:renormalization,ch:agent-network-rg}.
For the constructive continuation, read the cover and memory results in
\Cref{ch:neighborhood-parents,ch:inference-memory}, then the finite-depth
pointwise dynamics in \Cref{ch:finite-depth-formation} and the lattice theorem
in \Cref{ch:constructive-spatial-rg}. The geometry normalization and pressure
chapters, \Cref{ch:normalized-geometry,ch:geometry-pressure}, explain which
geometric factors enter the VFE and survive blocking.
\Cref{ch:thermodynamic-fisher-draft} gives the separate strict-phase,
fixed-tie, and reciprocal-volume Fisher results with their source and
regularity hypotheses.
The Gaussian spine then consists of the interaction cone,
recognition costs and Fisher geometry in
\Cref{ch:gaussian,ch:restrictions,ch:infogeometry}, followed by Gaussian
aggregation, congruence-diagonal Kron closure, holonomy, fixed-ray
qualifications, generalized spectra, and numerical gates in
\Cref{ch:gaussian-coarsegraining,ch:gaussian-renormalization}.  Finally,
\Cref{ch:obstructions,ch:philosophy} separate exact no-go results and repairs
from empirical and interpretive commitments.  Disintegration proofs,
convex-duality details, long rational witnesses, ledger rows, and protocol
metadata can be recovered through their semantic cross-references on a second
reading.
