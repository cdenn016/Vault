\section{Relation to Existing Programs}\label{sec:related_work}

Wheeler's participatory program asks how registered distinctions acquire physical meaning and how many participants can belong to one world~\cite{Wheeler1983,Wheeler1990}. The present construction treats this as a question about records, conditional laws and the compatibility of local descriptions. It begins with classical probability. It does not infer a Hilbert space, the Born rule or quantum complementarity from Bayesian normalization. Relational and personalist interpretations of quantum theory provide philosophical context~\cite{Rovelli1996,Fuchs2014}, but their physical postulates are not supplied by the present classical model.

Variational inference supplies the central identity between free energy, evidence and posterior KL. Active inference additionally specifies a generative process, policy variables and preferences or decision criteria~\cite{Friston2010,friston2016active,DaCosta2020DiscreteSynthesis}. Its distinction between inference about states, learning parameters and selecting models is directly relevant to the question of fast beliefs and slow models. These can be coordinates or marginals of one joint law. Their differing time scales still require dynamics, information accumulation or a controlled elimination, as Section~\ref{sec:timescales} shows.

The Friston program has also pursued structure learning and multiscale generative models. Supervised structure learning learns aspects of a model within a specified representational scheme~\cite{Friston2024StructureLearning}. Renormalizing generative models construct hierarchical descriptions by spatial and temporal grouping, compression and transition learning~\cite{Friston2025PixelsPlanning}. That construction supplies grouping and time blocking; its tractable latent hierarchy assumes that children are not shared by several parents. This concerns the generative graph, not a general prohibition on overlapping spatial input patches. It is a precedent for learned effective models, not a proof that every smooth overlap network develops an autonomous hierarchy without further assumptions.

Gauge formulations of neuronal free energy relate local representation changes, prediction errors and precision~\cite{Sengupta2016NeuronalGauge}. Here the geometric distinction is explicit: a coordinate transition is invertible, whereas a message need not be. The interaction $L_{ij}=R_iS_j$ can erase directions and is represented probabilistically by a normalized channel. Flat reference maps $\Omega_{ij}$ remain a useful specialization. Independent invertible interaction links and general matrix links carry different notions of loop structure. This distinction connects the framework to geometric consensus while keeping statistical laws, rather than only vectors, as the objects being compared~\cite{SingerWu2012,HansenGhrist2021Discourse}.

Markov blankets enter only when a specified probability law has the relevant conditional independence. Geometric overlap alone supplies no such independence. Bayesian-mechanics constructions and critiques clarify why a graphical or dynamical separation must not be substituted for a statement about the joint law~\cite{dacosta2021bayesian,biehl2021technical,bruineberg2022emperor}. In this monograph, a local agent is defined geometrically and statistically; a blanket is an additional property to prove in a particular generative model.

Statistical distance and thermodynamic length motivate the use of Fisher length to quantify distinguishable change~\cite{Wootters1981,SalamonBerry1983,Crooks2007}. The resulting information clock depends on the chosen record and state description. It is not automatically physical proper time. Likewise a Fisher pullback gives a positive-semidefinite distinguishability tensor; its positivity prevents it from being a Lorentz metric without additional structure. Section~\ref{sec:signature_resolution} therefore uses the standard determinant geometry of two-dimensional complex Hermitian forms, familiar from polarization and spinor descriptions~\cite{HanKimNoz1997Stokes,SimonEtAl2006LorentzTurns}. The additional step identifying that internal geometry with spacetime is stated separately.

Finally, exact marginalization and projected dynamics are established tools of statistical physics. They generate effective interactions and, in dynamics, memory kernels~\cite{Zwanzig1961Memory,Mori1965Transport}. They do not generally preserve the form of the fine model. Here these issues arise for normalized multi-agent laws, selective interfaces and retained states. This monograph develops their physical interpretation and finite worked constructions, including where closure, recursive formation and a physical identification remain to be established.
