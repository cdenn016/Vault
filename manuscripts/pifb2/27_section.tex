\subsection*{Data and Supporting Material}
This is a theoretical monograph. It reports analytic constructions and limited symbolic checks, with no new empirical dataset, simulation study or experimental validation of the proposed physical interpretation. The companion theory and the derivations cited in the text supply the mathematical context. The local research archive preserves source records, analytical reconstruction notes and the checks accompanying this revision.
