\section{Notation and Conventions}\label{sec:notation}

An edge $ij$ carries information from $j$ to $i$. All probability divergences use natural logarithms. The base point $c$, inference time $t$, hierarchy level $\ell$ and source index $j$ refer to different kinds of variables. Specialized appendices define their additional symbols locally.

$\mathcal C$, $c$, $c_*$ & Continuous base manifold, a base point, and the fixed point used for formation analysis.\\
$\mathcal U_i$, $V(c)$, $\chi_i$ & Domain of agent $i$, available agents at $c$, and an optional presence weight or indicator.\\
$P$, $G$, $\mathfrak g$ & Principal frame bundle, structure group, and Lie algebra.\\
$\mathsf Y$, $\mathcal B$, $\mathcal S$ & Statistical sample space, regular family of laws, and associated statistical bundle $P\times_G\mathcal B$.\\
$\zeta_i$ & Joint statistical section of agent $i$. When a state--model split is used, $\zeta_i(dk,dm)=q_i(dk\mid m)s_i(dm)$.\\
$k$, $m$; $q_i$, $s_i$ & State and model presentation; belief about the state and law over model presentations. A model becomes predictive through its normalized kernel.\\
$p_i$, $r_i$ & Prior on states and prior on model presentations. A prior is not a synonym for a generative model.\\
$\mathbb P$, $P_o$, $Q$, $Z_o$ & Full generative law, its posterior at the observed record, recognition law, and evidence. Some local proofs write $P$ for a probability law when no principal bundle is involved.\\
$U_i$, $\phi_i$, $g_i$ & Frame presentation, a local Lie-algebra chart with $U_i=\exp\phi_i$, and passive coordinate change.\\
$\Omega_{ij}$ & Flat reference identification $U_iU_j^{-1}$. It does not encode a general selective message.\\
$\Theta_{ij}$ & Optional independent invertible interaction link; group-valued loop products can have nontrivial holonomy.\\
$S_j$, $R_i$, $L_{ij}$ & Sender readout, receiver reconstruction and selective content map $L_{ij}=R_iS_j:E_j\to E_i$.\\
$\mathcal K_{ij}$, $N_{ij}$ & Normalized communication kernel and the noise covariance in its Gaussian realization.\\
$A_{ij}$, $B_{ij}$, $\Gamma_{ij}$ & Comparison maps into an observation space and its Gaussian noise shape. These need not equal the content maps.\\
$J_i$, $\pi_{ij}$, $\beta_{ij}$ & Chosen source, source-prior probability, and variational or conditional source probability.\\
$E_{ij}$, $\tau$, $\tau_\gamma$ & Comparison energy, source temperature, and optional model-row temperature. Context distinguishes a vector fiber $E_i$ from a scalar comparison cost.\\
$\mathcal F_V$ & Exact collective variational free energy of a declared normalized generative law.\\
$\mathcal E$ & Channel-consensus objective. An exact generative identification requires a separate derivation.\\
$\gamma_{ij}$, $(q)$, $(s)$ & Optional product-model attention and state/model sector decorations in specialized calculations.\\
$\alpha_i$ & Optional gain multiplying a divergence in $\mathcal E$; it is not automatically a generative precision.\\
$\mu_i$, $\Sigma_i$, $\Lambda_i$ & Gaussian mean, covariance and precision $\Sigma_i^{-1}$.\\
$G$, $g^F$, $h^\omega$ & A Fisher matrix, Fisher metric, and its pullback using a declared connection. The structure group $G$ is distinguished by context.\\
$\eta$, $\mathsf M$ & Mobility or mobility operator; additional kinetic tensors are defined where introduced.\\
$C$, $Q^c$, $P_o^c$ & A normalized retained channel and the induced recognition and posterior laws. The base manifold is calligraphic $\mathcal C$.\\
$H_o$, $H_o^c$, $\rho$ & Dimensionless fine and coarse negative log densities and their reference law.\\
$T_{\mathrm{cfg}}$ & Temperature of a separately declared Gibbs ensemble over belief configurations; distinct from source and physical temperatures.\\
$t$, $\vartheta_i$ & Evolution parameter and information clock, the Fisher length along an appropriate smooth belief history.\\
$\ell$, $I$, $U_I$ & Hierarchy level, parent label or cluster, and chosen parent frame.\\
$W$, $\mathcal H(W)$, $X$ & Complex rank-two communication bundle, its real Hermitian tensor bundle, and a Hermitian tensor.\\
$\sigma_\mu$, $x^\mu$ & Pauli basis including the identity and the four real components of $X=x^\mu\sigma_\mu$.\\
$e:T\mathcal C\to\mathcal H(W)$ & Additional soldering map used to identify internal Lorentz geometry with tangent geometry.\\
