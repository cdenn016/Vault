## Model (time-invariant framework of `def:fw-system`, `Collective_Agents/sections/02_framework.tex`, lines 11-17)

- World: one scalar AR(1) component, $\eta_{t+1}=F\eta_t+w_t$, $w_t\sim\mathcal N(0,Q)$, stationary, never renewed. $F=0.9$, $Q=0.05$.
- Population $V=\{S\}\cup A$ with $A=\{1,2,3,4\}$ (the audience). The source $S$ is not a member of $A$.
- Model state: every agent filters $x_t=(\eta_t,\eta_{t-1})$ with an exact Kalman filter. Computation noise $q_cI_2$, $q_c=10^{-4}$, is added to the predicted means and included in the prior covariance $P^-$.
- Sensors: $S$ has an own sensor with noise variance $R_S=0.01$; each member has an own sensor with noise variance $R=1$. Own sensors are read every round.
- Broadcasts: every agent writes its raw reading $u_a(t)=s_a(t)$ every round. This is a fixed speech act, not a choice.
- Channels: a message arrives as $y_{ij}(t+1)=u_j(t)+\nu_{ij}$ with $\nu_{ij}\sim\mathcal N(0,R_c)$, $R_c=0.002$, independent across receivers, senders and rounds. Latency is exactly one round, so the message read in round $t+1$ informs $\eta_t$, the second component of $x_{t+1}$, with noise variance $R_j+R_c$, and the receiver's filter uses that exact variance.
- Attention: $S$ reads nothing except its own sensor. In every round each member $i$ chooses one set $J$ among all subsets of size at most 2 of $\{S,m_1,m_2,m_3\}$ (its three co-members), eleven sets in all, drawn with probability proportional to $E(J)e^{-\gamma G_i(J)}$, with uniform habits $E$ and $\gamma=40$. $G_i(J)$ is minus the information that its own next sensation and the messages of $J$ carry about its next model state $x_{t+1}$, under its own predictive model, with no pragmatic term. Members draw independently given their own covariance states.
- Contrast coordinates: prior errors $e_a(t)=m^-_{a,0}(t)-\eta_t$ (the $\eta_t$ component of each agent's prior mean, minus $\eta_t$), for every agent including $S$.

## World-conditional law and conventions

- `def:col-world-conditional` (`Collective_Agents/sections/04_collectives.tex`, lines 130-141): condition on the world path and on sender-side broadcast noise (there is none in this model). An agent's own sensing noise is a private input and is not conditioned on, even when the agent broadcasts its reading. Receiver-specific channel noise is private to the receiver.
- Second-moment convention (`04_collectives.tex`, line 187): with attention drawn at random each round, $\Sigma^c=\E[\Cov(e(t)\mid\eta)]$, estimable as $\tfrac12\E[(e^1-e^2)(e^1-e^2)^\top]$ from two replicas that share the world path and have independent private noises and independent attention draws.
- Test values: a compound-symmetric block $\Sigma=\alpha I_n+\beta\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^\top$ with no cut has contrast $r=1+n\beta/\alpha$ (at $\alpha=1$, $\beta=0.5$, $n=4$: $r=3$). $\KL(\mathcal N(0,1)\Vert\mathcal N(0,4))=\ln2-3/8$.

## Declarations (made before the analysis)

- Baseline: clause (c) of `def:col-baseline` (`04_collectives.tex`, lines 197-209) with the mean coupling. Beside it, compute the medium generated by the attention mechanism with five agents of the members' type (each senses with $R=1$, broadcasts its raw reading, and attends at most 2 of the 4 others by expected free energy at $\gamma=40$, with the same channels).
- Selector: contrast at level $r_0$ (`def:col-collective`, lines 214-221), with an agent that lies in no collective left unassigned.
- Coarse variable: the collective average of the members' $\eta_t$ prior means.
- Memory clause (`def:agy-closure`, `Collective_Agents/sections/05_agency.tex`, lines 13-24): input-conditional form. The members' attention choices are part of the blanket path; given the blanket path the members' filter dynamics is linear but time-varying, so compute the Gaussian conditional mutual information for each sampled blanket path and report its average over paths (the standard conditional mutual information) and, as a sensitivity, its spread over paths. Two Gaussian initial laws: (i) the stationary law of the noise-driven part; (ii) the framework's deterministic start (initial priors fixed).
- Inference clause: the belief that the coarse variable encodes, a Gaussian with mean $y$ and (a) the best constant variance, (b) the members' claimed prior variance, against the posterior predictive of $\eta_t$ of the union model (one filter that receives all members' data). State the union model you use.
- Partner randomization $\mathfrak N_{\mathrm{pr}}$ (`05_agency.tex`, lines 332-360, row of the table at line 350), redrawn every round: in every round each member's set is two partners drawn uniformly from the four other agents, independently across members and rounds.
- Admitted class: the box of $\pm5$ percent about $(F,Q,R,R_S,R_c,q_c,\gamma)$.
- Agency index (`def:agy-group-model` and `def:agy-agency-index`, `05_agency.tex`, lines 156-260): the stationary state as the only decision state; the class of group models whose policies are the members' joint attention choices, whose slots are the members' readings of the source (a reading of a co-member opens no slot), whose external states are the world and the source's broadcast, with habits held at the uniform law, the group belief of the stationary coarse state, no pragmatic term and $\gamma_C\ge0$.
- Interaction order parameter (`def:agy-order-parameter`, `05_agency.tex`, lines 296-302): $\zeta_S$ denotes the source's private inputs (its sensing-noise path).
