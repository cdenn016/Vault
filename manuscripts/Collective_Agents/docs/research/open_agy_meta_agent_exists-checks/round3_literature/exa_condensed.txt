--- topic_i
The index measures as-if agency at a specified interface; it does not find the interface. Let R record who listens to whom and A the public joint act. A model of P(R,A) must explain routing. A model of P(A) sums over routes: a member’s vote or message can help cause A without itself being a group action. [Waade et al., §§2.3–2.4](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/27/2/143) implement exactly this distinction with votes and a group bandit choice. [List and Pettit, ch. 3](https://epdf.tips/group-agency-the-possibility-design-and-status-of-corporate-agents.html) distinguish an aggregation rule, its organizational implementation, and enactment. The group can also decide to change its procedure; that is a different action variable.

There are different tests even with the interface fixed. [MacDermott et al., Defs. 3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04758) ask how much better goal-constrained policies predict the chosen decisions than uniform play. [Jørgensen et al., Defs. 13–15](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00248) instead ask whether a higher-level decision mechanism survives aligned interventions on the underlying mechanisms. State-to-state information, synergy and effective information answer still different questions: joint dynamics can be informative without positing a policy or goal. Report the action readout, the allowed group models and, for a causal claim, which interventions should preserve the group description.

Covert action: yes, in a model with selectable attentional alternatives. In [Sandved-Smith et al., Figs. 5–6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8396119/) “stay” and “switch” are controls; precision at the lower level implements their effects. Weighting sensory evidence alone need not be a decision ([Parr and Friston](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-15249-0)). Blanket-active state: not on that ground. A variable changing only the group’s internal processing need not be on its active blanket; membership is a structural property of the chosen internal–blanket–external partition, not a label for everything under control ([Friston, Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2013.0475); [Friston et al., §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.06387)). A member-level action and a group-internal event can therefore be the same event.
--- topic_ii
Three different meanings of “reproduce the planner” should be kept separate. (1) Optimal decision rules: the team planner chooses rules subject to the same information constraints as its members, not acts with pooled private observations. Person-by-person optimality alone only rules out unilateral improvements. [Radner, §3, Thm. 1](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~rradner/publishedpapers/9TeamDecisionProblems.pdf) gives a stationary-to-global result with joint concavity, differentiability and finiteness hypotheses; discrete source choices do not inherit it. For a fixed measurement law, [Choi and Lee, Lemma 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.0811) obtain an exact potential by paying each member its conditional information contribution. This aligns unilateral differences, not every Nash equilibrium with a global maximum.

(2) Exact sampling at finite temperature: here is a sufficient condition for the paper’s nonuniform habit law (deduction). Fix the context x and a finite joint policy space. For γ>0 the distribution minimizing 𝔼_q[G]+D(q∥E)/γ is P_g; this is [Ortega and Braun, Eqs. (9)–(10)](https://arxiv.org/html/1204.6481v2) with U=−G. Suppose Δᵢuᵢ=Δᵢ(−G) for every feasible unilateral change at x. Select one member i with fixed positive probability and draw b with probability proportional to E(b|π₋ᵢ) exp[γuᵢ(b,π₋ᵢ;x)]. The difference uᵢ−(−G) is constant in b, so this update is exactly the full conditional of P_g. Each update satisfies detailed balance; so does their mixture. On support connected by such updates the stationary law is unique. Ordinary unweighted logit gives the uniform-reference case; a correlated habit generally requires its conditional, not a product of individual habits. Members must have enough information to implement these conditionals. See [Monderer and Shapley, §2](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/%7Emansour/sem-game-02-03/monderer-potential-96.pdf) and [Alós-Ferrer and Netzer, Prop. 1](https://twi-kreuzlingen.ch/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/twi-rps-028-alos-ferrer-netzer-2008-06.pdf).

(3) Maximization or stochastic stability: only as γ→∞ does that law concentrate on the minimizers of G in the reference support (ties weighted by E). Independent simultaneous draws from old-profile conditionals need not preserve it. Other revision protocols have their own zero-noise results, not necessarily the same finite-noise law ([Marden and Shamma, Thm. 4.2](https://kipdf.com/revisiting-log-linear-learning-asynchrony-completeness-and-payoff-based-implemen_5b4f1a2f097c476c498b45a9.html)). If sensing changes the posterior, available links or environment x, the potential identity is conditional on the old x. Stationarity must then be proved for the joint state–policy transition, or the fixed-context result used explicitly as a within-stage approximation. The additional inequality for a state transition at unchanged action is visible in [Liu et al., Def. 19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.00192).
--- topic_iii
Let P=P_actual, D₀=D(P∥E), with 0<D₀<∞. The index is sup_g 𝔼_P[log(P_g/E)] / D₀: the best gain in expected log score over habit, divided by the gain available to a model that knows P. [Cameron and Windmeijer, Eqs. (2)–(6)](https://cameron.econ.ucdavis.edu/research/je97preprint.pdf) give the deviance-ratio counterpart; [Schneidman et al., Fig. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-bio/0512013) give an exact distributional special case with an independent baseline and pairwise maximum-entropy fit. McFadden’s ratio of log likelihoods is related, not generally identical. The difference of KLs is not in general D(P_best∥E); that identification needs a projection identity such as [Amari, Thms. 5 and 10](https://people.csail.mit.edu/jrennie/trg/papers/amari-ig-hierarchy-01.pdf).

Component bound (deduction, not a quotation). Write π=(a,s), Dₐ=D(Pₐ∥Eₐ), C=ΣₐPₐ(a)D(P(s|a)∥E(s|a)). If (P_g)ₐ=Eₐ for every g, the [KL chain rule, Thm. 2.5.3](https://samfinlayson.com/files/notes/Cover_and_Thomas_ch2_entropy.pdf) gives D₀=Dₐ+C and index = [C−inf_g ΣₐPₐ(a)D(P(s|a)∥P_g(s|a))]/(Dₐ+C) ≤ C/(Dₐ+C). Equality holds iff the infimum of the conditional residual is zero; attainment is unnecessary. No independence of a and s is assumed. Fixing the marginal of a is not the same as fixing its conditional given s.

Interpretive limits (algebra of the proposed index): the score is ≤1, is undefined when P=E, and can be negative if every candidate does worse than habit by a positive margin. Including E, for example by permitting constant G, ensures nonnegativity. Report support and denominator explicitly. With unrestricted G and positive P,E, setting G(π)=−γ⁻¹log[P(π)/E(π)]+constant reproduces P exactly. Restrictions on G that hold across contexts, and tests on new contexts, are therefore substantive parts of an agency claim. Comparing a joint-policy score with a public-action score changes both the numerator and the denominator.
--- topic_iv
[Albarracin et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3390/e24040476) are the closest match: agents choose whom to read on an available graph using expected free energy and habits. Selective attention can form an epistemic community without creating a single edge. [Sethi and Yildiz (2016)](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/ecta13320Final.pdf) provide a different Bayesian mechanism: learning a source’s perspective makes future opinions from that source easier to interpret. Neither result requires the resulting community to choose as one.

Distinguish this from [Golub and Jackson](https://bengolub.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/homophily.pdf) (learning on an exogenously formed graph), [Baumann et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.12325) (opinion-dependent contact probabilities), and [Sasahara et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42001-020-00084-7) (actual unfollowing and replacement). A trace can also guide later members without any contemporaneous audience choice: [Constant et al., §4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5938575/) treat lasting environmental modifications as sources of evidence; [Heylighen, Part I, §2](https://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/Papers/StigmergyICognSystems.pdf) defines coordination through traces of actions. For the proposed index, distinguish choosing a source, updating on its signal, and changing which sources will be available next. Only the first and third are candidate decisions, and neither is necessarily a decision of the aggregate.

The strongest precedent for treating member communication as an internal mechanism is Waade et al.: members vote, but the fitted group policy predicts the resulting bandit choice, not the votes. Sandved-Smith et al. show when the opposite treatment is appropriate: redirecting attention is itself an action when the model specifies selectable alternatives. The distinction turns on what the group policy is supposed to explain.

For the proposed index, distributed choices reproduce the central softmax under exact potential differences and one-at-a-time sampling from the correct habit-weighted conditionals. Person-by-person optimality alone does not suffice. The closest normalization precedents are Cameron and Windmeijer’s KL-based R² and Schneidman et al.’s fraction of multi-information captured. The fixed-marginal ceiling follows directly from the KL chain rule; it is a deduction, not a theorem attributed to those authors.
