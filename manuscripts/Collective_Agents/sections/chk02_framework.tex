\subsection{Checks for \texorpdfstring{Section~\ref{sec:fw}}{the framework}}\label{chk:fw}

\begin{numcheck}[Value of one message under different retained information]\label{chk:fw-levels}
% src: L1-P1(d)
In a scalar static world with prior precision one, one common source and one private source per agent, each of precision one, and a noiseless message carrying the sender's posterior mean, the mutual information between the state and the message given the receiver's retained information was computed by exact Gaussian conditioning on the full joint law. A receiver that keeps its raw data gains $0.1438$ nats, equal to $\tfrac12\ln(4/3)$; one that keeps only its posterior mean gains $0.1003$ nats, equal to $\tfrac12\ln(11/9)$; a level-1 receiver believes it gains $0.3466$ nats, equal to $\tfrac12\ln2$ (\cref{prop:fw-level-optimality}).
\end{numcheck}

\begin{numcheck}[Update subspace and prior completion]\label{chk:fw-reception}
% src: L1-P2, L1-P3
For \cref{prop:fw-update-subspace}, 200 random instances were drawn with state dimension $K$ between 3 and 6, code rank between 1 and $K-1$, random positive-definite priors and noises, and every third instance given a rank-deficient code by repeating a scaled row. The largest component of $m_i^+-m_i$ outside $\operatorname{col}(P_i\hat H^\top)$, together with the largest component of the columns of $P_i\hat H^\top$ outside $\operatorname{col}(P_i-P_i^+)$, was $1.4\times10^{-14}$; the rank of $P_i-P_i^+$ equaled $\rank\hat H$ in every instance. For a functional $f$ with $f^\top P_i\hat H^\top=0$ ($K=5$, rank two) the changes of the mean and the variance of $f^\top\eta$ were $2\times10^{-16}$ and $6\times10^{-17}$. The factorization \eqref{eq:fw-decode-lift} held to $2.2\times10^{-17}$ for the gain and $2.5\times10^{-16}$ for the mean. For \cref{prop:fw-prior-completion}(a), a two-component Gaussian mixture prior on $\R^2$ was discretized on a $401\times401$ grid and updated with a likelihood that depends on the first coordinate only: the conditional law of the second coordinate given the first was unchanged to $7\times10^{-18}$ on every row with mass, while the marginal of the first coordinate moved by total variation $0.63$.
\end{numcheck}

\begin{numcheck}[Two receivers and the disagreement example]\label{chk:fw-two-receivers}
% src: L1-P4, L1-X1
For \cref{prop:fw-two-receivers}, 200 random pairs of receivers (state dimension 2 to 5, code rank 1 to $K$) gave a largest error of $1.1\times10^{-15}$ in the decomposition (a) and a largest relative error of $2.7\times10^{-15}$ in the identity (d), with the expectation computed in closed form. When the two prior covariances differed by a matrix of norm $1.31$ whose product with $\hat H^\top$ vanishes, the gains differed by $2.2\times10^{-16}$; when the priors differed along a sent direction they differed by $0.22$, as part (b) requires. The equal-covariance identity (c) held to $1.7\times10^{-14}$, and in the noiseless limit (e) both receivers reproduced the message exactly in the coded coordinates while their posterior means differed by $0.107$ in $\ker\hat H$. For \cref{ex:fw-disagreement} at $\varrho=0.8$, $\sigma^2=0.5$, the prior divergence was $3.5556$, equal to $2\varrho^2/(1-\varrho^2)$; at $y=0,1,2,3$ the gaps of the posterior means were $0$, $1.0667$, $2.1333$ and $3.2000$, equal to $2\varrho y/(1+\sigma^2)$, and the posterior divergences were $1.1852$, $2.7654$, $7.5062$ and $15.4074$, equal to \eqref{eq:fw-disagreement}; the coefficient of $y^2$ was $1.5802$ and the predictive variance at which the expected divergence equals the prior one was $1.5000$.
\end{numcheck}

\begin{numcheck}[Communication energy]\label{chk:fw-comm-energy}
% src: numerical checks of 2026-11-20, kept with the coupling-regimes note
For \cref{lem:fw-reception-identity}, random Gaussian beliefs in state dimension 4 with code rank 2 or 4 and mistranslated code models $\hat H=H_j\Upsilon^{-1}$, with $\Upsilon$ drawn near the identity and of condition number below 10, gave a largest relative difference of $1.5\times10^{-9}$ between the divergence of $q_i$ and $\hat q_{ij}$ computed on $\R^K$ and the divergence of $q^\circ_{ij}$ and $q^\circ_j$ computed on $\R^{r_c}$. This covered 1,951 finite cases of relative entropy and of R\'enyi divergences of orders 0.3, 0.5 and 2, in both argument orders, and perturbing the covariance of $\hat q_{ij}$ along $\ker\hat H$ moved the left side by at least $2\times10^{-5}$ relative. The R\'enyi divergence of order 2 was infinite on both sides together in 943 further cases and on one side only in none. A separate computation, which derived $\hat q_{ij}$ from \eqref{eq:fw-reconstruction} by Gaussian conditioning and confirmed it by Monte Carlo sampling of the two-step definition, agreed with the closed form (b) to $7\times10^{-11}$ over 240 cases in state dimension 3 to 5 with code ranks 1 to 5, full rank included, and with (a) in both argument orders, for relative entropy and for R\'enyi orders 0.3, 0.7, 1.5 and 2, to $2.3\times10^{-10}$, with 683 infinite cases matched and none unmatched. The splitting (c) held to $7\times10^{-13}$ with the conditional divergence integrated exactly. In a discrete non-Gaussian case with $\hat H\ne H_j$, relative entropy in both orders, total variation, squared Hellinger distance, the $\chi^2$ divergence, the Jensen--Shannon divergence and R\'enyi divergences of orders 0.3, 0.5, 2 and 4 satisfied (a) to $10^{-12}$. For \cref{prop:fw-full-rank-energy}, the computed reconstruction at full rank equaled $(\Upsilon_{ij})_\#q_j$ to $2.2\times10^{-11}$ in 60 cases. The world and internal forms of the energy, recomputed with 50 significant digits for 60 random frames and translations, agreed to $10^{-41}$; their double-precision values differed by up to $4\times10^{-3}$ relative for ill-conditioned frames, which is rounding error.
\end{numcheck}

\begin{numcheck}[Communication energy on noisy channels]\label{chk:fw-comm-energy-noisy}
% src: numerical checks of 2026-11-20, kept with the coupling-regimes note
For \eqref{eq:fw-noisy-reconstruction}, the gain form and the information form of the posterior, mixed over $q_j\mathsf K$, agreed to $1.4\times10^{-14}$ relative in 360 cases in state dimension 2 to 4 at every code rank, while exchanging $R_c$ and $\hat R$ changed the covariance by at least $6\times10^{-4}$ relative; Monte Carlo sampling of the two-step definition with $4\times10^5$ samples matched the mean to $9\times10^{-4}$ and the covariance to $2\times10^{-3}$. The inequality $\Lambda_i+\hat H^\top\hat R^{-1}\hat H\succeq(1+\mathrm{snr}^{\min}_{ij})\Lambda_i$ held in every case, $\mathrm{snr}^{\min}_{ij}$ was below $10^{-13}$ at every rank below $K$, and at full rank it matched the least eigenvalue of $\hat R^{-1/2}\hat HP_i\hat H^\top\hat R^{-1/2}$ to $10^{-13}$. For \cref{prop:fw-consensus-form}, with $K=3$ and $r_c=2$ the energy vanished to $10^{-14}$ at 20 shifts of a zero along $\ker\hat H$, on a noiseless channel and on a noisy one; with $r_c=K=3$ and $R_c\ne\hat R$ it vanished at the predicted law and was $1.4\times10^{-2}$ at $(\Upsilon_{ij})_\#q_j$. In the counterexample of the proof the energy matched $\tfrac12\Vert(I-\mathsf G)\mathsf d\Vert^2$ to $8\times10^{-12}$ in 50 cases and lay at least $1.6\%$ below $\KL(q_i\Vert(\Upsilon_{ij})_\#q_j)$. For \cref{prop:fw-noisy-energy}(a), with $K=3$ and $0\preceq R_c\preceq1.5\hat R$, $\mathrm{snr}^{\min}_{ij}$ times the difference settled at $-2.576$ as $\mathrm{snr}^{\min}_{ij}$ grew from 3 to $3\times10^5$, the distance of the covariances stayed below $0.42$ times the bound of the proof, and the scalar formula held to $10^{-13}$. With $R_c=1$ and $\hat R=10^{-2}$, $10^{-4}$ and $10^{-6}$ the energy at $m_i=m_j$ was $0.0929$, $0.09654$ and $0.096573$, against the limit $0.096574$. For (b), the energy with $R_c=\hat R$ stayed below the noiseless energy in 540 cases at every rank, with smallest gap $6\times10^{-4}$, while with $R_c=30\hat R$ it exceeded it in 78 of them. For the per-edge form of \cref{prop:coh-frustration}(a), on directed three-cycles with $K=2$, $\tfrac12\mathfrak F(\mu)$ matched the mean-dependent part of $\mathfrak E^{\mathrm{cons}}$ to $2\times10^{-14}$, and the nullity of $\mathcal L$ was 0, 1 and 2 for holonomies with 0, 1 and 2 fixed directions. A separate computation, given only the definitions of the two reconstructions, derived the closed form, the zero sets and the classes of channels with a consensus form; its values of the reconstruction and of both energies on three instances in dimensions 2 and 3, computed with 50 significant digits and confirmed by Monte Carlo sampling and by Gauss--Hermite quadrature of the two-step definition, agreed with \eqref{eq:fw-noisy-reconstruction} to the 12 digits reported, and its exact scalar difference and its limit $(2\ln2-1)/4$ agreed with the statements. A reconstruction of the proofs found no false step; its checks confirmed the bound of (a) with explicit constants on 1,436 instances, the necessity of $R_c=\hat R$ in (b) in 24 of 24 cases with $R_c\ne\hat R$, and the per-edge form on a weakly connected graph of four agents.
\end{numcheck}

\begin{numcheck}[Communication energy under code learning and local transformations]\label{chk:fw-comm-energy-learning}
% src: numerical checks of 2026-11-20, kept with the coupling-regimes note
For \cref{prop:fw-energy-convergence}(a), with $K=4$, code ranks 2 and 4, and 60 belief parameters with means in $[-2,2]^4$ and covariance eigenvalues in $[0.5,2]$, the largest difference quotients of the energy and of its derivative in the belief parameters settled at 9.63 and 19.09 for rank 2 and at 16.65 and 39.36 for rank 4 as the step along a fixed direction fell from $\sigma_{\min}(\hat H^\infty)/2$ to $10^{-3}\sigma_{\min}(\hat H^\infty)$. On the boundary of the ball of radius $\sigma_{\min}(\hat H^\infty)/2$ the least singular value stayed above $\sigma_{\min}(\hat H^\infty)/2$ in 2,000 draws, and at full rank the energy at $\hat H^\infty$ equaled $\KL(q_i\Vert(\Upsilon^\infty_{ij})_\#q_j)$ to $2\times10^{-14}$. In the scalar case of (b) the difference quotient at $\hat H=1.001$ grew from $10^{-3}$ at $m_i=0$ to $1.0\times10^6$ at $m_i=1000$, and both closed forms held to $10^{-15}$. For (c), least squares on $2\times10^5$ samples per edge of a directed three-cycle with $K=2$ came within $3.3\times10^{-3}$ of $H_jM_\phi(M_\phi+R^g)^{-1}$; the limit mistranslations equaled $I+R^gM_\phi^{-1}$ to $10^{-15}$, with determinants 2.31, 2.97 and 2.82, and the holonomy had determinant 19.36, their product, while with the calibrated regressor its distance from the identity was $9\times10^{-3}$, the sampling error. For \cref{prop:fw-energy-symmetry}, with $K=4$, every code rank, well-conditioned frames, translations and transformations, and 200 draws per rank, transformations with $g_j$ in $\GL(K)_{\ker T_c}$ and arbitrary $g_i$ changed the energy by at most $1.3\times10^{-12}$ relative, generic $g_j$ changed it by up to $0.998$ relative below full rank and by $5\times10^{-14}$ at full rank, and the consensus energy changed by at most $6\times10^{-14}$. The changes in the proof of (b) matched $\tfrac12\mathsf s^2\Vert T_cg_j\mathsf x\Vert^2_{(\mathsf V'_j)^{-1}}$, and for a loop $\tfrac12(\mathsf s-1)^2\Vert T_cg_j\mathsf x\Vert^2_{(\mathsf V'_j)^{-1}}$, to ten digits at $\mathsf s=\tfrac12$, 1 and 2. On a three-agent graph whose third agent sends only on edges of weight zero, transformations in $\GL(K)_{\ker T_c}$ at the other two agents and a generic one at the third changed the energy by $2\times10^{-14}$ relative, and giving one of the third agent's edges the weight $\tfrac12$ made the change $0.38$. The dimensions $K^2-r_c(K-r_c)$ and $K(K-r_c)$ and the homomorphism onto $\GL(r_c)$ were confirmed for $K=4$ and every rank, and the noisy scalar example after the proposition gave $0.125$ and $0.32$. For the equality clause of \cref{prop:fw-noisy-energy}(b), with $R_c=\hat R$ the energy lay strictly below the noiseless energy in 600 cases at every rank, with smallest relative gap $0.049$; both vanished to $2\times10^{-10}$ when the coded laws agreed, and with $R_c=30\hat R$ the inequality failed in 79 of the cases. A separate computation, given only the definitions, derived the uniform convergence on compact sets with its linear rate and its failure on the set of all belief parameters, the attenuated limit $I+R^gM_\phi^{-1}$, the invariance group of the edge energy with its dimension and kernel, the condition on weights, and the equality condition of (b). Its exact rational holonomy for an instance with three receivers, with determinant $1808375/408576$ and Frobenius distance $1.710943209$ from the identity, and its two closed-form values in (b) agreed with the statements to the ten and twelve digits reported. A reconstruction of the proofs found no false step. Its checks covered codes of ranks 1, 2 and 3 on one graph, cycles of length 2 and 3, a two-cycle whose limit holonomy has eigenvalues 1 and 36 and so keeps a consensus space of dimension one, and noisy channels, on which transformations with $B=2I$ changed the energy by 4.29 at $K=r_c=2$ and by 0.249 at $K=3$, $r_c=1$; each of its six planted errors was detected.
\end{numcheck}
