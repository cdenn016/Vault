## 2. Recommendation and summary

**Recommendation: reject in the present form, with encouragement to resubmit a restructured manuscript of about 17,000 words.** A physics journal would return the current 79,455-word, 531-word-abstract document without review, and a referee who read it would find that the thesis the author wants it to carry is not the thesis it argues. The mathematics underneath is largely sound, and a restructured paper built from it could reach a major-revision verdict at Foundations of Physics.

**Soundness.** The load-bearing algebra survives recomputation: the GL(K) KL invariance theorem, the Gaussian Fisher blocks and their curvature, the softmax/Gibbs value and envelope identities, the clock-dissipation identity, the Fisher pullback and its rank theorem, the M-projection barycenter and its M-step reading, the parent-frame non-identifiability and the equivariance no-go at 1575, the four-generator signature construction, the one-loop and two-loop Laplace coefficients, the Appendix A gradients, and the Appendix D fixed-point algebra all check to floating-point precision (Section 10). What fails is a set of claims built on that algebra. The Regime I frames are pure gauge at a point, so the frame-twist signature form can be set to zero by a symmetry of the functional; "observable" at 1975 is false. The consensus metric is not "the only candidate gauge-invariant observable" (2022): the covariant Fisher block is invariant without any orbit average. The representation theorem covers only the frozen-neighbor best response, not the canonical functional it is cited for at 678. The cross-scale shadow double counts evidence, and its functional falls below the surprisal of the joint it is said to reduce to. The Karcher uniqueness radius is off by a factor of two. The Appendix B covariance back-action does not vanish. The kinetic-metric postulate gives unit frequency for every mode. The force-residual bound of Appendix I is false at nonzero configuration temperature. The Appendix G Banach bound is false for anisotropic couplings. The data-availability statement names files that do not exist.

**Significance.** For a physics readership the significant, defensible content is small and good: an agent-relative clock with an exact dissipation identity, an agent-relative induced geometry by Fisher pullback, consensus as the stationary point of an explicit collective functional (a formal model of Wheeler's "one world out of many observer-participants"), and the gauge bookkeeping that makes comparisons between agents' laws frame-independent. The renormalization-group vocabulary, the mass reading, the Lorentzian signature, the gravity reading, and the consciousness material do not add physics and draw objections.

**Fitness.** The paper does not stand alone as a realization of Wheeler's participatory it-from-bit (Section 3). Its abstract never mentions Wheeler; line 81 names an "RG spine" as the organizing claim; the it-from-bit section is 715 words (0.9 percent). It depends on an uncited "companion exact-ELBO treatment" (678) and on code behavior in the abstract. It never cites the Theory manuscript, which now proves stronger versions of several of its statements and contradicts several others (Section 5).

## 3. Does PIFB2 stand on its own as a realization of Wheeler's participatory it-from-bit?

No. The manuscript supplies a classical, multi-agent, information-geometric model of some strands of Wheeler's program, and it states in five places that it "realizes" the program (1677, 1727, 1830, 1872, 1892; also 2091 and 2158) while its own fence F5 (114) concedes that the participatory reading "has no identified falsifying observation and may be unfalsifiable as currently stated." None of the five realization sentences carries the F5 pointer (L13-1). The Theory's interpretation chapter (`c14_interpretation.tex`, `sec:phil-participatory`) describes the same loop as resembling Wheeler's proposal "without being derived from it or evidence for it," and adds that none of the three loci of participation "separates the program from a hierarchical model with learned priors; formation could." Under a Duhem-Quine and Mayo-severity analysis, a passed tower test has zero severity against that rival (L13-1).

The architecture does not argue the thesis either (L17-1). The 530-word abstract has zero occurrences of "Wheeler" or "it from bit" and lists the attention recovery, the WikiText fit, and a statement about "the checked-in multi-agent implementation." Line 81 states the organizing claim as a "renormalization-group spine." Between lines 128 and 1676 (33,692 words) Wheeler is not mentioned. The conclusion of Part I calls the attention recovery "the framework's most concrete contribution" (1806), and the global conclusion never names Wheeler. The Wheeler-relevant theorems (the clock identity, the pullback tensor, the regulator discussion) sit in Part II, which the paper asks the reader to treat "as Outlook rather than as Results" (1812), while the Level-3 ontology and the language-and-cognition conjectures sit in the "Defensible Core" (L17-6).

Where the correspondence is asserted, it conflicts with Wheeler's own text (L12, L13-2, L11-3; Wheeler 1990 read in full text by L12, quotations below use the reprint pagination of *Feynman and Computation*, pp. 309-336; the `Wheeler1990` bib entry, pp. 3-28 of the Zurek volume, was confirmed by L15). Wheeler's bit is an elementary registration, "binary yes-or-no indications," and primary; PIFB2's bit is KL/ln 2 of a continuous Gaussian update, and PIFB2 itself concedes (1835) that this count is not additive under refinement (script: 0.7213 bits for one step, 0.0072 bits summed over 100 substeps of the same unit-Fisher-length path). The self-excited circuit is self-sustaining through time, whereas PIFB2's own 1729 proves by the Łojasiewicz inequality that the flow relaxes and "only observation injection supplies persistent exogenous drive"; 1677 declares the top hyper-prior a fixed boundary condition while 1719 pools it self-referentially. Wheeler's loop runs from information back to physics, but PIFB2's pulled-back metrics enter no free-energy term (the integration measure is the flat background chart, 509), so the loop closes only inside the information category (L12-3). Law without law is claimed at 1767 with gauge invariance "a consequence of consensus rather than an axiom," which 2028 and `rem:kretschmann` contradict, and a functional fixed in advance is the pre-established law Wheeler rejects (L12-4). The smooth base C is a presupposed continuum, "logically prior" (153), while Wheeler lists "No continuum" among his four no's; 146 also calls C "derived from agentic structure," which contradicts 153 (L12-6, L13-6, L11-3). "No external physical time is presupposed" (1830) sits beside an external flow parameter and the postulated shared update parameter P5 (L12-7, L13-7, L07-7). No agent chooses a question and the classical fiber has no complementarity, so "no question, no answer" is not represented (L12-8).

**What PIFB2 does deliver.** The strongest defensible correspondence is the one the paper never cites: Wheeler's third question, "How come 'one world' out of many observer-participants?", and his adoption of Føllesdal's dictum that meaning is "the joint product of all the evidence that is available to those who communicate" (p. 318). Wheeler's bit semantics can be recovered, rather than renamed, by adding a binary registration channel: for a yes-no observation the expected belief update equals the mutual information I(X;O) and is at most one bit per registration (L12_bits_clock: 0.135, 0.962, 0.0145 bits for three channels), and the registration count is additive.
