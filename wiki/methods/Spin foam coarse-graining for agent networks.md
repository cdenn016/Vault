# Spin foam coarse-graining for agent networks

## Judgment and existing research

The most useful transfers are boundary descriptions, compatible graph diagrams, and gauge-preserving interface compression. The companion [[dennis-2026-spin-foam-boundary-methods]] now proves a conditional toolkit for these operations at $c_*$. Its 28 target ancestors and auxiliary finite regulator identity retain explicit admission conditions. Group-field-theory graph sums remain longer-term tools. Neither the selected papers nor the companion establish endogenous recursive formation or autonomous parent dynamics.

The live PIFB2 coarse-graining and open-problem sections and the MultiAgentELBO theory already supply exact reference/evidence pushforwards, generated hyperedges, marked loop/cut parents, and conditional memory. Supplement B already has conditional projective extension and asks for uniform control in system size and reduction depth. These are existing research, not discoveries imported from gravity. [[Coarse Graining]] and [[Graphon limits of agent networks]] retain the distinct network-limit comparisons.

Spin foams are labeled two-complexes; spin networks label boundaries. Fixed complexes, refinement families, and sums over complexes are distinct. Many coarse-graining demonstrations use lattices. A random attention graph requires its own specified law and is not automatically a spin foam or a quantum amplitude. [[asante-2022-spin-foams-refinement-renormalization]], [[bahr-2011-operator-spin-foams]], and [[oriti-2006-group-field-theory]] establish the relevant distinctions.

## Boundary laws and refinement

The consistent boundary formulation carries generated interactions by allowing richer boundary data and compatible embeddings. [[dittrich-2012-continuum-limit-spin-foams]] and [[dittrich-2014-time-evolution-refining-coarse-graining]] distinguish kinematical choices from embeddings informed by the model's amplitudes. [[bahr-2014-background-independent-renormalization]] supplies complete-observable consistency and selected-observable approximations.

> [!note] Editorial: pointwise transfer
> Treat a chosen agent block through its exterior-facing positive likelihood/law and its retained attention, ordered cut, and loop marks at c_*. Boundary means a graph interface. Use the existing common kernel on reference, evidence, and recognition, then compare alternative graph-reduction routes. A correlated reference is not silently factorized at a cut.

A reference-conditioned lift is a stochastic reconstruction, not recovery of every original microstate. Recovering both reference and evidence with one fiber-supported lift requires that the record likelihood descend to the retained state. A posterior-dependent embedding need not satisfy that sufficiency condition. Neither static boundary consistency nor increasing agent count proves closed dynamics.

## Gauge and exterior-sensitive compression

[[dittrich-2016-decorated-tensor-networks]] retains gauge decorations during truncation, with finite Abelian demonstrations. [[delcamp-2017-nonabelian-decorated-tensor-networks]] extends the machinery to finite non-Abelian groups. [[delcamp-2017-fusion-basis]] and [[cunningham-2020-fusion-charge-coarse-graining]] organize coarse boundary sectors and generated excitations. [[hauru-2018-graph-independent-local-truncations]] evaluates discarded bond directions through their environment. [[yosprakob-2024-reduced-nonabelian-tensor-networks]] supplies exact internal magnetic-index contraction before truncation in its pure-gauge lattice setting.

> [!note] Editorial: interface construction
> Retain jointly rooted loop/cut and source-event data. Decompose an admitted compact-unitary feature interface into representation carriers and multiplicities, and truncate only with intertwining projectors. Rank discarded directions by their effect on declared exterior records. Contract only genuinely closed internal representation indices; exposed feature and matter legs remain.

A finite metric-dependent projection can be passively GL-covariant when its metrics and coordinate maps transform together. That is not a noncompact Haar-probability construction or a universal GL harmonic algorithm. A symmetry-preserving tensor approximation also need not define an entrywise-positive density. Tensor/SVD tails alone do not bound support-aware KL. The positive action residual or an admitted common Markov statistic must supply the probability-law comparison.

Dense 512-agent graphs can have large cuts and many physical loop marks. Neither gauge fixing nor a fixed bond budget guarantees compression. Interface width, singular-spectrum tails, positive normalization, and exterior error remain assumptions to establish on a declared graph family.

## GFT, functional RG, and large parameters

[[benedetti-2015-functional-renormalization-tgft]] develops an exact functional-RG equation followed by a specified U(1) tensorial truncation, whose flow is nonautonomous. [[carrozza-2014-renormalization-su2-tgft]] proves perturbative renormalizability for one SU(2) kinetic/interaction family. [[gurau-2014-random-tensor-universality]] proves tensor-index universality under normalized cumulant assumptions.

> [!note] Editorial: longer-term transfer
> A positive joint graph/state model and a fixed-feature generating functional can organize connectivity, correlation functions, and generated operators. They need a declared graph law, proper link measures, regulator, source moments, and comparison scale. The Legendre effective action of a regulated generating functional is distinct from the project's evidence-preserving pushed-reference action.

Tensor rank, representation cutoff, tensor-index size, graph volume, GFT occupation, and agent count are different parameters. No current map makes melonic dominance a universality result for learned attention. GFT condensation is not a proved meta-agent mechanism. [[Renormalization group flow]] and [[Graphon limits of agent networks]] keep the other scale and limit constructions separate.

## Conditional derivations and remaining applications

[[dennis-2026-spin-foam-boundary-methods]] now constructs common recovery for a declared exterior likelihood catalog and extends it to specified integrable families at the measure level (Theorem 2.2, Corollary 2.11). Minimality is restricted to exterior-independent lifts; general recovery has its separate likelihood-descent criterion (Proposition 2.5). Correlated gluing retains conditionals and induced joint factors from one proper reference (Proposition 2.6). Ordered forest-rooted marks compose, while full probabilistic graph comparisons require admitted factor-through squares (Propositions 2.8–2.9).

Compact-unitary whole-carrier compression and passive GL projection with transformed positive metrics retain different admissions (Propositions 3.3–3.4). The positive-law bridge requires a common statistic chosen before recognition and slice evaluation and a positive normalized kernel at both feature inputs, with an envelope on its full log density (Theorem 3.5). Spectral tails alone do not supply this envelope. Support-preserving residuals separately control posterior KL, evidence/full-VFE offsets, and accumulated route error when the exact full channels agree (Chapter 4). The finite regulator identity is separately proved and auxiliary (Proposition 6.2).

Predictive error is a separate finite-horizon quantity. The standard-Borel history-information plus row-risk identity requires joint dominance, finite conditional information, and finite source-context risk (Theorem 5.4). The finite-alphabet forgetting estimate requires conditional-filter control (Theorem 5.8). Activation uses the actual microscopic law conditional only on an already completed prefix, retaining every feedback control needed for prediction. Unconditional hidden mixing does not establish conditional memory loss.

Four applications remain `INCONCLUSIVE`: a usable finite regular non-Abelian interface with actual cost and normalized-likelihood error bounds; full interface diagrams for changing boundary coordinates; predictive estimates for an evolving-link completed-prefix activation family; and size/depth-uniform budgets (Chapter 7). The conditional results admit arbitrary finite networks, including $N\ge512$, while their costs and constants may depend on size. No useful low dimension, autonomous own-law parent, recursive formation, thermodynamic limit, base transport, or emergent geometry is proved. Finite/Gaussian examples ground the formulas without setting the theory's direction.
