# Rigorous theory search

## What it is

Rigorous theory search is the research program's protocol for sustained proof, construction, and
effective-theory searches. It permits an optimistic **discovery prior**—including a request to keep
searching for an affirmative construction—while preventing that prior from entering the
**certification logic**. The method combines a frozen problem contract, a mechanism-diverse search
portfolio, an atomic claim ledger, an acyclic dependency graph, domain-specific mathematical gates,
adversarial reconstruction, and an oracle-erasure audit. Its archived design and mathematical
reconstruction are recorded in [[2026-08-02-rigorous-theory-search-skill]].

It is a coordination and evidence discipline, not an automatic theorem prover. Its structural
validators can reject malformed or circular proof records, but only an actual derivation, formal
proof, or hypothesis-checked theorem can close a mathematical claim.

## How it works

1. **Freeze the target.** Record exact quantifiers, types, regularity, measures, boundary
   conditions, symmetry actions, equivalences, premises, postulates, falsifier, and literature
   policy. Keep a desired affirmative conclusion separate from admissible premises.
2. **Pass the foundational gate.** Check that probability laws normalize, supports and conditional
   laws exist, analytic interchanges are justified, bundle maps and pullbacks are typed, units are
   consistent, and physical interpretations have explicit bridge principles.
3. **Open a mechanism-diverse portfolio.** Classify routes by representation, invariant,
   construction, or obstruction rather than by wording or solver identity. Do not broadcast a
   favored narrative during the independent discovery phase.
4. **Demand mathematical objects.** Admit only proved lemmas, typed constructions, rigorous
   counterexamples, or named obstructions with exact discharge conditions. Retire a route unless a
   follow-up contributes a materially new mechanism or interface.
5. **Certify atomic claims.** Store assumptions, evidence, falsifiers, and dependencies one claim at
   a time. A cycle must be replaced by a genuine fixed-point or existence theorem.
6. **Assemble globally.** Prove overlap, cocycle, normalization, boundary, interaction, and
   correction terms. For coarse-graining, define the exact effective functional before asking
   whether a finite ansatz is closed.
7. **Attack the result.** Use independent reconstruction and adversarial checks for hidden
   existence assumptions, gauge artifacts, singular cases, missing generated operators, invalid
   local-to-global steps, and overstrong physical readings.
8. **Erase the search prior and release.** Recompute the target's full dependency closure without
   the affirmative discovery prior. Release a complete affirmative theorem, a complete negative
   certificate, or an inconclusive record with the strongest proved result and minimal remaining
   obligations.

## Domain gates used by this program

For [[Agents as fibre-bundle sections|agent sections]], the method requires the covariant vertical
first jet before a fiberwise Fisher tensor is pulled back. The result is called a semimetric unless
nondegeneracy is proved; passive gauge invariance and dependence on the selected connection are kept
separate. A fixed-fiber belief history is vertical, while horizontal transport over a base curve
requires a connection. Fisher arclength is agent-relative information duration, not physical time
without an operational bridge.

For [[Evidence lower bound (ELBO)|ELBO]] and local/global VFE claims, the method requires normalized
generative and recognition laws, support and finiteness conditions, regular conditional laws, and
all dependence corrections. A local coordinate objective may reproduce the corresponding global
variation without summing to the global objective. An observation likelihood may be represented by
an interaction kernel without proving an agent-only ontology.

For [[Renormalization-group flow of beliefs|renormalization]], the method first asks for an exact
pushforward, marginalization, conditional expectation, or variational contraction. A projected
finite ansatz is exact only after a closure theorem; otherwise generated operators and a typed
truncation residual must be retained. Cross-scale subtraction and beta data require a common
reference space and a declared scheme. Composition of scale maps is not an autonomous semigroup
without additive scale and translation invariance. A driven cocycle generally uses invariant
sections or other scale-dependent invariant objects; a common fixed object can occur as a special
case, while ordinary fixed-point stability belongs to a declared autonomous flow.

## Strengths and limitations

The main strength is persistence without epistemic coercion: several incompatible routes can stay
alive while the release gate remains proof-based. The claim graph exposes the precise bridge between
local formulas and a global theory, and the adversarial phase converts plausible objections into
explicit falsifiers or additional hypotheses.

The method cannot guarantee that a target is true, solvable, novel, or open. Solver agreement is not
evidence, structural validation is not semantic proof, finite computation does not prove a universal
statement, and a restricted literature search cannot support a priority claim. The archived
no-target-skill baseline is also not a closed efficacy benchmark: its reported 240 assertion grades
lack durable per-assertion records, so paired evaluation remains required
([[2026-08-02-rigorous-theory-search-skill]]).

## Relation to this work

This is the default orchestration method for new research-level construction requests spanning the
[[VFE Transformer Program]] and [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]]. It is especially useful
for the program's recurring load-bearing interfaces:

- local versus collective [[Multi-agent variational free energy]];
- belief/model fibers versus perceived base geometry;
- observation kernels versus agent/environment interaction interpretations;
- fine agents versus meta-agents and cross-scale sections;
- exact effective functionals versus projected RG or attention ansatzes;
- mathematical duration versus claims about emergent physical time.

The method complements, rather than replaces, the domain foundation in [[Gauge VFE ELBO curriculum]],
the current theorem record [[gauge-vfe-rg-pullback-geometry-2026-08-01]], formal or symbolic checks,
primary-source review, and manuscript peer review.
