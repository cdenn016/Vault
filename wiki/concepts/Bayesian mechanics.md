# Bayesian mechanics

## Definition

**Bayesian mechanics** is the program, consolidated under that name in [[ramstead-2023-bayesian-mechanics|Ramstead et al. 2023]] and given its technical core in [[dacosta-2021-bayesian-mechanics|Da Costa et al. 2021]], that reads the stochastic dynamics of certain physical systems as inference. A system whose state space partitions into internal $\mu$, external $\eta$, and blanket (sensory + active) states with the conditional independence $\mu \perp\!\!\!\perp \eta \mid b$ of a [[Markov blanket interpretation debate|Markov blanket]] is shown, at non-equilibrium steady state, to have internal states that *parametrize a probability density over external states*. A synchronization (or "bold") map $\sigma: \mu \mapsto \eta(\mu)$ sends the most likely internal state to a posterior over external causes, so the internal dynamics can be read as minimizing [[Variational free energy]]. Ramstead et al. capture the dual claim in their subtitle, "a physics *of and by* beliefs": physics *of* beliefs because internal dynamics are lawful, physics *by* beliefs because those same dynamics realise approximate Bayesian inference. How literally the reading holds is disputed, and the lineage has narrowed it. It is exact only for the conditional modes of internal states, given an invertible synchronization map and restrictions on solenoidal coupling ([[millidge-2021-mathematical-walkthrough-fep]], [[friston-2021-interesting-observations-fep]]), and the most recent statement by its authors calls it an as-if description supplied by the modeler ([[friston-2026-generative-modelling-nonequilibrium]]); the dated sections below and [[Markov blanket interpretation debate]] give the details. The construction is geometric: starting from the stationary Fokker–Planck density and its Helmholtz decomposition into dissipative (gradient) and solenoidal (divergence-free) parts, free-energy descent becomes a Riemannian gradient flow tied to the [[Fisher information metric]] and [[Natural gradient]] descent. The field thus organises around three pillars — *partition* (Markov blanket), *map* (synchronization), and *flow* (free-energy gradient on a statistical manifold).

## Why it matters here

Bayesian mechanics is a principal single-system precursor to the [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]], not yet a theorem that the latter formally generalizes. The shared move is that internal states parametrize probability laws over external or latent states, so information geometry can describe inferential motion. Gauge-VFE adds agent-indexed statistical fibers, explicit inter-agent transport, correlated population recognition, and declared multiscale coarse channels.

The additional Bayesian-mechanics obligations are not inherited from those objects. Da Costa et al. construct one particular-state partition, one Markov blanket, a synchronization map, and a stationary stochastic flow. The current Gauge-VFE theory does not derive individual or group blankets, nonequilibrium steady state, ergodicity, compatible individual and group synchronization maps, or physical time. Its relation to Bayesian mechanics is therefore a proposed gauge-covariant multi-agent extension program rather than a completed multi-agent Bayesian mechanics.

The Helmholtz or solenoidal sector remains a dynamical precursor to the project's [[Hamiltonian belief dynamics]] and [[Belief inertia]], not a consequence of fixed-joint ELBO algebra. The [[participatory-it-from-bit]] pullback construction pursues the inference-as-geometry stance by inducing base-manifold tensors from belief fields. [[sengupta-2016-neuronal-gauge|Sengupta et al. 2016]] and [[sengupta2017gauge|Sengupta & Friston 2017]] supply the gauge-axis precursor; PIFB makes the group, connection, and transport more explicit while retaining the missing stochastic-mechanics bridge as open.

## Details

The lineage runs from [[friston-2019-particular-physics|Friston 2019]] ("a free energy principle for a particular physics") through [[parr-2020-markov-blankets-thermodynamics|Parr et al. 2020]], which grounds the partition thermodynamically, to the Da Costa stationary-process formalism and the Ramstead review. [[friston-2023-fep-simpler]] restates the principle in its leanest form. [[sakthivadivel2022-bayesian-mechanics-geometry|sakthivadivel-2022-geometry-bayesian-mechanics]] supplies the explicit information geometry of the synchronization map and steady-state flow, and [[friston-2008-dem]] (DEM) is the earlier generalized-coordinates inference engine on which the dynamical reading rests. Two formulations coexist: *mode-tracking* (steady-state, most-likely internal state) versus *path-tracking* (path-integral over trajectories), each carrying its own assumptions — non-equilibrium steady state, conditional independence, ergodicity. Those assumptions are precisely what the [[Markov blanket interpretation debate]] (Bruineberg, Aguilera, Biehl) contests, distinguishing literal from merely instrumental blankets; PIFB must engage that debate when it claims its agents *are* inferential particles rather than convenient descriptions.

The "physics-by-beliefs" half of the claim only has teeth if the underlying inference is itself well-founded, and Bayesian mechanics inherits this from variational Bayes. The free energy the internal states descend is the same negative evidence lower bound (ELBO) that grounds modern variational inference ([[blei-2017-variational-inference]]), and the synchronization-map reading — internal states *parametrize* a variational density $q_\mu(\eta)$ — is the amortized / structured-posterior idea that hierarchical latent-variable models exploit, e.g. the ladder VAE's top-down precision-weighted refinement ([[sonderby-2016-ladder-vae]]). The factorised, locally-coupled form a blanketed system imposes on its beliefs is also exactly the product-of-experts trick, where a joint density is built by multiplying many simple expert distributions ([[hinton-2002-products-of-experts|hinton-2002-poe]]) — a useful lens on how nested blankets compose beliefs across scales. A coherence guarantee comes from generalized Bayes ([[bissiri-2016-general-bayesian-updating|bissiri-holmes-walker-2016-general-bayes]]): minimising expected loss plus a KL-to-prior penalty is the *unique* rational belief update, so the free-energy E-step is legitimate inference rather than ad hoc energy descent, with precision playing the role of a learning-rate / temperature (a *tempered* free energy). This decision-theoretic footing matters when the inference is not a textbook likelihood update — which is generically the case for blanketed steady-state systems.

## Multi-agent boundary (2026-08-20)

The established Bayesian-mechanics results treat one particular-state partition, one blanket, and one synchronization map, even when the state vectors are high-dimensional. Da Costa et al. identify partitioning the external space into several systems that are themselves Markov blankets as a possible extension, not as a completed interacting-blanket theorem. [[palacios-2020-hierarchical-markov-blankets]] is the nearest constructive result: suitably equipped microscopic blankets form a macroscopic blanket in simulation, while the paper rejects automatic emergence for arbitrary coupling. [[waade-2025-as-one-and-many]] and [[maisto-2025-flock-joint-agency]] likewise treat group agency as conditional on a demonstrated group-level blanket.

A multi-agent Bayesian-mechanics claim therefore owes more than coupled free-energy descent. It must specify the individual and group partitions, establish the required stationary or nonequilibrium steady-state assumptions and conditional independences, construct the individual and group synchronization maps, and prove their compatibility under composition or coarse-graining. A composite vector-valued internal state presents no dimensional obstacle, but it does not by itself induce individual blankets or reciprocal agent-wise maps. [[heins-2023-spin-glass-active-inference]] is an exact collective special case under restrictive assumptions, not a general Bayesian-mechanics extension.

## Lineage before and after the stationary-process formalism, November 13, 2026

The flow decomposition predates the blanket theorems. [[friston-2012-free-energy-value-attractors|Friston and Ao 2012]] attach Ao's form $f=(\Gamma+Q)\nabla V$, with $V$ the log ergodic density and $Q$ antisymmetric, to the free energy principle and prove for constant $Q$ that $e^{V}$ is stationary; their Markov blanket is stipulated through a graphical model. [[friston-2012-fep-biological-systems|Friston 2012]] states the principle for an ergodic random dynamical system with deterministic internal states and no blanket, proving sufficiency of free energy minimization only when the proposal density can match the posterior. [[friston-2020-sentience-markovian-monism|Friston, Wiese and Hobson 2020]] restate [[friston-2019-particular-physics|the 2019 monograph]] without new proofs, pairing an intrinsic geometry of the density over internal states with an extrinsic [[Fisher information metric]] on the beliefs those states parameterize, and call the belief description as-if unless further conditions hold. Replying to [[biehl-2021-technical-critique]], [[friston-2021-interesting-observations-fep|Friston, Da Costa and Parr 2021]] concede that flow sparsity and steady-state conditional independence do not imply one another, separate an exact reading (belief form unspecified) from an approximate one (a parametric family), and confine the inferential reading to conditional expectations of internal states. [[parr-2021-memory-markov-blankets|Parr et al. 2021]] argue, with linear-Gaussian and categorical simulations, that a blanket constrains the stationary density only, so a past observation leaves transient dependence across it. [[dacosta-2021-neural-dynamics-active-inference|Da Costa et al. 2021]] show that discrete-state belief updates agree with [[Natural gradient]] steps to first order in the step size, with nearly equal simulated information lengths on two tasks. [[ueltzhoffer-2021-thermodynamic-efficiency-dissipative|Ueltzhöffer et al. 2021]] is a thermodynamic neighbor with no blanket and no inference claim.

The latest statement, [[friston-2026-generative-modelling-nonequilibrium|Friston, Ramstead and Sakthivadivel 2026]], qualifies the Definition above: its free-energy flow comes from adding a KL term stipulated to vanish, the inferential reading is an as-if description supplied by the modeler, and its two simulations are constructions, not empirical tests. The Definition above was revised on November 13, 2026 for this reason: it earlier said that such systems are treated as literally doing inference, which claims more than this latest statement asserts.

## Maximum entropy, maximum caliber and the gauge reading, November 13, 2026

A second strand states Bayesian mechanics through constrained entropy. [[sakthivadivel-2022-entropy-maximising-parallel-transport|Sakthivadivel 2022]] reads constrained [[Maximum entropy]] for a stationary diffusion with gradient drift as a gauge theory: the constraint function $J$ gives an exact connection $dJ$ on a principal bundle with structure group $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$, a change of constraint acts as $p \mapsto e^{-\lambda J'}p$, and $p = e^{-\lambda J}$ is a covariantly constant section of the associated line bundle. The connection is exact, so the bundle is flat and every [[Holonomy]] is trivial, and the link to the Helmholtz decomposition is suggested without proof. [[sakthivadivel-2023-worked-example-classical-objects|The worked example]] applies maximum caliber with mean and variance constraints to a classical particle and obtains a Gaussian path density whose infinite-precision limit concentrates on the Newtonian path; the author grants that the derivation presupposes the classical force law. [[sakthivadivel-2022-regarding-flows-fep|The reply to Aguilera et al.]] concedes their linear-system conclusions and conjectures that a path-based formulation restores the content the state-density formulation lacks. [[sakthivadivel-2022-functorial-statistical-physics|The functorial announcement]] sketches a proof that maximum caliber is a functorial field theory whose sewing law composes Gibbs-type kernels.

Two papers outside the free energy literature supply the curved case. [[polettini-2012-nonequilibrium-thermodynamics-gauge|Polettini 2012]] treats local rescaling of probabilities on a Markov graph, $\rho_i \to e^{-\varphi_i}\rho_i$, as a change of prior with gauge potential $A_{ij} = \log(w_{ij}/w_{ji})$: detailed balance is flatness, and the logarithms of the Wilson loops are the cycle affinities. [[feng-2011-potential-flux-decomposition|Feng and Wang 2011]] give the continuous-state counterpart, an abelian connection whose curvature is nonzero exactly when detailed balance fails.

> [!note] Editorial: abelian single-system gauge versus transport between agents
> All three connections live on the state space of one system, with a line of probability weights as fiber and abelian rescaling as group: flat for gradient drift, curved exactly when detailed balance fails. The [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]] instead transports Gaussian beliefs between agents under a nonabelian group. None of the three constructions is a special case of the model, and a derivation of November 13, 2026 (`docs/research/2026-11-13-abelian-sector-of-gauge-transport.md`) states what is exact. The absolute value of the determinant maps the model's transport to a positive-scalar field on the agent graph with Polettini's group and transformation laws; this field is what makes the log-determinant term of each Gaussian coupling energy frame invariant, it carries no rates or currents, and it is exact for pure-gauge transport. Polettini's construction applies as it stands to the attention matrix read as a jump process on agents when attention has reciprocal support, with cycle affinities given by cycle sums of the asymmetry of the coupling energy and of the prior, and changes of frame act trivially on it. The connections of Sakthivadivel and of Feng and Wang live on the sample space of one agent, a fiber of the model's bundle. Beyond these statements the correspondence is an analogy.

## Approximate and path-space blankets, November 13, 2026

[[ramstead-2022-notation-minima-comment|Ramstead and Sakthivadivel 2022]] accept the linear-system results of [[aguilera-2022-particular-physics|Aguilera et al.]] and correct the reading of the flow equation to an expected rate, $\langle \dot{\eta}(b_t) \rangle = (Q - \Gamma) \nabla \mathfrak{I}(\boldsymbol{\eta}_t, b_t)$, rejecting the identification of the flow of an average with the average of a flow. They argue that the surprisal gradient over external states and the free energy gradient over internal states are related through the Jacobian of the synchronization map and share zeros, not trajectories. [[heins-2022-particular-flows-attracting-sets|Heins 2022]] shows numerically, in one three-dimensional linear example, that solenoidal coupling between blanket and internal states leaves the attracting set of particular states on the line of conditional modes, while coupling between external and blanket states rotates it away.

On prevalence, [[sakthivadivel-2022-weak-markov-blankets|Sakthivadivel 2022]] defines a blanket index and proves that its normalized value concentrates at zero exponentially fast in dimension. The probability is over an assumed ensemble of independent zero-mean couplings, and the conditional dependence itself is not shown to be small in absolute terms. [[pearson-2026-flux-defined-markov-blanket|Pearson 2026]] tests the condition numerically on Gray-Scott spots: conditioning on boundary flux raises the conditional [[Mutual information]] between interior and exterior, which the author attributes to a collider effect, and a thick band of ambient field screens organisms and empty placebo regions alike.

Three papers treat dependence across time. [[virgo-2022-sensorimotor-history-markov-blankets|Virgo, Rosas and Biehl 2022]] argue that a discrete-time sensorimotor loop makes internal and external states independent given the sensorimotor history and initial states, and does not imply the time-synchronous condition. [[parr-2021-memory-markov-blankets|Parr et al. 2021]] grant transient dependence given the current blanket state and measure it by conditional mutual information. [[sakthivadivel-2026-markov-interacting-particles|Sakthivadivel 2026]] proves criteria for a path-space boundary $Y_{[0,T]} \perp X_{[0,T]} \mid B_{[0,T]}$, shows that it is not equivalent to the instantaneous statement, and grades its failure by the conditional mutual information $G_T$, which bounds total variation and is logically independent of entropy production. It builds no synchronization map and makes no inference claim, so it adds to the partition pillar only.

## Mathematical foundations of the flow decomposition, November 13, 2026

The Helmholtz split that the Definition takes as given has rigorous statements. [[dacosta-2023-entropy-production-stationary-diffusions|Da Costa and Pavliotis 2023]] prove that $\rho$ is stationary exactly when the drift is $b = b_{\mathrm{rev}} + b_{\mathrm{irr}}$ with $b_{\mathrm{rev}} = D\nabla\log\rho + \nabla\cdot D$ and $\nabla\cdot(b_{\mathrm{irr}}\rho) = 0$, and that for smooth positive $\rho$ the irreversible part is $Q\nabla\log\rho + \nabla\cdot Q$ with $Q$ antisymmetric. The split is the antisymmetric and symmetric parts of the generator in $L^2_\mu$; the irreversible part conserves relative entropy to $\rho$ and the reversible part dissipates it. When the irreversible drift lies in the range of the volatility (given an integrability condition), the entropy production rate is

$$e_p = \int b_{\mathrm{irr}}^\top D^- b_{\mathrm{irr}} \rho dx,$$

and the paper states that it is infinite when the range condition fails on a set of positive stationary measure (Theorem 4.4, for twice-differentiable volatility), as for underdamped Langevin dynamics under the definition without momentum reversal.

[[barp-2021-measure-preserving-diffusions|Barp et al. 2021]] give the coordinate-free form on an arbitrary manifold: a diffusion with Stratonovich noise fields $Y_i$ preserves $P$ exactly when its drift is locally $\mathrm{curl}_P(A) + \frac12\mathrm{div}_P(Y_i)Y_i$ for an antisymmetric bi-vector field $A$, with a further cohomological term $P^\sharp(\gamma)$ on topologically nontrivial spaces. The Euclidean recipe with an antisymmetric matrix $Q$ is the case of trivial twisted cohomology, and the underlying cohomological theorem comes from the first author's thesis and is labeled unpublished. [[barp-2022-geometric-methods-sampling|Barp et al. 2022]] survey the same family, state with citations that a measure-preserving diffusion is irreversible exactly when the antisymmetric part is nonzero, and derive expected free energy for a precise agent without a blanket condition, the cancellation being shown only for countable path spaces.

For the path-tracking formulation, [[dacosta-2025-generalized-coordinates-sde|Da Costa et al. 2025]] prove that [[Generalised coordinates of motion]] represent solutions of equations with additive, sufficiently differentiable noise up to an error $o(|t|^N)$, and exactly on an interval whose radius depends on the noise path, and can be arbitrarily small, when flow and noise are analytic. The identification of states with paths and the generalized-coordinate Lagrangian rest on those conditions, and convergence of the generalized filtering flow is not proved.

## Other formulations under the same name, November 13, 2026

The name also covers constructions outside the particular-partition program. [[isomura-2023-bayesian-mechanics-self-organising|Isomura 2023]] reverse-engineers a path-space Hamiltonian $\mathcal{H}$ for a Langevin system and declares $p_m \propto e^{-\mathcal{H}}$ the generative model, so a non-steady-state Helmholtz energy equals [[Variational free energy]] by construction, with no steady-state blanket or synchronization map. His proposed alignment of system and environment Hamiltonians, the route to [[Generalised synchrony]], is argued and simulated, not proved. [[kim-2021-bayesian-mechanics-perceptual-inference|Kim 2021]] takes the Laplace-encoded free energy as a Lagrangian and derives Hamilton's equations whose momentum is a precision-weighted prediction error and whose masses, so called as a metaphor, are generative precisions; for a linear model the flow is traceless, so its fixed points are saddles or centers. [[kim-2024-bayesian-mechanics-synaptic-learning|Kim 2024]] identifies the free energy of a synapse with an Onsager-Machlup action under which activity and weight evolve by one Hamiltonian.

Two papers extend the particular-partition language itself. [[sandved-smith-2024-metacognitive-particles|Sandved-Smith and Da Costa 2024]] separate a particle, which has a blanket over some period, from a cognitive particle, whose most likely internal path encodes a posterior, and define metacognitive particles in which some internal paths hold beliefs about the sufficient statistics of others (see [[Metacognition]]); the contribution is definitional and existence conditions are left open. [[spisak-2026-self-orthogonalizing-attractor-networks|Spisak and Friston 2026]] apply the partition recursively to subparticles with continuous Bernoulli states and deterministic blanket states. For symmetric couplings the network-scale posterior is explicit, approximate orthogonality of the learned attractors is argued and simulated (see [[Attractor networks]]), and the asymmetric case is not rigorously established.

> [!note] Editorial: Kim's mass and Fisher information
> Kim's mass, a generative precision, coincides in value with the Fisher information of a Gaussian location parameter (see [[Mass as Fisher information]]). The papers do not draw that identification. A Hamiltonian flow preserves phase-space volume and so has no attracting fixed point, which is in tension with the relaxing trajectories that the 2024 paper reports; the note on that paper records the discrepancy.

## Independent accounts of systems as inferrers, November 13, 2026

A separate line defines when a system may be read as inferring, without a stationary density or a blanket. [[virgo-2021-interpreting-dynamical-systems-bayesian|Virgo, Biehl and McGregor 2021]] define a consistent Bayesian filtering interpretation of an input-driven machine: a map from machine states to beliefs over a hypothesized hidden variable, with a model kernel, under which the machine's own update realizes exact Bayes' rule. The interpretation depends on the machine alone, is not unique in general, and may be consistent yet wrong about the environment; in the discrete case a machine with all transitions possible admits only trivial interpretations. Their appendix proposes the density $q_\mu$ given by the synchronization map as a candidate interpretation map and leaves its consistency open. [[biehl-2023-interpreting-systems-pomdps|Biehl and Virgo 2023]] add action: the output must be the POMDP-optimal action for the encoded belief, which then provably follows the standard belief-state update, and approximate inference lies outside the definition. [[baltieri-2025-bayesian-internal-model-principle|Baltieri et al. 2025]] prove that under the regulation assumptions of the internal model principle the controller on the attracting set is a surjective, dynamics-preserving image of the full system, and that any such model induces a possibilistic filtering interpretation, a degenerate one in which observations are ignored and beliefs are disjoint sets.

[[smithe-2023-compositional-bayesian-brain|Smithe 2023]] proves that exact Bayesian inversions compose by the lens rule while free energy composes only laxly, and conjectures that the claim that every random dynamical system performs inference should be an adjunction between systems and statistical games. [[baltieri-2020-predictions-eye-beholder|Baltieri, Buckley and Bruineberg 2020]] recover, under two chosen assumptions, the linearized engine equation of a Watt governor from the action update of a linear-Gaussian active inference model and conclude that such a description does not license attributing a model to the system. [[millidge-2021-mathematical-walkthrough-fep|Millidge, Seth and Buckley 2021]] audit the 2019 derivation and separate three readings: exact by stipulating that the variational density equals the true conditional, approximate for a general family, and exact for conditional modes given an invertible differentiable synchronization map, a [[Laplace approximation]] and solenoidal coupling block-diagonal across the partition.

## Symmetry-matched beliefs of an external observer, November 14, 2026

[[rosas-2026-symmetry-hierarchical-emergence]] belongs with the accounts of the preceding section in that it relates the structure of a process to the structure of beliefs about it without a stationary density, a blanket or a synchronization map, and it differs from them in whose beliefs are at issue (version read: the arXiv preprint arXiv:2512.00984v1). The beliefs are those of an external observer who runs an exact Bayesian filter on a hidden Markov model; no state of a physical system is interpreted as a belief. The result is that when the latent dynamics, the measurement channel and the initial law are equivariant under a finite group, the orbit variables of state and measurement form a hidden Markov model (Theorem 2). The belief about each symmetry-derived level, which the paper calls an abstract belief, then has an exact filter driven by coarse measurements alone (Corollary 3), and it equals the marginal of the fine belief when the coarse measurements retain all the information that the fine ones carry about the coarse state (Corollary 4). The paper relates its conclusion to the good regulator idea of Conant and Ashby and cites [[baltieri-2025-bayesian-internal-model-principle]] and a good regulator paper by Virgo and coauthors as category-theoretic relatives with which it might be combined. Set beside Baltieri et al., whose induced beliefs are disjoint sets that ignore observations, the abstract beliefs here are probability vectors updated by observations, at the price of a symmetry hypothesis on the channel and the prior. The paper also separates its hierarchy from two neighbors: hierarchical Bayesian modeling, in which coarse descriptions are generally not updated without the fine layers, and variational inference ([[blei-2017-variational-inference]]), which, in the paper's words, simplifies the class of models considered at the cost of approximation error, whereas abstract beliefs are exact and restrict the target of inference. It suggests that the two could be combined, citing [[friston-2025-pixels-planning]]. According to the abstract of the published version, which was not read, the applications there include Boolean models of gene regulation and epidemic modeling.

> [!note] Editorial: bearing on a multi-agent Bayesian mechanics
> The paper constructs no partition, blanket or synchronization map, so it discharges none of the debts listed under "Multi-agent boundary (2026-08-20)". Its relevance to the [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]] is interpretive: it exhibits a symmetric special case in which, under the condition of Corollary 4, beliefs at two scales are exactly compatible under marginalization, a compatibility of the kind that a group-level synchronization map would have to satisfy with the individual ones. An exact transfer is blocked by the finite group and discrete state space of the paper, by its global action of one group on one system where the model has a frame change per agent, by its discrete time and Markov assumption, and by its exact filtering where the model uses variational Gaussian beliefs.

## Additions to the multi-agent boundary, November 13, 2026

Five papers ingested on November 13, 2026 extend the section "Multi-agent boundary (2026-08-20)" above. [[heins-2026-collective-sensing-bayesian-inference|Heins 2026]] fits a school-level generative model to a simulated fish school whose individuals hold no beliefs, pooling patch posteriors about a gradient by precision-weighted sums of natural parameters (see [[Federated inference]]). In simulation the posterior mean recovers gradient direction, less well at school level for the largest schools; the patch posteriors grow overconfident with group size, and the pooled posterior stays overconfident even when each agent is assigned to a unique window. The author names overlapping windows, shared agents and temporal correlation as violations of the independence behind pooling. The realization claim is stated as a hypothesis: the test that physical evolution and belief update commute under a fixed coarse-graining is formulated and not run, and no group blanket, steady state or synchronization map is established. [[spisak-2026-self-orthogonalizing-attractor-networks|Spisak and Friston 2026]] add a constructive case beside [[heins-2023-spin-glass-active-inference]]: node-level free energy minimization gives an explicit network-level posterior for symmetric couplings, with subparticles conditionally independent by construction. [[sandved-smith-2024-metacognitive-particles|Sandved-Smith and Da Costa 2024]] nest blankets inward, with existence conditions deferred.

Two papers constrain how blankets may be stated. [[virgo-2022-sensorimotor-history-markov-blankets|Virgo, Rosas and Biehl 2022]] argue that a sensorimotor loop makes internal and external states independent given the interaction history and initial states, and not given the current blanket state. [[sakthivadivel-2026-markov-interacting-particles|Sakthivadivel 2026]] proves that a sensor and actuator diffusion with independent noise blocks and a conditionally independent initial law has an exact boundary on histories, and that coarse-grained observation distinguishes coupled from separated ensembles by at most $G_T$.

> [!note] Editorial: what a multi-agent Bayesian mechanics would still owe
> None of these papers composes individual and group synchronization maps or transports beliefs between agents' frames, so the debts listed in the earlier section stand for the [[Gauge-Theoretic Multi-Agent VFE Model]]. Three additions follow by analogy and are not proved for that model: blankets between agents with memory belong on histories, or should be graded by a defect such as $G_T$; a [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence|meta-agent]] claim should pass the commuting test Heins formulates; and pooling must account for evidence shared between agents.
