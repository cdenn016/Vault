# Mathematical consciousness science

## Definition

**Mathematical consciousness science** is the program of treating theories of consciousness not as informal verbal frameworks but as explicit mathematical objects — sets, maps, functors, irreducibility functionals — so that their claims can be proved, compared, and falsified rather than merely asserted. Its paradigm case is **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**, which posits that the quantity and quality of an experience are fixed by a system's integrated information $\Phi$ and its associated cause–effect structure. Two complementary strands give IIT mathematical content. The first, Kleiner and Tull's reconstruction, strips IIT 3.0 down to a chain of well-defined maps — physical system and state $\to$ cause/effect repertoires $\to$ irreducible "concepts" found by minimizing over a partition lattice $\to$ a system-level cause–effect structure ("Q-shape") $\to$ $\Phi^{\max}$ under the exclusion postulate — and abstracts the distance measure, mechanism space, and partition scheme into parameters, yielding a *generalized* IIT of which classical and quantum IIT are special cases. The second, the Tsuchiya–Taguchi–Saigo proposal, supplies the missing structure-to-experience bridge using **category theory**: model qualia as objects whose morphisms encode experiential relations, and invoke the **Yoneda lemma** — an object is determined up to isomorphism by its complete profile of morphisms — to license the thesis that fixing all relations fixes the phenomenal character. This is phenomenal structuralism with a formal engine.

## Details

The two strands are siblings, not rivals: Kleiner–Tull formalize *what the structure is* (an irreducibility functional over a partition lattice, with exclusion as maximization), while Tsuchiya–Saigo formalize *how that structure could fix experience* (a Yoneda-style relational determination). Their IIT-shaped notion of emergence holds that a collective is a genuine higher-level unit only when irreducible to independent parts. See [[tononi-2016-iit]] for the originating verbal theory the formalizations target.

IIT is only the most-formalized member of a broader landscape that mathematical consciousness science seeks to render precise and comparable. The **global neuronal workspace** theory (Dehaene's synthesis [[dehaene-2014-consciousness-brain]]; the primary statement Mashour et al. [[mashour-2020-global-workspace]]) locates conscious access in a non-linear "ignition" that broadcasts a representation for global availability — a candidate to be formalized as a dynamical bifurcation. The **free-energy / active-inference** route of Solms and Friston [[solms-friston-2018-how-consciousness-arises]] makes a near-*identity* claim: feeling is what it is like to be a free-energy-minimizing, self-evidencing system. Information-theoretic *markers* supply candidate order parameters for a quantitative science: neural entropy as a correlate of conscious-state richness ([[carhart-harris-2014-entropic-brain]]) and a consciousness-specific balance of integration and functional diversity ([[luppi-2019-consciousness-integration-diversity]]). Finally, Lahav and Neemeh's *relativistic* proposal ([[lahav-2022-relativistic-consciousness|lahav-neemeh-2022-relativistic-consciousness]]) asserts frame-dependence of consciousness but supplies no transformation law. The quantum-collapse route of Orch-OR ([[hameroff-penrose-1996-orch-or]]) rounds out the landscape as a formalized but contested proposal. The wider synthesis of how these theories relate is the [[Consciousness and the hard problem]] theme.
