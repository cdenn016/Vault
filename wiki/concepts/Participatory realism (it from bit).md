# Participatory realism (it from bit)

## Definition

**Participatory realism** names the interpretive thread in which observers are participants in, rather than passive recorders of, the reality they describe. Wheeler's slogan **"it from bit"** holds that physical reality ("it") derives its existence from registered information ("bit"): in his phrasing, no phenomenon is a phenomenon until it is an observed phenomenon [[wheeler-1990-it-from-bit]]. Fuchs later used the name "participatory realism" for the common interpretive thread running through QBism and relational quantum mechanics, the observer-as-participant reading [[fuchs-2017-participatory-realism]].

## Details

Wheeler's essay pairs "it from bit" with the image of a "participatory universe" and of a "self-excited circuit," in which observership closes the loop that brings the observed world into being [[wheeler-1990-it-from-bit]]. Neighboring programs develop related observer-relative or information-first readings: QBism [[fuchs2014-qbism-locality|fuchs-2014-qbism]] and relational quantum mechanics [[rovelli-1996-relational-qm]] make quantum states relative to an agent or a system; the no-go theorem against observer-independent facts [[brukner-2018-no-go-observer-facts]] and the cross-perspective links that restore consistency between observers [[adlam-2022-cross-perspective|adlam-rovelli-2022-cross-perspective]] sharpen the "observer-dependent reality" reading; entropic dynamics [[caticha-2019-entropic-dynamics-qm|caticha-2019-entropic-dynamics]], [[caticha-bartolomeo-reginatto-2015-entropic-dynamics]] reconstructs quantum theory from information geometry; and the emergent-geometry line [[VanRaamsdonk-2010-spacetime-entanglement|vanraamsdonk-2010-entanglement-spacetime]] ties spacetime connectivity to entanglement.
