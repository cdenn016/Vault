# Symplectic geometry

Symplectic geometry studies smooth manifolds equipped with a closed, non-degenerate 2-form (the symplectic form), the natural arena for Hamiltonian mechanics and phase space. The symplectic form converts a Hamiltonian function into a flow via Hamilton's equations and underlies the covariant phase-space / extended-phase-space formulation of gauge theory and gravity, where boundary symplectic structure gives rise to edge modes and surface charges.
