# Observer-dependent facts and Wigner's friend

## Definition

The **Wigner's-friend** scenario asks what happens when one observer (Wigner) treats another observer (the friend) as a quantum system. The friend, sealed in a lab, measures a superposed system and obtains a definite outcome; Wigner, outside, describes friend-plus-system by a unitarily evolving entangled state in which no outcome has yet occurred. Whether the friend's result is a *fact* therefore appears to depend on whose description is taken. **Extended Wigner's-friend** experiments — Frauchiger–Renner's nested-agent thought experiment and Brukner's no-go theorem — sharpen this into formal impossibility results. They show that a small set of intuitively appealing assumptions, jointly that measurement outcomes are **observer-independent facts** (single-world absoluteness), that agents may reason about one another's correct conclusions (consistency/self-reference), that records can be measured (universal validity of quantum mechanics), and locality/freedom-of-choice, cannot all hold. Either some measured outcomes are not absolute facts shared across all perspectives, or one of the reasoning principles fails. A **observer-dependent fact** is then an outcome that is real *relative to* a measuring agent's perspective without being a perspective-independent element of reality.

## Details

One repair on the physics side is **relational quantum mechanics**' move to **cross-perspective links** ([[rovelli-1996-relational-qm]]; Adlam–Rovelli). Brukner's theorem leaves RQM exposed if facts are purely private to each observer; the cross-perspective-link postulate restores enough inter-observer structure — that an outcome recorded relative to one observer is recoverable when a second observer later measures the record — to block paradox without reinstating absolute facts. Shared reality is then reconstructed from links between perspectives rather than presupposed beneath them. Neighboring interpretive options frame the same data differently. [[QBism]] reads quantum states as one agent's personal degrees of belief, dissolving the paradox by denying the friend's state has third-person content at all. The reconstruction programs of [[hardy-2001-five-axioms|Hardy's five axioms]] and [[spekkens-2007-toy-theory|Spekkens' toy theory]] locate observer-dependence in an epistemic restriction on knowledge rather than in ontic facts, suggesting the perspectivalism is information-theoretic at root. [[Quantum reference frames]] make the frame-dependence literal and transformable. Zurek's decoherence and einselection supply the complementary story of *how* robust, redundantly recorded outcomes (quantum Darwinism) become the candidate shared facts.
