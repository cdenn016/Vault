# Belief dynamics

Belief dynamics studies how agents' beliefs (point estimates or full distributions) evolve over time under evidence, social influence, and interaction. It spans opinion-dynamics models (bounded-confidence, DeGroot, Friedkin-Johnsen). Belief traps, attractors, and hysteresis are characteristic phenomena.
