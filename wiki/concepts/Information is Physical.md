# Information is Physical

Landauer's thesis that information is not an abstraction but is always tied to a physical substrate, so that information processing is subject to physical law: erasing one bit of information necessarily dissipates at least k_B T ln 2 of heat. The corollary, pursued by Lloyd and others, bounds the computational capacity of physical systems (the universe as a computer) by their energy and entropy.

The implication cannot simply be reversed. Landauer starts with a physical temperature and Boltzmann
constant and derives a bound on a physical erasure process; it does not show that dimensionless
information alone generates energy, action, or a unit system. In particular, one bit is `ln 2`
nats and does not equal $\hbar$ without an independently fixed action-per-nat scale.
