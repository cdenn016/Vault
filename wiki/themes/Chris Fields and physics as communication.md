# Chris Fields and physics as communication

Fields's work provides a sustained comparison program for treating physical interaction as information exchange, observations as operations at interfaces, and persistent systems as agents with memory. The closest connection to the gauge-VFE program is the requirement to specify what an agent can distinguish, record, communicate, and interpret. The shared vocabulary does not establish an identity of mathematical frameworks: much of Fields's recent work starts from quantum theory, whereas the present classical agent constructions specify normalized probability laws and gauge-covariant comparisons. Two paper notes already existed and were preserved. Source notes document the pages and version used for a targeted reading. This is a literature ingestion, not a full proof review of every paper. ## Interaction, identification, and meaning

In the minimal-physicalist description, a boundary interaction is represented as alternating preparation and measurement of finite information-bearing degrees of freedom. The classical records available to each participant depend on its reference frame and physical resources. A received string is therefore only part of the communication problem: the receiver must identify a carrier, recognize a code, and interpret the result. The earlier system-identification papers ask what finite observations can establish when the underlying implementation and decomposition are not supplied. Their unrestricted identification question differs from inference within a declared model. [[fields-2021-minimal-physicalism-as-a-scale-free-substrate-for-cognition-and-consciousness]], [[fields-2018-some-consequences-of-the-thermodynamic-cost-of-system-identification]], [[fields-2014-equivalence-of-the-symbol-grounding-and-quantum-system-identification-problems]], [[fields-2013-imposing-system-observer-symmetry-on-the-von-neumann-description-of-measurement]]

Chu spaces and Channel Theory supply an explicit language for those semantic relations. A classification relates tokens to types, and an infomorphism preserves the relevant satisfaction relation across descriptions. Cone-cocone diagrams organize abstraction and contextual constraints. The universal property used for semantic integration is categorical; it is not itself a KL minimization principle. These tools are useful for stating which distinctions survive an interface, but probabilities, dynamics, and statistical sufficiency must be added when needed. [[fields-2019-chu-spaces-i-category-theory]], [[fields-2019-chu-spaces-ii-object-identification]], [[fields-2020-do-process-1-simulations-generate-the-epistemic-feelings-that-drive-process-2-decision-making]]

> [!note] Editorial: Comparison with the current agent framework
> The supplied receiver/transcript laws in [[dennis-2026-interacting-cue-result]] answer a conditional statistical question. A peer's message has a likelihood determined by its encoder and unknown evidence. Fields's operational perspective motivates making those ingredients explicit; it does not replace their specification or turn a transported peer belief automatically into independent evidence.

## Memory, time, and persistent agents

Fields and Levin treat memory as an activity of interpretation as well as a durable record. Reading an engram or a public trace is another interaction that can change the reader and the record. Their discussion connects persistence, identity criteria, and the temporal reach of an agent's concerns. The ready-state papers supply an older architectural account: a functioning observer needs recognizers, memory, and ordering conventions. Their requirements differ between a fixed finite task and a task that permits unbounded observation periods and new recognizers. [[fields-2025-thoughts-and-thinkers-on-the-complementarity-between-objects-and-processes]], [[fields-2009-information-contents-and-architectural-requirements-of-observer-ready-states]]

The connection to [[Meta-agents and hierarchical emergence]] is therefore strongest at the level of explicit operational obligations. A stable collective response, a semantic grouping, and an autonomous agent with an inherited interface are different achievements. Prentner and Fields's perceptual-network study illustrates collective attractors under supplied input and payoff rules, while explicitly leaving endogenous embodiment and fitness feedback unresolved. The conscious-agent network construction introduces memory and composition through specified kernels; its interpretation should retain that supplied structure. [[prentner-2019-using-ai-methods-to-evaluate-a-minimal-model-for-perception]], [[fields-2018-conscious-agent-networks-formal-analysis-and-application-to-cognition]]

> [!note] Editorial: Time comparison
> Memory-dependent temporal depth and reference-relative clocks are relevant to the information-clock discussion recorded in [[participatory-it-from-bit]]. They do not establish a formula equating clock rate with Fisher speed, accumulated KL, or a universal slowdown under aggregation. Any such correspondence must identify the clock observable, joint law, and aggregation assumptions.

The memory literature also includes a categorical construction of recall and planning as present operations constrained by observed and constructed events. A separate temporal-depth paper concerns remembered and anticipated horizons in a self-model, with clinical and computational validation deferred. These concern the organization and reach of memory; they do not by themselves define the same quantity as an information-geometric clock. [[dobson-2025-constructing-condensed-memories-in-functorial-time]], [[tolchinsky-2025-temporal-depth-in-a-coherent-self-and-in-depersonalization-theoretical-model]]

## Free energy and collective inference

Three uses of free energy must be kept distinct: a statistical variational bound for a normalized model, a physical energy budget for operating an agent, and a broader interpretation of persistent systems as active-inference systems. The generic-quantum FEP paper assumes quantum formalism; the thermodynamic-cost paper adds physical resources and control structure. Neither supplies a derivation of quantum mechanics from an arbitrary classical ELBO. [[fields-2022-a-free-energy-principle-for-generic-quantum-systems]], [[fields-2024-making-the-thermodynamic-cost-of-active-inference-explicit]]

Federated inference models communicated beliefs through an observation and action scheme. It is a useful comparator for interacting agents, but its likelihood and common-model assumptions are substantive. The earlier immutable note [[friston-2024-federated-inference]] overstates its relationship to the project's peer-KL energy when it calls that energy the same functional and identifies a flat-connection reduction. This synthesis does not adopt that equivalence. A reduction requires a common generative law, explicit message likelihoods, and treatment of dependence and reused evidence. [[Collective active inference]], [[dennis-2026-interacting-cue-result]]

The biological formation papers propose more specific mechanisms: protective somatic organization under supplied costs and exposure, compartmentalization through differentiated boundary reference frames, and neuromorphic development with morphological freedom and resource constraints. The evolutionary/developmental variational synthesis instead works with particular partitions, a nonequilibrium steady-state setting, timescale assumptions, and a genotype–phenotype likelihood. These are substantive constructions to compare; their hypotheses should remain attached when asking whether they yield a retained parent law in another model. [[fields-2019-somatic-multicellularity-as-a-satisficing-solution-to-the-prediction-error-minimization-problem]], [[fields-2024-the-free-energy-principle-induces-compartmentalization]], [[fields-2022-the-free-energy-principle-induces-neuromorphic-development]], [[friston-2023-a-variational-synthesis-of-evolutionary-and-developmental-dynamics]]

## What the geometry papers add

The communication/QECC work supplies error operators, encodings, and decoding conditions; the TQFT extension adds categorical and topological structures. The resulting geometric interpretation is a construction within those choices. In the gravity paper, ambient Lorentzian spacetime is introduced before the gravity specialization. In the ER=EPR paper, a finite-screen and monogamous-entanglement limit supports an operational topological identification. These are different claims from deriving dimension, Lorentzian signature, a physical clock, or Einstein dynamics from classical belief geometry. [[fields-2024-qecc-tqft-i-protocols]], [[fields-2024-qecc-tqft-ii-spacetimes]], [[addazi-2021-generalized-holographic-principle-gauge-invariance-and-the-emergence-of-gravity-a-la-wilczek]], [[fields-2025-er-epr-is-an-operational-theorem]]

The 2026 amplituhedra version extends the quantum-computation representation using TQNN and Reshetikhin–Turaev/Turaev–Viro structures. Its representation of computation and its claimed uniqueness of a geometric representation are separate assertions; gravity, anomalies, purity, isolation, and coarse-graining affect the stated scope. It is included as a research comparison, not as evidence that the project's Fisher geometry is already a scattering-amplitude geometry. [[fields-2026-amplituhedra-generic-quantum-processes]], [[Emergent spacetime and holography]]

## Claims to carry with their limitations

Several arguments encountered during reading should not enter the wiki as established universal results. The 2016 decompositional-equivalence paper's weighted-projector expression is not made unitary merely by normalizing its scalar coefficients, and its Born-rule discussion is explicitly pragmatic. The 2014 no-mereotopology argument uses a commutation condition that does not imply a product state: local factor projectors commute also on entangled states. The source notes preserve the proposed constructions and identify the affected pages. [[fields-2016-decompositional-equivalence-a-fundamental-symmetry-underlying-quantum-theory]], [[fields-2014-consistent-quantum-mechanics-admits-no-mereotopology]]

The frame/halting paper's displayed argument conflates predicting a next computational step with deciding eventual halting. The data-definition preprint's strongest quantum-speedup conclusions depend on its table and oracle access assumptions. These limitations do not erase the useful questions about unobserved environments, interpretation, and complete interface costs; they limit which conclusions can be imported. [[dietrich-2020-equivalence-of-the-frame-and-halting-problems]], [[fields-2010-computational-costs-of-data-definition-at-the-quantum-classical-interface]]

The perception papers likewise distinguish mathematical results under stipulated strategies and function ensembles from broad claims about reality. A MAP-based action rule differs from posterior expected-utility maximization; non-preservation by generic payoff functions is not a proof that every useful perceptual model discards accurate structure. The 2024 living-systems experiment reports no demonstrated entanglement. Consciousness discussions remain interpretations or research proposals rather than empirical consequences of the communication formalism. [[prakash-2021-fitness-beats-truth-in-the-evolution-of-perception]], [[prakash-2020-conscious-agents]], [[fields-2024-search-for-entanglement-between-spatially-separated-living-systems-experiment-design-results-and-les]], [[fields-2021-what-is-a-theory-of-consciousness-for]], [[fields-2026-the-question-is-not-are-they-conscious-but-what-could-they-be-conscious-of]]
